# 04 — Policies, Resources & Document Control

*Source: Official exam guide, sections 11-16 + 18. Yeh registration, exam-day rules, aur legal/renewal
policies ka full detail hai jo pehle single-file README mein sirf ek chhota summary tha.*

## Registration + Scheduling (Section 11)

Registration + scheduling **Anthropic Partner Academy** aur **Pearson VUE** ke zariye handle hoti hai:

1. Apne exam ke certification page par jao Anthropic Partner Academy par aur exam details review karo
2. Exam Guide download karo aur registration se pehle Certification Terms and Conditions + Certification
   Exam Policy review karo
3. Exam ke liye register karo aur checkout complete karo — checkout par dikhi fee aapke partner-tier
   discount reflect karti hai
4. Confirmation instructions follow karo apna Pearson VUE account banane ke liye, phir sign in karo apna
   exam session schedule karne ke liye
5. Ek available date choose karo aur online proctoring ya Pearson test center select karo — exam Pearson
   VUE deliver karta hai
6. Aap apni appointment se 24 hours pehle tak cancel/reschedule kar sakte ho. 24 hours ke andar changes
   exam fee forfeit kar dete hain

## Exam Policies (Section 12)

**Identification:** Exam day par ek valid, unexpired, government-issued photo ID present karni zaroori
hai. ID par naam registration ke naam se exactly match karna chahiye. Registration ka naam correct
karne ke liye, exam schedule karne se pehle `certifications-support@anthropic.com` contact karo.

**Accommodations:** Documented disabilities/needs wale candidates ke liye reasonable accommodations
available hain applicable law ke mutabiq. Accommodations exam schedule karne se **pehle** Pearson VUE
se request + approve honi chahiye — approval se pehle appointment schedule mat karo. Request:
`pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-takers/accommodations`.

**Retake policy:** Jo candidates pass nahi karte wo ek required waiting period ke baad retake kar sakte
hain. Waiting periods har failed attempt ke sath badhte hain: **14 din** pehli failure ke baad, **30 din**
doosri ke baad, **90 din** teesri ke baad. Aap ek exam **rolling 12-month period mein max 4 baar** de
sakte ho. Limits per-exam apply hoti hain — ek exam pass na karna aapko doosre exam ke liye register
karne se nahi rokta. Exam fee har attempt par apply hoti hai.

**No-show/late arrival:** Jo candidates scheduled exam mein nahi aate, ya permitted late-arrival window
ke baad arrive karte hain, exam fee forfeit karte hain aur dobara register karna parta hai.

## Exam-Day Experience + Rules of Conduct (Section 13)

Exam ek standardized, secure, proctored environment mein administer hota hai — online ho ya Pearson
VUE test center par, yeh rules apply hote hain credential ki integrity protect karne ke liye:

**Exam ke dauran zaroori hai:**
- Proctor aur webcam ki view mein rehna poori session ke liye (agar online test kar rahe ho)
- Workspace notes, books, phones, secondary monitors, aur doosre materials se clear rakhna
- Exam ke dauran kisi aur se communicate na karna
- Exam content ko kisi bhi form mein capture, copy, photograph, ya reproduce na karna

**Prohibited items:** mobile phones, smart watches, headphones, study materials, aur koi bhi recording
device. Permitted items (jaise proctor-provided scratch paper) Pearson VUE specify karta hai.

**Misconduct ke consequences:** Cheating, prohibited resources access karne ki koshish, ya exam
content disclose karna result invalidate kar sakta hai, credential revoke kar sakta hai, aur future
exams se ban kar sakta hai.

## Confidentiality + Non-Disclosure Agreement (Section 14)

Exam shuru hone se pehle ek confidentiality/NDA accept karna zaroori hai. Accept karne se, aap agree
karte ho ke sab exam content — questions, answer options, scenarios sameet — Anthropic ki confidential
aur proprietary property hai, aur aap iska koi bhi hissa disclose, reproduce, ya distribute nahi karoge.
**Agar agreement accept nahi karte, exam session end ho jata hai aur koi refund nahi milta.**

## Credential Maintenance + Recertification (Section 15)

CCAR-F credential award-date se **12 months** valid hai. Underlying technology rapidly evolve hoti hai
isliye credential time-limited hai taaki holders current knowledge maintain karein.

**On-time renewal:** aap review karte ho ke certify hone ke baad kya change hua, phir Anthropic Partner
Academy par ek **free, non-proctored assessment** complete karte ho. On-time renewal ki koi fee nahi
hai. **Agar credential lapse ho jaye,** full fee par poora exam dobara dena parta hai regain-certified-status
ke liye.

Agar exam content significantly change ho, Anthropic renewal-assessment ki bajaye holders ko full exam
retake karwa sakta hai recertify karne ke liye. Holders Section 13 ke conduct rules ke subject rehte hain.

## Candidate Support, Appeals + Privacy (Section 16)

**Support:** Registration ka naam correct karne ke liye `certifications-support@anthropic.com` contact
karo. Baaqi sab questions (registration, scheduling, accommodations, results) ke liye Pearson VUE
support: `pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic.html`.

**Appeals + complaints:** Aap ek decision ko **14 din** ke andar appeal kar sakte ho notification-date se
(ya exam-result concerns ke liye exam-date se 14 din). Appeal Pearson VUE support ko submit karo.
Appeals program ki appeals policy ke tehat review hoti hain. **Standard-setting outcome aur individual
exam items ka content appeal ke subject nahi hain.**

**Privacy:** Registration/testing ke dauran collect hone wala personal data Anthropic ki privacy policy,
Pearson VUE ki privacy policy, aur applicable data-protection law ke mutabiq handle hota hai.
