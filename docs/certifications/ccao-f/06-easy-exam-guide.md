## 3. 3 "Bare Patterns" Jo Baar-Baar Aate Hain

Exam ke sample questions dekho to ek hi shape baar-baar milti hai — yeh 3 patterns yaad rakho,
zyada tar scenario questions inhi mein se ek honge:

1. **"Claude confident tha, lekin..."** → hamesha **verify karo** bhejne se pehle (citations,
   numbers, specific-sounding facts) — self-reported confidence kabhi bhi verification ka
   substitute nahi.
2. **"Speed/cost matter karta hai, deep reasoning nahi"** → **chhota/sasta model** chuno, sabse
   capable model **hamesha** use karna galat hai — yeh cost/latency budget waste karta hai.
3. **"Sensitive data upload karna hai"** → **pehle redact/anonymize karo**, phir upload — "internal
   hai isliye theek hai" ek trap hai.

## 4. Registration — 6 Steps (Yaad Rakhne Layak Order)

1. Anthropic Partner Academy par apne exam ka page kholo
2. Exam Guide download karo + Terms/Policy padho
3. Register + checkout (partner-tier discount automatic apply hota hai)
4. Pearson VUE account banao (confirmation email se)
5. Date + delivery mode (online/test-center) choose karo
6. **24 ghante pehle tak free reschedule/cancel** — us window ke andar fee forfeit hoti hai

## 5. Exam Day — Chhoti Checklist

- [ ] Government photo ID, registration name se **exact match**
- [ ] Clear workspace — koi notes, phone, second monitor nahi
- [ ] Webcam ke view mein raho (agar online proctored)
- [ ] NDA accept karo shuru mein (na kiya to session khatam, refund nahi)
- [ ] Kisi se baat mat karo exam ke doraan
- [ ] Exam content kabhi copy/photograph mat karo (misconduct = credential revoke + ban)

## 6. Agar Fail Ho Jao

- Retake wait: **14 din** (1st fail) → **30 din** (2nd) → **90 din** (3rd)
- **4 attempts max** rolling 12 months mein, har attempt full fee
- Ek exam fail hona doosre exam (jaise CCAR-F) ko block nahi karta

## 7. Pass Hone Ke Baad

- 12 months valid — **on-time renewal free** hai (non-proctored refresher)
- Lapse ho jaye to full exam **full fee** par dobara dena parta hai — renewal date calendar mein
  daal do
