## Purpose Aur Value (Guide Section 2)

Yeh credential ek independent assessment hai ke koi individual Claude ko **real-world business
workflows mein effectively aur responsibly** use kar sakta hai. Earner yeh demonstrate karta hai:

- Structured business tasks minimal guidance ke sath complete karna
- Built-in platform features/tools se workflows streamline karna
- Claude use kar ke process-improvement opportunities identify karna
- Quality/efficiency/cost balance karne wale approaches select karna
- Limitations recognize kar zyada complex/technical implementations escalate karna

## Kya Test Hota Hai

Validate karta hai ke koi individual **Claude ko business/productivity tasks complete karne ke liye
apply kar sakta hai, minimal guidance ke sath**: built-in platform features/tools se workflows
streamline karna; process-improvement opportunities identify karna; quality/efficiency/cost balance
karna; limitations recognize kar **Claude Architects/Developers ko escalate karna**.

## Intended Audience (Guide Section 3)

Roles jaise operations, marketing, project management, education, communications, aur general
knowledge work — jo Claude ko **core productivity tool** ki tarah use karte hain aur apne roz ke kaam
mein Claude Projects banate hain. Audience mein 2 tarah ke log shamil hain:

- **Internal staff** jo ongoing AI-enabled workflows maintain/optimize karte hain
- **External consultants** jo implementation, use-case identification, aur process redesign support
  karte hain

Candidates generally **limited-to-moderate technical expertise** rakhte hain — casual AI prompt users
aur technical AI practitioners ke beech mein khare hote hain. In ki pehchan yeh hai:

- Business objectives ko effective AI interactions mein translate karna
- Appropriate tools/features select karna
- Structured prompts banana
- AI-generated content ko critically evaluate karna
- Outputs ko different audiences ke liye adapt karna
- Pehchanna kab human expertise, validation, ya escalation zaroori hai

## MQC (Minimally Qualified Candidate) Profile (Guide Section 4)

MQC ek professional hai jo Claude ko core productivity tool ki tarah use karta hai aur real-world
workflows mein effectively apply kar sakta hai — efficiency, quality, aur outcomes improve karne ke
liye. Foundational, applied knowledge rakhta hai: prompt structuring, task orchestration, Projects/
Artifacts/workflow-based interactions se familiarity. Basic Q&A usage se aage badh kar **process
reimagination, task automation, aur project development** tak jata hai.

MQC organizational context bhi samajhta hai — Claude kaise value create karta hai, adoption risks
kahan hain, aur usage ko business needs + responsible-AI practices ke sath kaise align karna hai.

**Recommended experience** (mandatory nahi):

- Regular, hands-on Claude experience professional setting mein
- Structured problem-solving, workflow design, digital-tool usage ki foundational understanding
- Roles jaise business analyst, project manager, operations lead, marketing/communications/HR/
  education professional, consultant, ya knowledge worker
- AI limitations ki practical understanding — hallucinations, context constraints, data sensitivity

**Prerequisites: koi mandatory nahi** — na koi required course, na software-development/API
experience chahiye. Upar wala experience *recommended* hai, *required* nahi. **Credential sirf exam
performance pe award hota hai.**

## Not Intended For

Software developers jo APIs ke against build karte hain ya agentic systems design karte hain, na hi
ML/software-engineering/advanced-AI-system-design specialists. Candidates se enterprise-scale AI
architectures/integrations design karne ki expectation nahi hai — **yeh scope Claude Architect aur
Claude Developer credentials ka hai**, jahan Associates complex/technical kaam escalate karte hain.

## Non-Technical Tier Note

**CCAO-F Claude Partner Network tier eligibility mein count nahi hota** — sirf teen technical
credentials (Developer Foundations, Architect Foundations, Architect Professional) count hote hain.
