## In-Scope vs Out-of-Scope

**CCAO-F ka official exam guide, CCAR-F ke unlike, ek explicit in-scope/out-of-scope appendix nahi
carry karta.** Scope sirf [Section 6 domain blueprint](01-domain-blueprint.md) ke objectives se
define hota hai — agar koi topic un 7 domains ke objectives mein explicitly list nahi, wo tested nahi
hoga (jaise: API integration, agent/tool building, ML model internals — yeh sab "Not Intended For"
audience statement se implicitly out-of-scope hain, dekho [00](00-quick-facts-and-audience.md)).

## How the Exam Is Scored (Guide Section 9)

**Criterion-referenced assessment** — har candidate ek fixed performance standard ke against measure
hota hai, doosre candidates se compete nahi karte. Pass hone ka matlab hai blueprint mein defined
knowledge/skills demonstrate karna, kisi percentage of peers ko outperform karna nahi.

**Passing standard:** Cut score (720) ek formal standard-setting study se aaya — trained subject-matter
experts ne judge kiya ke minimally-qualified candidate se kis level ki performance expect ki jaati hai.
Score 100–1,000 scale pe report hota hai.

**Result reporting:** Pass/fail status + scaled score (100–1,000), plus har content domain mein
percent-correct. **Domain-level percentages sirf understanding ke liye hain — pass/fail decide karne
mein use nahi hote, sirf total scaled score karta hai.**
