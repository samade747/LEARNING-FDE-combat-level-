# 04 — Policies, Resources + Doc Control (Guide Sections 10–16)

## Registration and Scheduling (Section 10)

Anthropic Partner Academy + Pearson VUE ke zariye:

1. Anthropic Partner Academy pe apne exam ka certification page kholo, exam details review karo
2. Exam Guide download karo, Certification Terms and Conditions + Certification Exam Policy padho
   register karne se pehle
3. Exam ke liye register karo, checkout complete karo (fee mein partner-tier discount reflect hota hai)
4. Confirmation instructions follow kar ke Pearson VUE account banao, sign in kar ke session schedule
   karo
5. Available date choose karo — online proctoring ya Pearson test center
6. **24 hours+ pehle free cancel/reschedule** — 24 hours ke andar changes exam fee forfeit karti hain

## Exam Policies (Section 11)

- **Identification:** valid, unexpired, government-issued photo ID chahiye — registration ka naam
  exactly match hona chahiye. Naam correct karna ho to `certifications-support@anthropic.com` pe
  contact karo scheduling se pehle
- **Accommodations:** documented disabilities/needs ke liye available, Pearson VUE se pehle
  request+approve karwana zaroori (schedule na karo jab tak approve na ho) —
  pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-takers/accommodations
- **Retake policy:** rolling 12 months mein max **4 attempts**; waits **14/30/90 din** (1st/2nd/3rd
  failure ke baad); limits per-exam hain, ek exam fail hona doosre exam ke liye register karne se
  nahi rokta; har attempt pe fee lagti hai
- **No-show/late arrival:** scheduled exam miss karna ya late-arrival window ke baad aana = fee forfeit
  + re-register

## Exam-Day Experience + Rules of Conduct (Section 12)

Standardized, secure, proctored environment — online ho ya test center, dono mein:

- **During exam:** proctor/webcam view mein rehna (online), workspace clear (koi notes/books/phones/
  secondary monitors), koi communication nahi, exam content capture/copy/photograph/reproduce mana hai
- **Prohibited items:** mobile phones, smart watches, headphones, study materials, recording devices
- **Consequences of misconduct:** cheating, prohibited resources access, exam-content disclosure —
  result invalidation, credential revocation, future-exam ban

## Confidentiality + NDA (Section 13)

Exam shuru hone se pehle confidentiality/non-disclosure agreement accept karna zaroori hai — sab exam
content (questions, options, scenarios) Anthropic ki confidential/proprietary property hai. **Decline
karne par session end ho jata hai, koi refund nahi.**

## Credential Maintenance + Recertification (Section 14)

12-month validity, award-date se. **On-time renewal:** free, non-proctored assessment (Anthropic
Partner Academy pe) — review karo kya change hua hai. **Lapse hone par:** full exam full fee pe dobara
dena padta hai. Agar exam content significantly change ho, Anthropic renewal-assessment ke bajaye full
retake bhi require kar sakta hai.

## Support, Appeals, Privacy (Section 15)

- **Support:** naam-correction `certifications-support@anthropic.com`; baaqi sab (registration,
  scheduling, accommodations, results) Pearson VUE support — pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic.html
- **Appeals:** decision-notification ke 14 din ke andar (ya exam-result concern ke liye exam-date ke 14
  din ke andar), Pearson VUE support ke zariye. **Standard-setting outcome aur individual exam items
  appeal ke subject nahi hain.**
- **Privacy:** Anthropic + Pearson VUE privacy policies + applicable data-protection law ke mutabiq
