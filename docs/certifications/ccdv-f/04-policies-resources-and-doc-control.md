# 04 — Policies, Resources & Document Control

## Registration and Scheduling (Official Guide, Section 10)

Registration + scheduling **Anthropic Partner Academy** aur **Pearson VUE** ke zariye hote hain:

1. Apne exam ki Anthropic Partner Academy certification page pe jao, exam details review karo
2. Exam Guide download karo, Certification Terms and Conditions + Certification Exam Policy parho
   registration se pehle
3. Exam register + checkout complete karo (fee partner-tier discount reflect karta hai)
4. Confirmation instructions follow karke Pearson VUE account banao, sign in karke exam session
   schedule karo
5. Available date choose karo — online proctoring ya Pearson test center
6. **24 hours+ pehle free reschedule/cancel; iske andar exam fee forfeit**

## Exam Policies (Full Detail)

- **ID:** government-issued photo ID, expired nahi honi chahiye; registration name se exactly match
  karna zaroori — mismatch ho to `certifications-support@anthropic.com` pe contact karo scheduling se
  pehle
- **Accommodations:** documented disabilities/needs ke liye available — Pearson VUE se pehle approve
  hona zaroori, approval se pehle schedule mat karo (pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-takers/accommodations)
- **Retakes:** rolling 12 months mein max 4 attempts; waits 14/30/90 din (1st/2nd/3rd failure ke baad);
  limits per-exam apply hote hain (ek exam fail hona doosre exam ko block nahi karta); fee har attempt
  pe lagti hai
- **No-show/late arrival:** exam fee forfeit, dobara register karna padta hai
- **Conduct (online + test center dono):** webcam view mein rehna (online), workspace clear (notes/
  books/phones/secondary-monitors mana), koi communication nahi kisi doosre se, content capture/copy/
  photograph/reproduce mana hai
- **Prohibited items:** phones, smart watches, headphones, study materials, recording devices — sirf
  proctor-provided scratch paper jaisi cheezein allowed
- **Misconduct consequences:** result invalidation, credential revocation, future-exam ban
- **NDA:** exam shuru hone se pehle accept karna zaroori — content confidential/proprietary hai;
  decline = session end, **no refund**
- **Appeals:** decision ke 14 din ke andar (exam-result concern ho to exam date ke 14 din andar),
  Pearson VUE support ke zariye; **standard-setting outcome aur individual item content appeal ke
  scope mein nahi**
- **Renewal:** on-time free non-proctored refresher assessment; lapse ho jaye to full exam full fee pe
  dobara
