# 02 — Scope, Scoring & Book Gaps

## In-Scope vs Out-of-Scope

**Correction vs is chapter ke pehle draft:** CCDV-F ke official exam guide (v1.0, 16 sections) mein
[CCAR-F](../ccar-f/README.md) jaisi ek explicit "in-scope vs out-of-scope appendix" **nahi hai** — poora
guide dobara `Read` tool se verify kiya gaya. Scope Section 6 ke domain/skill descriptions se hi implicit
hai (jo bhi 8 domains ke sub-skills mein list hai, wahi in-scope hai). Agar aage koi revision (v1.1+)
explicit appendix add kare to yahan update karo.

## How the Exam Is Scored

**Criterion-referenced** — fixed performance standard, doosre candidates se compete nahi karte. Cut
score 720 ek formal standard-setting study se aaya (trained subject-matter experts ne minimally-
qualified-candidate ka performance level judge kiya, scaled range 100–1,000 pe).

Score report **pass/fail + scaled score + domain-by-domain percent-correct** deta hai — lekin domain
percentages pass/fail decide nahi karte, sirf total scaled score karta hai.
