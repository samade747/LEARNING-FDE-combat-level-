## "Same Blueprint, One Level Up"

Book: PCAO-F **poora CCAO-F blueprint** examine karta hai (published weights pe), **phir aage** —
vendor-neutral. Study guide Claude aur ChatGPT ko side by side rakhta hai. Product-surface question
ki jagah architecture question; product feature ki jagah evaluation discipline; ek vendor ki
usage-policy ki jagah regulated client mein governance obligation. **CCAO-F wo credential hai jo
employer Anthropic ke registry mein verify kar sakta hai; PCAO-F uske neeche khada hai** — readiness
gate + internship qualification.

## Kyun Pehle

Associate Foundations wo baseline judgment establish karti hai jo har AI professional ko specialize
karne se pehle aani chahiye: effective prompting, output evaluation + validation, hallucination /
inconsistency detection, results validation, appropriate workflow selection, product capabilities +
limitations, governance + responsible use, aur "kab human review chahiye". PCAR-F ki architecture
usi judgment par bnti hai.

## Domain Weights (CCAO-F Blueprint — Guide's Own 1–7 Order)

Gen 61 pe book page bhi yeh table publish karta hai (guide ki apni numbering, weights = "approximate
proportions of the scored items"):

| # | Content Domain | Weight |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | Prompting and Task Execution | 14% |
| 2 | **Output Evaluation and Validation** | **21%** |
| 3 | Product and Model Selection | 12% |
| 4 | Workflow Integration and Solution Design | 16% |
| 5 | Configuration and Knowledge Management | 12% |
| 6 | Governance, Risk, and Responsible Use | 15% |
| 7 | Troubleshooting and Optimization | 10% |

**Domain 2 + 4 + 6 = 52% of the exam.** Sab se bara domain prompt-likhna nahi — output evaluate
karna hai. **No per-domain minimum** — result total scaled score vs 720. Poori objective-level
detail: [`../ccao-f/01-domain-blueprint.md`](../ccao-f/01-domain-blueprint.md).

Panaversity sitting saat domains + aage: product/model selection → "class of decision" (speed ka
cost, long context ka cost, session restart vs stretch); configuration/knowledge management →
instruction+knowledge layer in general; governance → regulated-client obligation.
