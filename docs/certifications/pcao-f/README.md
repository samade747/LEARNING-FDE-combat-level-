# PCAO-F — Panaversity Certified Associate: Foundations

*Source: book page **[`/docs/certifications/pcao-f`](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/certifications/pcao-f)**.
Re-fetched 2026-09-13 (Zia Tutor corpus **gen 71**, full lesson via `read_agent_factory_lesson`,
all 8 book sections in one window). Pichla sync 2026-09-02 tha (gen 61) — us waqt sirf ek
one-paragraph summary tha; ab neeche har book section apni jagah doc hui hai.*

Yeh FDE path ka **pehla exam** hai, aur sirf ek layer test karta hai: **judgment**. AI use karna,
uska output evaluate karna, aur usay kahan operate karne dena hai — yeh govern karna. **Build karna
nahi.** Likha gaya hai us insaan ke liye jo already roz AI ke sath kaam karta hai bina integration
code likhe — operations, marketing, project management, communications, teaching, consulting,
analysis. Yeh do logon ko cover karta hai: wo internal staffer jo AI-enabled workflow ko honest
rakhta hai, aur wo external consultant jo usay redesign karne aata hai.

**Yeh ek stranger ko kya prove karta hai — narrow aur useful hai.** Jisne kabhi aapka kaam nahi
dekha, wo verify kar sakta hai ke ek independent proctored exam ne yeh confirm kiya: aap achhe
output ko plausible-lekin-galat se alag bata sakte ho, use move hone se pehle validate kar sakte
ho, aur jaante ho ke kab human review chahiye.

## "Same Blueprint, One Level Up"

Book: PCAO-F **poora CCAO-F blueprint** examine karta hai (published weights pe), **phir aage** —
vendor-neutral. Study guide Claude aur ChatGPT ko side by side rakhta hai. Product-surface question
ki jagah architecture question; product feature ki jagah evaluation discipline; ek vendor ki
usage-policy ki jagah regulated client mein governance obligation. **CCAO-F wo credential hai jo
employer Anthropic ke registry mein verify kar sakta hai; PCAO-F uske neeche khada hai** — readiness
gate + internship qualification.

|                | PCAO-F | CCAO-F |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run by | Panaversity, independently administered + proctored | Anthropic, delivered by Pearson VUE |
| Blueprint | Har CCAO-F objective published weights pe, phir product surface se upar | Saat published domains |
| Cut score | 720 / 1000 | 720 / 1000 |
| Cost | Panaversity students: 2 free attempts; retake + external proctoring fee abhi announce nahi | $99 list price |
| Kis liye | Panaversity ka readiness gate + FDE Internship Program qualification | Wo credential jo employer Anthropic ke sath verify karta hai |

**Gate do wajah se hai:**

1. Ek Anthropic attempt fee kharch karta hai, aur fail hone par waqt bhi — retry wait **14 din
   (1st fail ke baad), 30 din (2nd), 90 din (3rd)**. Panaversity ka same-blueprint proctored exam —
   students ke pehle 2 attempts free — sasta tareeqa hai yeh pata karne ka ke aap kahan khare ho.
2. Credential khud — Panaversity apna naam aur internship un logon ke peeche khara karta hai jo
   pass karte hain, isliye standard ka matlab hona zaroori hai.

## Domain Weights (CCAO-F Blueprint — Guide's Own 1–7 Order)

Gen 71 pe book page ab har domain ke sath **"where it is taught"** bhi deta hai — course jahan us
domain ka centre of mass hai, phir wo 1-2 course jo baaqi objectives carry karte hain:

| # | Content Domain | Weight | Kahan Padhaya Jata Hai |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Prompting and Task Execution | 14% | AI Prompting in 2026 · Just Delegate It · AI Fluency |
| 2 | **Output Evaluation and Validation** | **21%** | AI Fluency · Just Delegate It · Governance (numbers jin par decision khara hai) |
| 3 | Product and Model Selection | 12% | ChatGPT & Claude Quick Reference · What AI Actually Is · AI Prompting in 2026 |
| 4 | Workflow Integration and Solution Design | 16% | Workflow Design & Diagnosis · AI Prompting in 2026 · Governance |
| 5 | Configuration and Knowledge Management | 12% | What AI Actually Is (appendix) · Skills & Connectors · Quick Reference |
| 6 | Governance, Risk, and Responsible Use | 15% | Governance · Just Delegate It · AI Fluency · Code You Never Write |
| 7 | Troubleshooting and Optimization | 10% | Workflow Design & Diagnosis · AI Prompting in 2026 · General Agents on the Web · Skills & Connectors |

**Domain 2 + 4 + 6 = 52% of the exam** — aur inhi teeno domains ka centre-of-mass teen courses mein
hai: AI Fluency, Workflow Design & Diagnosis, aur Governance. Yeh percentages se bhi sharper study
hint hai. **No per-domain minimum** — result total scaled score vs 720 hai; ek weak domain akela
fail nahi karega, lekin agle question-draw pe wohi domain fail karwa sakta hai agar strong total
uske peeche chhupa ho. Poori objective-level detail:
[`../ccao-f/01-domain-blueprint.md`](../ccao-f/01-domain-blueprint.md).

Panaversity sitting saat domains + aage: product/model selection → "class of decision" (speed ka
cost, long context ka cost, session restart vs stretch); configuration/knowledge management →
instruction+knowledge layer in general; governance → regulated-client obligation. Upar ek
professional layer bhi hai: client ko pehle batao system kya **nahi** karega, is se pehle ke woh
khud pata karein.

## Schedule Your Exam

PCAO-F proctored hai. Seat tak do routes hain:

- **Enrolled** (matching Panaversity course mein): attempt **free**, teacher ke sath arrange karo
- **Not enrolled**: ek attempt **buy** karo, one-on-one proctoring ke sath sit karo

Enrollment sirf **free attempts** ka criterion hai, exam access ka nahi — external candidate bhi
sit kar sakta hai.

## After You Pass

Agla: [PCAR-F](../pcar-f/README.md) — Architect Foundations. Order deliberate hai: PCAO-F poochta
hai ke kya aap AI responsibly use + evaluate kar sakte ho; PCAR-F poochta hai ke kya aap wo system
design kar sakte ho jismein AI chalega. Judgment pehle, architecture doosra.

Dono pass karne se **Panaversity qualification stage** complete hoti hai — jo **FDE Internship
Program** ke liye qualify karta hai.

**Internship mechanically bhi matter karta hai:** Anthropic registration ke liye eligible
**organisational account** chahiye — abhi koi **individual sign-up route hai hi nahi**. Panaversity
qualifying internship participants ki registration mein assist karta hai. Phir, agar choose karo,
official pair usi order mein: CCAO-F, phir CCAR-F.

**Ek honest limit:** PCAO-F ek readiness credential + access gate hai — wo cheez nahi jo employer
Anthropic ke apne registry mein dekhta hai. Wo **CCAO-F** hai. Yeh exam sirf itna hai: wahan tak
pahunchne ka tareeqa, bina ek attempt kharch kiye yeh pata karne ke liye ke aap kahan khare ho.
