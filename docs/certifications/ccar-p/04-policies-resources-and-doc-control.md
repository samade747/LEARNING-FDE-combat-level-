# 04 — Registration, Policies, Resources, aur Document Control

## Registration and Scheduling (Section 10)

Registration aur scheduling **Anthropic Partner Academy** aur **Pearson VUE** ke zariye hoti hai:

1. Apne exam ki certification page Anthropic Partner Academy pe jao aur exam details review karo
2. Exam Guide download karo aur Certification Terms and Conditions + Certification Exam Policy
   registration se pehle parho
3. Exam ke liye register karo aur checkout complete karo — checkout pe dikhne wali fee aapke partner
   tier ka discount reflect karti hai
4. Confirmation instructions follow karke apna Pearson VUE account banao, phir sign in karke apna
   exam session schedule karo
5. Available date choose karo aur online proctoring ya Pearson test center select karo — exam Pearson
   VUE deliver karta hai
6. Appointment se **24 hours pehle** cancel/reschedule kar sakte ho free mein; usse andar changes
   **exam fee forfeit** kar dete hain

## Exam Policies (Section 11)

**Identification:** Government-issued photo ID zaroori hai, valid aur unexpired. ID pe naam
registration ke naam se **exactly match** hona chahiye. Correction ke liye
`certifications-support@anthropic.com` pe contact karo, scheduling se pehle.

**Accommodations:** Documented disabilities/needs wale candidates ke liye reasonable accommodations
available hain, applicable law ke mutabiq. Accommodations **schedule karne se pehle** Pearson VUE se
request + approve karwane zaroori hain — approval se pehle appointment schedule mat karo.
(`pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-takers/accommodations`)

**Retake policy:** Fail hone wale candidates ek required waiting period ke baad retake kar sakte hain —
**14 din** pehli failure ke baad, **30 din** doosri ke baad, **90 din** teesri ke baad. Rolling
12-month period mein max **4 attempts** per exam. Limits per-exam apply hoti hain — ek exam fail hona
doosre exam mein register karne se rokta nahi. Har attempt pe exam fee lagti hai.

**No-show/late arrival:** Scheduled exam miss karna ya permitted late-arrival window ke baad aana —
exam fee forfeit hoti hai aur dobara register karna padta hai.

## Exam-Day Rules of Conduct (Section 12)

Exam ek standardized, secure, proctored environment mein hota hai — online ho ya Pearson VUE test
center pe, yeh rules apply hote hain:

**Exam ke dauran zaroori hai:**
- Online testing ke case mein poori session proctor aur webcam ke view mein rehna
- Workspace clear rakhna — notes, books, phones, secondary monitors, aur baaqi materials se
- Kisi bhi doosre insaan se communicate na karna
- Exam content ka koi bhi hissa capture/copy/photograph/reproduce na karna kisi bhi form mein

**Prohibited items:** mobile phones, smart watches, headphones, study materials, aur koi bhi recording
device. Permitted items (jaise proctor-provided scratch paper) Pearson VUE specify karta hai.

**Consequences of misconduct:** Cheating, prohibited resources access karne ki koshish, ya exam content
disclose karna — result invalidation, credential revocation, aur future exams se ban ho sakta hai.

## Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement (Section 13)

Exam shuru hone se pehle ek **confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement accept karna zaroori hai**.
Accept karke aap agree karte ho ke sab exam content (questions, answer options, scenarios) Anthropic ki
confidential aur proprietary property hai, aur aap uska koi hissa disclose/reproduce/distribute nahi
karoge. Agar agreement accept nahi karte, **exam session end ho jata hai aur koi refund nahi milta**.

## Credential Maintenance and Recertification (Section 14)

Credential **12 months** valid hai award date se. Technology fast evolve hoti hai isliye credential
time-limited rakha gaya hai taake holders current knowledge maintain karein.

**On-time renew** karne ke liye: certify hone ke baad kya change hua review karo aur Anthropic Partner
Academy pe ek **free, non-proctored assessment** complete karo. On-time renewal ki koi fee nahi.
**Agar credential lapse ho jaye, full fee pe full exam dobara dena padta hai.**

Agar exam content significantly change ho, Anthropic holders ko **renewal assessment ki bajaye full
exam retake** karne ka bhi require kar sakta hai. Holders hamesha Section 12 ke rules of conduct ke
subject rehte hain.

## Candidate Support, Appeals, and Privacy (Section 15)

**Support:** Registration ka naam correct karwane ke liye `certifications-support@anthropic.com`.
Baaqi sab (registration, scheduling, accommodations, results) ke liye Pearson VUE support —
`pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic.html`.

**Appeals:** Decision ke notify hone ki date se **14 din** ke andar, ya exam result concern ke liye
exam date se 14 din ke andar appeal kar sakte ho — Pearson VUE support ke zariye submit karo. **Standard-
setting outcome aur individual exam items ka content appeal ke subject nahi hain.**

**Privacy:** Registration aur testing ke dauran collect hui personal data Anthropic ki privacy policy,
Pearson VUE ki privacy policy, aur applicable data-protection law ke mutabiq handle hoti hai.
