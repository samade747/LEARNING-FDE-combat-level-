## Kya Test Hota Hai (Section 1 — About + Section 2 — Purpose and Value)

Yeh credential validate karta hai ke koi individual **production-grade AI solutions design, build, aur
deliver** kar sakta hai Anthropic ke Claude platform pe — ek architect role mein jo appropriate
models/architectures/API patterns select karta hai, prompt aur context engineering apply karta hai,
Claude ko enterprise systems mein integrate karta hai, aur evaluation, security, compliance, aur
governance considerations apne designs mein shamil karta hai.

Credential earn karne wale candidates yeh demonstrate kar sakte hain:

- Real business problems address karne wale AI-driven solutions **design aur prototype** karna
- Specific use cases ke liye appropriate **models, architectures, aur API patterns select** karna
- Model behavior guide karne ke liye **prompt engineering aur context strategies implement** karna
- Claude ko production systems mein **integrate** karna — APIs, orchestration tools, data pipelines
- Solution quality ensure karne ke liye **evaluation, monitoring, aur observability practices apply** karna
- System design mein **security, compliance, aur governance considerations** incorporate karna
- Cross-functional stakeholders ke sath **collaborate** karna aur architectural decisions **effectively
  communicate** karna

**Is Chapter ka farq CCAR-F se:** CCAR-F "design/implement" tak focused hai (solution architect ka
technical scope); CCAR-P **poori lifecycle ownership** test karta hai — discovery se lekar
governance/compliance/stakeholder-facing decisions tak.

## Intended Audience (Section 3)

Credential **mid- se senior-level technical professionals** ke liye hai jo large language models
(khaas kar Claude) use karke production-grade AI solutions design/build/deliver karte hain. Primary
audience: **solution architects, AI/ML engineers, technical leads, aur senior software engineers** jo
business requirements aur technical implementation ke intersection pe kaam karte hain.

Yeh professionals business problems ko scalable AI-driven solutions mein translate karte hain — model
selection, prompt engineering, tools/agents ka orchestration, context management, aur system
safety/compliance/governance ensure karna sab shamil hai. Yeh aksar **stakeholder engagement** mein
involved hote hain — clients ya internal teams ko advise karna, architectural decisions lead karna
(security, legal, aur executive discussions sameet). Candidates typically in industries kaam karte
hain: **financial services, healthcare, retail, technology, education, aur government**.

**Not intended for:** entry-level developers, Claude-based applications ke casual users, ya aise log
jinke paas end-to-end AI systems design karne ka experience na ho. Yeh un roles ko bhi exclude karta
hai jo purely non-technical hain ya isolated tasks (jaise prompt-writing bina broader system-design
responsibility ke) tak limited hain.

## Minimally Qualified Candidate (MQC) Profile (Section 4)

MQC ek **experienced practitioner** hai jo engineering mindset ko practical, real-world AI deployment
experience ke sath combine karta hai. MQC Claude-powered AI solutions ko production environments mein
**design, implement, aur govern** karta hai — business requirements ko scalable, secure, aur reliable
architectures mein translate karte hue.

MQC yeh demonstrate karta hai: end-to-end AI systems design karna — prompt aur context engineering,
**retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)**, API integration, orchestration, aur evaluation frameworks —
aur cost, latency, performance, safety, aur maintainability ke tradeoffs samajhna.

### Recommended Experience

- Software engineering best practices ki foundation (modular design, separation of concerns, scalability)
- **3+ saal** systems architecture ya platform engineering ka experience
- **6+ months** hands-on experience Claude ya comparable LLM-based systems ke sath **production mein**
- End-to-end systems deliver karne ka experience — discovery se lekar deployment aur
  operationalization tak

### Prerequisites

**Koi mandatory prerequisite ya course nahi hai** is exam ke liye. Upar wala experience recommended
hai, required nahi. Credential sirf exam performance ke basis pe award hota hai.
