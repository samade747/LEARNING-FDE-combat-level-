## In-Scope vs Out-of-Scope — Explicit Appendix Nahi Hai

**CCAR-F ke guide mein ek dedicated in-scope/out-of-scope appendix tha** (fine-tuning, model internals,
tokenization wagera explicitly out-of-scope declare kiye gaye thay). **CCAR-P ke exam guide (v1.0,
July 2026) mein aisa koi dedicated appendix nahi hai** — scope sirf Section 6 ke 7 domains + unke
detailed objectives se define hoti hai (dekho [01-domain-blueprint.md](01-domain-blueprint.md)). Agar
koi topic un objectives ke bahar hai, safe assumption yehi hai ke wo out-of-scope hai — jaisa CCAR-F
mein explicit tha (fine-tuning/training, API billing internals, Constitutional AI/RLHF, model weights,
tokenization algorithms, wagera), CCAR-P mein bhi wahi cheezein tested nahi hoti honge, lekin guide
khud isay likh kar confirm nahi karta.

## How the Exam Is Scored (Official Guide, Section 9)

**Criterion-referenced assessment** — har candidate ek **fixed performance standard** ke against
measure hota hai, doosre candidates se compete nahi karta. Pass karne ka matlab hai blueprint mein
defined knowledge/skills demonstrate karna, kisi percentage of peers ko outperform karna nahi.

**Passing standard:** Cut score (720) ek formal **standard-setting study** se establish hua — trained
subject-matter experts ne judge kiya ke minimally-qualified candidate (MQC) se kis level ka performance
expect hota hai. Score scaled range 100–1,000 pe report hota hai.

**Result reporting:** Pass/fail status + scaled score (100-1,000), plus har content domain mein
percent-correct. **Domain-level percentages sirf performance samajhne ke liye hain — pass/fail decide
nahi karte**, sirf total scaled score karta hai.
