### Q1. Panaversity student, PCAR-F 1st attempt — cost?

**Q:** Ek Panaversity student apna pehla PCAR-F attempt de raha hai. Kitna kharch hoga?

- Proctoring fee (TBA)
- **Free — Panaversity students ko har exam ke 2 free attempts milte hain** ✅
- $125, jaisa CCAR-F
- Free sirf agar PCDV-F bhi ek sath diya jaye

**Explanation:** PCAR-F Panaversity ka apna proctored exam hai, CCAR-F (Anthropic, $125) se alag.
Panaversity students ko har exam ke pehle 2 attempts free milte hain — yeh unlimited attempts se
bachne ke liye jaan-boojh kar rakha gaya hai (real second chance, lekin trial-and-error nahi).
*(Source: README.md — Quick Facts)*

### Q2. PCAR-F pehle kyun, PCDV-F baad mein?

**Q:** Kyun PCAR-F ko Stage One mein **pehla** exam rakha gaya hai, PCDV-F se pehle?

- Alphabetical order
- **Architect Foundations mental model deti hai (system design, agentic architecture, prompt/tool/
  context) jo Developer Foundations ki implementation-heavy questions ke liye zaroori hai** ✅
- PCDV-F harder hai isliye warm-up chahiye
- PCAR-F sasta hai isliye pehle diya jata hai (dono free hain)

**Explanation:** "Kyun Pehle" section explicit hai: architecture reasoning deti hai jo implementation
se pehle chahiye — pehle design karna seekho, phir banana.
*(Source: README.md — "Kyun Pehle")*

### Q4. Sabse bara domain, aur usay kitna study time milna chahiye?

**Q:** PCAR-F ka sabse bara-weight domain kaunsa hai, aur 6-week study plan usay kitna time deta hai?

- Prompt Engineering, 50% time
- **Agentic Architecture (27%) — sab se zyada time milna chahiye, proportion ke hisaab se** ✅
- Context Management, sab se kam time (15% hone ki wajah se)
- Sab domains ko equal time — weight matter nahi karta

**Explanation:** Agentic Architecture 27% ke sath sab se bara domain hai, aur book khud 4 alag
courses se isay sab se zyada cover karti hai. Domain-weighted study ka matlab proportional time hai.
*(Source: README.md — Domain Weights table)*

### Q5. PCAR-F pass karne ka matlab kya CCAR-F guarantee hai?

**Q:** Ek candidate PCAR-F mein 850/1000 score karta hai. Kya iska matlab CCAR-F mein bhi pass
guaranteed hai?

- Haan, 100% guarantee hai
- **Nahi guarantee — lekin agar PCAR-F pe 720 clear nahi hota, farz mat karo CCAR-F different jayega
  bhi; PCAR-F ek sasti, same-blueprint readiness-check hai, guarantee nahi** ✅
- Nahi, PCAR-F aur CCAR-F ka koi taluq nahi
- Haan, lekin sirf agar 900+ score ho

**Explanation:** High PCAR-F score confidence deta hai, guarantee nahi. Ulta zyada important hai:
agar PCAR-F pe fail ho, CCAR-F alag jaane ki umeed mat rakho.
*(Source: parent 01-stage-one-panaversity.md — "Why the Gate Exists")*

### Q8. Domain weights "subject to change without notice" ka matlab kya hai practically?

**Q:** Book note karti hai domain weights change ho sakte hain. Iska candidate ke liye practical
implication kya hai?

- Kuch nahi, weights kabhi actually nahi badalte
- **Exam book karne se pehle current official guide dobara download/check karo — study plan ek fixed

**Explanation:** Architect Foundations guide v0.1→v0.2→v1.0 kuch mahinon mein badal chuki hai. Isi
liye "book se current guide download karo, exam book karne se pehle" explicit instruction hai.
*(Source: parent 00-overview.md — "Version Note")*

### Q9. PCAR-F ke liye "real attempt ko practice ki tarah" use karna kyun mistake hai?

**Q:** Ek candidate apna pehla free PCAR-F attempt "bas dekhne ke liye kaisa hota hai" istemal karta
hai, bina serious prep ke. Yeh kyun sub-optimal hai?

- Kuch farq nahi padta, dono attempts free hain to koi loss nahi
- **Sirf 2 free attempts hain — teesra fee leta hai; ek attempt "dekhne ke liye" waste karna real
  second-chance budget kam kar deta hai** ✅
- PCAR-F attempts unlimited hain, koi mistake nahi
- Free sample tests available hi nahi hain, isliye real attempt hi practice hai

**Explanation:** Free sample tests exist karte hain exactly is mistake se bachne ke liye — unhe pehle
do, phir real 2 free PCAR-F attempts serious prep ke baad istemal karo.
*(Source: README.md — "Real-attempt rule")*
