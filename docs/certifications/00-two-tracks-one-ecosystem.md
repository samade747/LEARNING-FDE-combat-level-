# 00 — Do Tracks, Ek Ecosystem

Panaversity **do parallel certification tracks** offer karta hai. Kaunsa track aapke liye sahi hai, yeh depend karta hai aapke background par.

## Track 1 — Builders Ke Liye: Agentic AI Professional Track

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Courses** | AI-101 → AI-201 → AI-251 → AI-321 → AI-491 |
| **Duration** | 5 Courses |
| **Primary Tool** | Claude Code + OpenCode + OpenClaw + OpenAI Agents SDK + Paperclip |
| **Core Focus** | Use · Code · Build · Compose · Ship |
| **Builds** | Digital FTEs, AI Workforce, AI-Native Companies |
| **Claude Cert** | CCA-F (Anthropic, Level 5) ke liye prepare karta hai |
| **Final Cert** | Certified Agentic AI Architect |

Yeh track un logon ke liye hai jo asal mein **code likhte hain aur systems banate hain**. Progression ek assembly-line jaisi hai: pehle AI tools **use** karna seekho, phir Python mein **code** karna, phir Digital FTEs **build** karna, phir unhein AI-Native Companies mein **compose** karna, aur aakhir mein cloud scale par **ship** karna.

## Track 2 — Domain Experts Ke Liye: Agentic AI Business & Domain Expert Track

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Courses** | BAI-100 → BAI-200 → BAI-300 |
| **Duration** | 3 Quarters |
| **Primary Tool** | Claude Cowork |
| **Core Focus** | Thinking, domain knowledge, enterprise workflows |
| **Builds** | SKILL.md libraries, Cowork plugins, Agentic Office |
| **Claude Cert** | — (koi Anthropic-specific cert nahi) |
| **Final Cert** | Certified Agentic AI Business Strategist |

Yeh track un logon ke liye hai jo **code nahi likhte**, lekin apni industry ko gehraai se jaante hain — accountants, lawyers, consultants, operations managers. Yeh log Claude Cowork jaise no-code tools se apni domain knowledge ko SKILL.md files aur plugins mein tabdeel karte hain.

## Yeh Do Tracks Kyun Zaroori Hain FDE Ke Liye

[What You Carry In](../what-you-carry-in/README.md) chapter yaad karo: asli asset ek **profession ka governed knowledge** hai, jo tab hi banti hai jab ek technical builder ek real domain expert ke sath mil kar kaam kare. Professional Track us builder ko training deta hai. Business Track us domain expert ko training deta hai — taake woh apni knowledge ko SKILL.md aur governed content ki shakal mein likh sake.

Vendor-neutral vertical FDE dono halves ko apne andar combine karta hai, ya phir ek Professional-Track graduate ek Business-Track-trained (ya sirf domain-experienced) expert ke sath partnership banata hai — jaisa [How to Get Paid](../how-to-get-paid/README.md) mein Ayesha aur uski aunt ki misal mein dikhaya gaya.
