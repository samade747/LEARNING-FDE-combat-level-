# 00 — Overview: Yeh Reference Kya Hai

**ChatGPT & Claude Quick Reference** koi numbered course nahi hai — yeh ek **reference** hai, book ke
"References & Companions" group mein. Iska companion hai [Just Delegate It](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/just-delegate-it-crash-course),
Foundations ka pehla course, jo sikhata hai AI ko job dena aur result check karna. Woh course har
control introduce karta hai **jab ek job usay maangti hai**. Yeh page wahan hai jahan tum aao jab tumhe
**dono workspaces ka poora naqsha** ek sath chahiye ho.

Yeh page zaroori nahi hai Foundations khatam karne ke liye. Tum already basic idea jante ho: AI ko kaam
do, phir result check karo. Jo tumhe shayad nahi pata — **sab kuch kahan hai**.

## See It in Two Minutes

Tour shuru karne se pehle, cockpit ko khud apna introduction dene do.

Claude khol kar yeh paste karo:

```text
List every control I can see in this workspace right now, and tell me
in one line what each one is for. Just the controls, no advice.
```

Ab ChatGPT alag tab mein khol kar **exactly same** text paste karo. Dono lists side-by-side rakho.

Tumhe do vocabularies milenge, mostly **same saat cheezon** ke liye: ek "artifact" kehta hai, doosra
"writing block". Ek "connector" kehta hai, doosra "app". Ek "project knowledge" kehta hai, doosra
"project files". Dono "model", "memory", "instructions", "search", "research" kehte hain.

**Yeh poora reference isi ek screenshot mein hai.** Controls ke naam alag hain, lekin unse jo kaam karte
ho woh same kaam hai.

Ek honest warning — aur yeh kaam ki hai: assistant khud apne interface ke baare mein hamesha reliable
nahi hota, to list mein kuch aisa aa sakta hai jo wahan nahi hai, ya kuch miss ho sakta hai jo hai. Yeh
notice karo. **Confident answer ko asli cheez se check karna Concept 9 hai** — aur tum isse do minute mein
hi mil gaye.
