# 05 — Ayesha Ki Poori Walk, Definition of Ready, aur Honest Label

## Ayesha Poora Rasta Chalti Hai

**Plain words mein:** ek insan, poora rasta, do paragraphs mein. Ek step khaas dekho: uska pehla
customer uske expert se alag country mein hai. Yeh free nahi aata. Vertical ek profession ek country
mein hai, isliye border cross karna design reuse karne wala ek naya build hai, aur isay naye jagah apna
expert sign-off chahiye. Usne crossing ko apni cost wala repeat treat kiya aur usi tarah price kiya —
isi liye sale kaam kar gayi.

Aap Ayesha se har station par mil chuke ho. Yahan uski poori walk ek paragraph mein hai.

Woh graduate hoti hai aur role map parhti hai: FDE wahan hai jahan demand hai, aur vendor gate uske liye
band hai (Station 1). Woh ecosystem ko use kar ke seekhti hai — Zia Tutor AI se sawal poochti hai aur
apna agent book ke System of Record se connect karti hai (Station 2). Woh paanch layers seekhti hai aur
dekhti hai har ek kahan pay karta hai (Station 3). Ownership argument uska plan palat deta hai, isliye
woh résumé polish karna chhor kar expert dhoondna shuru karti hai (Station 4). Uski khala, 20 saal ki
accountant apni likhi hui material ke sath, jawab hain. Audit working-paper preparation screen, tests,
aur chaar gates pass karta hai, aur woh isay "accounting" ke bajaye ek profession-ek-jurisdiction ki
tarah scope karti hai. Customer support, uska sabse zyada screen score, uska service domain ban jata hai
aur poori walk ke doran uske bills pay karta hai (Station 5). Woh apni khala ki 41-item checklist par
archaeology chalati hai, teen bins mein sort karti hai, aur ek complete slice design karti hai (Station
6). Woh isay kernel par build karti hai: uski khala ka material ek Docusaurus site ki tarah jo uski khala
ke students parhte hain, aur wahi content jo uske Workers MCP par cite karte hain (Station 7).

Phir doors, aur yahan jurisdiction rule apni value prove karti hai. Uska pehla buyer ek mid-size Chicago
firm hai, remotely Lahore se serve ki gayi, aur ek US firm ke working papers uski khala ki jurisdiction
nahi. Isliye engagement model ke per-jurisdiction repeat ki tarah price hoti hai: slice ki shape carry
hoti hai, aur sources/invariants/checker US audit requirements ke liye rebuild hote hain, wahan qualified
ek reviewer sign-off ke sath. Firm ke andar woh apna connection pattern unki duniya mein fit karti hai
(Station 8): 22 saal ke working papers SharePoint mein indexed, trial balance live query hoti hai kabhi
indexed nahi, aur permissions ek first-year junior ki tarah test hoti hain kisi bhi jawab test hone se
pehle. 6 hafte baad ek Worker uske record se standard cite karta hai, firm ka 2023 lease memo alag se
prior treatment ki tarah attach karta hai, report karta hai ke dono disagree karte hain, aur partner ko
escalate karta hai — jo aisi treatment correct karta hai jo uski firm 3 saal se repeat kar rahi thi.
Working-paper file 4 ghante se 40 minute tak aati hai ek contract of success ke tehat. Engagement ek
retainer ban jata hai. Retainer domain startup ka pehla revenue ban jata hai jo woh aur uski khala saath
own karti hain (Station 9, teenon doors, order mein).

Us middle step ko dhyan se parho, kyunke yeh is poore rastay ki sabse common misreading hai. Ek vertical
ek profession ek jurisdiction mein hai. Border cross karna ek naya build hai jo design reuse karta hai,
purani ki free extension nahi, aur isay naye jurisdiction mein apna expert sign-off chahiye. Ayesha ki
pehli sale isliye kaam karti hai kyunke usne crossing ko apni cost wala repeat treat kiya, aur usi tarah
price kiya.

## The Definition of Ready — Checklist

Aap Station 9 ke doors kholne ke liye ready ho jab har box check ho jaye. Honestly check karo, kyunke
ek buyer karega:

- [ ] Aap FDE role, uski demand, aur vendor lock-in problem apne alfaz mein explain kar sakte ho.
- [ ] Aap ne [Getting Started](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/getting-started) route chal
  liya hai aur Mode 2 choose kiya hai, kyunke yeh rasta manufactured Workers becha hai.
- [ ] Aap ne us route se kam az kam ek horizontal capstone ship kiya hai: ek deployed Worker, ek plugin,
  ya ek connector-native app.
- [ ] Aap service ladder par Layer 1 aur Layer 4 kaam se kama rahe ho, ya kamane ke liye ready ho.
- [ ] Aapki vertical screen aur 8 tests pass kar chuki hai, ya honestly ek written condition ke sath
  parked hai.
- [ ] Aapke expert ne sign kiya hai — principle mein maana nahi, **signed**.
- [ ] Aapki slice ke har source ka documented rights basis hai.
- [ ] Aapki thin slice complete hai: outcome contract, invariants, reflex, checker, aur ugly cases wala
  evaluation set.
- [ ] Slice kernel par live hai: website ki tarah readable, MCP par citable.
- [ ] Aap bata sakte ho kya kisi khaas customer ko System of Context chahiye, aur us se connect hone
  wale sources naam le sakte ho.
- [ ] Aapka portfolio page slice se lead karta hai, aur har bullet ek outcome bayan karta hai, activity
  nahi.

## What This Page Does Not Cover

**Plain words mein:** teen cheezein yeh page kisi aur ke liye chhorta hai. Technology Getting Started
mein hai. Selling skills Strategist track mein hain. Aur aapke apne business ki legal/tax shape — company
register karna, contracts likhna, cross-border invoicing, payment lena — yeh real kaam hai jo yeh book
nahi sikhati. Rules country se country badalti hain aur aksar change hoti hain, isliye jaldi ek local
accountant ka ek ghanta khareed lo.

Do boundaries pehle naam li ja chuki hain: technology [Getting Started](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/getting-started)
ki hai, aur selling hissa Strategist track ka hai. Teesri naam nahi li gayi thi, aur yeh matter karti
hai: aapke apne business ki legal/tax shape — company registration, contracts, cross-border invoicing,
aur payment kaise lete ho — yeh real kaam hai jo yeh book nahi sikhati. Rules country se country badalti
hain aur aksar change hoti hain, isliye jaldi ek local accountant ka ek ghanta khareed lo. Ek engagement
jise aap legally invoice nahi kar sakte, income nahi hai.

## The Honest Label — Kya Measured Hai, Kya Sirf Reasoned

**Plain words mein:** **Measured:** role ki demand, pay bands, billion-dollar units, aur company AI
projects ki failure rate — yeh sab evidence hai, page ke neeche sourced. **Not measured:** ke ek governed
slice pehle client mein badalti hai — yeh book ki reasoning hai, kyunke category nayi hai aur abhi tak
kisi ne count nahi kiya. Claim ka safer version negative hai: slice ke bina, aap meeting le sakte ho aur
phir bhi bina kisi number ke chhor sakte ho. Aur pay table aapki income ka forecast nahi — jo employers
ne post kiya aur marketplaces jo advertise karte hain, wahi hai. Aapka apna pehla number aapke pehle
buyer ke sath discover hoga.

FDE role ki demand measured hai: posting counts, salary bands, aur billion-dollar units sab neeche aur
[roles page](../roles-this-book-trains/README.md) par sourced hain. Enterprise AI pilots ki failure rate
measured hai. Lock-in problem khud vendors ki apni stages se conceded hai. Yeh sab evidence hai.

Pay table ko apni ek line chahiye, kyunke table mein ek number jitna hard lagta hai utna hai nahi. Har
band ek real published figure hai, aur koi bhi aapki income ka forecast nahi. Salary bands woh hain jo
employers ne post kiye — aisi markets mein jahan is page ke zyada tar readers abhi apply nahi kar sakte.
Marketplace bands woh hain jo platform advertise karta hai, jo ek naya profile pehle mahine mein kamata
hai woh nahi. Startup row honestly khaali hai, kyunke is jitni jawan category ke liye kisi ne outcomes ki
distribution publish nahi ki. Table ko market ki prices ki tarah parho, aur apna pehla number us cheez ki
tarah jo aap apne pehle buyer ke sath discover karoge.

Services compression arithmetic ek projection hai, apne label ke sath udhar li gayi. Upar wala time
budget is book ke apne experience se draw ki gayi ek planning shape hai, graduates ke across measured
average nahi. Aur is roadmap ka central promise — ke governed slice pehla client mein convert hota hai —
statistic ke bajaye is book ki reasoning hai: category nayi hai, aur graduates jinhon ne conversion kiya
unka koi verified count abhi exist nahi karta. Negative claim safer hai. Ek reader jo achi slice banata
hai aur 3 mahine tak client ka intezar karta hai usne rasta ghalat sabit nahi kiya.

Ek aur claim ko apna label chahiye, kyunke yeh page ka load-bearing claim hai, sabse exciting nahi. Ke
service ladder expected outcome hai aur vertical exceptional — yeh ek judgment hai, jo rasta ko gate
karne wali cheez se draw ki gayi: expert access aur rights clearance, jinhein akela effort solve nahi kar
sakta. Yeh jaan-boojh kar judgment ki tarah state ki gayi hai. Aur yeh judgment hai jise hum number se
replace karne ka irada rakhte hain. Ecosystem hazaron readers train karta hai, isliye funnel measure ho
sakta hai — publishing empty hi commitment hai:

| Stage | Graduates |
| --- | --- |
| Manufacturing courses khatam kiye | abhi measure nahi hua |
| Ek candidate domain expert naam liya | abhi measure nahi hua |
| Ek signed expert agreement mila | abhi measure nahi hua |
| Ek documented rights basis clear kiya | abhi measure nahi hua |
| Ek live thin slice publish ki | abhi measure nahi hua |
| Pehla paid engagement close kiya | abhi measure nahi hua |

Har row ek number carry karegi, ya yeh label carry karegi. Koi story carry nahi karegi.

## Where to Go From Here

Rasta ab aapke saamne hai, aur pehla step kuch kharch nahi karta: [The Roles This Book Trains](../roles-this-book-trains/README.md)
kholo aur apni seat dhoondo. Agar aap apni seat pehle se jaante ho, [paanch preconditions](02-nine-stations-roadmap.md)
chalao aur apne jawabon ko agla move chunne do. Paanch "haan," aur Station 5 agla hai. Question 1 par
khaali jagah, aur instruction wahi hai jo yeh page shuru se de raha hai: aaj hi service ladder par kamana
shuru karo, aur engagements ko expert le aane do.

Ek aakhri framing, us din ke liye jab walk lambi lage: jis disruption ne aapko is rasta par dhakela, woh
reverse nahi hone wala. Intelligence sasti hoti rahegi, aur value un logon ki taraf move karti rahegi jo
isay real companies ke andar, real rules ke khilaf, proof ke sath deploy kar sakein. Is rasta ka har
station aisi cheez banata hai jo machine absorb nahi kar sakti: ek governed profession, ek naamzad
expert ka bharosa, aur outcomes ka ek record jis par aapka naam hai. Zyada tar readers service ladder par
kamayenge, aur yeh ek real practice hai jo real skill par bani hai. Kuch expert dhoondenge, vertical
banayenge, aur factory own karenge. Dono endings us ending se behtar hain jahan machine ne aapka kaam
absorb kar liya aur aapke paas room mein le jaane ke liye apna kuch nahi tha.
