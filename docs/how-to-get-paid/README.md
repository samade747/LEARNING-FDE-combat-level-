## Yeh Chapter Kis Baare Mein Hai

Yeh page business series ka **capstone** hai — iska subject **monetization** hai: yeh book jo kuch sikha chuki hai, woh paison mein kaise badalti hai. Pehle wale pages ne pieces di thi: roles, ecosystem, model, ownership argument, vertical choice, design method, aur connecting layer. Yeh page unhein **walking order** mein rakhta hai.

Yeh us sawal ka jawab hai jiske liye poori series bani thi: **agentic AI era mein aap paisa kaise kamate ho?** Teen jawab hain, aur yeh roadmap teenon ka naqsha banata hai: ek salaried job, freelance aur retainer income, ya ek domain startup jo aap khud own karte ho.

Ek claim teenon ke neeche chalti hai. Jab intelligence aur code sasti ho jati hai, market aapki *ability* ke liye kam pay karta hai, kyunki zyada rivals wohi kar sakte hain. Yeh **aap jo hold karte ho** uske liye pay karta hai. To yeh road ek aisi cheez banati hai jo aap hold kar sako — ek governed profession, ek jurisdiction mein — aur phir usay un companies tak le jati hai jinhein iski zaroorat hai. Market is insaan ko **Forward Deployed Engineer** kehta hai. Market ka apna version ek vendor ke platform se weld hota hai. Humara nahi — yehi cheez humein kahin se bhi reachable banati hai.

Poora roadmap **9 stations** par khara hai — pehli 4 reading hain (aur decision par khatam hoti hain), agli 4 building hain (aur asset par khatam hoti hain), aakhri ek selling hai.
