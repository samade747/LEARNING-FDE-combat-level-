# 02 — Kya Expect Karo, aur Nau Stations Ka Poora Roadmap

## Kya Expect Karo, Aur Pehle Kya Check Karo

Do cheezein yahan honi chahiye, is par saal kharch karne se pehle. Pehli hai yeh road **aam taur par kya paida karti hai**. Doosri ek chhota test hai jo predict karta hai ke vertical wala hissa aapke liye khula hai ya nahi.

**Expected outcome service ladder hai, aur yeh consolation prize nahi.** Zyada tar readers clients ke liye governed Systems of Record banake aur unke liye Workers manufacture karke monetize karenge, sirf shared method se. Is kaam ko koi expert, koi licensed corpus, koi vertical nahi chahiye. Yeh hafton ya kuch mahinon mein pay karta hai, ek reputation mein compound hota hai, aur yahi jagah hai jahan zyada tar readers ko shuru karna chahiye.

Vertical business exceptional outcome hai, usual nahi. Yeh us reader ke liye available hai jo ek committed expert dhundta hai, aur yeh talent ya effort se zyada us **ek relationship** se gate hoti hai. Baaki page isi proportion ke sath parho: service ladder woh hai jo aap **expect** karein pohanchna, aur vertical woh hai jo aap **aim** karein.

**Ab test.** Yeh paanch Station 5 se pehle likhit mein jawab do:

| # | Sawal | Kyun Itna Matter Karta Hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Kya aap ek senior practitioner ka naam le sakte ho jo aaj aapki call le lega? | Poori vertical ek insaan ke sign par khadi hai. Ayesha ka jawab uski aunt thi — yehi usual shape hai |
| 2 | Kya aapki candidate vertical public law se governed hai, licensed standards se nahi? | Statutes aur regulator circulars publish ho sakte hain. Commercial standards aksar nahi ho sakte, aur permission dheemi hoti hai |
| 3 | Kya aap 6-9 mahine fund kar sakte ho, service ladder ya savings se? | Build first, sell second matlab asset us paise se pehle aata hai jo woh kamaata hai |
| 4 | Kya aapki jurisdiction ke rules English mein khaali documented hain? | Yahin ek general model corpus ke bagair jawab nahi de sakta, to governed source pay karne layak hai |
| 5 | Kya aap aaj legally cross-border payment le sakte ho? | Ek engagement jise aap invoice nahi kar sakte, income nahi hai |

**Apne jawab kaise parhein.** Paanch "haan" ka matlab hai vertical ladder genuinely khuli hai, aur Station 5 agla step hai. Sawal 3 ya 5 par "nahi" ek schedule problem hai — kamate hue fix karo. Sawal 1 ya 2 par "nahi" serious wala hai, aur instruction wahi hai jo [ownership argument](../what-you-carry-in/README.md) deta hai: abhi service ladder par kamao, aisi vertical mat forcibly banao jo aap supply nahi kar sakte, aur asli engagements ko expert le kar aane do.

## Roadmap: Nau Stations

Nau stations, order mein. Pehli chaar reading hain, aur decision par khatam hoti hain. Agli chaar building hain, aur asset par khatam hoti hain. Aakhri selling hai. Har station us page ka naam leta hai jo sikhati hai, aur woh ek cheez jiske sath aap nikalte ho — kyunki woh cheez agle station ka input hai.

### Station 1: Roles Samjho, aur Inmein FDE Kahan Hai

**Parho:** The Roles This Book Trains

Kahin bhi chalne se pehle, naqsha seekho. Agentic era ne kaam ko naye roles mein phaila diya hai: Outcome Architect jo intent own karta hai, Digital FTE Builder jo Workers manufacture karta hai, AI-Native Company Architect jo enterprise design karta hai, aur Cloud AI Engineer jo usay chalata hai. FDE koi alag skill set nahi — yeh in chaaron roles ka combination hai, ek insaan client ki company mein le kar jata hai.

**Aap yahan se nikalte ho:** "kaunsi seat mujhe fit karti hai" ka clear jawab, aur yeh samajh ke FDE describe karta hai **aap kahan kaam karte ho**, aap kya jaante ho, yeh nahi.

### Station 2: Ecosystem Samjho

**Parho:** The Ecosystem

Ecosystem woh working proof hai ke poora yeh approach chalta hai. Iske teen parts hain: [Agent Factory System of Record](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ecosystem/system-of-record) jo yeh book insaano aur agents ko serve karta hai, [Zia Tutor AI](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ecosystem/zia-tutor-ai) jo usse sikhata hai, aur [Zia Developer AI](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ecosystem/zia-developer-ai) jo usse banata hai. Aap isi exact pattern ko reuse karoge: aapki vertical ka apna System of Record, apna expert twin, aur apna builder hoga, isi components se assemble kiya hua.

**Aap yahan se nikalte ho:** trio pattern dimaag mein, aur apna agent Agent Factory System of Record se connect kiya hua.

### Station 3: FDE AF Model Samjho

**Parho:** The FDE AF Model

Yeh blueprint hai jismein aap operate karoge: paanch layers, foundation machinery (Layer 0) se customer engagement (Layer 4) tak, ek law ke sath jo sab ko jorta hai — **repeated kaam neeche move hota hai**. Yeh page business model bhi hai — dikhata hai platform kahan kamata hai, aap har layer par kahan kamate ho, aur graduate ka earning path kyun climb karta hai: services Layers 1 aur 2 par, ownership Layers 3 aur 4 par.

**Aap yahan se nikalte ho:** paanch layers, promotion law, aur earning map.

### Station 4: Samjho Kya Aap Own Kar Sakte Ho

**Parho:** [What You Carry In: The Ownership Argument](../what-you-carry-in/README.md)

Yeh station road ka **mor** hai, aur zyada tar readers isay resist karte hain. Yeh prove karta hai ke jo bhi aap value karte ho, sirf ek cheez ownership test pass karti hai: ek profession ka governed knowledge, us expert ke sath jointly hold kiya jisne judgment likha. Yeh baaki roadmap ka sequence rule bhi bata deta hai: **build first, sell second.**

**Aap yahan se nikalte ho:** two-Systems-of-Record picture, aur pitch se pehle build karne ki discipline.

### Station 5: Apni Vertical Choose Karo

**Parho:** Choosing Your Vertical

Guess mat karo. Method follow karo: professional work ka ek body naam lo, isay selling-ease ke liye screen karo, aath tests chalao, beachhead choose karo, aur 60-90 din mein char gates ke against validate karo. Ek rule sab scoring se upar hai: **koi vertical launch nahi hoti bina committed domain expert ke**, kyunki expert ka judgment hi product hai. Aur ek honest exit aapko protect karta hai: ek domain jo achi bikti hai lekin trio carry nahi kar sakti, ek **service domain** hai, aur usme kamaana failure nahi.

Do advice ek solo builder ke liye hain, is method ke oopar:

**Pehla build ke liye public law ko licensed standards par prefer karo.** Ek statute, tax ordinance, customs schedule, registrar's filing rules — yeh publishable hain, aur inka rights basis aksar ek license hai jise aap parh sakte ho, ek negotiation nahi jo aapko jeetni pare.

**Woh jurisdiction prefer karo jahan rules badly documented hon.** Poocho ek strong general model bina kisi corpus ke pehle se kya jawab de sakta hai. Jahan rules local hain, kharab translate hain, aur sirf practitioners ko pata hain, wahan governed source pay karne layak hai.

**Aap yahan se nikalte ho:** ya to ek validated vertical, signed expert ke sath, ya ek service domain jo kamati hai jab tak expert search chalti rahe. Dono jeet hain.

### Station 6: Vertical System of Record Design Karo

**Parho:** Designing the Vertical System of Record from First Principles

Ab asset design karo. Profession ke aaj ke workflows copy karke nahi, kyunki woh insaani limits aur purani technology ke around bane the. Method hai: first-principles, legacy-informed redesign. Outcome contract likho. Apne expert ke sath workflow archaeology chalao. Har purane element ko teen bins mein sort karo: **Bin 1** — law aur trust require karte hain, rakho. **Bin 2** — insaani limits require karti hain, ek Worker ke liye dobara design karo jinke paas woh limits nahi. **Bin 3** — purani technology require karti thi, delete karo.

**Aap yahan se nikalte ho:** chhe filled templates, aur ek designed pehli slice — ek outcome, uske invariants, uska reflex, uska checker, aur uska evaluation set.

### Station 7: Agent Factory SoR Framework Par Banao

**Course:** Vertical SoR build course *(abhi taiyar ho raha hai)*

Design yahan software banti hai. Agent Factory SoR Framework Layer 1 kernel ka open-source implementation hai. Yeh char parts par bani hai jo aap pehle se jaante ho: Docusaurus (insaan-parhne-layak website), FastMCP (agent-facing tools), Postgres (governed store), pgvector (meaning se retrieval). Aap apni designed slice isme load karte ho aur wahid cheez paate ho jo poora model maangta hai: **ek governed source, do readers**.

**Aap yahan se nikalte ho:** aapki thin slice, live — ek website jise aapka expert approve karta hai, aur ek MCP endpoint jise aapke agents cite karte hain.

### Station 8: System of Context Ko Customer Ke Asli Kaam Se Jodo

**Parho:** The System of Context: Connecting the Records to Real Work

Aapki slice live hai, aur ek real company ke andar yeh kamre ki sabse chhoti cheez hai. Us company ke paas woh information hai jo aapki record kabhi nahi rakhegi. Aapke Worker ko ek kaam poora karne ke liye sab kuch chahiye — aur usay kabhi bhi isme se kisi ko **law** samajhne ki galti nahi karni chahiye.

To is road ka aakhri architectural masla ek naya source of truth nahi. **System of Record decide karta hai kya sach hai. System of Context decide karta hai kya pohanchta hai.** Yeh layer aapki do governed records, customer ke operational systems (jaise ERP, ledger), aur customer ke ungoverned working material ko jorta hai, phir Worker ko ek task ke liye ek cited bundle deta hai.

Teen cheezein is page par money decide karti hain:

**Pattern asset hai, fitting service hai.** Layer 3 reusable pieces rakhta hai jinme koi customer data nahi: authority map, packet shape, connector patterns, citation format, conflict tests — yeh aapka hai, har client ke sath jata hai. Layer 4 fitting hai: unki apps, unka identity provider, unke thresholds — yeh deployment kaam hai jo aap har customer par karte aur pay hote ho.

**Yeh doosri engagement ko pehli se sasta banata hai.** Pehla customer fitting ke liye pay karta hai jabke aap abhi pattern bana rahe ho. Doosra ek fitting ke liye pay karta hai jo usay reuse karti hai. Yehi promotion law hai jo connection layer par kaam karti hai.

**Pattern bechne se pehle taiyar karo, engagement ke andar fit karo.** Is station ka deliverable Layer 3 pattern hai, jismein koi customer data nahi, aur ise bina kisi aur ki permission ke banaya ja sakta hai.

Do honest boundaries: **har engagement ko yeh layer nahi chahiye**, aur isay jaldi banana ek poora quarter kho sakta hai. Aur ek gate negotiable nahi hai: System of Context tab tak mat banao jab tak aapki thin slice finished na ho aur pehla Worker usay cite na kar raha ho.

**Aap yahan se nikalte ho:** apni vertical ke liye ek portable connection pattern, aur honest judgment ke kisi diye gaye engagement ko yeh layer chahiye bhi ya nahi.

### Station 9: Kaam Par Jao

**Parho:** roles page ke FDE sections, aur business model

Ab aap woh cheez rakhte ho jo market par lagbhag koi candidate nahi rakhta: shared method, deep aur governed, ek real profession ki ek governed slice, ek named expert ke sath bani aur real review ke against proven, aur ek connection pattern jo us slice ko company ke asli systems mein fit karta hai bina kisi ki authority ko flatten kiye.

Sale asal mein kaise hoti hai, koi salesforce ya brand ke bagair, is page ke doosre half ka subject hai: [How to Sell in the Agentic AI Era](../how-to-sell/README.md).

Ek boundary doors se pehle. Yeh book role ka **technical core** train karti hai. Selling ka gehra craft — negotiation, discovery questioning, ROI framing, discipline ek unrealistic ask ko refuse karne ki — Certified Agentic AI Business Strategist track ka hissa hai, [courses and certifications page](../certifications/README.md) par.

**Aap yahan se nikalte ho:** income, aur pehla real baseline number jispar aapki vertical kabhi measure hui.
