# 03 — Teen Doors, Kya Har Door Pay Karta Hai, aur Pricing

## Aakhri Station Se Teen Doors

Ek hi asset ko convert karne ke teen tareeqe. Yeh dono/or nahi hain, aur common path teenon ko order mein use karta hai.

**Door 1: Job.** Wahan shuru karo jahan tenure gate nahi hai: independent services firms, aur clients jinhone pehle se aapki slice dekh li. Salaried vendor units baad ka door hain, pehla nahi. Aapka résumé us line se leta hai jo kisi screener ne pehle nahi dekhi: ek profession ki governed slice, dono readers ke liye publish ki hui. Door ka asli advantage **exit** hai: ek client aapko FDE ki tarah rent kar sakta hai aur phir outright hire kar sakta hai unke AI-Native Company Architect ki tarah, kyunki aapka leverage aapke sath andar aata hai. Sirf vendor-neutral version yeh trip kar sakta hai.

**Door 2: Freelance aur fractional market.** Marketplace category exist karti hai, aur uske neeche supply abhi bhi zyada tar generalists hai jo FDE-type kaam describe hi nahi karte. Shelf banaya ja chuka lekin kisi ne stock nahi kiya — trained reader ke liye yehi khaalipan mauka hai. Iska advantage yeh hai ke labour market local nahi: wohi contract Karachi, Lagos, ya Bangalore se jeeta ja sakta hai. Payment, tax, compliance, trust abhi bhi border cross karte hain, aur preconditions test ka sawal 5 isi liye hai. Iski discipline hai **over-communication**, kyunki remote embedding remote coding se harder hai.

**Door 3: Startup.** Aapki vertical ek domain startup ban jati hai jo aap apne expert ke sath co-own karte ho, twin, domain education, ready-made domain Workers, aur retainers wale engagements se kamati hui. Iska advantage **compounding** hai: har engagement vertical ko thick karta hai, aur promotion law har repeated fix ko shared builder mein wapis fold karti hai — is liye asset year 3 mein year 1 se zyada qeemti hai.

Doors exclusive nahi, aur common path teenon ko order mein use karta hai: freelance engagements build ko fund karte hain, pehla proven engagement aapko hirable banata hai, aur thickening vertical startup ban jati hai. Asset har door ke peeche wohi hai. Sirf contract badalta hai.

## Har Door Kya Pay Karta Hai

Ek monetization page numbers deta hai. Yahan hain, unki limits ke sath:

| Door | Kya Pay Karta Hai | Isay Aise Parho |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job | Median ~$190,000/saal, typical band $170K–$200K+. Anthropic ki posted FDE bands $200K–$300K. Frontier labs ke senior/staff engineers $450K–$600K clear karte hain | Real postings se measured. Zyada tar US/European roles, zyada tar senior |
| Freelance, per project | ~$2,000–$5,000 pehli integration, $5,000–$15,000 custom implementation, $15,000+ enterprise deployment | Published marketplace bands. Unproven profiles kam se shuru hote hain |
| Freelance, per hour | ~$150–$250/ghanta strategic consulting. UK contract day rates £600–£750 mid-level, £1,200–£2,000 principal level | Premium demonstrated outcomes se kamaya jata hai, kabhi list nahi hota |
| Retainer | ~$4,000–$10,000/mahina ongoing operation ke liye | Recurring layer, jo compound karti hai |
| Startup | Koi band nahi, kyunki aap khud price set karte ho. [Char layers ek sath](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ecosystem/fde-af-model#the-fde-af-business-model-where-each-layer-earns) se kamata hai: client SoR builds, manufactured Workers, expert twin, aur retainers | Salary nahi. Ek business, apni ceiling aur risk ke sath |

**Reality anchor, bands parhne se pehle.** In mein se koi bhi aapka **pehla** number nahi. Ek pehla paid engagement, locally becha hua, kisi bina track-record wale ke through, aksar ek chhoti firm ke liye chhota build hota hai — kuch sau se kuch hazar dollars tak, koi marketplace band nahi. Yeh failure nahi — yehi service ladder apna kaam kar rahi hai, aur zyada tar log isi tarah upar wali bands tak pohanchte hain. Table ko waisay parho jaisay road jahan le jati hai, aur apni pehli invoice ko is baat ka saboot samjho ke aap us par ho.

Table par pattern hi is poore page ka argument hai, paise mein. Top row sabse bara single number hai aur ek salary hai, capped aur gated. Bottom row ka koi number nahi, koi ceiling nahi, aur yehi wahid row hai jahan asset ek aise mahine mein bhi kamata rehta hai jab aap kuch na bechein.

## Kaam Ko Price Kaise Karo

Doors mukhtalif pay karte hain kyunki mukhtalif price karte hain, aur ek shift sab ke andar chalta hai: **aap waqt bechna chhor kar ek result bechna shuru karte ho.** Char instruments yeh kaam karte hain.

**Contract of success pricing ka instrument hai.** Kisi bhi building se pehle, aap aur customer likhit mein teen numbers par agree karte ho. Baseline hai kaam aaj kya cost karta hai, customer ke apne workflow mein measure kiya hua. Target hai kya success ginega. Acceptance criteria hai reviewer kaise check karega. "Char ghante per file se chalis minute" koi marketing line nahi — yehi woh number hai jise price argue karti hai, aur yehi wahid number hai jo buyer verify kar sakta hai.

**Baseline buyer se aana chahiye.** Aap isay contact se pehle nahi jaan sakte, kyunki yeh unke process ke andar measure hota hai. Isi liye slice pehle aati hai: ek buyer jisay kuch nahi dikhaya gaya, apne numbers disclose nahi karega — to slice ke bagair, meeting ho sakti hai lekin baseline, contract, ya price ke bagair.

**Retainer Workers ko price karta hai, aapke hours ko nahi.** Build ke baad, monthly fee aapko un Workers ko chalate rehne aur behtar karte rehne ke liye pay karti hai — yehi Digital FTE subscription model hai, aur yeh farq hai ek project jo khatam hota hai, aur ek practice jo compound hoti hai.

**Products asset khud ko price karte hain.** Layer 3 par, wohi vertical expert twin ke subscriptions, domain ki education, aur ready-made domain Workers bechti hai, ek baar banaye, kai baar becha hua. Yahan kuch bhi hour se billed nahi.

Ek caution char ke sath jati hai, aur ownership argument isay saaf kehta hai: result ke liye charge karna matlab ek result ki cost carry karna jo aap deliver na kar sakein. Outcome pricing sirf tab safe hai jab aapka checker aur evaluation set real hon. Ownership aapko outcome price karne ka haq deta hai. Yeh aapko pehle proof dene se excuse nahi karta.
