# 04 — Chalte Hue Kamana, Kitna Waqt Lagega, aur 8 Failure Modes

## Earning While You Walk

**Plain words mein:** yeh wo kaam hai jo bari cheez banate hue aapko pay karta hai. Aap client ke apne
manuals/standards/procedures system mein load karte ho, governance set up karte ho, aur build+upkeep ki
fees leti ho. Aap sabko milne wale generic tools se clients ke liye AI Workers banate ho, aur client
outcome ke liye pay karta hai, aapke hours ke liye nahi. Inmein se kisi ko bhi apni vertical ki zaroorat
nahi.

Layer 1 par, aap client ki apni manuals/standards/procedures kernel mein load karte ho, governance set
karte ho, aur build+upkeep ki fees leti ho. Layer 2 aur Layer 4 par, aap deployed generic tools se
clients ke liye AI Workers manufacture karte ho, aur client outcome ke liye pay karta hai, hours ke liye
nahi. Kisi ko bhi slice ki zaroorat nahi, kyunke koi bhi vertical nahi bech raha. Dono paid kaam hain jo
aap manufacturing courses khatam karte hi shuru kar sakte ho.

Do cheezein isay ek stopgap se zyada banati hain: (1) yeh portfolio banata hai jise screener aur
marketplace dono credential ki tarah parhte hain, aur (2) yeh aapko practitioners ke sath rooms mein
dalta hai — yehi tareeqa hai jis se zyada tar readers accountant, customs broker, ya recruiter se milenge
jinke 20 saal question 1 ka jawab dete hain.

**To do ladders ek sath chalti hain.** Service ladder abhi kamati hai. Vertical ladder baad mein
compound hoti hai. Koi bhi ek doosre ka intezar nahi karti.

*(Diagram: "Two tracks, one walk" — service ladder [Layer 1 SoR builds + Layer 4 Worker manufacturing,
koi vertical zaroori nahi, walk ke liye pay karti hai, aksar yahin Station 5 ka expert milta hai] abhi se
chalti hai; vertical ladder [Stations 1-4 read/decide, Station 5 screen/test/4 gates, Station 6
archaeology/design, Station 7 slice build, Station 8 connection pattern, Station 9 doors open] baad mein
compound hoti hai. **Pehli live slice ke liye honest range 3-6 mahine part-time hai, aur expert search
wo hissa hai jo schedule nahi ho sakta.**)*

## How Long the Road Takes

**Plain words mein:** ek planning shape, promise nahi. Aapki speed aapki starting skill, hafte mein
ghante, aur sabse zyada aap apna expert kitni jaldi dhoondte ho par depend karti hai.

Reading/deciding ek-do hafte leti hai. Courses pehli Digital FTE tak ~15 focused ghante lete hain.
Vertical choose karna 60-90 din leta hai. Slice design karna 3-6 hafte, aur build karna 2-4 aur hafte.
Connection pattern 1-2 hafte. Pehli sale 1-3 mahine.

| Stations | Aap Kya Kar Rahe Ho | Typical Waqt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-4 | Reading aur deciding | 1-2 hafte |
| Crash courses | [Getting Started](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/getting-started) mein banana seekhna | ~15 focused ghante pehli Digital FTE tak, ~28 ghante governed workforce tak |
| 5 | Screening, tests, 4 gates | 60-90 din — yehi canonical window hai |
| 6 | Archaeology aur ek slice design karna | 3-6 hafte, apne expert ki speed par chalti hai |
| 7 | Slice ko kernel par build karna | Design approve hone ke 2-4 hafte baad |
| 8 | System of Context taiyar karna | 1-2 hafte, fitting har engagement ke andar billed hoti hai |
| 9 | Pehla sponsor aur pehla close | 1-3 mahine, buyer yeh clock set karta hai |

Total par 2 notes: pehla, stations overlap karte hain — technical courses expert-search ke sath chalte
hain, Station 5 ki validation window service ladder par kamate hue chalti hai — isliye calendar rows ka
sum nahi. Doosra, ek row aapke control mein nahi: Station 5 kisi insan ke "haan" kehne par depend karti
hai, Station 9 buyer ki purchasing timeline par. Jo rows aap control karte ho unki planning karo, jo
nahi karte unka promise apne aap se mat karo.

Jo number total se zyada matter karta hai woh yeh hai: **aapka pehla paid kaam Station 9 ka intezar nahi
karta.** Yeh shuru hota hai jaise hi aap client ko ek governed System of Record bana kar de sakte ho.

## The Failure Modes — 8 Tareeqe Jin Se Yeh Rasta Ghalat Ho Sakta Hai

**Plain words mein:** har ek common hai, har ek survivable hai, aur har ek ko abhi pehchanna month-nine
se sasta hai. Sabse zyada likely bhi sabse natural hai — job apply karna progress jaisa lagta hai, isliye
log résumé polish karte hain us ek cheez banane ke bajaye jo unhein meeting dilati hai. Ek outcome
jaan-boojh kar list mein nahi hai, kyunke woh failure nahi — poora method chala kar vertical ke bajaye
ek service domain par khatam hona ek sahi result hai, aur yeh page zyada tar readers ke liye isi ki
ummeed rakhta hai.

1. **Slice banane ke bajaye résumé polish karna.** Disrupted developer ke liye sabse natural ghalti,
   kyunke apply karna progress jaisa lagta hai. Lekin slice hi credential hai, aur jis buyer ko kuch
   dikhaya hi nahi gaya woh apna baseline disclose nahi karega. **Cure:** [sequence rule](../what-you-carry-in/README.md) —
   pehle banao, phir becho.
2. **Signed expert ke bina launch karna.** "Usne principle mein maan liya" Gate 1 nahi hai. Committed
   author ke bina koi twin nahi, koi derived reflexes nahi, koi licensed material nahi — sirf ek document
   collection hai. **Cure:** [launch rule](../ecosystem-choosing-your-vertical/README.md) — signed, ya
   service ladder se hi domain serve karo.
3. **Sabse bara market choose karna, sabse aasan real sale nahi.** Healthcare aur banking prize jaisi
   lagti hain aur sabse dheeri kharidti hain. Ghalat beachhead ek quarter le leta hai, ghalat vertical ek
   saal. **Cure:** [screen aur 8 tests](../ecosystem-choosing-your-vertical/README.md).
4. **Slice ko demo ki tarah ship karna.** Ek clean example, koi missing evidence nahi, koi wrong
   jurisdiction nahi, koi escalation case nahi — yeh aapki apni review pass karega aur client ki fail
   karega. **Cure:** [normal path se pehle exceptions design karo](../ecosystem-designing-the-vertical-sor/README.md)
   aur ugly cases wala evaluation set banao.
5. **Staff augmentation accept karna aur usay engagement kehna.** Client jo kehta hai "hum understaffed
   hain" aapke hours rent karna chahta hai — yehi pyramid hai jise yeh poora rasta chhorne ke liye bana
   hai. Field test ka sawal: mere sath working team mein kaun hoga? Agar koi naam nahi le sakta, yeh
   body-shopping hai, chhupi hui.
6. **Vertical ko bara dikhane ke liye widen karna.** 4 professions ek jaisi shape share karti hain,
   isliye chaaron serve karna efficient lagta hai. Lekin shape hi method hai, aur method pehle se shared
   hai — isliye us par bana corpus khaali hota hai. **Cure:** [narrowing argument](../what-you-carry-in/README.md) —
   ek profession, ek jurisdiction.
7. **Vertical force karna jab question 1 mein koi naam na ho.** Preconditions test poochta hai kya ek
   senior practitioner aapka call lega. Jab honest jawab na ho, tempting move yeh hai ke phir bhi aage
   barho aur ummeed rakho ke kuch dikhane ko banne ke baad expert mil jayega. Kam hi hota hai — aur
   ungoverned corpus banane mein guzre mahine woh mahine hain jo service ladder pay kar sakti thi. **Cure:**
   deadline wala sabr — kamao, publish karo, real engagements se practitioners se milo, question 1 ko
   khud jawab dene do.
8. **Connect karne ke liye kuch banne se pehle connecting layer banana.** Bina finished slice ke ek
   System of Context ek search box hai ambitions ke sath, aur poora quarter absorb kar sakta hai. **Cure:**
   [is page ka gate](../ecosystem-system-of-context/README.md) — slice pehle ship hoti hai, ek Worker
   usay cite karta hai, tab hi customer ki duniya connect karo.

Ek failure mode list mein nahi hai, kyunke woh failure nahi. Poora method chala kar vertical ke bajaye
service domain par exit karna ek sahi outcome hai, honestly likha hua, revisit karne ki condition ke
sath. Yeh page zyada tar readers ke liye isi outcome ki ummeed bhi rakhta hai.
