## 00 — Do Problems, Ek Gap

- **Key terms:** FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer), Vendor-neutral, Vertical, System of Record, System of Context, Slice, Service ladder, Vertical ladder, Contract of success.
- **2 problems, 1 gap:** Developers ko kaam nahi mil raha (agents routine code likh rahe, junior roles patli), companies AI se kaam nahi bana pa rahin (~95% custom pilots koi measurable return nahi dete — models weak nahi, koi unhein real data/rules/approvals mein fit nahi karta). Yeh **deployment gap** hai.
- **3 routes:** Freelance (SoR builds + Workers, shared method) → projects retainers ban jate hain; Job (proven ability deploy) → salary; Startup (governed vertical) → owned asset. Zyada tar readers Row 1 se shuru karte hain.
- Aapki taraf ka gap: value execution ke around ke kaam (kya banana, specify, deploy, verify) ki taraf move hoti hai. Unki taraf ka gap: model ko specific outcome mein badalna deployment ka kaam hai, glamorous nahi.

## 01 — Market Ka Jawab: FDE, Do Gates, Humara Jawab

- FDE Palantir mein invent hua — customer ki company ke andar embedded, real data pe, real value tak. Demand: postings 9 mahino mein 800%+ bar gayin, candidate pool sirf ~50% bara; AWS+Microsoft ne $3.5B commit kiya. Boundary: yeh numbers enterprise buying describe karte hain, aapka pehla buyer ek 20-200 person firm hai jahan ek partner decide karta hai.
- **2 gates market band karti hai:** (1) Lock-in — client ka nuksan (har vendor FDE apne platform pe banata hai), (2) Hiring door — aapka nuksan (senior door: 5+ saal, US/EU address).
- **Humara jawab: Vendor-neutral Vertical FDE.** Vendor-neutral = lock-in solve karta hai (koi platform bandish nahi). Vertical = empty-hands problem solve karta hai (profession pe land karta hai — Agent Factory System of Record MCP par live hai, koi bhi connect ho sakta hai).
- Technology "Getting Started: Crash Courses" mein hai (Mode 2 chahiye — 15 ghante first Digital FTE, 28 ghante governed workforce).

## 02 — Nau Stations Ka Roadmap

- **Expected outcome = service ladder** (consolation prize nahi, expected outcome). Vertical business rarer hai, relationship se gate hoti hai.
- **5-question pre-Station-5 test:** senior practitioner ka naam le sakte ho? public-law-governed vertical hai? 6-9 mahine fund kar sakte ho? English-documented jurisdiction hai? cross-border payment le sakte ho?
- **9 Stations:** 1) Roles Samjho (FDE = 4 roles ka combination), 2) Ecosystem Samjho (SoR + Zia Tutor + Zia Developer trio pattern reuse karo), 3) FDE AF Model (5 layers, "repeated kaam neeche move hota hai"), 4) Ownership Argument (build-first-sell-second, sirf governed knowledge ownership test pass karta hai), 5) Vertical Choose Karo (8 tests, committed expert zaroori, public law &gt; licensed standards), 6) Vertical SoR Design (Bin 1 rakho/Bin 2 redesign/Bin 3 delete), 7) SoR Framework Par Banao (Docusaurus+FastMCP+Postgres+pgvector), 8) System of Context Jodo (pattern=asset, fitting=service; SoR "kya sach hai" vs SoC "kya pohanchta hai"), 9) Kaam Par Jao (selling → How to Sell page).

## 03 — Teen Doors, Kya Har Door Pay Karta Hai, aur Pricing

- **3 doors (exclusive nahi, order mein use hote hain):** Job (tenure-gated nahi jagah se shuru, exit = outright hire), Freelance/fractional (labour market local nahi, over-communication discipline), Startup (compounding advantage, year 3 &gt; year 1).
- **Pay table:** Job ~$190K/saal median ($170-200K+ band, Anthropic FDE $200-300K, frontier senior $450-600K); Freelance per-project $2K-$15K+; per-hour $150-250/ghanta; Retainer $4K-$10K/mahina; Startup koi band nahi (khud price set karo).
- Reality anchor: pehla paid engagement chhota hota hai (kuch sau-hazar dollars), yeh failure nahi — service ladder apna kaam kar rahi hai.
- **Pricing ke 4 instruments:** Contract of success (baseline+target+acceptance criteria, buyer se aana chahiye — isliye slice pehle), Retainer (Workers ko price karta hai, hours ko nahi), Products (khud price karte hain). Caution: outcome pricing sirf tab safe jab checker/eval set real ho.
