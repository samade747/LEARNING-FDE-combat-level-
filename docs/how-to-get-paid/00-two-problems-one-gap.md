## Do Problems, Ek Gap

Do cheezein is waqt ek sath sach hain, aur zyada tar log sirf ek dekhte hain.

**Developers ko kaam nahi mil raha.** Agents routine code ka barhta hissa likh rahe hain, junior roles patli hoti ja rahi hain, aur freelance boards capable generalists se bhare hue hain jo ek dusre se aur khud model se bhi compete kar rahe hain. Anxiety ke neeche asli sawal "job kaise milegi" nahi — "jab intelligence aur code sasti ho, main asal mein kya contribute karta hoon?"

**Companies AI se kaam nahi bana pa rahin.** Woh hama jagah AI projects shuru kar rahi hain, aur roughly **95 percent** custom enterprise pilots koi measurable financial return nahi dikhate. Models kamzor hone ki wajah se nahi. Kyunki koi bhi unhein company ke real data, real rules, real approvals, real logon mein fit nahi karta.

Ab in do sentences ko sath rakho, kyunki yehi is page ka poora idea hai. Ek taraf capable log hain jinke liye koi raasta nahi. Doosri taraf real paisa hai jiska koi raasta nahi. Yeh doori na talent ki kami hai, na technology ki. Yeh ek **deployment gap** hai, aur yeh exactly ek job ki chaurai hai.

Jo insaan is gap ko band karta hai, uska naam hai, aur ek market billions kharch kar isay dhundh rahi hai. Iske baaki hisse mein hai ke woh insaan apni sharton par kaise bane, aur uske liye kaise paid ho.

## Jawab, Ek Nazar Mein

| Route | Pehle Kya Bechte Ho | Pehle Kaun Kharidta Hai | Kya Ban Jata Hai |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Freelance** | System of Record builds aur manufactured Workers, sirf shared method se | Chhoti aur mid-sized firms, ek decision-maker | Projects monthly retainers ban jate hain |
| **Job** | Aapki proven ability ke real company ke andar ek outcome deploy karne ki | Ek services firm, ya woh client jinhone pehle se aapka kaam dekha | Salary, aur enterprise-scale problems tak access |
| **Startup** | Ek governed vertical: expert twin, domain products, aur engagements | Aapke expert ki apni profession, ek jurisdiction | Ek asset jo aap own karte ho, har saal zyada qeemti |

Zyada tar readers row 1 se shuru karte hain, kyunki isay koi expert, koi licensed corpus, koi vertical nahi chahiye. Row 3 sabse bara inaam hai aur sabse mushkil gate — aur gate ek insaan hai, skill nahi.

**Is page ko kaise use karo.** Pehle problem aur jawab parho — yeh batate hain yeh road kyun exist karti hai. Phir 9 stations ko order mein chalo. Har station us page ka naam leta hai jo sikhati hai, aur ek deliverable jiske sath aap wahan se nikalte ho. Koi station skip mat karo, kyunki har station ka deliverable agle station ka input hai. Phir doors, time budget, aur failure modes parho, shuru karne se pehle.

## Aapki Taraf Ka Gap, Poori Tafseel Se

Ek nasal ke liye, safe advice thi: code likhna seekho. Yeh advice hamare saamne toot rahi hai, aur har disrupted developer ka sawal wohi hai jise yeh book Yuval Noah Harari se udhaar le kar khol rahi hai: agar hum nahi jaante job market kaisi hogi, to kya seekhein?

Yahan jawab ki shape hai. Jab execution sasti ho jati hai, value execution ke **around** wale kaam ki taraf move karti hai: kya banana hai decide karna, precisely specify karna, real company ke andar deploy karna, aur verify karna ke kaam hua. Syntax absorb ho rahi hai. Judgment, specification, aur deployment nahi ho rahe. Is book ka [poora role map](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/roles-this-book-trains) isi ek sentence par khada hai, aur yeh roadmap us naqshe ko ek walking path mein badalta hai.

Woh list dekho jo yeh section deti hai. Har item kisi doosri company ke andar hota hai, unke data ke against, unke rules ke tehat. Yehi doosri taraf ka gap hai, aapki apni taraf se describe kiya hua.

## Unki Taraf Ka Gap, Poori Tafseel Se

95 percent wala number aksar be-dhyani se quote hota hai. Yeh yeh nahi kehta ke models fail huye, ya software kabhi chala hi nahi. Yeh kehta hai ke **paisa nahi aaya**, aur wajah seedhi cause ki taraf ishara karti hai. Model ek general capability hai. Company ko ek specific outcome chahiye: yeh workflow, yeh rules, yeh approval chain, yeh log, yeh regulator. Pehli ko doosri mein badalna deployment ka kaam hai, yeh glamorous nahi, yeh on-site hota hai, aur yehi exactly woh step hai jo zyada tar pilots skip karte hain. Ek pilot jismein demo impressive ho aur return na aaye, aksar wahi pilot hota hai jo demo par ruk gaya.

To buyer ka masla yeh nahi ke AI kaam nahi kar sakti. Masla yeh hai ke kisi ne isay fit nahi kiya. Yeh ek services problem hai, aur services problems **kharide** jate hain.
