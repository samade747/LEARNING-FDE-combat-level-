# 09 — Test Your Understanding

Book ka apna `<Quiz>` block (English, verbatim options summarized + answer/explanation) — 12
scenario-based questions. Reasoning se answer do, wording se nahi.

**Source:** Workflow Design & Diagnosis Assessment. (Zia Tutor AI connector, window 2, fetched
2026-10-23.)

---

1. **Expense workflow Step 3: report total karta, policy-limit se upar amount compute karta.
   Classification + carrier?** **Answer: AI-appropriate** — total reversible hai, approval step
   par checked hota, **code execution** carry karta (arithmetic hai). _Concept 3._

2. **Proposal team 4 mahine se AI se client responses draft kar rahi, lead low-value responses
   direct send karne dena chahta.** **Answer: Halo delegation** — sending irreversible+external
   hai, drafting quality sending ke baare mein evidence nahi hai. _Concept 7._

3. **Fresh chat mein supplier contract summary mangi, pehla answer hi 2 payment terms miss karta,
   koi degrade nahi hui.** **Answer: Under-specification** — pehli response se galat, context ko
   drift ka time nahi mila. _Concept 11._

4. **Call checklist tumne khud banayi, 6 mahine mein 3 account managers use karte, HR new-hire
   pack mein link kar diya.** **Answer: Escalation signal** — doosre log infrastructure ki tarah
   depend karte, prompt-and-iterate se outgrow hui, developer/architect expertise chahiye.
   _Concept 10._

5. **Analyst har mahine chat mein reminder type karta (canceled orders exclude karo). 2 hafte
   chhutti par gaya, colleague ne report chalayi, canceled orders reh gaye.** **Answer:** Fix
   kabhi conversation se configuration mein **promote** nahi hui — sirf ek insaan ke liye exist
   karti thi. _Concept 14._

6. **3 coordinators har ek internally consistent weekly update likhte, director har Monday 1
   ghanta sab ko ek voice mein rewrite karta.** **Answer: Variance** — cost sirf logon ke beech
   exist karti, shared Skill source par hataati. _Concept 15._

7. **Client ke risk function ko contract workflow describe karna — kaunsi description sabse
   zyada trust kamati?** **Answer:** "Drafting AI-assisted hai. Qualified lawyer har term review+
   approve karta, bina sign-off kuch nahi jata." — gate plainly stated hai. _Concept 18._

8. **4 requirements mein se kaunsa 2 teams ko alag cheez banwayega?** **Answer:** "Report accounting
   period close hone ke baad **promptly** deliver honi chahiye" — "promptly" decidable nahi, 2 din
   aur 2 hafte dono kisi ke liye prompt hain. _Concept 2._

9. **Map "AI drafts, manager reviews" refund approvals ke liye. 6 hafte mein drafts reliable rahi,
   manager ab Friday ko batch mein bina individual cases khole approve karta.** **Answer:**
   **Automated in practice** — collaborative step jiski real review nahi ho rahi, automated step
   hai jo map kuch bhi likhe. _Concept 7._

10. **Output disappoint karta, instinct sabse capable model switch karna hai.** **Answer:**
    **Ladder ke neeche se move** — prompt dobara parhne/context-length check karne se pehle, aur
    usually unnecessary. _Concept 12._

11. **Contract map mein "playbook departures flag karna" AI-appropriate hai. Expense workflow mein
    "travel policy se check karna" collaborative aata. Same kisam ka step, alag column — kya decide
    karta?** **Answer:** Kya agle step par koi flags parhta — galat flag jo galat redline ki
    tarah surface hoti, catch ho jati; expense mein koi downstream re-read nahi karta. _Concept 6._

12. **Coordinator supplier shortlist draft karta jo procurement manager line-by-line review karta
    supplier contact karne se pehle. Koi argue karta drafting human rehni chahiye kyunke manager
    accountable hai.** **Answer: Galat hai** — accountability wahi step par rehti jo decision hai;
    shortlist input hai jo wo judge karti supplier contact hone se pehle, isliye drafting delegate
    ho sakti. _Concept 5._
