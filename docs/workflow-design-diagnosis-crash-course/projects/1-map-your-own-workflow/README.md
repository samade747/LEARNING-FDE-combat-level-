# Project 1 — Map Your Own Workflow

**Time:** ~20 min · **Tool:** any free AI assistant

Same workflow jo opening exercise mein use ki thi, ab 3 criteria ke sath.

## Steps

1. Apni workflow ordered list mein likho (5-6 steps kaafi hain, plain language, jo tumhari team
   actually bolti).
2. **Delegate + why:** ek step jo AI ko dete, aur **kaunsa criterion** (reversibility/stakes/
   accountability) usay safe banata — explicitly naam do.
3. **Retain + why:** ek step jo human rakhte, aur kaunsa criterion decide karta. "Feels important"
   bola to push karo jab tak 3 mein se ek naam na ho jaye.
4. **The gate:** koi step collaborative hai to — kaun specifically review karta, kis moment par,
   kya check karta? Named person+moment naam nahi kar sakte to wo step automated hai.

## Common Mistake Jise Yeh Catch Karta Hai

**Halo delegation** — delegated step ki justification "AI ne pehle achha kiya" par rest karti,
3 criteria par nahi. Agar aisa hai, khud se pucho: pichla step achha jana **is** step ke baare mein
kya batata? (Usually kuch nahi.)

## Done

- [ ] Delegated step ka reason explicitly 1 criterion naam karta
- [ ] Retained step ka reason "feels important" se zyada specific hai
- [ ] Collaborative step (agar ho) ka named reviewer + moment hai
