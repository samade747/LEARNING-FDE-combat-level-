# 02 — Gate 2: Once, or Every Week? (Mode 1 vs Mode 2)

> **Rokti hai galti:** "Maine ek permanent helper bana diya jo kaam sirf ek dafa hona tha — ya, zyada
> aksar, maine wahi kaam har hafte haath se kiya mahino tak, aur kabhi notice hi nahi kiya ke mai
> iske liye ek helper bana sakta tha."

Jab Gate 1 kahe "haan, agent," to agla sawal hai *yeh kis type ka agent kaam hai*. Yeh poori book ka
**sabse important idea** hai, isiliye dono types ko naam diya gaya hai.

## Mode 1 aur Mode 2, Aasan Lafzon Mein

- **Mode 1 — ek dafa solve karo.** Agent kholo, kaam karo, result lo, chale jao. Kuch bhi peeche nahi
  rehta. Zyada tar kaam yehi hota hai.
- **Mode 2 — ek helper banao.** Ek permanent AI worker banao jo kaam baar baar khud karta rahe, aap ke
  bina. (Book isay **Digital FTE** kehti hai — "digital full-time employee.") Isay banane mein zyada
  mehnat lagti hai, aur sirf tab worth hai jab kaam bar bar wapis ata ho.

## Teen Dials

Ek task **Mode 2** tab hi hai jab **teenon dials upar** ho:

1. **Kitni baar?** Ek dafa ya kabhi kabhi (dial neeche → Mode 1), ya baar baar, har hafte/din (dial
   upar → Mode 2)?
2. **Kitna same?** Har baar **same shape** hai — same tarah ka input, same steps, same result (dial
   upar → Mode 2)? Ya har baar alag dikhta hai aur naye sochne ki zaroorat hai (dial neeche → Mode 1)?
3. **Worth it?** Kya kaam itna bara hai ke helper banane ki mehnat wapis mil jaye (dial upar → Mode 2)?
   Sirf "kitni baar" nahi — yeh bhi dekho: har run kitna time leta hai, kitne items handle karta hai,
   galti ki cost kya hogi, aur doosra kaam rok kar yeh karne ka hassle. Ek task jo har hafte repeat
   hota hai lekin sirf 4 minute leta hai, 2 items handle karta hai, aur slip ho jaye to koi nuksan nahi
   — shayad banane layak nahi.

Agar **ek bhi** dial neeche hai — kabhi kabhi hota hai, **ya** har baar badalta hai, **ya** itna chota
hai ke bother na kare — **Mode 1** mein raho. Helper sirf tab banao jab kaam **repeat ho, har baar same
ho, aur worth ho.**

## Sabse Mehnga Galti

Gate 2 ghalat karne ke do tareeqe hain. Ek-dafa kaam ke liye permanent helper banana chhota mistake hai
— ek dopeher waste hoti hai, notice ho jata hai, aage barh jate hain.

Mehnga wala **quiet** hota hai, aur taqreeban sab isay karte hain: **Mode 2 wala kaam haath se karte
rehna, baar baar, hamesha.**

Yeh chhupta hai kyunki har baar chota lagta hai. Har Monday aap 25 minute lagate ho agent se hafte ke
messages sort karwa kar summary likhwane mein. Har baar theek chalta hai. Aap kabhi jodte nahi. Lekin
saal bhar mein yeh 20+ ghante ban jate hain, ek aise kaam par jo repeat hota hai, same hai, aur clearly
banane layak hai. Aap ne helper isiliye nahi banaya kyunki kisi Monday ko itna bara mehsoos hi nahi hua
ke ruk kar decide karein.

**Fix:** Faisla ek *deliberate check* banao, kisi feeling ka intezaar mat karo.

> **Rule: Teesri baar jab aap wahi kaam wahi tareeqe se karo, ruko aur Gate 2 chalao.** Agar teenon
> dials upar hain, to aap ab *problem solve* nahi kar rahe — aap *woh helper ban gaye ho* jo abhi tak
> banaya nahi. Yehi cue hai isay worker mein cross karne ka.

## Work Example — David

David ek 12-log wali company ka roz ka kaam sambhalta hai. Do tasks jo dekhne mein repeat lagte hain:

- *Naye employee ke liye account/access set karna.* Yeh shayad har kuch mahine mein ek baar hota hai,
  aur har naya banda thora alag hota hai — alag role, alag apps, alag special cases. **Kitni baar? Low.
  Kitna same? Low.** Do dials neeche. **Mode 1** hi rahega — har baar fresh solve karo agent se. Rigid
  helper pehle hi unusual case pe break ho jayega.
- *Monday message summary.* Har hafte, same tarah ka input, same result, 25 minute lagte hain har baar.
  **Kitni baar? High. Kitna same? High. Worth it? Yes.** Teenon dials upar. Yeh **Mode 2** hai. David ka
  move: cross karo isay worker mein — haath se solve karte raho jab tak method proven na ho jaye, phir
  promote karo ke woh khud chale.

Same banda, same hafta, do opposite answers. New-employee task *lagta* repeat hai, lekin "kitna same"
dial fail karta hai. Monday summary teenon paar karta hai. Gate 2 hi inko alag batata hai — **pehle**,
na ke pehle wale ko over-build karke ya doosre ko saal bhar haath se karke.
