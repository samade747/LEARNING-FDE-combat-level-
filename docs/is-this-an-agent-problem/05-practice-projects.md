# 05 — Practice Exercises

Course ke andar teen "Your Turn" exercises hain, har gate ke baad ek. Inhe apni asli life ke tasks par
try karo — yehi tareeqa hai jisse gates habit bante hain.

## Exercise 1 — Gate 1 Sorting

Apne hafte se **5 tasks** lo — koi bhi cheez jo aap ne "AI se poochne" ka socha ho. Har ek ke liye
likho woh kaunsa exit leta hai: **regular tool**, **chatbot**, ya **agent** — aur ek line mein *kyun*.

> **Red flag check:** Agar sab 5 "agent" ban jayein, aap shayad force kar rahe ho — dobara dekho aur
> woh dhundo jo asal mein spreadsheet ya chatbot hai. Agar koi bhi "agent" na bane, yeh bhi ek real
> finding hai — shayad is hafte agent-shaped kaam tha hi nahi.

## Exercise 2 — Gate 2 Mode Sorting

**3 tasks** lo jo aap ne agent se ek se zyada baar karwaye hain. Har ek ke liye 3 dials set karo (kitni
baar, kitna same, worth it) aur verdict likho: Mode 1 ya Mode 2.

> **Sabse valuable finding:** Woh task jo aap ko surprise kare — jo aap haath se karte rahe ho, lekin
> nikle Mode 2 — yehi is poore page ki sabse qeemti cheez hai. Yeh woh ghante hain jo aap wapis lene wale
> ho.

## Exercise 3 — Gate 3 Three Lines

Apna sabse recent real agent task lo (ya jo agla dene wale ho). Teen lines likho:

- **Works from:** exactly kya dekhna chahiye
- **Chahiye akhir mein:** shape ke sath
- **Done when:** ek testable check, "acha hai" jaisa vague nahi

Phir gaur se dekho: kya done-check waqai ek minute mein confirm ho sakta hai?

## Research Ke Peeche (Optional Reading)

Course teeno gates ko established research se jorta hai:

- **Gate 1** — Kaplan (1964) ka *law of the instrument* aur Maslow (1966) ka "agar sirf hammer hai, to
  har problem nail lagta hai." Plus Goodhue & Thompson (1995) ka *Task-Technology Fit* model.
- **Gate 2** — Martin Fowler's *Rule of Three* (Refactoring, 1999, credited to Don Roberts): teesri
  baar hi build karne ka signal hai, pehli baar nahi. Plus Knuth (1974) ka "premature optimization"
  warning, aur xkcd 1205 ("Is It Worth the Time?").
- **Gate 3** — Locke & Latham (2002) ki *goal-setting theory*: specific, measurable goals "do your
  best" se bohat behtar perform karte hain. Plus software teams ka *Definition of Done* practice.
