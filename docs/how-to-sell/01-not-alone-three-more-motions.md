# 01 — Kya Yeh Agents Se Bech Rahe Hain? Aur 3 Aur Motions

## The Question: Are They Selling With Agents?

**Plain words mein:** honest jawab — nahi, mukhya taur par nahi. Koi evidence nahi ke Palantir ne apne
salespeople AI agents se replace kiye jo outreach aur closing karte hain. Jo salespeople ko replace
karta hai woh upar wala model hai: human engineers + ek product jiske agents tez visible results
produce karte hain.

Agents ka sale mein asal role alag hai aur zyada zaroori hai. **Agents khud proof hain.** Jab customer ki
apni data par ek agent minutes mein woh kaam karta hai jismein team din leti thi, wo result hi sales
pitch hai — jo koi human salesperson kabhi deliver nahi kar sakta tha.

Yeh "nahi" is page ki sabse achi khabar hai. Agar secret ek AI sales agent hota, aap ek khareed sakte the,
aur baaqi sab bhi khareed sakte. Secret ek **motion** hai, aur yeh book Station 1 se hi aapko isi mein
train kar rahi hai.

Jab 2026 mein shrinking-salesforce headline kisi reader ki screen se guzarti hai, natural guess yeh hai
ke Palantir ne apni sales team automate kar di: AI agents prospecting, qualifying, emailing, closing.
Reporting is guess ko support nahi karti. Record jo dikhata hai woh upar wala model hai: bootcamps entry
motion ki tarah, embedded FDEs/strategists field force ki tarah, land-and-expand contract shape ki
tarah. Insan automate away nahi huye. Alag sales job ka bara hissa deployment job mein absorb ho gaya.

Jahan agents genuinely sale mein enter hote hain wahan ek level neeche hai, aur yeh decisive hai. Bootcamp
isliye kaam karta hai kyunke AIP ke agents, customer ki apni data se wired, din ke andar ek visible
outcome produce karte hain. Agents bech nahi rahe. Agents wo cheez hain jo demonstrate ho rahi hai, aur
unki speed hi hai jo ek proof ko quarters ke bajaye din mein pahonchati hai. Product ki capability, buyer
ke apne problem par dikhayi gayi, hi persuasion hai. Seedha kaho: **demo itna acha ban gaya ke woh zyada
tar selling carry kar sake.**

Agents motion ko increasingly assist karenge — Palantir ke liye aur aapke liye: account research,
follow-up drafts, baseline evidence, aur deployed Workers ka monitoring jinke numbers expansion case
banate hain. Inhein accelerants maano, motion khud nahi. Jis team ke paas dikhane ko koi proof nahi, usay
ek aisa agent nahi bachayega jo uske baare mein zyada emails bhejta hai.

## Palantir Akela Nahi: 3 Aur Motions, Aur Har Ek Kya Sikhata Hai

Shrinking-salesforce quarter era ka sabse loud data point hai, akela nahi. Teen aur companies ne AI bech
kar exceptional growth report ki hai, har ek ne isi sawal ka alag jawab diya jo Palantir ne diya. Teenon
study karo, kyunke har ek us rasta ka ek alag hissa validate karta hai jo aap pehle hi chal chuke ho.

### The Wedge: Cursor Aur Product-Led Growth

Palantir ki motion ko customer ke room mein engineers chahiye. Doosri motion ko koi meeting bilkul nahi
chahiye, aur usne isse bhi tez growth produce ki.

**Cursor**, Anysphere ka AI code editor, history ki sabse tez-growing business software company report
hoti hai: ~$100 million annualized revenue Jan 2025 mein, $500 million June tak, $1 billion November tak,
$2 billion Feb 2026 tak, aur ~$4 billion mid-2026 tak — jab SpaceX ne company ko $60 billion stock mein
acquire karne par raazi hua, ab tak ka sabse bara venture-backed startup acquisition. Yeh ~300 employees
ke sath $1 billion revenue cross kar gaya, aur uska enterprise page report karta hai ke 64% Fortune 500
companies isay use karti hain.

Ab poocho ek itni chhoti company America ki sabse bari companies ke do-tihai tak kaise pahonchi. Kisi ne
unke liye koi bootcamp nahi chalayi. Sequence ulti chali: individual developers ne ek free/cheap tool
adopt kiya kyunke usne unka apna working day behtar bana diya, aur jab enough log us par depend karne lage,
unke employer ke paas usay properly khareedne ki wajah thi — kyunke tool pehle se building ke andar tha,
company ki apni code par khud ko prove karte hue. 2026 ki shuruaat tak, enterprise customers reportedly
~60% revenue bante the: individuals se shuru hui adoption firm-wide contracts par khatam hui.

**Aap kya udhar lete ho:** wedge **individual practitioner** hai, firm nahi. Aapki vertical mein bhi
juniors hain jaise software mein developers hain: trainee accountant, junior associate, customs clerk jo
asal mein filings karta hai. Jo bhi aap publish karo jo ek practitioner ka din behtar banaye — twin ka
free tier, ek open cheatsheet, SoR ke public pages — woh us firm mein ek wedge hai jo unhein employ karti
hai. Jis partner se aap eventually milte ho, unhone ideally pehle hi aapki vertical ka naam ek junior se
suna hona chahiye jo isay use karta hai.

Ek honest note, kyunke is story ka pure version misleading hai. Reporting batati hai ke jaise Cursor ka
enterprise business barha, uska sales-led revenue ek saal mein ~100-fold barha. Sabse pure product-led
company ne bhi firm-wide deals close karne ke liye insan add kiye. **Product-led growth meeting earn
karta hai. Ek insan usay close karta hai.** Yeh Palantir wali hi division of labor hai, ulti direction se
pahonchi hui.

### The Price: Sierra Aur Jis Outcome Ke Liye Customer Pay Karta Hai

Business software ka har buyer dashkon se ek chupi hui na-insaafi ke sath jeeta hai. License fee due hoti
hai chahe software deliver kare ya na kare. Seats ka pay hota hai. Results ki ummeed ki jati hai. Teesri
motion is na-insaafi ko hata deti hai, aur is hatane ne era ki sabse tez-growing companies mein se ek
banayi.

**Sierra**, 2023 mein former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor aur former Google executive Clay Bavor ne
banayi, large enterprises ko customer-facing AI agents bechti hai, aur mid-2026 tak ~$200 million annual
recurring revenue aur $15.8 billion valuation tak pahonchi, company ke apne account ke mutabiq nearly
half Fortune 50 customers ke sath.

Is page ke liye jo matter karta hai woh product nahi, price hai. Sierra ka signature model **outcome-based**
hai: customer ek fee pay karta hai (third parties ke mutabiq ~$1-2.5) har baar jab agent asal mein ek
interaction resolve karta hai, aur zyada tar cases mein kuch pay nahi karta jab conversation unresolved
rahe ya human tak escalate ho. Economics founder ne saaf state kiye: ek human-handled service call
company ko shayad $10-20 ki lagti hai, isliye ek resolved automation saved cost ka ek hissa qeemti hai.
July 2026 mein company ne model ko long-horizon outcomes tak extend kiya: ek agent jo hafton tak ek
business result (claim ya renewal) pursue kare, phir bhi result par priced.

**Aap kya udhar lete ho: validation, aur ek warning.** Roadmap ka contract of success — baseline, target,
acceptance — un logon ke liye scrappy improvisation nahi jo day rates charge nahi kar sakte. Yeh era ka
flagship pricing model hai, ek aise founder ke through jo pehle Salesforce (software ki sabse bari sales
organization) chala chuka, ek aisi valuation par jo batati hai market isay believe karta hai. Jab koi
buyer outcome pricing par hesitate kare, aapke paas ab ek line ka jawab hai: **yehi tareeqa hai jis se
leading AI companies charge karti hain, kyunke yeh seller ke incentive ko buyer ke sath align karta hai.**

Warning bhi isi ke sath aati hai, aur roadmap ne pehle hi de di thi. Outcomes par paid hona matlab
failures par paid na hona. Sierra millions interactions ke across unresolved conversations absorb kar
sakta hai. Aap unhein ek muthi-bhar engagements ke across absorb karte ho. Aapka checker aur evaluation
set hi is pricing ko aapke scale par survivable banate hain — isi liye neeche ki "definition of ready"
aapko inke bina bechne se rokti hai.

### The Trust: Harvey Aur Vertical Door

Ek profession market se alag tareeqe se khareedti hai. Ek managing partner se poocho firm ne AI adopt
kyun nahi ki, aur honest jawab kam hi technology ke baare mein hota hai. Yeh ek **dar** hai. Koi bhi city
mein pehli firm nahi banna chahta jo client work ko ek machine par bharosa kare, aur koi bhi yeh decision
kisi regulator, insurer, ya client ko explain nahi karna chahta agar galat ho jaye. Ek motion jo ek
profession mein becha jaye, isay features se pehle is dar ka jawab dena hoga.

**Harvey** ne isay naamon se jawab diya. Harvey legal work ke liye AI banata hai — ek profession — aur Jan
2026 tak ~$190 million annual recurring revenue tak pahoncha, 5 mahine mein nearly double, $11 billion
valuation March 2026 tak. Yeh platform par 100,000+ lawyers report karta hai, ~1,300 organizations ke
across, jinmein 100 sabse bari US law firms ka aadha ya zyada shamil hai.

Uski motion na bootcamps hai na self-serve. Harvey koi pricing page publish nahi karta: entry ek demo
request se ek enterprise sales process mein hoti hai, aur deployments firm-wide commitments hote hain.
Jo cheez profession ko khola woh **trust** tha, jaan-boojh kar bana hua: early flagship law firms Harvey
ke sath design partners ki tarah kaam karti thin, aur unke naamon ne wo kiya jo koi advertising nahi kar
sakti — kyunke law firms dekhti hain unke rivals kya adopt karte hain. Product lawyers se unki apni jagah
milta hai — Microsoft Word aur Outlook ke andar, profession ko move karne ke liye kehne ke bajaye.

**Aap kya udhar lete ho: vertical door naamon par khulta hai, features par nahi.** Yehi aapka expert
argument aur aapka first-client argument hai, scale par chalta hua. Harvey ke early design partners
aapka committed expert aur aapki pehli firm hain: wo reference jo vertical buyer ke sabse gehre dar ka
jawab deti hai, jo hai pehle jaana. Isi liye roadmap zid karta hai ke koi vertical bina signed expert ke
launch na ho. Aur isi liye aapka pehla engagement ek deliberate introductory price justify kar sakta hai,
jab agreement mein likhit shamil ho: ek named outcome publish karne ka haq, aur introductions maangne ka
haq. Mahinon ka kaam ek aisi reference ke badle mat do jise client ne dene par raazi nahi. Aur placement
lesson note karo: Harvey Word ke andar bechta hai kyunke wahin legal work hoti hai. Aapke Workers client
ke asal tools ke andar surface hone chahiye — practice management system, email, spreadsheet — ek aur
portal ke andar nahi jo firm ko yaad rakh kar kholna pare.

## Four Motions, One Law

Chaaron ko saath rakho aur era ka selling law nazar aata hai:

| Motion | Flagship (reported scale) | Kya Persuade Karta Hai | Aap Kya Lete Ho |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proof-led | Palantir, ~$8.15B guided 2026 | Buyer ki apni data par ek working result, din mein | Slice session + embedded engagement |
| Product-led | Cursor, ~$4B ARR (June 2026) | Practitioner ka apna daily experience | Wedge: firm ke andar juniors recruit karne wala free tier |
| Outcome-priced | Sierra, ~$200M ARR (mid-2026) | Ek price jo sirf tab exist karti hai jab result kare | Contract of success, ab market-scale precedent ke sath |
| Trust-led | Harvey, ~$190M ARR (Jan 2026) | Ek profession ke andar naamzad early adopters | Signed expert + quotable first client |

Chaaron mein se koi bhi classical salesforce ko apni growth ka engine nahi maanta. Harvey enterprise
sales process chalata hai, aur Cursor ne scale karte hue sellers hire kiye, aur dono cases mein persuading
zyada tar salesperson ke aane se pehle ho chuki hoti hai: naamon se, ya us product se jo pehle se building
ke andar hai. Har company ne persuasion ka bara hissa kisi structural cheez mein move kiya: proof, product,
price, ya trust. Yeh rival strategies nahi. Yeh persuasion rakhne ki chaar jagahein hain taake kisi
salesperson ko usay carry na karna pare. Aur yahan striking baat hai one-person vertical FDE ke baare
mein: aapki motion, pichle do pages mein assemble hui, pehle se chaaron rakhti hai. **Slice session aapka
proof hai. MCP par standing demo aapka product wedge hai. Contract of success aapki outcome price hai.
Aapke expert ka signature aapka trust hai.** Agla section inhein assemble karta hai.
