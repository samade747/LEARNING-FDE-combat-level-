## Sources & Evidence Grades (05 se recap)

| Claim | Grade | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palantir 93% revenue growth, shrinking sales headcount, ~500 net hires | **Measured** | Public company earnings release + shareholder letter (3 Aug 2026) |
| Cursor's sales-led revenue "100-fold in a year" | **Reported, not audited** | Company statements + press reporting (private company) |
| Sierra's per-resolution outcome pricing | **Structure documented, dollar amounts estimated** | Third-party estimates (private company, rates never company-confirmed) |
| Harvey's trust-led vertical door | **Reported, not audited** | Company statements + press reporting (private company) |
| "Buyers research vendors in AI before contact" | **Direction solid, exact % approximate** | Multiple 2026 business-buyer surveys (self-reported behavior) |
| "This scales down to a one-person vertical FDE" | **Reasoning, not a measured result** | This book's own structural-identity argument (bootcamp ≅ slice-first rule) |

**Causal boundary:** Palantir's quarter shows results, not which mechanism caused how much of the
growth — no public source isolates that. Treat it as strong evidence *for* deployment-led selling, not
a controlled measurement.
