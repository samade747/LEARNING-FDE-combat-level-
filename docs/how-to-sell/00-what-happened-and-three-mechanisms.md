# 00 — 3 August 2026 Ko Kya Hua, aur Palantir Ke 3 Mechanisms

**3 August 2026 ko, Palantir ne ek record quarter report ki: revenue ek saal mein 93% barh gaya — uski
industry ke sabse tez growth rates mein se ek. Aam taur par company aisi quarter ko apni sales team ki
tareef kar ke celebrate karti hai. Palantir ke CEO ne ulta kiya — unhone shareholders ko likh kar bataya
ke yeh growth ek aisi sales head count se hui jo **choti hai aur jaan-boojh kar aur choti ho rahi hai**.**

Yeh unusual boast aapke liye matter karta hai us din ki wajah se jab aapke paas becha jaane wala kuch
real hoga. Sale asal mein kaise hoti hai jab aapke paas koi sales team nahi, koi brand nahi, outreach ke
liye koi budget nahi? [Monetization roadmap](../how-to-get-paid/README.md) ne aapko asset aur price
banaya. Yeh page uska missing half deta hai: **selling motion**. Aur Palantir hi seekhne ke liye sahi
company hai, kyunke usne wahi Forward Deployed Engineer role invent kiya jo woh roadmap sikhata hai, aur
ab usne is role ki selling motion ko sabse bare scale par kaam karte hue dikhaya hai.

> **Ek sentence poora page carry karta hai: agentic era mein, deployment hi sales motion hai — working
> proof jo buyer ki apni data par bani ho aur buyer ke apne baseline ke against measure ki gayi ho, wohi
> kaam karta hai jo salesforce kiya karta tha.**

*(Grey road "Pitch" hai — ek promise, persuasion se carry hota, aur toot raha hai. Gold road "Proof" hai
— buyer ki apni data par ek working result, aur ab growth wahin chalti hai.)*

## What Happened on August 3, 2026

Pehle numbers, kyunke woh wajah hain jo koi sun raha hai. 2026 ki doosri quarter mein, Palantir ne $1.9
billion revenue report ki, saal-dar-saal 93% zyada, aur ~$1.1 billion profit — ek quarter mein itna
profit jitna pichle saal usi period mein company ka poora revenue tha. US commercial revenue saal-dar-saal
149% barha, US government revenue 90% barha, aur company ne apna full-year revenue guidance ~$8.15
billion tak barha diya. CEO Alex Karp ne CNBC ko bataya ke, unki jaankari mein, Palantir ke scale ka koi
business kabhi itni tezi se — aadhi speed se bhi — nahi barha. Quarter mein $1 million+ ke 220 deals close
huye, jinmein se 73 $10 million+ ke the.

Phir woh sentence jo isay earnings page se sales page banata hai. Shareholder letter mein, CEO Alex Karp
ne likha ke results ek "minuscule and shrinking" sales head count se aaye, aur isay company ka
conventional wisdom discard karne ka ek aur case bataya. Unhone add kiya ke US commercial business mein
28% quarter-over-quarter acceleration ka matlab hai jo doosri companies ko haasil karne mein ek saal ya
zyada lagta hai, Palantir woh ~90 din mein kar sakta hai.

3 mahine pehle unhone "minuscule" par ek number rakha tha, aur yehi number yaad rakhne laayak hai.
First-quarter call par unhone kaha Palantir ke paas ~70 salespeople hain, aur inmein se sirf ~7 hi asal
mein "bechte" hain — jo kaam ek comparable company 7,000 logon se staff karti. 7,000 ko rhetoric maano.
70 ko wo fact maano jo matter karta hai, kyunke yeh cheez ki shape batata hai: ek ~$8 billion annual
revenue ke qareeb company, ek sales organization se becheti hui jo ek mid-sized firm ke ek department se
chhoti hai.

90-din wala claim dhyan se parho, kyunke isay galat parhna aasan hai. Karp growth rates compare kar rahe
the, sales cycle describe nahi kar rahe the. Palantir ka US commercial business ek quarter mein 28%
barha, jo roughly ek healthy software company saal bhar mein achieve karne ki ummeed rakhti hai. Woh yeh
report nahi kar rahe the ke Palantir enterprise deals 90 din mein close karta hai. Defensible conclusion
zyada narrow hai, aur phir bhi remarkable hai: ek deployment-led model commercial value itni speed se
create/expand kar sakta hai jitni conventional enterprise sales models kam hi pahonchti hain. Is book ka
apna naam hai is machinery ka: **proof cycle** — first contact aur ek working result ke darmiyan din jo
buyer measure kar sake.

Ek boundary yahan honi chahiye, aur neeche ka honest label isay repeat karta hai. Sales team choti ho
rahi hai, lekin Palantir ne 2025 mein overall ~500 employees add kiye. Company ne product aur customer ke
darmiyan insano ko hataya nahi. Usne badla ke woh insan **kya karte hain**. Yeh distinction is poore page
ka lesson hai — isay pakre rakho.

## How Palantir Actually Sells: Teen Mechanisms

**Plain words mein:** teen cheezein wo kaam karti hain jo salesforce kiya karti thi.

1. **Bootcamp** — chand din jismein Palantir engineers customer ki apni data par kuch real banate hain.
   Customer apna problem solve hote dekhta hai, aur room mein baithe log khud salespeople ban jate hain.
2. **Embedded engineer** — FDE, jo client ki company ke andar baithta hai aur real value tak build karta
   hai. Uske hafte ka bara hissa customer se unke problems ke baare mein baat karne mein jata hai — yeh
   wahi kaam hai jo ek account manager karta tha, us insan se jo mauqe par usay fix bhi kar sakta hai.
3. **Land and expand** — pehla contract chota hota hai. Value prove hoti hai, phir account ek-ek use
   case karke barhta hai, aur revenue services se software ki taraf shift hoti hai.

### The Bootcamp Replaces the Pitch

Jab Palantir ne 2023 mein AIP launch ki, usay demand ki lehar mili aur koi traditional salesforce process
karne ke liye nahi tha. Ek salesforce banane ke bajaye, usne ek event format banaya: **software
bootcamp** — half hackathon, half conference, chand din mein customer ki apni data aur customer ke asal
operational problem par chalta hai, aakhir mein slide deck ke bajaye ek **functioning capability** ke
sath khatam hota hai. Company ne analysts ko saaf bataya ke bootcamps hi AIP bechne ka tareeqa honge, aur
ek hi earnings call mein 21 baar unka zikr kiya. Company ke apne head of business development ne
bootcamp room ke peeche se effect describe kiya: ek baar customers product try karte hain, woh stage par
pahonch jate hain, apne peers ko results present karte hue.

Dhyan do bootcamp structurally kya hai. Yeh ek demo nahi, kyunke demo vendor ki data par chalta hai aur
buyer ki duniya ke baare mein kuch prove nahi karta. Yeh ek **compressed deployment** hai: real data,
real problem, real output, din mein. Kisi institution ke AI par shak ko cross karne ka sabse tez tareeqa
usay uske apne operation ka ek hissa dikhana hai, already improved. Aap platform nahi bech rahe. Aap
buyer ko unki apni reality ki ek jhalak bech rahe ho, behtar kaam karte hue.

### The Embedded Engineer Replaces the Account Executive

Doosra mechanism wahi hai jo yeh book shuru se sikha rahi hai, money side se dekha gaya. Palantir sales
team ko software aur customer ko manual nahi thamata. Woh FDEs aur Deployment Strategists client ki
operations ke andar embed karta hai: builders jo client ke environment mein tab tak kaam karte hain jab
tak solutions production mein na chalein, strategists ke sath jo confirm karte hain ke kaam sahi
problems target kare aur adopt ho.

Yahan wo detail hai jo sales page ke liye matter karti hai. Ek detailed analysis ke mutabiq, ek Palantir
AI-era FDE apne working week ka ~30-40% conversational customer discovery mein kharch karta hai. Yeh
number dobara parho. Engineer ke waqt ka ek-tihai bilkul wohi kaam hai jo ek account manager karta tha:
sunna, agla problem dhoondna, relationship banana. Farq yeh hai ke jab yeh insan agla problem dhoondta
hai, woh solution bhi bana sakta hai — kabhi kabhi usi hafte. Discovery aur delivery ek role mein collapse
ho gaye, aur dedicated salesperson ke kaam ka bara hissa isi mein move ho gaya.

### Land and Expand Replaces the Enterprise Deal Cycle

Teesra mechanism contract ki shape hai. Palantir ke engagements chote shuru hote hain: ek bootcamp aur
licenses ka ek limited set. Agar value prove ho jaye, zyada use cases, workflows, aur data domains layer
hote jate hain, aur waqt ke sath revenue mix services se software subscription ki taraf tilt hota hai. Ek
consulting firm ke ulta, services product adoption drive karne ke liye hain, business khud nahi. Aur
zyada tar software vendors ke ulta, Palantir ek qeemat wale customer ko land karne ke liye apna
engineering waqt pehle se fund karega.

To deal ko kabhi bara bech-na nahi parta. Isay bas chota prove karna parta hai, phir yeh barhta hai.
Deals aksar C-suite ya mission-critical program level par sponsor hote hain, jo field team ko sirf ek
tool install karne ke bajaye workflows reframe karne ki authority deta hai. Quarter ke $1 million+ ke 220
deals is model se consistent hain, aur ek limit record par honi chahiye: Palantir disclose nahi karta
inmein se kitne existing accounts ke expansions the, naye customer wins nahi.

## The Law: Deployment Is the Sales Motion

Ab Palantir ke model ko us cheez ke bagal mein rakho jo yeh book aap se pehle hi karwa chuki hai, aur
dekho woh same shape hain.

[Monetization roadmap](../how-to-get-paid/03-three-doors-and-pricing.md) ka sequence rule tha **build
first, sell second**: slice ek aisa step nahi jo buyer ka intezar kare, yeh woh step hai jo buyer paida
karta hai — kyunke koi bhi apna baseline uske saath disclose nahi karta jise kuch dikhaya hi na gaya ho.
Yehi bootcamp logic hai, ek insan ke liye state ki gayi. [Contract of success](../how-to-get-paid/04-earning-while-walking-and-failure-modes.md)
kaam ko 3 numbers se price karta hai jo buyer verify kar sake: baseline, target, acceptance. Yehi land
and expand hai, ek engagement ke liye state ki gayi. Aur khud FDE role — embedded jab tak outcome prove
na ho — wahi collapsed discovery-and-delivery role hai jisne account executive ke kaam ka bara hissa
absorb kar liya.

To law, ek line mein: **proof ne pitch ko replace kiya, aur jo insan proof banata hai usne us insan ko
replace kiya jo pitch deta tha.** Palantir ne apni salesforce isliye chhoti nahi ki kyunke software
magically khud bikna shuru ho gaya. Usne salesforce chhoti ki jabke commercial kaam ka zyada hissa un
engineers/strategists ko diya jo deploy kar ke bechte hain. Head count move hua — isi liye 500 net hires
matter karte hain: selling kaam ki tarah gayab nahi hui, yeh ek alag job mein kam concentrated ho gayi.

Isi fact ki ek aur honest reading, kyunke yeh aapko sabse tempting ghalat conclusion se bachati hai.
Sales head count choti hai aur choti ho rahi hai, aur zero nahi. Palantir abhi bhi salespeople employ
karta hai, Harvey ek enterprise sales process chalata hai, aur Cursor ne barhte hue sellers hire kiye,
kyunke kuch commercial kaam irreducible hai chahe proof kitna bhi acha ho: kaunse buyers ko approach
karna, ask karna, contract negotiate karna, price hold karna, aur signature lena. Proof persuasion carry
kar sakta hai. Yeh paper sign nahi kar sakta. Aapke one-person version mein, wo irreducible remainder
gayab nahi hota. Yeh aap par aata hai, aur aapko isay real weekly kaam ki tarah plan karna chahiye: agla
partner choose karna, ask bhejna, boundary sentence bolna, scope negotiate karna (price nahi), aur
introduction request karna. Iske liye hafte mein ~1 din ki ummeed rakho, aur isay ek planning shape ki
tarah lo, measurement nahi. Neeche ke failure modes bilkul isi din ki discipline hain. Jo reader "proof
replaces the pitch" ko "koi bechta hi nahi" samajh le, usne is page ko galat parha. Selling itni choti ho
gayi ke ek insan usay engineering ke bagal carry kar sake — aur yehi wo poori wajah hai jis se yeh model
aapke liye khula hai.

Purane services pyramid ke liye, yeh ek business model ka ant hai. Aapke liye, yeh darwaza hai, aur wajah
structural hai. Personal relationships aur brand recognition par bana sales motion dashkon leta hai aur
paisa jo aapke paas nahi. Proof par bana sales motion ek thin slice, ek laptop, aur din leta hai. Yeh
enterprise software mein ek rare cheez hai: ek sales motion jo Lahore ya Lagos mein baitha ek skilled
individual apne scale par chala sakta hai, kyunke yeh motion engineering hai.
