## Ek Line Mein Poori Cheez

> **3 August 2026, Palantir ne 93% revenue growth report ki — ek "minuscule and shrinking" sales team
> se.** Lesson: agentic era mein **deployment hi sales motion hai** — buyer ki apni data par working
> proof dikhana, salesforce ki jagah. **Pitch toot jaati hai, proof nahi.**
