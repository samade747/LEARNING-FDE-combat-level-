# 02 — Your Version of the Model: The One-Person Bootcamp

**Plain words mein:** aap Palantir ka balance sheet copy nahi kar sakte, lekin uski motion copy kar
sakte ho, kyunke motion un cheezon se bani hai jo aapke paas pehle se hain: ek slice, ek laptop, aur
kaam ke din. Aapka bootcamp ek chhoti firm ke ek partner ke sath ek afternoon hai, jismein aapki slice
unki chand real files par chalti hai. Aapka embedded engineer aap khud ho, retainer par. Aapka land and
expand contract of success hai jo agle outcome mein barhta hai. Aur aapke paas ek tool hai jo Palantir
ke model mein kabhi nahi tha: **aapka System of Record ek website ki tarah public hai, aur ek technical
buyer aapse milne se pehle hi ek agent ko iske MCP endpoint se connect kar ke test kar sakta hai yeh kya
jaanta hai.**

Obvious objection pehle: Palantir apni motion hazaron engineers aur ek aisi balance sheet se chalata hai
jo mahinon ka free kaam pay kar sakti hai. Kya ek insan wahi motion bilkul chala sakta hai? Row-by-row,
haan, kyunke har mechanism apna ek sasta version banata hai. Left column woh hai jo $8 billion/saal
revenue wali company karti hai. Right column ko sirf discipline chahiye, paisa nahi.

| Palantir Ka Mechanism | Aapka Version (Vendor-Neutral Vertical FDE) |
| --- | --- |
| AIP bootcamp: customer ki data par din, room mein engineers | **Slice session:** ek partner ke sath ek afternoon, aapki thin slice unki 3-5 real (zaroorat ho to anonymized) files par chalti hai, unke apne baseline number ke sath whiteboard par khatam |
| Embedded FDE, hafte ka ek-tihai discovery par | Aap, engagement ke andar, discovery aur delivery ek insan ki tarah kar rahe ho: har problem ki baat agli slice ka requirements document hai |
| Ek chote pehle contract se land aur expand | Ek outcome par contract of success, phir retainer, phir doosra outcome confidence se price kiya kyunke connection pattern pehle se hai |
| C-suite sponsorship jo workflows reframe karne ki authority deta hai | 20-200-person firm mein ek decision-making partner: roadmap ka honest boundary ke aapka real buyer kaun hai |
| Product demo jo ab zyada tar selling carry karta hai | **Standing demo:** SoR website jo koi bhi buyer aaj parh sakta hai, plus ek MCP endpoint connection guide unki technical team ke liye, koi kuch bhi bech nahi raha |
| Motion ke around agents accelerants ki tarah | Aapke Workers prospect research karte hain, follow-up draft karte hain, aur har engagement ke baad before-and-after evidence pack compile karte hain |

Table par 3 notes, kyunke har row ek discipline chupati hai.

**Slice session ki ek definition of done hai, aur woh ek number hai.** Palantir ka bootcamp ek functioning
capability ke sath khatam hota hai. Aapka is se behtar cheez se khatam hota hai, aapke scale ke liye:
buyer ka apna baseline, room mein measured. 4 ghante/file, 60 filings/mahina, 2 din partner review/engagement.
Yeh number le kar niklo aur contract of success arithmetic ban jata hai. Bina isake niklo aur aapne ek
free demo diya. Session ki boundary usi saans mein honi chahiye, ek sentence jo aap bol sako:

> Yeh session free hai, aur aapke baseline number ke sath khatam hota hai. Aapki zyada files par ek
> pilot pehla priced outcome hai — [X] files, [price] ke liye, ek contract of success ke tehat, aur agar
> deliver na ho to exit kaisa dikhega yeh bhi.

**Poora session, ek sheet par:**

**Session se pehle, taiyari ke chand ghante.**
- [ ] Thin slice sample files par improvisation ke bina end-to-end chalti hai
- [ ] Anonymization checklist print hai, client ne dekh li hai
- [ ] 3-5 real files pehle se agree, zaroorat ho to anonymized
- [ ] Whiteboard sawal unke apne alfaz mein likha hua hai: ek file/filing/review aaj aapko kya cost karti hai?
- [ ] Contract of success template bag mein hai: baseline, target, acceptance, exit clause

**Anonymization checklist, kisi bhi file ko chhoone se pehle unke reviewer ke sath approved.**
- [ ] Client names, addresses, identifiers replace ya mask kiye
- [ ] Financial figures structure mein preserved, value mein scrambled jahan kaam allow kare
- [ ] Koi file client ki machine se bahar nahi jati jab tak engagement letter ijazat na de
- [ ] Client ka apna reviewer session se pehle is list ko sign karta hai, copy rakhta hai
- [ ] Session mein process hui har cheez unke saamne delete hoti hai end mein, jab tak likhit mein alag agree na ho

Agar unka reviewer list approve nahi karta, session unke jaisi shape wali synthetic files par chalao. Ek
chota proof ek compliance incident se behtar hai.

**Room mein, ek afternoon.**
1. 10 minute: woh apne alfaz mein kaam describe karte hain. Unke alfaz likho, kyunke har complaint ek
   requirements document hai.
2. Slice unki files par chalao. Present mat karo. Unhein apna problem solve hote dekhne do.
3. Agar aapki vertical ka expert twin live hai, partner ko keyboard do: ek sawal, unke apne alfaz mein,
   aur unhein citation aate dekhne do.
4. Jab output aaye, unke sath baseline measure karo: unka number, unki units, unka process.
5. Number whiteboard par likho. Permission se photograph karo.
6. Boundary sentence bolo, aur ruk jao.

**Boundary script — exactly bolo, phir chup ho jao** (upar diya gaya). Agar woh free pilot maangein,
jawab wahi sentence hai, garmjoshi se, doosri baar. Agar woh price par push karein, scope narrow karo,
price-per-outcome kabhi nahi: kam file types, ek workflow, ek quarter. Agar koi decision-maker aur date
naam na le sake aapke jaane se pehle, yeh ek deal nahi ek conversation thi: ek baar follow up karo, phir
agle partner ki taraf move karo.

**Session ke baad, usi hafte.**
- [ ] One-page outcome note: unka baseline, proposed pehla outcome, price, exit clause
- [ ] Contract of success 2 din ke andar bheja, jab whiteboard photo fresh ho
- [ ] Agar woh sign karein, pilot Outcome 1 hai, aur checker ki monthly report renewal case banana shuru karti hai
- [ ] Agar nahi karein, phir bhi ek introduction maango, kyunke ek profession mein sab ek doosre ko jaante hain

**Standing demo aapka structural advantage hai, isay usable banao.** Palantir ke proof ko room mein
engineers schedule karne padte hain. Aapko nahi, lekin iske do halves alag friction carry karte hain, aur
yeh dikhawa karna ke aisa nahi, buyers kho deta hai. Website half truly frictionless hai: koi bhi kisi
bhi waqt aapki governed corpus bina permission maange parh sakta hai. MCP half ek deeper channel hai real
setup ke sath, kyunke ek agent ko endpoint se connect karne ke liye compatible client, authentication,
aur kuch knowledge chahiye. To ek one-page connection guide, sample questions, aur low-friction
authentication path publish karo, aur buyer ka technical insan aapki vertical kya jaanti hai test kar
sakta hai bina aapko kabhi schedule kiye. Yeh sab kuch aapko kuch bhi extra kharch nahi karta jo aap
Station 7 par pehle hi bana chuke.

**Expansion pitch aapka checker likhta hai.** Land and expand proven value par chalta hai, matlab kisi
ko usay continuously prove karna hoga. Yehi aapka evaluation set aur checker ke liye tha. Ek monthly
one-page report, ek Worker se generate hui, baseline, current number, aur escalated exceptions dikhati
hui — yehi ek one-person firm ki poori expansion salesforce hai. Ek engagement jo khud ko measure karta
hai apna renewal case khud banata hai.

**Aapke proof ke peeche ek ecosystem hai, sahi mauqon par usay udhar lo.** Aap pehli meeting mein sirf
apni slice le kar nahi jaate, kyunke jo method aap bech rahe ho woh pehle se public mein khud par chal
raha hai. [Agent Factory Ecosystem](../ecosystem-overview/README.md) is pattern ka live reference hai:
yeh book insano ko ek website ki tarah aur agents ko MCP par serve hui, [Zia Tutor AI](../ecosystem-zia-tutor-ai/README.md)
ke sath working expert twin ki tarah jo isi se sikhati hai. Sale mein 3 moments, 3 borrowings. Day zero
par, kisi client ke bina, ecosystem skeptical buyer ke sabse mushkil sawal ka jawab deta hai — "kya yeh
pattern kahin kaam kar chuka hai" — ek link ke sath, claim ke bajaye. Room mein, ek baar aapka apna
vertical twin live ho jaye, yeh apni khud ki proof surface ban jata hai: partner ko unki profession ke
alfaz mein ek sawal poochne do, aur citation aate dekhne do — kyunke sources ke sath jawab deta expert
twin aisi demonstration hai jo koi slide match nahi kar sakti. Aur expansion par, [System of Context](../ecosystem-system-of-context/README.md)
wo proof hai jo pehle nahi, doosre outcome ko close karta hai: jab ek Worker aapki governed source aur
firm ka apna memo cite kare, aur report kare ke dono disagree karte hain, connecting layer ne khud ko
us insan ke saamne prove kar diya jo sign karta hai. Ek gate is aakhri borrowing ke sath chalti hai, wahi
gate jo System of Context page khud state karti hai: connecting layer slice ship hone aur ek Worker uske
cite karne ke **baad** banti hai, pehle kabhi nahi — isay pehli afternoon mein promise mat karo.
