# 03 — The Other Side of the Table: Buyer Ka Agent Pehle Se Shortlist Kar Raha Hai

**Plain words mein:** upar wala sab kuch seller ke badalne ke baare mein hai. Buyer bhi badle — zyada tar
aapke favor mein. Ab koi buyer kisi vendor se baat karne se pehle ek AI se poochta hai. 2026 ke business
buyer surveys batate hain zyada tar apni purchase mein kahin na kahin AI use karte hain, ~aadhe apni
research wahin se shuru karte hain, aur AI answers ab pehle source ki tarah websites aur salespeople ka
muqabla karte hain.

Iska matlab hai aapki selling ka ek hissa ab ek aisi answer ke andar hota hai jo aap kabhi nahi dekhte:
ek partner apni AI se poochta hai unki profession/country ke liye agentic automation mein kaun madad kar
sakta hai, aur ya to aapki vertical us jawab mein hai ya nahi. Achi khabar yeh hai ke aapke System of
Record ke public pages bilkul isi game ke liye bane hain: public, structured, saaf likhe, aur citable —
jahan zyada tar competitors ki marketing ek brochure hai jise AI quote nahi kar sakta. Aur ek baar buyer
ne aapko dhoond liya, aapka MCP endpoint unki technical team ko ek direct tareeqa deta hai test karne ka
ke aapki vertical asal mein kya jaanti hai.

Is hafte kahin, ek mid-sized firm ke partner ne apne AI assistant mein ek sawal type kiya: kya AI humari
tarah ka kaam handle kar sakta hai, aur hamare jaisi firms ke liye yeh kaun karta hai. Assistant ne 2-3
naamon se jawab diya. Us list ki har firm ab ek sales conversation mein hai jo unhone shuru karne ke liye
kuch nahi kiya. List se missing har firm ko pata bhi nahi ke koi buyer exist karta tha.

Yeh scene ab unusual nahi. **G2 ka 2026 AI Search Insight Report** ne paya ke 51% business software
buyers ab apni research Google se zyada AI chatbot se shuru karte hain, 71% kahin na kahin AI chatbots par
depend karte hain, aur chatbot ka answer aksar shortlist khud steer karta hai: 69% ne kaha ek chatbot ne
unhein plan se alag vendor select karne ki taraf le gaya, aur buyers AI chatbots ko shortlist shape karne
wala sabse influential source rank karte hain. **Forrester** ka 2026 survey (~18,000 business buyers) isi
direction ishara karta hai: 94% ne apni last purchase mein AI use kiya report kiya (89% se up ek saal
pehle), aur nearly half kisi bhi vendor se contact karne se pehle internal business case bana lete hain.
Market ne is response ka naam rakh diya hai: **answer engine optimization** — itna clear aur credible
content publish karne ka craft ke AI systems usay cite karein jab buyer poochay.

Isay us cheez se compare karo jo aap Station 7 par bana chuke, aur ek unusual baat dekho: **aapke public
pages pehle se buyer ke agent ke liye optimized hain — structurally, architecture ke ek side effect ki
tarah.** AI systems us content ko favor karte hain jo public, plainly written, question-shaped, specific,
aur corroborated ho. Ek governed System of Record bilkul yehi hai, apni construction se: ek source of
truth, plain language mein likha, un sawalon ke around organized jo ek practitioner asal mein poochta
hai, ek expert ke naam ke sath judgment par.

Ek clarification isay honest rakhti hai, kyunke do alag layers yahan aasani se mix ho sakti hain. **MCP
endpoint aapko discoverable nahi banata:** public AI search MCP servers nahi parhta, aur buyer ka agent
aapke endpoint tak sirf tab pahonchta hai jab koi jaan-boojh kar isay connect kare. Discovery aapke public
pages aur external corroboration ki hai. Endpoint jo add karta hai woh discovery ke **baad** aata hai, aur
yeh aisi cheez hai jo koi marketing site nahi de sakti: jis buyer ne aapko dhoond liya, woh apna agent
aapki governed corpus par point kar ke test kar sakta hai yeh kya jaanti hai — ek bhi meeting schedule
hone se pehle.

*(Diagram: "public AI search stops here, MCP endpoint deliberately connect hota hai" — question → answer
[public pages ki wajah se] → dashed line → deep test [MCP guide se] → proof [slice session] → renewal
[checker ki monthly report]. Public pages aapko discoverable banate hain, MCP endpoint testable, slice
provable, aur checker isay prove karta rehta hai.)*

Teen practical moves follow karte hain, aur koi bhi paisa kharch nahi karta.

**Wo pages likho jo buyer ke sawal ke haqdar hain.** Opening scene ka sawal aapki vertical ke liye ek exact
shape rakhta hai: kya AI Pakistan mein audit firms ke liye working-paper preparation handle kar sakta hai,
aur kaun yeh karta hai. Aapke SoR ke public pages mein is sawal ka plain, direct jawab hona chahiye,
jurisdiction naam ke sath — kyunke model sirf wahi cite kar sakta hai jo exist karta hai. Checklist 4
items ki hai: page public HTML hai, headings buyer ke apne sawal hain jurisdiction ke sath, direct answer
pehle 40 words mein hai, aur kam az kam ek independent source aapke baare mein wahi baat kehta hai. Yeh
corpus ke upar add ki gayi marketing nahi — yeh khud corpus hai, apna doosra kaam karte hue.

**Answer page ka fill-in template:**

Isay ordinary public HTML ki tarah apni SoR site par publish karo. Har bracket bharo. Isay gate mat karo,
PDF mein mat daalo, marketing ki tarah mat likho. Har heading buyer ka apna sawal hai, direct answer
pehle 40 words mein.

> **Kya AI [professional outcome] [firm type] ke liye [jurisdiction] mein handle kar sakta hai?**
> Haan, defined limits ke andar. [Ek sentence: Worker kya karta hai, kaunse inputs par, kya output.] Har
> jawab [governed source] cite karta hai, aur [exception types] [named human role] tak escalate hote hain.
>
> **Hamari jaisi firms ke liye yeh kaun karta hai?**
> [Aapka naam/firm naam] isay [firm type] ke liye [jurisdiction] mein banata aur chalata hai.
>
> **Yeh asal mein roz kya karta hai?** [3-5 plain sentences — files, checks, output, human sign-off moment.]
>
> **Yeh kya karne se mana karta hai?** [2-3 sentences — boundaries honestly: escalate hone wale case types,
> judgments jo kabhi nahi banata, jurisdiction line jo cross nahi karta. Yeh section kisi bhi capability
> claim se zyada trust banata hai.]
>
> **Hamari technical team meeting se pehle isay kaise test kar sakti hai?** Governed knowledge MCP par
> serve hoti hai. [Connection guide link karo.] Koi bhi MCP-compatible agent connect karo, phir 3 sawal
> try karo: [ek citable jawab wala real question], [ek jiska jawab jurisdiction ke rules chahta ho], [ek
> jise system escalate kare, jawab na de]. Teesra jaan-boojh kar hai: system ko sahi tareeqe se mana
> karte dekhna sabse tez trust test hai.
>
> **Kya yeh kahin real mein kaam kar chuka?** [Named outcome, ek paragraph — firm type, measured baseline,
> baad ka number, sign-off kis ne kiya. Agar abhi tak nahi hai, honest version likho: slice live hai,
> expert ne sign kiya hai, pehla contract of success khula hai. Kabhi outcome invent mat karo.] Dono
> soorat mein, method ka apna live reference link karo: [Agent Factory ecosystem](../ecosystem-overview/README.md)
> yehi exact pattern khud par chalata hai, public mein, ek book MCP par serve hoti hai aur ek [expert
> twin](../ecosystem-zia-tutor-ai/README.md) usi se sikhata hai.
>
> **Ek engagement kaisa dikhta hai?** Ek free afternoon: slice session, aapki 3-5 apni files par chalti,
> room mein measured baseline number ke sath khatam. Uske baad, ek priced outcome contract of success ke
> tehat: baseline, target, aur reviewer kaise check karega. [Contact page link karo.]

**Corroboration step:** upar wala page zaroori hai, kaafi nahi — kyunke AI systems un claims ko favor
karte hain jo independent sources ke across confirmed hon. In mein se kam az kam ek in bharosa karne se
pehle rakho: aapke expert ka article unki professional body ke publication mein jo vertical naam le,
client ka named outcome jahan client apna text control kare, ya industry event par recorded talk jismein
vertical title mein ho.

**Maintenance rule:** stale pages citations kho dete hain. Har quarter is page ko dobara parho, named
outcome aur date update karo, aur pehle 40 words ka jawab true rakho.

**Be corroborated somewhere you do not control.** Models un claims ko favor karte hain jo independent
sources ke across confirmed hon. Aapke expert ka professional-body article, client ka named outcome, ek
industry event par recorded talk — vertical ke ek-do external mentions, naam se, buyer ke answer ke liye
kisi bhi self-description se zyada kaam karte hain.

**Informed first meeting ki ummeed rakho, aur khush ho.** Ek buyer jo aap se pehle hi AI ke andar compare
kar chuka hai, usne aapke liye qualification kar diya. MIT ka finding ke zyada tar enterprise pilots kuch
return nahi karte, buyers ne bhi absorb kar liya hai — isi liye woh ab demos se skeptical hote hain aur
baselines maangte hain. Forrester ne apni 2026 read isi sentence se title ki jo yeh page shuru se argue
kar rahi hai: **risk-averse buyers proof maangte hain, promises nahi.** Ek skeptical buyer ek pitch ke
liye buri khabar hai. Yeh ek proof ke liye sabse achi mumkin khabar hai.
