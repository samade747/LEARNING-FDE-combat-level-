## Yeh Course Kis Baare Mein Hai

Imagine karo aapka Digital FTE raat 2 baje kaam kar raha hai jab aap so rahe hain. Woh ek expense file
karta hai, vendor book karta hai, reply bhejta hai. **Ek sawal decide karta hai yeh safe hai ya
disaster: uski identity kiski hai?**

Pichle courses mein aapki app sirf **borrow** karti thi jo bhi host chala raha tha uski identity. Yeh
theek hai jab tak insan chair mein baitha ho. Lekin ek worker jo tab chale jab koi insan chair mein na
ho, kisi insan ka login borrow nahi kar sakta — agar woh aapki tarah login kare, to woh **aap ban jata
hai**: sab kuch kar sakta hai jo aap kar sakte ho, hamesha ke liye, aur har log kehti hai aap ne kiya.
**Isay scope, time-box, ya wapis nahi liya ja sakta.**

Yeh course wahin hai jahan identity **borrow** hona band ho jati hai. Do halves mein banate hain: **pehle
apna sign-in own karo** (rented login ki jagah, apna production-grade OAuth/OIDC server). **Phir agent
ko apni identity do** — scoped, time-boxed, revocable, human-approved credential.
