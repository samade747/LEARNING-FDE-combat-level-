# 06 — Yeh Model Kahan Apply Nahi Hota, Aur Shuru Kahan Se Karein

## Model Kahan Apply Nahi Hota

Ek useful model yeh bhi batata hai ke woh kahan apply nahi hota. Har cheez ko shared layer mein promote
nahi karna chahiye. Woh kaam jo ek jurisdiction ke liye specific ho, contract se restricted ho, ya ek
customer ke unusual process se bandha ho, Layer 4 par hi rehna chahiye. Aisa kaam shared platform mein
move karne se platform manage karna mushkil aur unreliable ho jata hai. Do customers mein repetition sirf
ek signal hai dekhne ka — promotion review ke liye normal trigger teen ya zyada customers ka use hona
hai, saath strong strategic fit ke.

**Ek vertical bina committed domain expert ke launch nahi honi chahiye.** Expert twin vertical ka product
hai, aur bina expert ke vertical sirf ek corpus hai. Expert ke bina thin slice bhi nahi banti, kyunke
reflexes expert ki voice mein author hote hain aur uski apni files se derive hote hain. To jab tak expert
commit na kare, us domain ko Layer 1 aur Layer 4 engagements se serve karo — yehi service ladder hai, yeh
kamata hai, aur aksar isi se expert milta hai. Kaunsi vertical yeh rule pass karti hai, aur har candidate
ko kaunse tests survive karne hain, yeh apna discipline hai:
[Choosing Your Vertical](../ecosystem-choosing-your-vertical/README.md).

Model ek stable base bhi maanta hai: jab tak foundation beta mein hai, har vertical uske bugs multiply
karti hai. Isiliye hum pattern ko ek vertical par prove kar rahe hain, poori tarah kholne se pehle.

## Shuru Kahan Se Karein

Agar aap naye ho, [crash courses](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/getting-started) se shuru
karo. Yeh aapko foundations se le kar Digital FTEs banane tak, ek layer ek waqt mein le jaate hain.
Samajhne ke liye ke aap is model mein kaunsa role play kar sakte ho, book ka
*"roles this book trains"* page parho. Jab aap foundation par khud banane ke liye ready ho,
[System of Record](../ecosystem-system-of-record/README.md) se apna agent connect karo. Model destination
aur structure dikhata hai; courses wahan pohanchne ke steps dikhate hain.
