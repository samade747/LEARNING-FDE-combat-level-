# 03 — Layer 4: Customer Instances

Plain words mein: yeh sab kuch ek company ke liye kaam mein laata hai. AI Workers manufacture hote hain,
aur improvement measure aur prove hoti hai.

**Produce karta hai:** ek deployment jo ek company ke liye ek defined, measured business outcome achieve
kare. Sirf ek working system kaafi nahi.

## Customer Kahan Se Aata Hai

Sab kuch ek **customer** se shuru hota hai jo already ek number par razi ho chuka hai. Isse pehle ek
**sponsor** hota hai: ek real company ke andar ek named insaan jise starting number discuss karne ka
authority ho, sign hone se pehle. Ek company sponsor nahi hai. Bina authority wala job title sponsor
nahi hai. Aur jo starting number discuss hi na kare woh sponsor nahi hai.

**Yeh conversation System of Record kamata hai, pitch nahi.** [Choosing Your Vertical](../ecosystem-choosing-your-vertical/README.md)
profession aur beachhead chunti hai. [Designing the Vertical System of Record](../ecosystem-designing-the-vertical-sor/README.md)
pehla **slice** derive karta hai: ek professional outcome, poori tarah cover kiya hua, apne outcome
contract, invariants, decision map, exceptions, ek derived reflex, ek checker, aur ek evaluation set ke
saath. Ek outcome wala System of Record **thin** kehlata hai, kai wala **thick**. Words sirf outcomes
count karte hain, corners nahi.

To vertical business ka ek order hai, aur order customer se shuru nahi hota:

**expert → thin slice → sponsor → baseline → contract of success → engagement → thicker System of Record**

Ek arrow har commercial cheez se pehle aata hai, aur woh **slice** hai. Ek mid-size firm mein apni khud
ki profession ka ek governed page le kar jao, upar plain words aur neeche expert ke cited rules, aur
buyer pitch nahi sun raha — woh apna khud ka kaam parh raha hai, aur agla sawal uski taraf se aata hai.

**Do numbers, do sources, do lamhe.** Slice expert ki apni files se derive hota hai: real cases, jismein
ek jo review mein fail hui aur ek jo escalate hui. Baseline customer ke workflow mein measure hoti hai,
kyunke yehi wo number hai jo buyer verify kar sakta hai. To slice contact se pehle banta hai aur baseline
uske baad — dono mein koi contradiction nahi.

**Do ladders, ek-ek rule.** "Pehle banao, phir becho" vertical ladder ko govern karta hai. Yeh service
ladder ko govern nahi karta. Ek graduate jiska abhi koi committed expert nahi, Layer 1 aur Layer 4 par
method System of Record se kamata hai — clients ki apni manuals se governed Systems of Record bana kar,
aur deployed generic tools se Workers manufacture kar ke. Zyada tar graduates ke liye yeh pehle aata hai,
kamata hai, aur aksar isi se expert milta hai.

## Har Engagement Ko Do Fixed Points Chahiye

1. **Ek agreed starting point** — dono taraf se aaj ki performance aur target define ki jati hai, kaam
   shuru hone se pehle.
2. **Ek proven finishing point** — team production evidence se dikhati hai ke target reach hua.

Shuru hai **contract of success**. Koi bhi building shuru hone se pehle, graduate aur customer likhit
mein teen cheezon par razi hote hain: **baseline** (aaj kitni der lagti hai — jaise ek working-paper file
mein 4 ghante), **target** (kya success ginega — jaise wahi file 40 minute mein), aur **acceptance
criteria** (exact conditions jab kaam ho gaya maana jayega — jaise 95% files pehli baar sahi, aur firm
ke apne reviewers approve karein).

Finish hai **production mein proof**. Success company ke real roz ke kaam mein dikhana zaroori hai, real
data aur real users ke saath, sirf demo mein nahi. Teen tarah ka evidence chahiye:

1. **Business KPI measurement** — kya agreed target reach hua
2. **Adoption** — jo log is kaam ke zimmedar hain, kya woh system actually use karte hain
3. **[Agent evaluations](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/eval-driven-development-crash-course)**
   — tests dikhate hain system sahi behave karta rehta hai

Dono chahiye kyunke ek agent apni technical evaluations pass kar sakta hai bina business outcome behtar
kiye.

## Darmiyan Mein: Vertical Ko Company Ke Mutabiq Fit Karna

Start aur finish ke beech, team ek vertical ecosystem leti hai aur company ke gird fit karti hai: uska
ontology (company ke concepts ka map), data, workflows, log. Manufacturing tool Layer 3 ka domain
builder hai, Mode 2 mein chalta hua. Isi se graduate AI Workers (Digital FTEs) manufacture karta hai aur
unhe company ke AI-Native Company mein assemble karta hai.

**Consume karta hai:** us company ki human-agent teams. Do roles yeh kaam karte hain, aur yeh same role
nahi hain. **Outcome Architect** intent ka malik hai: business problem, redesigned workflow, target
outcome, adoption. **FDE** implementation ka malik hai: integrations, ontology, tools, evaluations,
production operation. Chhote engagement mein ek hi capable insaan dono play karta hai; bare ya regulated
mein woh ek pair ki tarah kaam karte hain.

> customer ke exist karne se pehle. Uski aunty ka profession ek Chicago mid-size firm ka partner sponsor
> deta hai, remotely Lahore se serve kiya hua. Baseline usi conversation mein aata hai: 4 ghante ek file
> ke liye. Target: 40 minute. Domain builder se woh ek working-paper Digital FTE manufacture karti hai,
> firm ke reviewers uska output supervise karte hain, aur measured result target prove karta hai.*
