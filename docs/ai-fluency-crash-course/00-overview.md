# 00 — Overview: AI Access Nahi, AI Fluency Chahiye

## Kahani: Talented Colleague

Socho ek talented naya colleague team join karti hai. Pehli subah tum kehte ho: "Prepare a course
outline on AI agents." Kuch ghanton baad polished outline aati hai — par PhD researchers ke liye
likhi hui, full semester assume karti hai, hands-on practice ke bina. Wo incapable nahi thi — tumne
usay audience, time, teaching style, aur end-goal kabhi bataya nahi.

AI ke sath bhi yehi hota hai, ek farq ke sath: **AI kuch bhi carry nahi karta ek chat se doosri
mein.** Insaan ko ek baar bata do, wo mahine baad bhi yaad rakhega. Har AI chat mein ek colleague
milta hai jo tumse pehle kabhi nahi mila — jo ek insaan ko ek baar batate ho, wo har baar dohrao ya
wahan rakho jahan AI khud parh le. Memory feature notes save kar ke agli chat mein bhejta hai, par
model khud har baar khali shuru hota hai.

Complete brief bhi correct answer guarantee nahi karti — AI fact ya source invent kar sakta hai aur
phir bhi confident lage ga. Behtar instructions odds improve karti hain, output ko true nahi banati
— checking isliye ek alag skill hai (Discernment, aage).

Yehi hai **AI fluency**: AI ko kya dena hai, kaise guide karna hai, uske kaam ko kaise judge karna
hai, aur kab use nahi karna — in sab ka maloom hona.

## AI Fluency Framework — 4Ds

Professor Rick Dakan aur Professor Joseph Feller (Anthropic ke sath) ne banaya — 4 human
competencies:

1. **Delegation** — decide karo AI kya kare
2. **Description** — explain karo kya chahiye
3. **Discernment** — judge karo AI ne kya diya
4. **Diligence** — responsibly use karo, outcome own karo

Yeh skills chat mein, coding agent mein, aur **Digital FTE** (full-time equivalent — AI worker jo
doosron ke liye defined job karta hai) mein sab jagah chalte hain.

## See It in Three Minutes

Pehla prompt: `"Write a welcome email for new members."` — competent, grammatical, mukammal
general, kyunke tumne kuch nahi bataya.

Dusra prompt: audience (Karachi mein nervous beginner cyclists), tone (warm, thoda funny), length
(<150 words), specific ending (Saturday 6am ride slow hai, koi dropped nahi hota) — same model,
same 3 seconds of machine-work, **bohot behtar result**.

Do cheezein hui:

1. Dono results ka gap tumse aaya, model se nahi.
2. Tum doosri email ko behtar sirf tab bol sakte ho jab tumhe cycling clubs/Karachi/nervous
   beginners ke baare mein kaafi pata ho.

Pehli baat **Description** hai. Doosri **Discernment** hai.

## Course Ka Naqsha — 8 Ideas, Ek Line Mein

1. Sab ke paas same AI hai — fluency yeh hai ke uske sath kya karte ho (effective/efficient/
   ethical/safe).
2. AI ke sath kaam karne ke 3 tareeqe: automation, augmentation, agency.
3. Delegation decide karta hai kaunsa kaam tumhara hai, kaunsa AI ka — typing se pehle.
4. Description AI ko wo deta hai jo chahiye — product, process, performance, teeno kisam.
5. Discernment judge karta hai jo wapas aaya — confident answer bhi wrong ho sakta hai.
6. Diligence responsibly AI use karta hai — creation, transparency, deployment.
7. Chaar skills ek loop ki tarah chalte hain, aur engineering mein scale hote hain.
8. Chaar beginner mistakes common hain, har ek alag skill miss karta hai.
