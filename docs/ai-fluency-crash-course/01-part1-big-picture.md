# 01 — Part 1: Start With the Big Picture (Concepts 1-2)

## Concept 1 — AI Access AI Fluency Nahi Hai

Do log same assistant, same plan, same subah open karte hain. Ek shippable kaam nikalta hai,
doosra polished result phenk deta hai. Tool same tha — **unhon ne usse jo kiya, farq daala.**

**AI fluency** matlab AI ke sath is tarah kaam karna jo:

- **Effective** — goal reach hota hai
- **Efficient** — time/effort/**tokens** waste nahi hote (tokens = chhote text pieces jo AI
  parhta/likhta hai, jinka tum payment karte ho)
- **Ethical** — AI fairly aur openly use hota hai
- **Safe** — logon, privacy, security, important info ka protection

Koi model training, model-building knowledge, ya "magic prompts" collect karna zaroori nahi.
Foundation hai **good human decisions around AI** — isiliye framework 4Ds par focus karta hai: ek
word mein, **Delegation = decide, Description = explain, Discernment = check, Diligence = own.**

> **Mode 1** (problem-solving) mein general agents use karte ho. **Mode 2** (manufacturing) mein
> Digital FTEs doosron ke liye banate ho. Agar ek AI assistant ke sath achha kaam nahi kar sakte,
> to hazaron users ke liye system design karne ke liye ready nahi ho.

### 3 Facts Pichle Course Se (What AI Actually Is)

1. **Sounding right ≠ being correct.** Confident answer galat ho sakta — **hallucination**
   invented info par bana confident answer hai.
2. **Same answer guaranteed nahi.** Same request alag answer de sakta next time.
3. **AI sirf available info se kaam karta hai** — training, conversation, documents, memory,
   search, tools. Agar zaroori info missing hai, AI guess karega.

AI output ko ek capable colleague ke kaam jaisa treat karo — useful, phir bhi review-worthy.
Colleague batata hai jab unsure ho; AI same confident tone rakhta hai dono surat mein.

## Concept 2 — AI Ke Sath Kaam Karne Ke 3 Tareeqe

1. **Automation** — AI specify kiya task perform karta hai
2. **Augmentation** — tum aur AI milkar kaam karte ho
3. **Agency** — AI tumhari taraf se goal ki taraf kaam karta hai

Modes alag hote hain is baat mein ke AI ko next decide karne ki kitni freedom hai.

### Automation — "Do This Task"

Exact task bolte ho: "Summarize this report in five bullets," "Translate into Urdu," "Extract
invoice number/date/total." Tum script writer ho. Clear + repeatable kaam ke liye fit.

### Augmentation — "Help Me Think"

Brainstorming, architecture review, lesson plan improve karna, strategies compare karna — AI
instructions follow nahi karta, **thinking partner** ki tarah act karta hai. Multiple turns: ask →
respond → challenge → revise → improve.

### Agency — "Pursue This Goal for Me"

Goal + boundaries dete ho, AI steps decide karta hai. "Read these five emails aur summarize" se
"Keep my inbox manageable — routine replies, important flag, jo unsure ho mujhse pucho" tak move
karte ho. Tum **director** ho — except director har take dekhta hai, tum aksar nahi dekhoge.

Framework definition: insaan AI ko configure karta hai ke wo **future** tasks independently kare,
**including for others**, unki taraf se.

- **Future** — tum room mein nahi ho; Monday setup karte ho, Thursday AI handle karta hai jab tum
  so rahe ho.
- **For others** — jisko AI serve karta hai wo tum nahi ho; customer/student/colleague AI se baat
  karta hai.

Automation + augmentation tumhein chair mein rakhte hain. **Agency wahan hai jahan tum chair se
uthte ho** — isiliye Mode 2 hard hai: har decision supervise nahi kar sakte, judgment pehle se
built-in honi chahiye.

| | Automation | Agency |
|---|---|---|
| Tum provide karte ho | Task ya steps | Goal aur boundaries |
| AI decide karta hai | Bohot kam | Many next steps |
| Tumhara role | Script writer | Director |
| Common failure | Step badly hota hai | Goal/boundary misunderstood |

Koi mode automatically behtar nahi — ek project teeno use kar sakta hai: data extraction automate
karo, exceptions augmentation se sochho, routine cases par agent ko limited authority do.

**Agent Factory terms:** Mode 1 automation+augmentation use karta hai. Mode 2 agency ko systematic
banata hai. Digital FTE sirf "AI doing things" nahi — job definition, permissions, rules,
governance ke andar AI act karta hai, apne **System of Record** (business ke official data+rules
ka trusted store) se kaam karta hai.
