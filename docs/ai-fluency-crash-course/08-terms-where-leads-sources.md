# 08 — Terms, Where This Leads, Sources

## Terms This Course Adds

(Machine ki vocabulary **What AI Actually Is** + book ke **Glossary** mein hai — yeh sirf is
course ke naye terms hain.)

- **AI fluency** — AI ke sath effectively, efficiently, ethically, safely kaam karna
- **The 4Ds** — Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence
- **Automation** — AI specify kiya task perform karta hai
- **Augmentation** — human aur AI thinking partners ki tarah kaam karte hain
- **Agency** — AI goal ki taraf kaam karta hai, many steps khud choose karta hai
- **Delegation** — decide karna AI kya kare, humans kya rakhein
- **Problem awareness** — goal, kaam, risks, aur success ka matlab jaanna
- **Platform awareness** — kaunsa AI system/tool task ke liye fit hai, jaanna
- **Task delegation** — har part human ya AI ko assign karna
- **Description** — AI ko info aur guidance dena jo kaam ke liye chahiye
- **Product description** — jo output chahiye, define karna
- **Process description** — AI kaam kaise approach kare, define karna
- **Performance description** — AI apne aap kaise behave kare, uske users ke liye, define karna
- **Discernment** — judge karna AI ka output/justification/behavior kaafi achha hai ya nahi
- **Product discernment** — result khud judge karna
- **Process discernment** — judge karna kya tumhara AI ke sath kaam karne ka tareeqa pay off kar
  raha
- **Performance discernment** — judge karna AI ka independent behavior users ko serve karta hai ya
  nahi
- **Diligence** — AI kaise use hua aur uske output ki responsibility lena
- **Creation diligence** — pehle/during tools aur data responsibly choose karna
- **Transparency diligence** — jab affect kare, AI ke role ke baare mein honest hona
- **Deployment diligence** — use/send hone se pehle AI-assisted work check karna
- **Context engineering** — AI ko chahiye saari info design karna: instructions, documents, tools,
  memory, policies
- **Automation bias** — insaan ka automated output ko bohot asaani se trust karna
- **Hallucination** — confident AI output jo sound right karta par invented/incorrect hai
- **Redaction** — AI ko data dene se pehle identifying details hatana, task ka pattern rakh kar
- **Digital FTE** — AI worker jo doosron ke liye defined job karta hai (FTE = full-time equivalent)
- **Tokens** — chhote text pieces jo AI parhta/likhta hai, jinka payment hota hai
- **System of Record** — business ka official data+rules ka trusted store
- **System prompt** — standing instruction jo AI har chat ki shuruat mein parhta hai
- **Eval suites** — repeatable tests jo AI system ka output score karte hain

## Where This Leads

Aage: **AI Prompting in 2026** — Description ko practical daily habits mein badalta hai. **How to
Think in the AI Era** — Discernment aur critical thinking strengthen karta hai.

Build path mein aage:

- **Spec-Driven Development** — Delegation+Description ko engineering method banata hai
- **Trusting the Checker** + **Eval-Driven Development** — Discernment ko evaluation
  infrastructure banata hai
- **System of Record** + **Designing the Vertical SoR** — Diligence+governance ko architecture
  banata hai

Exams ke liye dekho: **Certifications** page.
