# 02 — Objections 4-6: Geopolitics, Reality, Existential Risk

## 4. Geopolitical Arms Race: Sovereignty Banao, Dependence Nahi

Ek dahai ke andar, mumalik teen groups mein bat sakte hain: (1) woh jo AI intelligence export karte
hain, (2) partners jo apni khud ki AI capacity rakhte hain, aur (3) dependent states jo critical
kaam ke liye foreign systems par depend karte hain.

**Isi liye peeche hatna khatarnak hai.** Agar free societies ruk jaayen jabke kam accountable actors
chalte rahein, to frontier gayab nahi hota — yeh un groups ki taraf move ho jata hai jinke paas kam
safety commitments aur kam public oversight hai.

Yeh concern sirf superpowers tak mehdood nahi. Global South ke mumalik — Pakistan, Brazil, Nigeria
— ek basic choice ka saamna kar rahe hain: domestic capability banao ya kisi aur ki intelligence ke
permanent consumer ban jao. **Sovereign AI** ka matlab hai woh models aur systems jo local languages
samjhte hain, local industries serve karte hain, aur local rules ke tehat operate karte hain.

Open-source foundations isay zyada achievable banate hain. Lahore ya Lagos ki koi university ab ek
frontier-class model apni local zaroorat ke liye adapt kar sakti hai. **Asal race sirf AI banane
walon aur na banane walon ke darmiyan nahi hai. Yeh un mumalik ke darmiyan hai jo talent aur strong
systems banate hain, aur un ke darmiyan jo dono ko drain hone dete hain.**

## 5. Erosion of Reality: AI Se AI Verify Karo

Shared reality kho jaana ek genuine khatra hai. Lekin isay ek **verification problem** ki tarah
samajhna behtar hai. Printing press ne bhi pamphlets, propaganda, aur conspiracy tracts se
misinformation phailayi. Society ne journalism, peer review, scientific method, aur libel law bana
kar jawab diya.

AI-generated media ko usi institutional response ka **tezz version** chahiye hoga. AI is masle ka
hissa hai, lekin solution ka bhi hissa hai. Woh systems jo synthetic media generate karte hain,
usay detect karne mein bhi madad kar sakte hain. AI manipulated images pehchan sakta hai, suspicious
financial documents flag kar sakta hai, aur fraud ko us scale par detect kar sakta hai jo koi human
team match nahi kar sakti.

Trustworthy AI ko wahi basic controls chahiye jo koi bhi sound system maangta hai: clear
specifications, release se pehle checks, evidence jise trace kiya ja sake, aur high stakes par human
judgment. **Unreliable AI ka jawab kam design nahi — behtar design hai, jahan insaan supervise karte
hain, sirf operate nahi.**

## 6. Existential Risk: Recklessness Nahi, Safety Ko Accelerate Karo

Yeh objection sabse zyada caution deserve karti hai. Alignment problem real aur unsolved hai. Hum
abhi guarantee nahi kar sakte ke increasingly capable systems insaani flourishing ke sath compatible
goals hi pursue karengi.

Ek reckless builder is concern ko khaarij karta hai. **Ek serious builder isay central design
problem ki tarah treat karta hai.** Global pause enforce karna mushkil hoga — AI development
governments, companies, universities, open-source communities, aur independent researchers mein
distributed hai. Safety-conscious groups ka pause frontier ko nahi rokega — yeh usay kam
transparency aur kam safeguards wale actors ki taraf move kar sakta hai.

Behtar jawab hai sahi tarah ki acceleration: alignment, model understanding, evaluation, aur control
par zyada kaam, stronger models ke sath sath. Anthropic, DeepMind, aur university researchers is par
kaam kar rahe hain — model behavior study karna, insaani values ko clearer tareeqe se state karna,
aur stronger systems ko control mein rakhne ke methods.

Yeh kaam abhi early hai. Kaafi nahi hai. Lekin yeh sirf woh log kar sakte hain jo frontier systems ko
andar se samajhte hain. Insaniyat ne doosre khatarnak tools bhi manage kiye hain — nuclear systems,
engineered pathogens, industrial systems jo climate ko affect karte hain. Record perfect nahi, lekin
pattern clear hai: **governance ke liye expertise chahiye. Jo societies ek khatarnak tool se peeche
hat jaati hain, woh usay govern karne ka gyan bhi kho deti hain.**

Existential risk AI ignore karne ki wajah nahi. Yeh iski wajah hai ke sabse capable builders bhi
safety, transparency, aur public accountability se deeply committed hon.
