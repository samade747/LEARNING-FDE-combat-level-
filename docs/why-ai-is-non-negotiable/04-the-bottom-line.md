# 04 — The Bottom Line

Fears legitimate hain. Har ek serious engagement deserve karti hai. Lekin har fear bhi ek argument
hai AI ko **behtar** banane ka, kisi aur ke haath field chhorne ka nahi.

Yeh book apne framework ko **Agent Factory** kehti hai. Yeh ek spec-driven aur human-supervised
process hai. Specifications intent define karti hain. Verification loops errors pakadti hain. Insaan
high-stakes decisions par authority rakhte hain. Economic model opacity ki bajaye outcomes ko
reward karta hai.

**Hum safety aur progress ke darmiyan choose nahi kar rahe. Hum choose kar rahe hain ke AI ko
shape karte huay hum khud shape denge, ya kisi aur ko shape dene denge.**

Karachi ka shopkeeper ek useful tool chahta hai. Rural Mexico ka student ek tutor chahta hai. Ek
village ka patient jahan koi doctor nahi, usay care ki access chahiye. Unhein AI ki existence par
abstract debate nahi chahiye. Unhein aise systems chahiye jo unke liye kaam karein.

**AI non-negotiable hai. Hum isay kaise banayein — yehi faisla baaqi hai.**
