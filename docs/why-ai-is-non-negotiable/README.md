## Yeh Chapter Kis Baare Mein Hai

Insaan sirf biology se nahi badalte — hum un tools se bhi badalte hain jo hum banate hain. Fire,
agriculture, printing press, steam engine, computer — inme se koi bhi optional nahi raha. Jo
societies inhein master karti hain, unhein power aur prosperity milti hai; jo resist karti hain,
unhein woh log absorb kar lete hain jinhone nahi kiya. Historian Yuval Noah Harari ke mutabiq, AI
usi wheel ka agla mor hai — shayad sabse important — kyunke pehle wale tools ne jism ya routine
calculation ko augment kiya, lekin AI **cognition khud** ko augment karta hai.

Yeh chapter AI ke khilaf **nau serious objections** leta hai — mass unemployment se le kar existential
risk aur wealth concentration tak — aur har ek ko honest jawab deta hai: **koi bhi fear rukne ki
wajah nahi, balke behtar banane ki wajah hai.** Harari ka central idea yahan chalta hai: masla
manzil nahi, **raasta** hai. Industrial Revolution ne aakhir prosperity di, lekin raasta brutal tha —
AI usi pattern ko dobara chala sakta hai, jab tak hum jaan-boojh kar behtar raasta na banayein.
