# 03 — Objections 7-9: Environment, Bias, Wealth Concentration

## 7. Environmental Cost: Energy System Fix Karo

AI kaafi energy aur water use karta hai. Is cost ko minimize nahi karna chahiye. Goldman Sachs ka
estimate hai ke data-center electricity demand 2030 tak **160%** barh sakti hai — ek serious
technical aur policy challenge.

Lekin context bhi matter karta hai. Global data-center industry — AI, cloud computing, streaming,
e-commerce samet — abhi global electricity ka taqreeban **1-2%** use karti hai. Residential air
conditioning akela sab data centers se zyada electricity use karta hai. Fashion industry bhi global
carbon emissions ka significant share paida karti hai. Hum clothing, cooling, ya digital services ban
nahi karte — hum yeh behtar banate hain ke woh kaise produce hoti hain.

AI industry pehle se renewable energy aur next-generation nuclear power mein invest kar rahi hai.
Mixture-of-experts, distillation, aur quantization jaisi model techniques ek given performance level
ke liye zaroorat computation kam karti hain. Naya hardware bhi per-watt zyada computation deta hai.

AI environmental damage kam karne mein bhi madad kar sakta hai. DeepMind ke cooling system ne Google
ke data-center cooling energy ko **40%** kam kiya. AI power-grid management, precision agriculture,
climate modeling, battery research, solar materials, aur carbon-capture optimization ko support
karta hai.

**Sahi sawal yeh nahi ke AI energy use karta hai ya nahi — har human system energy use karta hai.
Sawal hai kya hum benefits se cost justify kar sakte hain, aur AI ke peeche wale power systems ko
clean energy ki taraf move kar sakte hain.** AI ko pause karna energy crisis solve nahi karta.
Cleaner power par AI banana dono problems mein madad kar sakta hai.

## 8. Bias and Discrimination: Bias Ko Visible Aur Auditable Banao

AI systems ne apne training data mein maujood harmful patterns dohraye hain. Amazon ne apna internal
hiring tool band kar diya jab pata chala ke woh women ke résumés ko downgrade kar raha tha. Ek
widely-used healthcare algorithm ne Black patients ko kam resources diye kyunke woh healthcare
spending ko medical need ka proxy treat kar raha tha — woh spending pehle se unequal access to care
ko reflect kar rahi thi.

Yeh structural failures hain aur structural responses maangte hain. Important point yeh hai ke
underlying bias AI se shuru nahi hua. Human hiring, lending, aur medical systems pehle se biased
thay — sirf observe, repeat, ya audit karna mushkil tha. **Ek AI decision log aur measure ho sakta
hai. Isse correction mumkin hota hai — kabhi automatic nahi, lekin mumkin.**

High-risk system ko demographic groups ke aar-paar test hona chahiye. Uska training data document
hona chahiye. Uske decisions independent review ke liye khule hone chahiye. Regulators ko fairness
aur performance ka evidence maangne ka haq hona chahiye. EU ka AI Act, Algorithmic Justice League,
aur NIST AI Risk Management Framework sab isi accountability ki taraf ishara karte hain.

Bina check kiye chhora, AI kisi bhi human institution se tez discrimination scale kar sakta hai.
Jawab yeh sochna nahi ke problem khud hal ho jayegi — jawab hai audits, impact assessments,
transparent documentation, aur correction cycles maangna. **Goal ek aisa AI system nahi jo insan
jitna hi biased ho. Goal hai ek system jo measurably kam biased ho aur har audit ke sath behtar
ho.**

## 9. Wealth Concentration: Yeh Democratize Karo Ke Kaun Bana Sakta Hai

Concentration problem real hai. Frontier training runs arabon dollars lete hain. Labs ko tens of
thousands high-end GPUs chahiye, har ek $25,000-$40,000. Chahiye systems banane ke liye das billion
dollars.

Yeh chand companies ko zabardast power deta hai. February 2026 mein, Anthropic ka $380 billion
valuation India ki paanch sab se bari IT-services companies ke combined market cap se zyada tha —
woh companies jo chaar dahaion mein bani thin aur millions logon ko employ karti hain.

**Default raasta isliye abundance paida kar sakta hai jabke zyadatar value California aur Beijing
mein concentrate ho jaye.**

Dario Amodei (Anthropic ke CEO) ne khabardaar kiya hai ke AI trillionaires bana sakta hai aur severe
public backlash paida kar sakta hai agar gains sirf top par concentrate rahen. Unhone AI ko ek
civilizational challenge ki tarah treat karne ki baat ki hai, sirf business opportunity nahi — aur
naye tax rules ki call di jo vast wealth creation ke daur ke liye fit hon.

Jawab poori progress cap karna nahi. Jawab hai **kaun bana sakta hai** aur **kise benefit milta hai**
dono ko democratize karna — jisme open-weight models, accessible tools, public AI literacy,
sovereign compute, progressive policy, aur useful AI systems ka wider ownership shamil hai.

Lahore ya Lagos ki koi university pehle se strong open models ko local zaroorat ke liye adapt kar
sakti hai. EU, India, aur Gulf states mein sovereign AI programs kuch foreign providers par
dependence kam karne ki koshish kar rahe hain. **Purani tech revolutions ne aakhir apne gains wide
phailaye. AI automatically aisa nahi karega kyunke entry ki cost unusually high hai. Isay design se
democratic banana hoga.**
