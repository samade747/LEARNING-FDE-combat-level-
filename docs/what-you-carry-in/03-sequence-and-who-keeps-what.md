# 03 — "Build First, Sell Second", aur Kaun Kya Rakhta Hai

## Argument Aapko Kya Karne Par Majboor Karta Hai

Ek ownership argument ka sequence par ek nateeja hota hai, aur yehi hissa practitioners resist karte hain.

Agar asset hi wahid cheez hai jo aapko alag banati hai, to yeh kisi customer ke fund karne ka intezar nahi kar sakta. **Build first, sell second.** Pehli slice ek aisa step nahi jo buyer ka intezar kare. Yeh woh step hai jo buyer **paida** karta hai.

Yeh book teen jagah pehle se yeh rule bina naam liye implied karti hai. Portfolio credential hai. Résumé un systems par judge hota hai jo aap ne asal mein ship kiye. Aur woh sponsor jo starting number discuss nahi karega, sponsor nahi hai. Sath mein yeh ek cheez kehte hain: koi bhi apna baseline ek ajnabi ko nahi batata jise kuch nahi dikhaya gaya.

Slice sirf credible hone se zyada karti hai — yeh **aapki jagah argument banati hai**. Ek mid-size firm mein chalo, ek governed page us firm ki apni profession par le kar, aur buyer pitch nahi sun raha. Woh apna kaam parh raha hai, apni firm se behtar likha hua, aur agla sawal uski taraf se aata hai. Kisi ko jaldi karne ki wajah invent nahi karni padi.

**Launch gates ke beech yeh kahan baithta hai.** [Char validation gates](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ecosystem/choosing-your-vertical#step-5-validate-against-four-gates-in-60-to-90-days) ek fixed order mein chalte hain, kisi bhi order mein nahi. Gate 1 hai ke aapke expert ne sign kar diya. Gate 2 hai ke rights basis likhit mein hai. Dono slice se **pehle** aate hain, kyunki slice dono par depend karti hai. Aapka expert iske procedures likhta hai aur apni files aapke liye kholta hai. Rights basis decide karta hai kaunse sources legally andar baith sakte hain. Phir slice. Phir gate 3, ek named sponsor jiske sath contract of success draft ho chuka. Contract of success teen numbers par likhit agreement hai: kaam aaj kahan khara hai, success kya hoga, aur reviewer kaise check karega. Phir gate 4, real suppliers se real prices, guesses nahi.

**Pehla sponsor kahan se aata hai.** Kisi cold marketplace se nahi. Aapka expert vertical ka pehla sales channel bhi hai, knowledge ka source bhi. 20 saal ki practice, 20 saal ke professional relationships bhi hai. Woh firm jo pehli meeting ke liye razi hoti hai, aksar wohi hoti hai jo pehle se usko trust karti hai.

**Buyer kya supply karta hai — number.** Baseline contact se pehle exist nahi kar sakta, kyunki yeh client ke apne workflow mein measure hota hai — aur yehi wahid measurement hai jo buyer verify kar sakta hai. Aapki slice banane wali digging aapke expert ke material par chali. Baseline aapke customer ke material par chalta hai. Do sources, do moments, aur koi contradiction nahi.

To sequence ek line mein: **expert, slice, sponsor, baseline, contract, engagement, thick.** Paise se jurey har step se pehle ek step aata hai, aur woh slice hai.

:::info Ek Rule Har Ek Ke Liye
Build first, sell second sirf **vertical ladder** ko govern karta hai. Service ladder ek client se shuru hoti hai. Vertical ladder ek slice se shuru hoti hai.
:::

## Kaun Kya Rakhta Hai

Ownership argument ko boundaries ke baare mein exact hona chahiye — un boundaries sameet jo aapke favour mein nahi. Yahan honest division hai.

**Aap kya own karte ho.** Vertical System of Record, jo domain startup hold karti hai jo aap aur expert ne banai — aap personally nahi. Uska judgment aur likha material isme licensed hota hai aur uska hi rehta hai. Third-party sources apne terms ke tehat andar baithti hain. Aap sath mein assembly, governance, aur derived procedures own karte ho — ek real asset, poore corpus se narrower.

**Jo aap hold karte ho lekin own nahi karte.** Shared System of Record, Agent Factory wali. Yeh ecosystem ki hai. Aapko jo protect karta hai woh ownership nahi, **openness** hai: plain versioned Markdown, standard Postgres with pgvector, aur open MCP protocol — to jo aap iske andar banate ho, woh kisi ek platform ke format mein trapped nahi.

**Client kya rakhta hai.** Production mein ek chalta hua system, unke chune stack par. Unka apna instance, unka data, unke thresholds, unki confidential ontology. Model, runtime, aur cloud switch karne ki azadi. Aur aapko outright hire karne ka option.

**Client kya nahi rakhta.** Vertical System of Record. Ek tempting sentence hai jo isay de deta hai, aur usay yahan naam lena zaroori hai taake koi na kahe: *"Main method aur aapki profession lekar aaya, aur jab main jaunga, doosri wali aap rakh lena."* Yeh generous lagta hai. Yeh **jhoot** hai, aur ownership se bhi sharper wajah se. Woh System of Record aapke expert ke licensed material se, aur third-party sources se, unke apne terms par bani hai. Ek standard ko apne system ke andar serve karne ka license, client ka license nahi ban jata sirf isliye ke aap ne offer kar diya. Corpus haath mein dena us agreements ki khilaf-warzi hoti jispar yeh khadi hai — expert ko us business se alag karne ki baat to alag hi hai jiski woh aadhi maalik hai.

**Employer kya rakhta hai.** Ek case jo upar wale chaar jawab cover nahi karte, aur yeh common case hai. Employed rehte hue ek governed profession banao, apne employer ke waqt par, unke expert ke sath — to yeh **employer** ki hoti hai. Aap ki nahi. Yeh unfair nahi, yehi employment hai, aur isi liye yeh argument un practitioners ke liye hai jo clients ko serve karte hain, ek employer ko nahi. Yehi sabse sharp wajah bhi hai ke service ladder ek **kamane** ki jagah hai, accumulate karne ki nahi.

Discipline hamesha portable hoti hai. Asset ka ek owner hota hai. Dono baaton ka kehna zaroori hai, warna argument woh promise kar deta hai jo yeh nibha nahi sakta.

Un jawabon ko caveats ki tarah nahi, ek **ledger** ki tarah parho, kyunki yehi hai jo buyer asal mein khareed raha hai. Ek vendor ke engagement mein leverage vendor ke column mein accumulate hoti hai aur client ka thin rehta hai. Yahan columns jaan-boojh kar aise banaye gaye hain ke koi bhi sab kuch na rakhe. Isi liye yeh khada rehta hai jab buyer isay test karta hai.
