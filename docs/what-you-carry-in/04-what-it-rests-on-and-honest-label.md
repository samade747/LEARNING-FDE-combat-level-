# 04 — Argument Kis Par Khara Hai, Honest Label, aur Aage Kya

## Argument Kis Par Khara Hai

Load-bearing claims, isi order mein jismein yeh page unhein establish karti hai. Koi bhi ek nikaalo, baaki khare rehna band kar dete hain. Yeh upar wali "five refusals" nahi hain, aur inke sath map bhi nahi hoti.

**Asset par.** Un assets mein se jinpar agent act kar sakta hai, sirf ek governed profession ownership test ke dono halves pass karti hai. Yeh us expert ke sath jointly hold hoti hai jisne judgment likha. Building ki unit ek vertical, ek jurisdiction hai — to doosre mulk ko serve karna ek naya build hai, pehle ka extension nahi. Aur ek committed author ke bina, banane ya sath le jaane ko kuch nahi hota.

**Sequence par.** Vertical ladder par, build first, sell second. Slice aapke expert ki files par bani hoti hai, aur koi bhi dekhne se pehle awkward cases pass karti hai — kyunki ek unfinished outcome thin nahi hota. Service ladder ek client se shuru hoti hai.

**Numbers par.** Baseline client ke apne workflow se aata hai, kyunki yehi wahid number hai jo buyer verify kar sakta hai.

**Boundaries par.** Do Systems of Record aapke sath travel karte hain, method aur profession. Teesra nahi karta: client ka apna instance, unka data aur thresholds hold karta hua, wahi rehta hai. [Promotion](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ecosystem/fde-af-model#the-one-law-of-the-model-repeated-work-moves-down) inke beech wahid bridge hai. Aur client vertical System of Record nahi rakhta, kyunki aapki domain startup usay hold karti hai aur woh licences par khadi hai jo client ki nahi.

## Honest Label

Is page par ek claim projection hai, aur usay waisa label kiya gaya hai — jaisay yeh book har udhar li hui projection ko label karti hai. Isay upar wali claim se alag rakhna zaroori hai, kyunki dono opposite direction mein point karti hain aur sirf ek uncertain hai. Ke slice **hi** aapko customer degi — yeh projection hai. Ke slice ke bagair aap sponsor conversation tak **nahi** pohanch sakte — yeh safer claim hai, kyunki yeh ek check-able baat se follow karti hai: buyers apna baseline un ajnabiyon ko nahi batate jinhein kuch dikhaya nahi gaya. Seedha: **negative claim zyada safe hai.** Hum is baat mein zyada confident hain ke aapko asset chahiye, is baat mein utna nahi ke asset akele aapko kaam dila degi.

Ke asset wahid durable hai — yeh upar wali elimination se follow karta hai, aur rad karna reasoning hai, measuring nahi. Jo measured hai woh role ki demand hai. Abhi koi verified count nahi ke kitne practitioners ne governed corpus ko pehle customer mein convert kiya, kyunki category nayi hai aur abhi tak lagbhag koi is se guzra nahi. Reasoning sound hai aur alternatives kamzor hain, lekin yeh abhi proof nahi. Ek reader jo achi slice banata hai aur meeting ke liye teen mahine intezar karta hai, usne argument ko disprove nahi kiya.

**Kya cheez isay badal degi.** Teen observations argument ko revise karne par majboor kar dengi, aur har ek dekhne layak hai.

Agar governed slice le jaane wale practitioners deployed Workers le jaane walon se zyada aasani se kaam nahi jeetein, to slice ek portfolio item hai, door-opener nahi. Ownership wala hissa bachta hai, kyunki asset ab bhi wahid durable hoga, lekin commercial hissa nahi bachega.

Agar ek practice chaar professions ko is kaam ki shape ke around bane asset se serve karke real scale par pohanch jaye, to chhata refusal galat hai, aur profession sahi unit nahi.

Agar ek general model + ek public corpus buyers ko apne jurisdictional sawal khud jawab dene de, bina kisi governed source ke, to surviving asset survive karna band kar deti hai, aur is page ko elimination se dobara likhna hoga.

In teen mein se koi bhi nahi hua. Yeh kehna ke argument ko kya tod sakta hai, argument banane ka hissa hai.

## Aage Kahan Jayein

Yeh page statement hai. Panch pages iske upar act karte hain.

[The Roles This Book Trains](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/roles-this-book-trains) argument ko career par lagata hai. [The FDE AF Model](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ecosystem/fde-af-model) woh five-layer blueprint hai jismein yeh pairing baithti hai, aur kaam ko price karta hai. [Choosing Your Vertical](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ecosystem/choosing-your-vertical) batata hai profession kaise choose karein. [Designing the Vertical System of Record](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ecosystem/designing-the-vertical-sor) batata hai asset kaise banayein. Aur [How to Get Paid in the Agentic AI Era](../how-to-get-paid/README.md) in chaaron ko walking order mein rakhta hai, phir batata hai teen doors mein se har ek asal mein kya pay karta hai.

Aur agar aaj aapka honest jawab hai ke aapke paas koi committed expert nahi, to yeh argument abhi aapke liye nahi hai. Service ladder par shuru karo, kamao, aur engagements ko expert le kar aane do.
