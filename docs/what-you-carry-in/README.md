## Yeh Chapter Kis Baare Mein Hai

Book **teen bare arguments** karti hai. [Thesis](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/thesis) **architectural** argument hai — batati hai ke company is era mein kya "own" karegi (AI Workers ka workforce, software licences ka catalogue nahi). [The Operating Layer](../the-agent-is-the-operating-layer/README.md) **interface** argument hai — batata hai ke agent chalane par software kya hona chhor deta hai. Yeh page **teesra** sawal poochta hai, aur yeh company ya product ke baare mein nahi — **aap khud** ke baare mein hai.

**Is era mein model rented hai, runtime badalta rehta hai, aur method ek kitab mein muft diya ja raha hai. To ek insaan asal mein kya "own" kar sakta hai?**

Yeh page ek **elimination test** chalata hai: har mumkin jawab ko baari baari rad karta hai (model, runtime, tumhare hours, vendor ka platform, khud yeh method), aur akhir mein sirf **ek** cheez bachti hai — ek profession ka governed knowledge, ek jurisdiction mein, jo aap ek real expert ke sath mil kar banate ho. Yeh sawal har practitioner ka hai. Jawab sabse zyada us insaan ko fit karta hai jo apna kaam kisi doosri company ke andar le kar jata hai — yani vendor-neutral vertical FDE.

Chapter ka scope: **five refusals**, ek surviving asset, uski commercial value, "two Systems of Record" ka pattern, coverage ke measurement (thin/thick), narrowing ka argument, sequence rule ("build first, sell second"), ownership ki boundaries (kaun kya rakhta hai), aur honest labelling (kya measured hai, kya sirf reasoning hai).

## Sabse Zaroori Baat

> **Model rent hota hai. Runtime badalta hai. Method sab ko diya jata hai. Sirf ek cheez bachti hai: ek profession ka apna, governed, written-down knowledge — jo aap akele nahi, apne expert ke sath mil kar hold karte ho.**
> Yehi cheez hai jo koi rival kharid nahi sakta aur koi vendor wapis le nahi sakta. Baaki sab kuch — reputation sameet — ya to kiraye par hai, ya sab ke paas hai.
