# 00 — Yeh Page Kiske Liye Hai, aur Paanch Cheezein Jo Aap Own Nahi Kar Sakte

## Words Yeh Page Use Karta Hai

Shuru mein hi kuch vocabulary seedh lo, baaki page aasan ho jayega:

| Lafz | Seedha Matlab |
| --- | --- |
| **Asset** | Woh cheez jo aap saal ke aakhir mein bhi hold karte ho. Skill jaisi nahi hoti |
| **Vendor-neutral** | Clients ko serve karna, koi bhi vendor ka platform sath liye bagair |
| **Refusal** | Un cheezon mein se ek jo yeh page rad karta hai — paanch candidates, paanch refusals |
| **Vertical** | Ek profession, ek mulk mein. "Pakistan mein tax accounting" ek vertical hai. "Accounting" nahi |
| **Jurisdiction** | Ek mulk ya region, apne rules aur apne regulator ke sath |
| **System of Record** | Woh ek governed source jo aapke agents parhte hain — logon ko website ki tarah, agents ko MCP se serve hoti hai |
| **Corpus** | System of Record ke andar source documents ka collection jise agents cite karte hain |
| **Slice** | Ek professional outcome, poori tarah cover hua, System of Record ke andar |
| **Thin** | Ek System of Record jo sirf ek outcome cover kare |
| **Thick** | Ek System of Record jo bohat sare outcomes cover kare, har ek complete |
| **Sponsor** | Ek possible pehle customer ke andar woh naamzad insaan jo starting number par razi ho sake |
| **Baseline** | Client ka kaam aaj kahan khara hai, unke apne workflow mein measure kiya hua |
| **Promotion** | Repeated kaam neeche move hona. Ek customer ke liye banayi cheez jo kai passes mein dohrai jaye, review paas kare, aur neeche wali layer mein chali jaye, taake har baad wale client ko mil jaye |

## Yeh Page Kiske Liye Hai

Sawal sab ka hai. Is era mein har practitioner ko poochna chahiye ke saal ke aakhir mein woh kya hold kar raha hoga. Jawab narrower hai, aur is mein ek lafz zyada tar kaam karta hai: **vendor-neutral**. Yeh sabse zyada un logon ke liye matter karta hai jo clients ko serve karte hain, ek employer ko nahi, aur koi vendor ka platform sath nahi le kar chalte. Teen tarah ke readers is mein fit hote hain: vendor-neutral Forward Deployed Engineer jo yeh book train karti hai, freelance ya fractional version usi role ka (fractional matlab kai clients ke liye part-time), aur Layer 3 par domain startup ka founder — jahan ek vertical products mein badalta hai.

Agar aap ek company ke andar employed ho, to bhi yeh reasoning chalti hai. Lekin ek limit sirf aap par lagti hai, aur woh [Who Keeps What](03-sequence-and-who-keeps-what.md) mein clearly bataya gaya hai: employer ke liye banaya gaya asset **employer** ka hota hai.

Aur agar abhi aapke paas aisa role nahi, phir bhi yeh page aapka hai. Zyada tar readers **service ladder** se shuru karenge, aur yeh discussion [Sequence aur Who Keeps What](03-sequence-and-who-keeps-what.md) mein aati hai.

## Paanch Cheezein Jo Aap Own Nahi Kar Sakte

Career ke baare mein "ownership" poochna ajeeb sawal hai — is liye pehle samjho yeh sahi sawal kyun hai. Ek skill jiske liye aapko hire kiya ja sakta hai, ek asset ke barabar nahi jo aap hold karte ho. Stable era mein farq zyada matter nahi karta, kyunki skill puri career chalti hai. Is era mein yeh poora matter karta hai, kyunki zyada tar skills absorb ho rahi hain aur zyada tar tools replace ho rahe hain. To durable sawal yeh nahi ke aap kya kar sakte ho — yeh hai ke **saal bhar kaam karne ke baad aapke paas kya bacha hai**.

To yahan test hai, iske do halves hain. **Aap ise hold karte ho** — matlab yeh rented nahi, aur koi isay wapis nahi le sakta. **Aur har koi ise hold nahi karta** — matlab yeh aapko agle candidate se alag kar sakta hai. Ek half fail ho, to yeh asset nahi, kuch bhi ho. Zyada tar cheezein jinhein practitioner value karta hai, kisi na kisi half mein fail hoti hain.

Har candidate ko chalao:

**Model aap own nahi kar sakte.** Aap isay rent karte ho, subscription ya token se. Wahi model aaj shaam wohi bidder rent kar raha hai jo aapke khilaf bid kar raha hai, usi price par. Jab behtar model aata hai, aap switch kar dete ho — yehi to point hai: yeh book isi liye bani hai taake behtar models muft upgrade ki tarah aayein. Lekin jo asset koi bhi rival tumse chheen nahi sakta, woh advantage nahi hota.

**Runtime aap own nahi kar sakte.** Harness woh software hai jo agent loop chalata hai, state hold karta hai, aur tools call karta hai. Yeh book jaan-boojh kar aapko **kisi bhi** harness use karne aur portable rehne ki training deti hai, ek banane ki nahi. Jo tool aap is saal use karte ho, agle saal wohi na ho, lekin aapki discipline dono se zyada chalegi. Yeh acha property hai, ownership nahi.

**Apna labour aap own nahi kar sakte.** Hours aur syntax sath fail hote hain, aur ek hi wajah se. Hours bechna woh layer hai jise machine absorb kar rahi hai. Yehi model purani services pyramid ka bhi tha, jismein bohat se junior log kam senior logon ke neeche hours bill karte the. To woh client jo aapko staff shortage cover karne ko kehta hai, asal mein aapke **hours rent** karna chahta hai. Aisa ek saal aapke paas invoices ke ilawa kuch nahi chhorta. Code likhna bhi isi wajah se fail hota hai, aur yahin se yeh book shuru hoti hai — Harari ke sawal par. AI real-time mein syntax absorb kar rahi hai. To syntax sikhana wahi skill sikhana hai jise machine sabse tez le rahi hai.

**Vendor ka platform aap own nahi kar sakte.** Ek engineer ko client ke paas ek vendor ka ontology aur tools pehle se banaye hue bhej do, to uske paas real leverage hoti hai: ek insaan ab hafton mein woh kaam karta hai jo pehle ek team saalon mein karti thi. (Ontology yahan matlab: company ke concepts aur unke rishton ka naqsha.) Lekin yeh leverage **uski nahi**. Yeh vendor ki hai, aur isi liye vendor engagement ke liye pay karta hai. Jab woh vendor ko chhorti hai, platform peeche reh jata hai, aur uske paas lane ko kuch nahi bachta.

Yeh refusal sirf paanch mein se ek nahi hai. Yeh wajah hai ke baaki chaar bhi matter karte hain. Vendor ke payroll par ek engineer ko yeh page ki zaroorat nahi — usay ek asset diya ja chuka hai, uska kaam sirf usay deploy karna hai. Vendor ko hata do, sawal na-guzir ho jata hai. **Vendor-neutrality koi virtue nahi jo yeh book recommend karti hai — yeh woh condition hai jo kuch own karna zaroori bana deti hai.** Aage jo bhi likha hai woh yeh hai ke ek vendor-neutral Forward Deployed Engineer iske baare mein kya karta hai.

**Method aap own nahi kar sakte, aur yeh sabse uncomfortable wala hai.** Jo kuch yeh book sikhati hai, woh sab ko sikhati hai. Spec-driven development, agent architecture, evaluation, oversight, deployment: har graduate ko same instruction milti hai. Wohi milti hai us insaan ko jo aapki chahi hui job ke liye apply kar raha hai. Ek method jo har reader share karta hai, aapko unse alag nahi kar sakta. Yeh book ki kami nahi — yehi wajah hai ke yeh ek **book** hai.

Paanch candidates, paanch refusals, aur test ne khud kaam kiya, personal preference ne nahi. Teen pehle half mein fail hue: model, runtime, aur platform — sab kisi aur ke hain aur wapis liye ja sakte hain. Do dusre half mein fail hue: aapka labour aur method aapke bhi hain aur sab ke bhi. Chhata candidate jaan-boojh kar apni jagah ke liye rakha gaya hai, kyunki iska objection ek line se zyada deserve karta hai — dekho [Kyun Na Widen Karo](02-two-sors-coverage-and-narrowing.md).
