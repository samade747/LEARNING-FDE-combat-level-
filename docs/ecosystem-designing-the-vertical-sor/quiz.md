### Q1. Kyun purana workflow blueprint nahi ban sakta

**Q:** Why does this page refuse to treat a profession's current workflow as the blueprint for its System
of Record?

**Explanation:** Most pieces of a current workflow are not the profession at all: they are accommodations

leave the museum of old steps behind.

### Q4. First principles vs copying

**Q:** What distinguishes first principles thinking from thinking by copying?

- It skips any study of how the work is done today
- It is faster than copying in nearly every case
- **It builds up from basic truths, not from imitation** ✅
- It always produces a solution that nobody has ever attempted before

**Explanation:** First principles breaks a problem down to the truths that remain when every habit is

ingredients themselves.
