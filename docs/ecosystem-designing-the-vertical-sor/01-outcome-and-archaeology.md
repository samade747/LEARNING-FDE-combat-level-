# 01 — Outcome Se Shuru Karo, Phir Workflow Archaeology

## Start From The Outcome, Not The Workflow

Purani process ko haath lagane se pehle, **professional outcome** likho — woh completed result jo
customer, regulator, ya reviewer recognize aur judge kar sake.

- **Weak outcome:** "process audit working papers." **Better:** "produce a complete, evidence-backed
  working paper that supports its conclusion, follows the applicable standards, lists unresolved
  exceptions, and is ready for reviewer approval."
- **Weak outcome:** "automate invoice review." **Better:** "decide whether an invoice is valid,
  supported, correctly coded, within authorization limits, and ready for payment or clearly sent to
  exception handling."

Pehle outcome ke liye **outcome contract** likho ([Template 1](06-templates.md)): Outcome, Trigger,
Inputs, Evidence, Acceptance criteria, Main number + guardrails, Forbidden actions, Final authority,
Record. Yeh contract [contract of success](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md) ka seed banta hai.

**Ek line jo abhi finish nahi ho sakti:** "main number" batata hai *kya* improve hona chahiye (hours per
file, days to close, tickets per day) — expert ka apna estimate design ke liye kaafi hai. Lekin signed
contract ka **baseline customer ka apna number hai**, uske apne workflow mein measure kiya gaya, kyunke
yehi ek number hai jo buyer verify kar sakta hai. **Design against the expert's world. Contract against
the customer's.**

## Workflow Archaeology

Ab, aur sirf ab, existing workflow study karo — copy karne ke liye nahi, **dig karne ke liye**. Boxes-
aur-arrows se shuru mat karo. Real cases se shuru karo: expert se poocho 5 files walkthrough kare — ek
normal case, ek difficult, ek jo fail hui, ek jo escalate hui, aur ek jahan ek experienced professional
ne woh pakda jo ek beginner miss kar deta.

**Yeh files kiski hain?** Abhi koi customer nahi hai, isliye archaeology expert ke 20 saal ke practice
par chalti hai, kisi client ki data par nahi — isi liye launch rule expert ko pehle rakhti hai: uski
files ke bina kuch study karne ko nahi, aur study ke bina buyer ko dikhane ko kuch nahi.

Phir written procedure ke neeche dig karo — kyunke written procedure kabhi poori sachaai nahi hoti:
- Experienced log kya check karte hain jo procedure mention nahi karta?
- Konse steps chupke se skip kiye jate hain, aur kyun?
- Konsi approval hamesha automatic hai, konsi kabhi real problem pakarti hai?
- Konsi reports banti hain lekin kabhi parhi nahi jaatin?
- Konsa spreadsheet chupke se essential ban gaya hai?
- Jab procedure case fit nahi karti, sab kis ko call karte hain, aur woh kya jaanta hai?

Written sources formal knowledge dete hain. Cases aur yeh sawal **unwritten judgment** dete hain — yehi
se **expert twin** shuru hota hai. Jawab, expert ki apni voice mein likhe hue, licensed material bante
hain jo kahin aur exist nahi karte.

**Practical warning:** expert ka waqt poore build mein sabse scarce resource hai. Usay judgment par kharch
karo, typing par nahi. Case walkthroughs record karo. Agent se recordings se draft likhwao. Phir expert
review/correct/approve kare, apni voice mein. Ghanton ki expert review haftton ki expert writing ki jagah
le sakti hai — aur review hi woh jagah hai jahan uske 20 saal asal mein corpus mein daakhil hote hain.
