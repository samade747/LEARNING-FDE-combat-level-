## Yeh Chapter Kis Baare Mein Hai

Ab tak is book mein aap ne concepts, crash courses, aur individual skills parhi hain. Yeh chapter ek
zoom-out hai — poori **"Agent Factory Ecosystem"** ka naqsha. Idea simple hai lekin powerful: book ko
ek dead PDF ki tarah treat mat karo, ek **System of Record** bana do — ek governed source of truth jise
insaan bhi parh sakein aur AI agents bhi query kar sakein. Phir usi source par teen products khare karo:
ek jo **sikhata** hai (Zia Tutor AI), ek jo **banata** hai (Zia Developer AI), aur khud System of Record,
jo dono ko feed karta hai. Yeh sab ek blueprint follow karte hain — **FDE AF Model** — jo batata hai yeh
ecosystem ek pura business model kaise ban jata hai, jahan Panaversity ke graduates upar ke layers pe
kama sakte hain.

"The thesis, shipped" — yani jo poori book ne sikhaya (System of Record, connector-native apps, AI
Workers), yeh chapter woh sab **live products** ki shakal mein dikhata hai.
