# 03 — Sabse Zaroori Seat: Outcome Architect

AI workforce jitni barhti jaati hai, utna hi zyada woh ek insaan par depend karti hai jo precisely bata
sake **kya banana hai** — yeh hai **Outcome Architect**, wo role jo intent ka malik hota hai. Yeh
chapter mein cover hue char products (System of Record, Zia Tutor AI, Zia Developer AI, FDE AF Model)
wahi cheezein hain jin ke saath yeh role kaam karta hai, aur poora ecosystem isi tarah banaya gaya hai
ke woh [vertical businesses mein grow ho sake jo yeh role chala sakta hai](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md).

Agent tools tezi se sasta aur zyada capable hote ja rahe hain — execution ki value girti hai. Jo cheez
nahi girti, woh hai: kisi ko yeh malum hona ke *bilkul theek theek* kya banwana hai, kis order mein, aur
kab "done" ka matlab hona chahiye. Yehi seat hai jo poora ecosystem — teaching, building, aur vertical
business dono — protect aur train karne ki koshish karta hai.

Agar aap yeh jaanna chahte ho ke is book mein aap kaunsa role play kar sakte ho, book ka apna page
padhein: *"Read about the roles this book trains"*. Aur agar aap poori ecosystem ki kahani shuru se
parhna chahte ho, [The Ecosystem Concept](../ecosystem-ecosystem-concept/README.md) chapter se shuru
karo — yeh batata hai yeh sab shuru kaise hua, aur textbook se System of Record tak yeh safar kaise
tay hua.
