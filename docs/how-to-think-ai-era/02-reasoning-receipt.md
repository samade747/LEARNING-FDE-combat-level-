# 02 — Discipline 2: The Reasoning Receipt

Subah Claude ke sath report par kaam kiya, result achha laga, bhej diya. 2 hafte baad koi pucche
"kaunse parts actually check kiye? kaunse change kiye?" — koi answer nahi, bas parha, theek laga,
use kar liya.

**Reasoning Receipt** = har AI output ke piece ke liye ek-line notes ka list. Har note batata
**kya kiya** aur **kyun**. Shop receipt end mein likhi jati; yeh kaam karte waqt likhi jati, aur
jo likhte ho uska decision par asar hota.

## 3 Columns, 5 Labels

| Column | Kya |
|---|---|
| What AI said | Future you yaad rakhe kya decide ho raha tha |
| What you did | 1-word label |
| Why | 1 sentence, row defensible rahe |

| Label | Kya Kiya | Kyun Likho |
|---|---|---|
| **ACCEPT** | Kept, no changes | Trust kyun kiya |
| **REJECT** | Galat decide kiya, hataya | Disagree kyun hue |
| **MODIFY** | Idea rakhi, part change ki | Kya change kiya, kyun |
| **SURFACED** | AI ne kuch naya raised kiya, kept | Kyun matter karta |
| **MISSED** | Tumne notice kiya AI ne bhool diya, add kiya | Kya missing tha, kyun matter karta |

ACCEPT/REJECT/MODIFY basic moves hain. **SURFACED** marks jab AI ne sikhaya jo tum khud nahi
sochte. **MISSED** marks jo AI ne nahi kaha par kehna chahiye tha — tumhara judgment catch karta.

**Good receipt mein 5 labels ka mix hota hai.**

## "Koi Audit Nahi Karta, Toh Kyun Likhun?"

Reasonable sawal hai. 3 cheezein receipt karti hain **bina kisi ke puche bhi:**

1. **Likhna khud decision change karta.** Silently accept karte waqt dimagh "sounds right" process
   karta. Label+reason likhna force karta examine karo. Zyadatar readers ek row mein "why" complete
   nahi kar paate — wohi row thi jo bina soche use hone wali thi.
2. **Receipt kaam ka hissa ban jati, sirf record nahi.** REJECT row aksar "considered alternatives"
   paragraph ban jati. SURFACED row meeting ki sabse interesting cheez.
3. **Future-you hi usually checker hai.** 3 mahine baad yaad nahi rahega kaunse parts tumhare the,
   kaunse AI ke. Receipt future-you ke liye note hai.

Audit sabse visible payoff hai, sabse rare bhi. Baqi 3 har baar hoti.

> **All-ACCEPT warning sign hai.** Har row "sounds right" ke sath ACCEPT = sahi mein socha nahi,
> sirf copy kiya. "Why" explain nahi kar sakte to actually decide nahi kiya, bas along chal gaye.

## Worked Example — Group Presentation

| AI Ne Kaha | Label | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| "Short video clip se start karo" | ACCEPT | Teacher ne bola visual openings better grades |
| "Har insaan ko 5 minute do" | REJECT | 15 min, 4 log, math nahi chalta |
| "Q&A se end karo" | MODIFY | Haan, par 3 backup questions ready rakhenge |
| "Live demo add karo" | SURFACED | Yeh nahi socha tha, stand out karega |
| (adapter kaun layega — nahi bola) | MISSED | Pichli baar bhool gaye, 5 min waste hui |

Teacher ne pucha kyun 5-min rule nahi follow ki — row 2 par point kiya: "15 min, 4 log, math nahi
chala." Ek sentence kaafi tha.

## Research Ke Peeche

- **Reflection-in-action (Donald Schön, 1983):** skilled professionals kaam karte-karte internal
  commentary rakhte, surprises notice karte — jo yeh commentary explicit karte wo fastest improve
  karte. Receipt wohi commentary hai, likhi hui, kaam ke beech mein.
- **Single/double-loop learning (Chris Argyris, 1977):** single-loop = immediate mistake fix karna.
  Double-loop = poora approach galat tha kya pucha. All-ACCEPT receipt single-loop thinking hai
  visible. Real "why" force karna double loop mein push karta.
- **Generation effect (Brown/Roediger/McDaniel, 2014):** jo apne words mein likhte ho, usay zyada
  yaad rehta re-read karne se. Real reason likhi row 3 mahine baad bhi yaad rahegi, "sounds right"
  wali row blank hogi.
