# 05 — Discipline 5: First Principles

**First principles** thinking = _tumhare_ case ke liye kya actually true hai figure karna, sirf
isliye accept na karna ke sab repeat karte hain.

Pehle ek term: **Named threshold** = specific condition jo batati advice kab kaam karna band karti.
"Yeh advice 30 se kam students wali class mein kaam karti" named threshold hai. "Yeh kabhi kabhi
kaam karti" nahi hai — "kabhi kabhi" kabhi nahi batata **kab**.

## Scenario

Coding club president ho. Har doosri club fees charge kar rahi. VP, advisor, 2 senior members,
aur AI — sab same bolte: "Fees charge karo." **Yehi agreement danger hai** — sab ek answer ke
peeche line up ho jayein to settled lagta, sochna band karna aasan hota. Par common advice **most**
clubs ke liye kaam karti — tumhari club exception ho sakti, koi check nahi kar raha.

**Check ka shape:** common advice lo, exact condition dhoondo jahan kaam nahi karti. Zyadatar log
vague complaint dete ("fees charge karna hamesha achha nahi"), jo useless hai — "hamesha nahi"
kabhi **kab** nahi batata. Skill: vague complaint ko **named threshold** mein badalna.

- _Vague:_ "Fees charge karna hamesha achha idea nahi."
- _Named threshold:_ "Jab club ka main goal first-year students attract karna ho jinhon ne kabhi
  code nahi kiya, aur zyadatar fee afford nahi kar sakte, fees exactly unko door kar degi jinke
  liye club bani hai."

Pehla shrug hai. Doosra **exactly** batata kab fail hoti aur **kyun**. Pehla kuch change nahi
karta, doosra decision change karta.

## Practice Kaise Karein

Common advice lo jo sab bol rahe (AI bhi). **3 rows likho**, har ek specific number/condition ke
sath jahan advice kaam nahi karti.

**Coding club president ki 3 boundaries:**

1. Jab 80%+ members first-year no-income hon, fee exactly unko door karegi
2. Jab club ki main value free workshops hon, fee barrier banati — matter karta jab campus mein
   3+ competing free clubs hon
3. Jab budget university grant se aata ho jo "open to all" require karta, fees se grant lose ho
   sakta

Advisor ko 3 boundaries presented, club free rakhi, sponsored hackathons se paisa banaya. Semester
ke end tak membership 40% badhi, jabke fee-charging clubs mein attendance giri. **Teeno boundaries
common advice mein nahi thi, AI ke first answer mein bhi nahi.**

**Without thresholds yeh likhti:** "Sometimes fees achha idea nahi" (kab nahi batata); "doosri
clubs ko pata nahi" (complaint hai, reason nahi); "depends on situation" (sab jaante hain depends
karta).

## Try It Yourself

Koi common advice lo jo log repeat karte ("follow your passion," "hamesha group mein study karo,"
"20% salary save karo"). 3 rows likho, har ek specific number/condition ke sath.

**Note:** "sometimes"/"often"/"it depends" thresholds nahi hain. **3rd row nahi aa raha?** Matlab
tum advice ko samjhe bina follow kar rahe the — doosri advice choose karo.

## Research Ke Peeche

- **Ecological rationality (Gigerenzer, Todd & ABC Research Group, 1999):** _heuristic +
  environment = outcome_. Simple rule khud achha/bura nahi hota — jis environment mein fit hota
  wahan achha, jahan nahi fit hota wahan bura. Threshold wahi environment specify karta.
- **Recognition-primed decisions (Gary Klein, 1998):** experts rarely options weigh karte — familiar
  pattern recognize karte, first-fit run karte. Fast, aksar sahi — par consensus advice bhi isi
  tarah unexamined slip karti. "Kab fail hota" likhna automatic match ko interrupt karta.
- **Falsifiability (Karl Popper, 1959):** claim sirf tab world ke baare mein kuch batati jab bata
  sako kya usay wrong prove karega. Named threshold wohi test advice par apply karta.
