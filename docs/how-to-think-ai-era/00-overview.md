# 00 — Overview: AI Era Mein Kaise Socha Jaye

## Person A vs Person B

2 log Monday morning same AI tool kholte, same task, same hafta. **Person A** Friday ko clear
recommendation de deti jo explain kar sakti — usne likha tha kaunse AI claims se agree kiya,
kaunse push-back kiye, kya change-mind karata. **Person B** Friday ko polished document deti jo
Monday ko AI ne bola wohi repeat karta — boss puche "kyun recommend kiya?" to apni hi reasoning
explain nahi kar sakti.

**Person A ne AI poochne se pehle apni opinion bana li. Person B ne AI ke pehle answer ko apni
opinion ban jane diya.**

6 thinking habits, 3 short parts, koi code nahi. Har habit AI ke ek misleading tareeqe ka jawab
deta. Bina inke AI **answer machine** hai (pucho, answer aaye, accept karo). Inke sath AI
**thinking partner** hai (pehle predict karo, answer aaye, compare karo, decide karo).

## Woh Force Jiske Khilaf Yeh Course Train Karta

Person B lazy nahi, careless nahi — ek force usay pull kar rahi jiska naam hai. MIT Sloan ke
Professor Eric So isay **AI gravity** bolte: "apni thinking ka zyada se zyada hissa AI ko
outsource karne ka constant pull aur push." Har din thodi aasan ho jata AI se sochwana, aur thoda
aasan apna sochna chhod dena.

**Gravity 3 directions se pull karti:**

1. **Dimagh energy bachana chahta** — har task AI ko dena aasan lagta, pehle khud likhna extra
   kaam lagta
2. **Kaam expert-level dikhna chahiye** — AI seconds mein expert-jaisa likh deta, bina uske race
   mein ek shoe se daudna lagta
3. **Pata nahi doosre log kitna AI use kar rahe** — classmates/coworkers announce nahi karte, sab
   assume karte sab zyada use kar rahe, koi peeche nahi rehna chahta

**Pull ki cost — MIT Media Lab study:** ChatGPT se essays likhne wale 83 mein se 100 log hand-in
karne ke foran baad apne hi essay ka ek sentence repeat nahi kar sake. Words screen se homework
tak gaye, writer ke zehan se guzre bina.

Professor So isko **cognitive capital** bolte — problem se guzarne, galat answer pehchanne, confident
answer mein chhodi hui cheez notice karne ki ability. **Muscle hai, bank balance nahi** — use na
karo to fade ho jata. Har discipline is muscle par chalti hai.

**Professor So ke 4 push-back tareeqe:** hard thinking khud karte raho; jaano bina AI ke kya kar
sakte ho; AI se bachaya time harder thinking par lagao; AI ko coach ki tarah use karo, answer
machine nahi.

> **Yeh section "AI kam use karo" nahi keh rehi.** 6 disciplines diet nahi hain — **counterweight**
> hain. AI use karte raho, par decide karne wali thinking khud karo.

**Prerequisites:** What AI Actually Is (mental model) + AI Prompting in 2026 (mechanics) pehle
parho — yeh course thinking discipline sikhata jo unhe pay off karati.

## Rule Ek Line Mein

**Deliverable kabhi answer nahi hota. Deliverable thinking ka documented evidence hota hai.**

2 claims: (1) **Deliverable** jo boss/professor/client ko do — ab sirf answer nahi, AI seconds
mein polished answer de deta. (2) Jo deliverable ko **trustworthy** banata hai: **tumne kaise
socha uska written record** — locked prediction, REJECT marked row+reason, cascade map. Koi pucche
"kyun decide kiya?" to evidence par point karo.

> Evidence usually deliverable ke andar hoti hai (footnote, "considered and rejected" paragraph,
> cascade map figure). Kabhi saath wali working doc mein. **Chat link khud evidence nahi hai** —
> raw material hai (jaisa academic paper ka raw data) — kya kaha dikhata, kya decide kiya nahi;
> busy reader ke liye bohot lambi; AI ki galtiyan tumhare zehan mein hoti, transcript mein nahi.

**Evidence kya deta hai?** (1) **Likhna khud thinking force karta** — specific prediction bina
decide kiye nahi likh sakte, REJECT bina explain kiye mark nahi kar sakte. (2) **Record khud ek
working tool hai**, sirf trail nahi — bank manager ne likha "close karo, zyada customers app-only
hain," AI ne data dikhaya 45% — yehi gap uski report ki opening line ban gayi.

**Jo change hua: likhne ka habit nahi — usay skip karne ki cost.** Pehle polished output
expensive thi, hard part banana tha. AI ne polished output free kar diya — bottleneck **producing**
se **evaluating** ki taraf shift hui, aur written evidence hi evaluation ka tareeqa hai.

## 5 Essentials (6th Ko Setup Chahiye)

1. **AI se pehle socho.** Apna answer likho AI kholne se pehle — parhne ke baad AI takeover kar
   leta.
2. **Written record rakho kya accept/reject kiya.** Har claim ke liye 1 sentence why likho.
3. **Polished writing = correct writing nahi.** 6 error types check karo bhejne se pehle.
4. **Obvious answer complete answer nahi.** Side effects trace karo decision se pehle.
5. **AI ke SAATH kaam karo, uske hawale nahi.** Tum sochte/decide karte, AI research/draft karta.

## 6 Disciplines — Poora Framework

| Part | Discipline | Failure Mode Jo Yeh Answer Karti | Action Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Foundations** | Prediction Lock | AI tumhari jagah sochta | PREDICT BEFORE YOU PROMPT |
| | Reasoning Receipt | AI sahi/galat ho, equally confident lagta | DOCUMENT EVERY DECISION |
| **2. Detection** | Error Taxonomy | AI confident hota galat hone par bhi | PREDICT WHERE ERRORS HIDE |
| | Thinking in Systems | AI side effects ignore karta | CASCADE MAPS & LOOPS |
| **3. Origination** | First Principles | AI apna judgment chhodne deta | FIND THE BOUNDARY |
| | Working WITH AI | AI oracle banna chahta, tumhara judgment fade hota | OVERRIDE & ITERATE |

Har Part agle ko enable karta — **Foundations** posture set karta, **Detection** jo AI miss karta
pakarta, **Origination** jo AI kabhi nahi kar sakta wo karta.
