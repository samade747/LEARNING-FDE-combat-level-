# Project 4 — The Decision Dossier (Capstone)

**Time:** ~30-45 min · **Tool:** any free AI assistant

Ek decision pick karo jo is hafte actually matter karti — jahan koi baad mein justify karne ko
bol sake: hire, bari purchase, project direction, career move, hard conversation. Sab 6
disciplines chalao, result ko **Decision Dossier** ki tarah rakho — ek private file, published
nahi, polished memo nahi — tumhari thinking ka documented evidence.

Blank doc kholo, har step ek short section add karta:

1. **Prediction Lock** (2 min) — 2-line lock: real decision + position with reversal-finding
2. **Reasoning Receipt** (5 min) — AI se recommendation lo, 3 claims ko ACCEPT/REJECT/MODIFY +
   1-sentence why label do (Project 2 ka self-audit prompt use kar sakte)
3. **Error Taxonomy** (3 min) — 6 types se 1 named error scan karo, exact sentence quote karo
4. **Cascade Map** (5 min) — 3 groups pick karo, har ek ke neeche 1 layer "aur phir kya?" likho,
   1 loop naam do
5. **First Principles** (3 min) — 1 boundary row: threshold jahan common advice tumhare case
   ke liye fail hoti
6. **Three-Path Comparison** (5 min) — recommendation ka 1 short paragraph Solo + 1 AI-help se,
   compare karo, override rakho (jo line tumne khud likhi jo AI ki version mein missing thi)

## Done

- [ ] Ek file mein sab 6 sections filled hain real decision ke liye
