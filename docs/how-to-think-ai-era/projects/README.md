# How to Think in the AI Era — Projects

4 projects jo chapter ko tumhari apni zindagi ke real decisions par chalate. Koi URL ship nahi
karte, koi app nahi banate. Ek real decision lo, discipline chalao jab tak kuch catch ho jo miss
ho jata, trail rakho.

**Win chhota aur real hota hai:** ek sentence jo loud bol sako — "main X decide karne wala tha,
par ek galti catch ki, plan change kiya." Jo rakhte ho uska naam **Decision Dossier** hai — ek
chhoti file, private, jo "kyun decide kiya?" ka jawab deti bina kuch yaad kiye.

1. [1-call-it-before-it-happens](1-call-it-before-it-happens/README.md) — 🎯 lock + premortem (~15 min)
2. [2-audit-the-confident-answer](2-audit-the-confident-answer/README.md) — 🧾 receipt + error scan (~20 min)
3. [3-question-that-unlocked-it](3-question-that-unlocked-it/README.md) — 🔁 sawal find karo, answer nahi (~20 min)
4. [4-decision-dossier](4-decision-dossier/README.md) — 🗂️ capstone, sab 6 disciplines (30-45 min)
