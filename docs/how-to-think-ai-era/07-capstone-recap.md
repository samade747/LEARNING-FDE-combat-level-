# 07 — Capstone: Ek Decision, Chhe Disciplines

Tum student council president ho. University ne surprise $10,000 budget diya, semester khatam
hone se pehle spend karna hai. Option A: ek bara farewell party (event planner hire karo). Option
B: AI tools kharido jo poore saal har council member ki event-planning better bana dein. Council
split hai. Friday ko recommendation present karni hai.

**Discipline 1 (Prediction Lock):** Real decision — "one big event vs sab future events behtar
banana." Settling question — kya AI tools actually enough members use karenge? Position: Option
B, kyunke yeh council saal bhar event-planning se struggle karta dekha hai. 55% sure — 6/8 members
use na karein to Option A switch. **Question AI ki nahi thi, logon se pucha** — 6/8 ne haan kaha,
bar clear hua, position stands, ab number ke sath.

**Discipline 2 (Reasoning Receipt):** AI: "party 500+ students ko lasting memories degi" — MODIFY
(venue sirf 300 hold karta). "AI tools event quality 35% barhate" — REJECT (no source, council ne
kabhi use nahi kiya). "doosri universities ne save kiya" — SURFACED. Event insurance+security
AI ne mention nahi ki — MISSED, khud add ki.

**Discipline 3 (Error Taxonomy):** 3 aur errors mile jo receipt miss kar gayi — **fabricated
source** ("2025 National Student Events Report" kahin nahi milti), **stale fact** (AI tools ki
price purani thi, abhi 15% zyada hai), **false confidence** ("one semester mein pay for themselves"
bina kisi basis ke).

**Discipline 4 (Cascade Map):** 5 groups: council members, students, admin, next year's council,
sponsors. **Loop mila:** sirf 4/8 members tools use karein to events behtar nahi hote, next
council koi benefit nahi dekhta, tools cancel ho jate — investment waste.

**Discipline 5 (First Principles):** "Bara event school spirit banata" — boundary: jab 20% se kam
students attend kar sakein (300/2000), party sirf chhote group ke liye spirit banati, baqi left
out feel karte.

**Discipline 6 (Working WITH AI):** Solo ne Option B ka solid case diya, par farewell-party-wale
members ke concerns address karna bhool gaya. AI-only ne "dono karo" bola bina budget-fit batae.
Collaborative mein khud core argument likha, AI se farewell-supporters ke concerns address karne
mein help li, aur ek specific safeguard rule add ki: 3 mahine mein 6/8 members training complete
na karein to baqi paisa party ko jaye.

**Council ne Option B + safeguard rule vote ki.** Har part explain kar sakte ho — kyunke khud
banaya, AI ki help se.

> **6 disciplines ne answer nahi diya — trail diya.** Bina inke: "mujhe lagta B behtar hai." Inke
> sath: evidence + backup plan.

## Har Decision Par 6 Disciplines Mat Chalao

Lunch kahan khana hai ke liye Cascade Map nahi chahiye. Har text message ke liye Reasoning Receipt
nahi chahiye.

| Decision Kitni Important | Example | Kaunsi Disciplines | Waqt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bilkul nahi | Lunch kahan, routine reply | Koi nahi | 0-1 min |
| Kuch important | Next-semester course, laptop | Prediction Lock + Error Taxonomy | 10-15 min |
| Important, deadline ke sath | Career choice, bari purchase | Prediction Lock + Receipt + Error Taxonomy + 1-2 aur | 30-60 min |
| Bohot important, log judge karenge | Thesis defense, job interview, council recommendation | Sab 6 | 90+ min |

## Short Recap

6 disciplines ke neeche ek rule: **deliverable kabhi answer nahi — thinking ka documented evidence
hai.**

1. **Prediction Lock** — AI se pehle apna position lock karo, flip-condition naam do
2. **Reasoning Receipt** — har AI claim ko label+reason do
3. **Error Taxonomy** — 6 named error types scan karo
4. **Thinking in Systems** — 3 layers deep trace karo, loop dhoondo
5. **First Principles** — named threshold dhoondo jahan common advice fail hoti
6. **Working WITH AI** — Solo/AI-only/Collaborative compare karo, overrides naam do

**Professor So ka test:** tools jo use karte wo skills jaisi nahi hain jo tum rakhte ho. Agar
tumhari thinking machine off hone par ruk jati, yeh kabhi tumhari thinking thi hi nahi.
