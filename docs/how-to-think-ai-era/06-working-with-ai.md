# 06 — Discipline 6: Working WITH AI

**Working WITH AI** = tum thinking+deciding karte, AI research+drafting karta. Jab yeh split
slip karti: subah AI ke sath important essay par kaam kiya, arguments clear, writing polished.
Professor pucche "kaunse parts tumhare ideas hain, kaunse AI ke?" — pata nahi chalta, kuch tumhare,
kuch AI ke, zyadatar mix.

## Three-Path Comparison

Same task **3 tareeqon se** karo, phir side-by-side compare karo:

1. **Solo** — 15 min, bina AI, sirf tum aur problem
2. **AI-only** — 5 min, AI se pucho, pehla answer accept, kuch change na karo
3. **Collaborative** — 10 min, AI se pucho, critically parho, disagree jahan zaroori ho, follow-up
   pucho, khud rewrite karo

Phir compare karo: kaunsa best hai? Collaborative ke kaunse parts _tumhare pushback ki wajah se_
behtar hain? **Real lesson:** exactly kahan tumhari thinking ne isay behtar banaya, dekhna.

**Email closing line example:**

- _Solo:_ "Thanks, sorry again for the trouble." (apologetic, weak)
- _AI-only:_ "Thank you for your time and consideration." (polished, kisi ke bhi email jaisa)
- _Collaborative:_ "I can show you what I have so far if that helps." (tumhari, kaam already shuru
  hone ka proof)

## Worked Example — Deadline Extension Email

Family emergency thi, honest email chahiye thi jo excuse na lage.

- **Solo (15 min):** honest, personal, par rambled — asal request (5 extra din) bottom mein buried
- **AI-only (5 min):** polished, well-structured, par generic — kisi specific detail ke bina,
  template jaisi
- **Collaborative (10 min):** apne words mein opening likhi, AI se structure help li (request top
  par), AI ka softening tone reject kiya (yeh professor directness prefer karta), AI ki closing
  line replace ki apne sentence se

**Collaborative jeeti 2 specific cheezon ki wajah se:** direct wording rakhi (AI soften karna
chahta tha), request top par rakhi (khud nahi sochti). Professor ne 1 ghante mein reply kiya,
extension diya.

**3 versions side by side hi documented evidence of thinking hain.** Win "email achhi thi" nahi —
win yeh hai ke line-by-line dikha sakti ho **kahan judgment ne result change kiya.**

**Kyun teeno chahiye, sirf Collaborative nahi:**

- Solo ke bina pata nahi chalega kya tumhara hai, kya AI ka
- Teeno compare kiye bina prove nahi kar sakte Collaborative behtar hai ("feels better" jawab nahi)
- AI-only ke bina pata nahi chalega sab accept kar liya ya nahi — Collaborative aur AI-only almost
  same lagein to copy kiya

> **Kab use karo, kab skip karo:** jahan personal experience matter kare (tumhare jaisi sound
> karne wali email, AI ko na pata situation, creative work). Simple tasks (table format karna)
> mein AI ko karne do.

## Try It Yourself

Landlord ko rent-reduction message, ya professor ko extension request — jahan tumhare pas context
ho jo AI ke pass nahi (payment history, relationship). **AI-only draft skip mat karo** — sabse
tempting drop karne wali, sabse revealing rakhne wali. Collaborative AI-only ke close ho to tumne
over-accept kiya — yeh sirf dono likh ke pata chalta.

## Research Ke Peeche

- **Centaur chess (Garry Kasparov):** Deep Blue se harne ke baad "advanced chess" popularize ki —
  human+computer. Strongest competitors grandmasters nahi, **machine manage karne mein skilled**
  ordinary players the. Teaming jeeti jab human kuch de jo machine ke pass nahi.
- **AI lifts the least-skilled (Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond, 2023):** 5,179 support agents par study
  — productivity 14% average badhi, par gain almost entirely beginners (~34%) mein, experienced
  agents par kam effect. Collaboration tab value deta jab tum kuch lao jo AI ke pass nahi.
- **AI compresses toward middle (Noy & Zhang, 2023):** 444 professionals ne writing tasks kiye,
  half ChatGPT ke sath. Time kam hui, average quality badhi, par range squeeze hui — weak writers
  improved, strong writers barely change, outputs similar ho gaye. AI ka draft jaisa hai lo to
  sab jaisi middle mil jati. **Tumhare overrides hi result ko tumhara banate hain.**
