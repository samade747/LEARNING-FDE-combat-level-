# 09 — Test Your Understanding

Book ka apna `<Quiz>` block (English, verbatim options summarized + answer/explanation) — 31
scenario-based questions. Reasoning se answer do, longest option se nahi.

**Source:** How to Think in the AI Era Assessment. (Zia Tutor AI connector, window 3, fetched
2026-10-23.)

---

1. **Manager polished AI-written memo deti, boss pucche kyun recommend kiya, wo "sounded right"
   kehti.** **Answer:** Deliverable mein documented evidence of thinking nahi hai — locked
   prediction, accept/reject claims, reasoning jo point kar sake. _The rule in one line._

2. **High-stakes board memo ke liye, chat link ko evidence maana jaye?** **Answer: Raw material
   hai** — kya AI ne kaha dikhata, kya tumne decide kiya nahi; bohot lambi; AI ki galtiyan tumhare
   zehan mein hoti, transcript mein nahi. _The rule in one line._

3. **"Discipline" vs "failure mode" ka farq?** **Answer:** Discipline tum karte ho (thinking
   habit), failure mode AI karta hai (misleading tareeqa) — 1-to-1 paired. _Six disciplines._

4. **Part 1 (Foundations) skip kar ke seedha Error Taxonomy shuru karna.** **Answer:** Part 1 ki
   2 disciplines AI ko tumhari jagah sochne se rokti — skip karo to baqi 4 apna kaam nahi kar
   saktin. _Part 1._

5. **4 lines carefully likhi, par baad mein position yaad nahi.** **Answer:** Lock kaam nahi
   kiya — ek test hai: kya position+flip-condition bol sakte ho. Line-counting matter nahi karta.
   _Discipline 1._

6. **Maya ka draft question: "Kaunsa camp better hai?"** **Answer:** Open question decision AI ko
   de deta. Line 2 closed/checkable honi chahiye ("bootcamp Python sikhata?"). _Discipline 1._

7. **Line 3+4 (decision+confidence) bhi prompt mein paste kar diye.** **Answer:** AI ko commitment
   pata chale to agree karega, comparison kho jati — sirf Lines 1+2 prompt mein jaayein.
   _Discipline 1._

8. **"Morning flight pick karta hoon" likha, par koi flip-condition naam nahi di.** **Answer:**
   Hope hai, commitment nahi — jab tak specific flip-answer naam na ho, pata nahi commit kiya ya
   AI ke pehle hint par drop kar dete. _Discipline 1._

9. **AI ka answer dekhne ke baad Line 4 revise ki taki flip na count ho.** **Answer:** Lock
   defeat hoti — test: kya bina AI ka answer dekhe yeh naya Line 4 likhte? Nahi to purana stands.
   _Discipline 1._

10. **Bank manager ne socha most app-only hain, data ne 45% dikhaya.** **Answer:** Gap ne
    overestimate dikhaya, recommendation flip hui, middle-path surface hui — lock ke bina kabhi
    nahi hoti. _Discipline 1._

11. **Reasoning Receipt row ki structure?** **Answer: 3 parts** — AI ne kya kaha, kya kiya
    (1-word label), 1-sentence why. _Discipline 2._

12. **Reader ne kuch important notice kiya jo AI ne nahi bola, khud add kiya.** **Answer: MISSED**
    label fit karti. _Discipline 2._

13. **10 rows, sab ACCEPT with "sounds right."** **Answer:** Sahi mein socha nahi — sign-off kiya,
    decide nahi — good receipt labels mix karti. _Discipline 2._

14. **"Koi audit nahi karta, kyun receipt rakhun?"** **Answer:** Likhna decision change karta,
    receipt kaam ka hissa ban jati, future-you usually checker hota — audit sabse rare payoff.
    _Discipline 2._

15. **"Yeh theek lagta hai?" ke jagah 6 named error types kyun scan karein?** **Answer:** Vague
    sawal se eyes slide over hote — naming ek specific hunt deti jo actually rukte ho check karne
    ke liye. _Discipline 3._

16. **AI: "Consumer Reports March 2026 ke mutabiq..." — exist nahi karta.** **Answer: Fabricated
    source.** _Discipline 3._

17. **AI: "Clean accident history, therefore no mechanical problems."** **Answer: Logical gap** —
    "therefore" conclusion jo evidence prove nahi karta. _Discipline 3._

18. **False confidence ka tell kya hai?** **Answer:** Debatable claim flatly bola, no hedge ("may"/
    "could" nahi), usually smoothest paragraph mein. _Discipline 3._

19. **Mistake slip through ho jaye to sabse pehle kaun hurt hota?** **Answer: Tum** — AI ki galti
    uske upar act karne wale ko hurt karti pehle. _Discipline 3._

20. **Topic nahi aata, claim 2 minute mein verify nahi ho rahi.** **Answer:** MODIFY label do with
    "not yet checked," exact source maango, precise-lagte bina-source numbers se suspicious raho.
    _Discipline 3._

21. **Ek group ke liye cascade chain kaise banayein?** **Answer:** First thing se start karo,
    "aur phir kya?" 3 baar pucho, 3 layers deep. _Discipline 4._

22. **Cascade map 1 layer ke baad neat/simple lagta hai.** **Answer:** Bohot jaldi roka — real
    risks deeper layers mein hain, mess hi goal hai, loop dhoondna hai jo decision undo kare.
    _Discipline 4._

23. **Cascade Mapping ko "partnership" kyun kehte, map pehle kaun draw karta?** **Answer:** Tum
    aur AI ke opposite blind spots hain — tum pehle draw karte (loops/forgotten-people sense karte
    ho), phir AI stress-test karta. _Discipline 4._

24. **"Loan officers AI se replace" map mein sabse easily-missed first effect?** **Answer:**
    Internal knowledge — tacit local lore lost (unwritten staff knowledge jo software mein kabhi
    nahi thi). _Discipline 4._

25. **"Fees charge karna hamesha achha idea nahi" — kyun fail hoti First Principles move ki tarah?**
    **Answer:** "Hamesha nahi" kabhi kab nahi batata. Named threshold exact condition bataye
    (jaisa "80% first-year no-income members"). _Discipline 5._

26. **VP, advisor, 2 senior members, AI — sab fee-charging par agree.** **Answer:** Agreement hi
    danger hai — settled feel hoti, sochna band karna aasan ho jata. Common advice most clubs ke
    liye hai, tumhari exception ho sakti. _Discipline 5._

27. **AI-only draft skip karna chahta "mujhe pata AI kya bolega."** **Answer:** AI-only sabse
    revealing hai — Collaborative uske close ho to pata chalta over-accept kiya, dono likh ke hi
    pata chalta. _Discipline 6._

28. **Collaborative email achhi bani, kya use win banata?** **Answer:** Line-by-line point kar
    sakte ho kahan judgment ne result change kiya — "feels better" kaafi nahi. _Discipline 6._

29. **Lunch kahan khana hai ke liye Cascade Map draw karna shuru ki.** **Answer:** Sab 6 har
    decision par mat chalao — sirf important decisions ke liye, baqi ke liye decide karo, chalo.
    _Capstone._

30. **"Somewhat important" decision (next-semester course, laptop) ke liye kaunsi disciplines
    fit karti?** **Answer:** Prediction Lock + Error Taxonomy top AI recommendation par,
    ~10-15 minute. _Capstone table._

31. **Person B "lazy nahi, careless nahi" — uska Monday-morning behavior kya explain karta?**
    **Answer: AI gravity** — 3 forces ek saath: dimagh energy bachana chahta, expert-level
    dikhna chahiye, pata nahi doosre kitna AI use kar rahe. _The force this course trains
    against._
