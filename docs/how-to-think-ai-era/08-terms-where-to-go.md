# 08 — Terms + Where to Go From Here

## Terms Glossary

- **AI gravity** — apni thinking ka zyada hissa AI ko outsource karne ka constant pull
- **cognitive capital** — tumhari thinking muscle, hard thinking khud karne se build hoti
- **discipline** — thinking habit jo tum practice karte (tum karte ho)
- **failure mode** — specific tareeqa jisse AI tumhe mislead karta (AI karta hai)
- **part** — disciplines ka group jo same job share karte (3 parts is course mein)
- **deliverable** — jo boss/professor/client ko do (answer + documented evidence)
- **documented evidence of thinking** — written record ke kaise answer tak pahunche
- **action line** — ek action jo discipline maangti, sticky-note-short
- **Prediction Lock** — tumhari apni written position, AI ke jawab se pehle committed
- **Reasoning Receipt** — har AI claim ke liye row (ACCEPT/REJECT/MODIFY/SURFACED/MISSED)
- **Error Taxonomy** — 6 named AI mistake types, ek ek check hoti
- **Thinking in Systems** — pehle effect par na rukna
- **Cascade Map** — decision ke causes ki 1-page drawing, 3 layers deep
- **loop** — chain jahan later effect wapas circle karke original decision change karti
- **First Principles** — common advice ko apne case ke against test karna
- **named threshold** — exact number/condition jahan advice kaam karna band karti
- **Working WITH AI** — tum sochte/decide karte, AI research/draft karta
- **three-path comparison** — ek task 3 tareeqon se (Solo/AI-only/Collaborative)
- **premortem** — plan already fail hua imagine karna, phir reasons list karna
- **Decision Dossier** — ek private file jisme ek real decision ka poora trail
