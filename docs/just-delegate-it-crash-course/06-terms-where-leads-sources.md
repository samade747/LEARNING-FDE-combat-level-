## Where This Leads

**Agla course:** What AI Actually Is — tumne machine ko ek job karte dekha hai, wo course batati
hai kya kiya, aur ghalat hone par bhi confident kyun hai.

**Brief barhti hai jaise kaam tumhari nazar se bahar jata hai:**

| Kahan | Brief Kya Ban Jati Hai |
| --- | --- |
| Yeh course | 6 lines, ek job jo tum dekhte ho |
| General Agents on the Web | +sources, destination, stop-rule — job jo tum nahi dekhte |
| Designing the Vertical SoR | Full outcome contract — trigger, evidence, acceptance criteria, guardrails, forbidden actions, named human, record |

**Loop book ka rhythm hai.** Thesis: *"Humans define intent. Agents execute. Humans verify
outcomes."* — yehi **10-80-10 Rule** hai. Tumne ise 11 baar ek chat window mein chalaya.

**6 steps jaise delegated work barhta hai:**

| Step | Tum AI Se Kya Kehte Ho | Kahan Taught |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prompt | Mujhe kuch batao | AI Prompting 2026 |
| Collaboration | Mujhe kuch karne mein madad karo | AI Fluency |
| Delegated job | Yeh outcome hai. Kaam karo. | **Yeh course** |
| Workflow | Yeh kaam baar-baar hota hai. | General Agents, phir Workflow Design & Diagnosis |
| AI Worker (Digital FTE) | Yeh responsibility own karo. | Mode 2: Manufacturing |
| Agent Factory | Workers ko method ki tarah banao, ek-ek nahi. | About, Thesis |
