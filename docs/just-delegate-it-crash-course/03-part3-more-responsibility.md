# 03 — Part 3: Give AI More Responsibility (Concepts 7-9)

## Concept 7: Kitna Job AI Own Kare

```text
Before you start, list the steps you plan to take, in order, and wait
for my go-ahead. Do not begin until I say go.
```

**Plan-first** = AI se steps list karne ko kaho, "go-ahead" ka wait karo kaam shuru hone se pehle.
Yeh sab se **cheapest moment** hai har job mein — plan parhna 1 minute, fix karna 1 sentence.
Baad mein pakdi gayi wahi mistake **poori run** cost karti hai.

Approved plan **cage nahi hai** — AI behtar route dhoond sakta hai. Jo nahi karna chahiye: koi
line cross karna jo tumne draw ki, ya chup-chaap kuch change karna jo matter karta hai. **Failure
silent change hai, change nahi.**

**Authority ke 2 chehre:** (1) method kitna AI decide kare (tumne method diya, veto rakha), (2) AI
bina poochhe kya kare (chat mein decisions, baad mein permissions).

| Boundary | Kyun |
| --- | --- |
| Research, lekin purchase mat karo | Paisa wapas lana mushkil |
| Draft, lekin send mat karo | Sending ek promise banati hai |
| Inspect, lekin delete mat karo | Delete evidence khatam karta hai |
| Choose, lekin bolo kab chose kiya | Silent decision review nahi ho sakti |

> Trust **record** se barhta hai — pehli baar har step parho, 5th clean baar sirf plan parho.

## Concept 8: AI Ko Job Dobara Do

Running job har concept par wapas aata hai. Real jobs bhi wapas aate hain — same report har
hafte, same comparison har mahine. **Repetition 2 cheezein maangti hai:**

1. **Context jo retype ho raha hai** — 3rd baar same background paste karo, use **Project** mein
   rakho (knowledge = files jo AI parh sakta hai, standing instructions = rules jo har chat mein
   follow hoti hain). Anthropic ka test: (a) task wapas aata hai? (b) background same hai? (c)
   output shape same hai? 2/3 yes = Project earn karti hai.
2. **Clock** — ChatGPT/Claude scheduled tasks dete hain (paid plans). Brief jo save ki wo
   schedule hogi.

⚠️ **Stored context zimmedari hai** — monthly review karo jo stored hai + jo AI remember karta
hai, jo ghalat ho hata do.

> Jab job tumhari se team ki ban jaye, yeh **workflow** ban gaya — [Workflow Design & Diagnosis](../workflow-design-diagnosis-crash-course/README.md)
> wahan sikhati hai.

## Concept 9: Jab AI Ghalat Ho, Intervene Karo

**6 Intervene moves**, symptom se pata chalta hai kaunsa:

| Jo Dekho | Move |
| --- | --- |
| Alag sawal ka jawab diya | **Stop**, outcome 1 line mein restate karo |
| Ek fact/line ghalat hai | **Correct**: error naam karo, kya hona chahiye batao |
| Ghalat direction, lekin abhi ghalat nahi | **Redirect**: direction naam karo, poora job nahi |
| Job khud ghalat thi | **Change requirement**, batao ke badla |
| Step is level par nahi kar sakta | **Escalate**: thinking on karo, stronger model | 
| Phir bhi fail ho raha | **Job wapas lo** — kuch jobs tumhari rehti hain |

**Escalate** sab se zyada missed move hai — "AI fail ho gaya" ka matlab kabhi hota hai "maine
wrong level use kiya". Free plan par stronger model nahi, to job ko chhota karo (ek step at a
time, checked).

> **Claimed fix ko result mein check karo, AI ke description mein nahi.** "I have corrected the
> table" ek sentence hai. Table dekho.
