# 04 — Part 4: Become the Supervisor (Concepts 10-11)

Loop wahan khatam hoti hai jahan shuru hui — tumhare paas. Verify aur Accept **far end** hain, aur
machine jitni bhi capable ho jaye, **kya check karna hai** aur **accept karna hai ya nahi** tumhara
rehta hai.

## Concept 10: Verify Before You Accept

"Check AI's work" advice hai, method nahi. Yeh method hai — **4 steps: Identify, Trace, Challenge,
Decide.**

**Identify** — kaunsi parts check karne layak hain? Sab kuch nahi — prices, dates, figures,
calculations, quotations, legal/safety claims, recommendation jis par act karoge, reverse karna
mushkil cheezein. **4 Review Thresholds** (ek crossed hona kaafi hai): **Stakes** (ghalti ki cost),
**Reversibility** (undo ho sakta hai?), **Audience** (client/public = zyada), **Regulatory
exposure** (regulated field/data?).

**Trace** — ek claim ke liye: source kholo, supporting sentence dhoondo, date check karo, source
achha hai ya nahi poocho.

**Challenge** — jawab ghalat kaise ho sakta hai? Yeh badal sakta tha? AI secondary source repeat
kar raha? Assumption ko fact ki tarah bola gaya?

**Decide** — 4 decisions: **Accept** (evidence kaafi hai), **Correct** (specific error fix ho
sakta hai), **Investigate** (evidence incomplete/contradictory), **Reject** (result reliable nahi,
job wapas lo).

> **Reject ka doosra form:** same result 3 baar correct kiya, har round pehle se kam improve karta
> hai. Yeh job ke baare mein signal hai, prompt ke baare mein nahi — agla move **field ka insaan**
> hai, behtar message nahi.

⚠️ **4 cheezein jo kabhi bina insaan ke nahi jati:** final client deliverables; audit-critical/
financially-material calculations; regulated/confidential/highly-sensitive data; public/legal
communications. Ek named person har baar parhta hai, chahe run kitna bhi clean laga ho.

> **AI ka kaam karna accountability AI ko transfer nahi karta. Result par naam tumhara hai.**

## Concept 11: Same Job, Different AI

Brief ka test yeh hai ke **kisi aur jagah bhi chale.** Ek doosra free assistant kholo (ya fresh
chat), apna complete brief paste karo, record karo: finish hua? kitne messages? Authority line
follow hui? kuch "unverified" mark hua? deliverable kaisa compare hua?

**Brief travel karta hai kyunke yeh job describe karta hai, tool nahi.** Tumhari 6 lines mein koi
button naam nahi hai. Buttons har assistant mein alag hain, naam har kuch mahine mein badalte hain
— **discipline nahi badalti.**

> Yeh **ek din ke 2 runs ka record hai, 2 assistants ki ranking nahi.** Models bohat fast badalte
> hain. Jab real kaam ke liye assistant choose karna ho, [Which AI Employees 2026](../which-agents-2026/README.md)
> current rehti hai.
