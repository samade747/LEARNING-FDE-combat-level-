# 00 — Overview: Asking Se Delegating Tak

**11 concepts. 1 loop. 1 brief. AI ko job do. Kaam karte dekho.**

Zyada tar log AI ko sawal poochte hain. Jawab acha hota hai, lekin kaam wahi rehta hai — tumhare
paas. Yeh course ulta karta hai: AI ko ek **job** do, kaam karte dekho, result check karo, decide
karo accept karna hai ya nahi. Yeh gyarah baar karte ho, har baar thoda behtar, ek free account ke
sath. Tumhare paas already kaafi AI hai delegating shuru karne ke liye — yeh page sirf **habit**
add karta hai.

## See It in Two Minutes

Koi free AI browser mein kholo. Naya chat shuru karo:

```text
Find three free online courses for learning AI agents, using current
information, and put them in a table with the provider, the language,
the time needed, and a link to each.
```

Jawab parho. Phir ek link khol kar 2 cheezein check karo: kya course asal mein free hai, aur kya
wo language mein hai jo table ne bataya? **3 mein se ek hoga:** page table se match karta hai, page
ek detail pe disagree karta hai, ya link course tak jata hi nahi. **Yehi poora course ek chhote
moment mein hai** — AI ne ek ghante ka kaam ek minute mein kiya, aur ek cheez nahi kar saka: tumhe
batana ke result par trust karna hai ya nahi.

## 11 Ideas, Ek Line Mein

1. Poochna jawab deta hai; job dena ek result deta hai jo tum check kar sakte ho.
2. Jo result chahiye describe karo, steps AI pe chhor do.
3. Delegation Brief 6 sawalon ka jawab deta hai AI shuru karne se pehle.
4. Jab job ko current facts chahiye, AI ko search karwao, aur check karo ke usne kiya.
5. Jab job ko sirf tumhare paas wala source chahiye, dedo, usi ke andar rehne ko kaho.
6. Finished thing maango (table/document), check karo "unverified" marks bachi hain.
7. Batao AI kya khud decide kar sakta hai, kya poochna zaroori hai.
8. Job wapas aaye to jo nahi badalta save karo, AI khud parhe wahan rakho.
9. AI drift kare to sab se chhota message likho jo fix kare, phir brief mein lesson likho.
10. Verification ke 4 steps hain, accountability tumhari rehti hai.
11. Acha brief doosre AI mein bina rewrite chalta hai — kyunke job describe karta hai, tool nahi.

## The Delegation Loop (6 Steps)

> **Define → Delegate → Observe → Intervene → Verify → Accept**

- **Define** — kya result chahiye, bolo (yours)
- **Delegate** — AI ko responsibility do result banane ki (yours)
- **Observe** — AI routine kaam kare, har step direct mat karo (AI's)
- **Intervene** — jab AI drift kare, correct/redirect/constrain karo (AI's, tum step in karte ho)
- **Verify** — claims/numbers/actions jo matter karti hain check karo (yours)
- **Accept** — decide karo result fit hai ya wapas bhejo (yours)

> **AI middle own karta hai. Tum dono ends own karte ho.** Define+Delegate shuru mein, Verify+Accept
> aakhir mein — AI madad kar sakta hai, lekin yeh decisions le nahi sakta.

**Rule jo har control ke liye hai: need first, then tool** — ek control tab seekho jab job usay
maangti hai, pehle nahi.
