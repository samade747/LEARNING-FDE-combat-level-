# 01 — Part 1: Stop Asking. Start Delegating. (Concepts 1-3)

## Concept 1: Asking Se Job Dene Tak

Zyada tar log sawal se shuru karte hain: *"What are some good courses for learning AI agents?"* —
yeh ek **list** deta hai, agle steps tumhare: har course kholna, compare karna, decide karna.

Opener wali job ("Find three free online courses...") ek **table with links** deti hai — AI ne
pehla pass kar diya, baaqi tumhara hai **checking**. Farq length ka nahi — farq yeh hai ke job ne
ek **cheez exist karne ko kaha** (3 courses, table, links). Yehi **delegate karna** hai: AI ko
result banane ki responsibility dena. Paid plan ya special model ki zaroorat nahi — yeh depend
karta hai tum **kaisa sentence** type karte ho.

> **Running job:** apna khud ka subject lo (accounting, Excel, Python, waghera), opener wali job
> dobara type karo apne subject ke sath. Yeh chat poore course mein carry karo — har concept par
> ek cheez add karo.

## Concept 2: AI Ko Outcome Do, Clicks Nahi

Do tareeqe hain ek job handover karne ke: **recipe** (har step batao) vs **outcome** (sirf result
batao). Recipe mein tum sources naam lete ho, exact steps batate ho — agar wo steps weak hain, plan
tumhara tha, result bhi weak hoga, aur result khud nahi batata ke weak kyun hai.

Ek-sentence job sirf **outcome** naam leti hai — AI khud decide karta hai kahan dekhna hai. Tum
result ko **outcome ke against judge karte ho**, apne khud banaye plan ke against nahi.

> Steps ki zaroorat tab hai jab koi source/order **zaroori** ho — yeh Concept 3 mein apni line
> paati hai. "Nervous hone ki wajah se steps likhna" galat hai — yeh feeling check-karne ke waqt ke
> liye hai, shuru ke liye nahi.

**Outcome sentence:** *"When this is done, I will have…"* + 2 limits (jaise "must start this
week", "max 5 hours/week").

## Concept 3: Delegation Brief Likho

Opener ki ek-sentence job **6 labeled lines** mein grow ho gayi:

```text
Outcome: three free online courses for learning AI agents, compared,
with one recommended for a complete beginner.

Context: I have never built or used an AI agent, and I do not know how
to program. I can study about five hours a week.

Constraints: only courses that can be started for free, and only
courses that do not expect programming knowledge. Search for current
information rather than answering from what you already know.

Authority: choose the sources yourself. If two courses are equal, pick
the shorter one without asking me. Say clearly if a course is paid, or
if it expects me to know how to program.

Deliverable: a short table with provider, language, time needed,
whether a certificate costs money, and a link. Then a two-sentence
recommendation.

Verification: mark anything you could not confirm as "unverified"
instead of guessing.
```

**Delegation Brief** = 6 sawal jo AI shuru karne se pehle answer karte ho, taake jab wo khatam ho
tumhare paas usay check karne ki cheez ho. **3 lines naye hain** (baaqi 3 tumne already answer kar
diye the bina label ke):

- **Context** — AI ko kya pata hona chahiye jo wo khud nahi jaan sakta
- **Authority** — AI **khud** kya decide kar sakta hai (chat mein bhi exist karta hai, chahe AI
  bhej/khareed na sake). "Say clearly if a course is paid" authority par ek **limit** hai — iske
  bina AI **silently** decide karta hai aur tumhe pata hi nahi chalta.
- **Verification** — kya check karoge accept karne se pehle, **kaam shuru hone se pehle** likha
  gaya. Pehle likhna result parhne ko checking mein badal deta hai.

> **Yeh thinking tool hai, form nahi.** Koi 6 boxes fill nahi karta 2-line job ke liye. Lekin jab
> bhi result disappoint kare, in 6 mein se ek sawal unanswered tha — ab tum bata sakte ho kaunsa.

**6 Lines → AI Fluency Competencies mapping:**

| Brief Line | Competency |
| --- | --- |
| Outcome | Delegation + Description |
| Context | Description |
| Constraints | Description + Diligence |
| Authority | Delegation + Diligence |
| Deliverable | Description |
| Verification | Discernment + Diligence |
