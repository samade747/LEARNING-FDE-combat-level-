# 07 — Test Your Understanding (12-Question Assessment)

Book ke apne `<Quiz>` block se verbatim (English, precision ke liye).

1. **Q:** Sana types 'What are the best free tools for making a CV?' and gets a good list. She then opens each tool, compares them, and picks one. Which step did she keep that she could have delegated?
   **A: The comparison itself**, because the question returned advice and a job asking for a table with links would have moved it. *(1. From Asking AI to Giving AI the Job)*
2. **Q:** Bilal writes a recipe-style job (go to two sites, open ten listings, average the salary). The assistant returns a neat table. What is the most likely problem?
   **A: He gave a recipe, so the result is only as good as the two sites he chose, and nothing in the result tells him so.** *(2. Give AI the Outcome, Not the Clicks)*
3. **Q:** A brief has outcome, context, constraints, deliverable — and the result recommends a paid course without saying so. Which missing line caused this?
   **A: Authority** — "say clearly if a course is paid" is a limit on authority; without it AI decided silently. *(3. Write the Delegation Brief)*
4. **Q:** Aisha's result lists three courses with confident descriptions and no links or dates. What does this tell her?
   **A: The assistant probably answered from what it remembered rather than from a search** — she should not assume it looked. *(4. Give AI Something to Research)*
5. **Q:** Usman pastes a notice and asks three questions. One answer describes a deadline not in the notice. What did the brief lack?
   **A: "Use only the text below" and "quote the sentence you used"**, so that "not in the source" becomes the right answer. *(5. Give AI the Source)*
6. **Q:** A chat answer carried two "unverified" marks. The one-page document made from it carries none. What happened?
   **A: Deliverables lose caveats** — find the two marks and put them back before the document leaves the chat. *(6. Ask for the Deliverable, Not an Answer)*
7. **Q:** Hira asks for a plan first, approves it, then finds the result used a source not in the plan. Which loop step is this, and the right response?
   **A: Observe and Intervene** — every difference between the approved plan and what happened is a question to ask. *(7. Decide How Much of the Job AI Owns)*
8. **Q:** For the third week running, Omar pastes the same two paragraphs of background. What is this a sign of?
   **A: The context belongs in a Project or a saved note** — the third time you paste the same background, put it somewhere AI reads by itself. *(8. Give AI the Job Again)*
9. **Q:** A result is wrong in one figure. The reader replies "Redo the whole thing." Which move would the course prefer?
   **A: Correct** — name the error and what should replace it; the smallest message that fixes it is the intervention. *(9. Intervene When AI Goes Wrong)*
10. **Q:** Zara's result has fourteen facts. She has ten minutes. What does the Identify step tell her to do?
    **A: Check the ones that matter most** — prices, dates, the recommendation she will act on. *(10. Verify Before You Accept)*
11. **Q:** The assistant cited a page for a claim. Ali opened the page and found it says something slightly different. Which decision fits?
    **A: Correct or Investigate** — a citation is not verification, and the sentence did not support the claim. *(10. Verify Before You Accept)*
12. **Q:** Maryam ran the same brief in two assistants and writes "Assistant A is better." What does the course say?
    **A: She has an observation about two runs on one day, not a ranking** — it belongs in her Delegation Record as such. *(11. Same Job, Different AI)*
