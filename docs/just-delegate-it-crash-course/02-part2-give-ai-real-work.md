# 02 — Part 2: Give AI Real Work (Concepts 4-6)

Part 1 ne Delegation Loop 3 baar chalaya bina naam liye. **Need first, then tool** rule: control
tab seekho jab job usay maange.

| Job Ko Kya Chahiye | Toh Tum | Kahan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current information | Maango, web search on karo | Concept 4 |
| Sirf tumhare paas wala source | Paste karo, ya file attach karo | Concept 5 |
| Kuch private job mein | Private parts hatao, temporary/incognito chat use karo | Concept 5 |
| Finished thing, answer nahi | Document/table/file khud maango | Concept 6 |
| Apna method choose karne ki jagah | Batao kya khud decide kare, kya poochhe | Concept 7 |
| Har baar same context | Project mein knowledge+standing instructions | Concept 8 |
| Same job clock par | Scheduled task (optional) | Concept 8 |
| Jo capability dikhayi usse zyada | Thinking on karo, stronger model | Concept 9 |

## Concept 4: AI Ko Research Karne Do

AI ki 2 tareeqe jaanne ke: **remember** karta hai (jab bana tha, purana) ya **search** karta hai
(abhi). Price/date/link/exist-karta-hai-ya-nahi ke liye **sirf search** kaam karegi.

**Pehchaan:** jo fact **search se aaya** usay source milti hai jo khol sako. Jo fact **remembered**
hai wo alone aata hai, lekin equally confident lagta hai. 2 cautions: current claim bina source ke
aaye to maan mat lo ke AI ne dekha; source date nahi hai, page khud khol kar date dhoondo.

> **"Using current information"** likhna AI ko batata hai: remember mat karo, dekho.

> **Ek citation verification nahi hai. Citation ko check karna verification hai.**

## Concept 5: AI Ko Source Do

Kuch jobs ko wo cheez chahiye jo AI search nahi kar sakta — syllabus, office notice, message
thread, policy page. Tumhare paas hai, AI ke paas nahi. **Tum dete ho.**

⚠️ **Paste karne se pehle:** naam, phone numbers, account numbers, kuch bhi jo public notice board
par nahi lagaoge — hatao. Dono assistants ek **temporary/incognito chat** dete hain (history+memory
se bahar), lekin yeh paste **gayab nahi** karti (provider kuch waqt ke liye copy rakhta hai).

```text
Using only the text below, answer three questions: what will I be able
to do at the end, how many hours a week does it expect, and is any
previous knowledge required? For each answer, quote the sentence you
used. If the text does not say, write "not in the source".

[paste the text here]
```

**2 instructions jo source ko source banati hain:** "Using only the text below" + "quote the
sentence you used". Bina inke, AI pasted text ko apni memory se mix kar deta hai. Inke sath, "not
in the source" ek **acha jawab** ban jata hai, gap nahi jo AI fill kar de.

## Concept 6: Deliverable Maango, Answer Nahi

Ek job jo chat message mein khatam hoti hai asal job nahi hai — real job ek **cheez** mein khatam
hoti hai jo koi use kar sake.

> Turn this into a one-page document I could send to a friend... Make
> it a file I can download if you can. Keep every "unverified" mark
> exactly where it is.

**Answer** tumhe reply karta hai. **Deliverable** shaped hai taake koi usay use/save/hand-on/act-
on kare — table, document, spreadsheet, file.

⚠️ **Deliverables caveats kho dete hain.** "Unverified" mark jo 3 chat replies survive kiya, tidy
document mein gayab ho sakta hai (tidy hi document ka point hai). **Count karo caveats chat answer
mein, phir document mein — agar doosra number chhota hai, dhoondo kya drop hua, wapas rakho.**
