# Job 1 — A Purchase You Are Actually Going to Make

**Time:** ~15 min · **Tool:** any free AI assistant

## Steps

1. Yeh brief fill karo (apna product/budget/country/currency):
   ```text
   Outcome: the two best options for [a phone / a laptop / a course
   subscription] under [your budget], compared, with one recommended.
   Context: [what you will use it for, and what you already own].
   Constraints: available to buy in [your country] this month. Prices in
   [your currency], with the date of each price.
   Authority: choose the sources. Do not recommend anything above the
   budget without saying so.
   Deliverable: a table with price, where sold, and a link, then a
   two-sentence recommendation.
   Verification: mark any price you could not confirm as "unverified".
   ```
2. Fresh chat mein run karo.
3. **Verify:** ek price trace karo — source khol kar confirm karo.

## Done Jab

- [ ] Table mein 2 options, price+source+link ke sath
- [ ] Kam az kam ek price trace ki aur confirm ki (ya "unverified" mark pakdi)
