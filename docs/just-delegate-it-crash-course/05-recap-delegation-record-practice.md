# 05 — Recap, Delegation Record, aur Practice

## Chhoti Recap (11 Concepts)

1. Poochna jawab deta hai aur kaam rakhta hai. Job dena result deta hai aur checking chhorta hai.
2. Batao job khatam hone par kya exist karna chahiye. Clicks machine pe chhoro.
3. Brief 6 sawal hain jo AI shuru karne se pehle jawab dete ho: outcome, context, constraints,
   authority, deliverable, verification.
4. Jab job ko aaj ka sach chahiye, AI ko dekhne do, aur check karo ke dekha.
5. Source do, usi ke andar rehne ko kaho, aur check karo ke raha.
6. Aakhir mein kya hold karna hai naam karo. Check karo caveats survive hue.
7. Batao AI kya alone decide kar sakta, kya poochna zaroori. Plan pehle maango.
8. Doosri baar job wapas aaye, jo nahi badalta save karo. Teesri baar, wahan rakho jahan AI khud
   parhe.
9. Sab se chhota message jo fix kare, wahi intervention hai. Phir brief mein lesson likho.
10. Identify, trace, challenge, decide. AI ka kaam karna accountability transfer nahi karta.
11. Job tumhara hai. Model replaceable hai.

Sab 11 ke neeche ek rhythm hai: **define, delegate, observe, intervene, verify, accept** — aur ek
screen rule: **need first, then tool**.

> **Yaad rakhne layak ek sentence:** *Give AI the job. Watch it work. Check what matters. Then
> decide.*

```text
Where it drifted, and the move that fixed it:

THE CHECK
Claim 1 that mattered:            Source opened:         Finding:
Claim 2 that mattered:            Source opened:         Finding:
Anything AI marked "unverified":

THE DECISION
Accept / Correct / Investigate / Reject, and the one sentence behind it:

THE SECOND RUN (Concept 11)
Assistant:            Finished?    Brief changed?    Where the results differed:

WHAT I WILL SAVE FOR NEXT TIME (Concept 8)
Lines that did not change:
```

Agar koi line empty hai, yeh bhi ek finding hai — matlab loop ka koi step abhi is job ke liye nahi
hua.

## Quick Self-Check (10 Sawal)

1. Asking aur job dene ka farq kya hai?
2. Delegation Loop ke 6 steps naam lo, kaunse tumhare rehte hain?
3. Outcome describe karna steps dictate karne se behtar kyun hai?
4. Delegation Brief ki 6 lines naam lo — log kaunsi 2 skip karte hain?
5. Fact search se aaya ya memory se, kaise pata chale?
6. Kaunsi 2 instructions pasted text ko "source" banati hain?
7. Job fix karne ka sabse cheap moment kaunsa hai, wahan kaise pahunchte ho?
8. Repetition kya maangti hai, Project mein context jaane ka signal kya hai?
9. Intervene ke 6 moves naam lo — kaunsa log miss karte hain?
10. Verification ke 4 steps aur 4 decisions kya hain?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Poochna jawab deta hai, agle steps tumhare. Job dena ek cheez exist karne ko kehta hai, checking
   chhorta hai.
2. Define, delegate, observe, intervene, verify, accept. Dono ends tumhare: define+delegate shuru
   mein, verify+accept aakhir mein.
3. Recipe result ko tumhare plan ki quality tak limit karti hai, aur AI tumhe nahi bata sakta ke
   plan narrow tha. Outcome AI ko method choose karne deta hai.
4. Outcome, context, constraints, authority, deliverable, verification. Authority aur Verification
   log skip karte hain.
5. Found fact source ke sath aata hai jo khol sako. Remembered fact alone aata hai, equally sure
   lagta hai.
6. "Use only the text below" aur "quote the sentence you used".
7. Run se pehle. Plan maango, go-ahead ka wait karo, phir ek step badlo.
8. Context jo retype ho raha hai, aur baad mein ek clock. 3rd baar same background paste karo —
   Project mein jana chahiye.
9. Stop, correct, redirect, change the requirement, escalate capability, job wapas lo. Escalate
   sab se zyada missed hai.
10. Identify, trace, challenge, decide. Accept, correct, investigate, reject.

</details>
