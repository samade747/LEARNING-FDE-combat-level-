# 01 — What This Is

## 8:07 A.M. Ki Kahani

Ek project manager report mein peeche hai. Ek finance lead un systems ke beech numbers reconcile kar
rahi hai jo aapas mein baat nahi karte. Ek team us jawab ka wait kar rahi hai jo kal aana chahiye
tha. Ab socho har ek ne woh kaam ek **tireless digital coworker** ko de diya — jo instructions
follow karta hai, wahi tools use karta hai jo woh khud use karte hain, apna kaam khud check karta hai,
aur wapis woh cheez deta hai jis par bharosa kiya ja sake. **Yeh coworker banana aur direct karna hi
yeh book sikhati hai.**

## Zaroori Alfaz

Poori book chaar alfaz par khari hai:

- **AI Worker** (ya **Digital FTE**) — ek AI jo asal job karta hai, sirf sawal ka jawab nahi deta.
  FTE ka matlab "full-time equivalent" — HR ki term ek employee ke barabar kaam ke liye. Socho ek
  naya hire jo kabhi sota nahi: aap bataate ho kya karna hai, woh karta hai, aur ek insan phir bhi
  sign-off karta hai.
- **General agent** — woh all-purpose assistant jise aap direct karte ho, jaise Claude Code, Claude
  Cowork, ya ChatGPT. Aap isay ya to apna **kaam** karwane ke liye use karte ho, ya ek Digital FTE
  **banane** ke liye.
- **AI-Native Company** — jab ek founder ek asal business chalata hai chand logon aur bohat sari
  AI Workers ke sath, badi staff ki jagah.

Bas itni si baat hai. Baqi sab is idea ko **acha karne ka tareeqa** hai.

Yeh book chatbot tricks, demos, ya short-lived prototypes ke baare mein nahi. Yeh **dependable AI
workers** banane ke baare mein hai jo asal business operations mein participate kar saken. Yeh
systems human judgment ki jagah nahi leti — woh usay extend karti hain, scale karti hain, aur
repeatable banati hain.

> **Terminology note:** *Digital FTE*, *Digital Worker*, aur *AI Worker* is book mein
> interchangeably use hote hain. Thesis technical term ke tor par *AI Worker* use karta hai; yeh
> book business-facing term ke tor par *Digital FTE* use karti hai.

## Five-Layer Cake of AI

Jensen Huang (NVIDIA ke CEO) ka metaphor: modern AI ek **five-layer cake** ki tarah bani hai.

1. **Energy** — data centers ko power dena.
2. **Chips** — specialized processors, trillions of calculations per second.
3. **Infrastructure** — global network of supercomputers aur cloud platforms.
4. **Models** — neural networks jo seekhte, reason karte, aur generate karte hain.
5. **Applications** — jahan AI technology hona chhod kar **useful** ban jaati hai.

Neeche waali chaar layers mein arabon dollars is liye invest hote hain taake yeh paanchwi layer
exist kar sake. **Yeh book isi paanchwi layer ke baare mein hai.** Yeh sikhati hai ke woh
applications, agents, aur digital workers kaise banayein jo AI capability ko un products,
workflows, aur value mein badalte hain jo organizations use aur capture karti hain.

Agli competitive gap sirf "kis ke paas best model hai" se nahi decide hogi. Woh decide hogi is se ke
**kaun intelligence ko repeatable execution mein badal sakta hai.** Jis tarah software ne manual
processes ko digital systems mein badla, Digital FTEs structured knowledge work ko scalable
operational capability mein badlenge.

## Core Idea

**Digital FTEs** reliable AI agents hain jo structured knowledge work continuously, real
organizations ke andar perform karte hain. Ek Digital FTE sirf "model + prompt" nahi hai — yeh ek
system hai jo char cheezein combine karta hai:

- **Structured Specifications** — kya karna hai, uski clear definition.
- **Domain Expertise** — "knowledge engine" jo reasoning aur decisions guide karta hai.
- **Engineering Architecture** — infrastructure jo reliability aur scalability yaqeeni banati hai.
- **Human Oversight** — feedback loops jo accountability aur governance maintain karte hain.

Digital FTEs sirf technical construct nahi — yeh **economic** construct hain. Yeh organizations ko
expertise package karne, execution bottlenecks kam karne, consistency behtar karne, aur naye
revenue streams banane deti hain.

## Yeh Book Kyun Exist Karti Hai

Zyadatar organizations AI ko **isolated experiments** ke through approach karti hain — ek prototype
yahan, ek chatbot wahan, ek demo jo kabhi daily operations mein nahi pohanchta. **Missing cheez
excitement nahi — method hai.**

Yeh book yeh method sikhati hai: valuable AI employee opportunities identify karna, expert
knowledge ko structured specifications mein badalna, bounded agent workflows design karna, unhein
reliable cloud-native infrastructure par deploy karna, aur unhein human oversight se govern karna.
Yeh process **spec-driven** (pehle clear specification likho, phir AI ko us par build karwao) aur
**human-supervised** hai, aur do agent tools se demonstrate hoti hai: **Claude Code** (Anthropic ka
frontier coding agent) aur **OpenCode** (open-source, model-agnostic alternative). Skills,
specifications, aur architectural patterns jo ek ke liye likhe jayein, doosre mein bhi chalte hain.
**Method constant hai. Tool variable hai.**

Doosri taraf bhi ek shortage hai: developers jo yeh gap band kar sakte the, unhein work milna mushkil
ho raha hai kyunke routine implementation har quarter sasti ho rahi hai. Ek book dono shortages ka
jawab de sakti hai kyunke woh do alag mauqon se dekha gaya same problem hai.
