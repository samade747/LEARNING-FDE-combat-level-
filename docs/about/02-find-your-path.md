# 02 — Find Your Path

Sab log **wahi chhoti ladder** chadhte hain, aur kisi bhi rung par ruk sakte hain.

## Char Rungs

**1. Foundations — yahan se shuru karo.** Ek handful chhote courses, browser mein. ChatGPT, Claude,
ya Gemini use kar sakte ho — kuch install nahi karna. Yeh skills har kisi ko chahiye — doctor,
accountant, student, engineer, sab yehi pehle lete hain.

**2. Mode 1 — apna kaam tez karne ke liye AI use karo.** Basics ke baad, aap apne real tasks par AI
lagate ho: writing, analysis, planning, code. Aap "doer" rehte ho, aur AI aapka power tool hai.
Zyadatar log yahan bohat value paate hain aur yahin ruk jaate hain.

**3. Mode 2 — AI Workers banao jo aapke liye kaam karein.** Aage badh kar, aap AI use karte ho un
tireless coworkers ko **banane** ke liye. Yeh Workers laptop band karne ke baad bhi kaam karte
rehte hain. Ab aap builder ho, sirf doer nahi.

**4. Ladder ke top ka ek naam hai.** AI Workers banana seekho aur unhein ek company mein combine
karo jo unhi par chalti hai. Ab aap woh insan ban chuke ho jise job market top salaries de kar dhoond
rahi hai: **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)**. Ek FDE ek organization mein jata hai aur uski poori
AI workforce end-to-end banata hai. [Roles This Book Trains](../roles-this-book-trains/README.md)
is insan ko poora define karta hai.

Poori book ek line mein: *aap* **AI Workers** banate ho, aur Workers milkar ek **AI-Native Company**
banate hain.

**_Mode 1_** ek general agent use karta hai session ke andar ek problem solve karne ke liye.
**_Mode 2_** ek general agent use karta hai ek custom AI Worker manufacture karne mein madad ke
liye jo session khatam hone ke baad bhi chal sake.

Poori ladder chadhna zaroori nahi. Foundations plus Mode 1 apne aap mein ek serious skill set hai.
[Getting Started](../getting-started) aapko course-by-course upar le jata hai.

## Orientation Se Shuru Karo

Naya ho? Pehle short orientation dekho — yeh chand minutes mein core idea deti hai, aur ek baar yeh
click ho jaye to har agla chapter parhna aasan ho jata hai. Iske baad [the Thesis](../thesis/README.md)
parho — us vocabulary ke liye jis par baqi poori book khadi hai: Digital FTE, AI-Native Company,
Two-Layer Model, 10-80-10 Rule. Wahan se, **Getting Started: Crash Courses** poora raasta bata deta
hai. Foundations pehle (achi entry point hai *AI Prompting in 2026*), phir aapka mode, phir woh
courses jo us mode se match karte hain. Yehi wahi 10-80-10 rhythm hai jo book sikhati hai — apply
kiya gaya khud is book ko seekhne par: thesis intent set karti hai, courses execution carry karte
hain, aur aapka professional judgment loop close karta hai.
