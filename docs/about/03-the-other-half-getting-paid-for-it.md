# 03 — The Other Half: Getting Paid For It

## Do Sachai, Ek Waqt Mein

**Developers ko kaam nahi mil raha.** Agents ab routine code ka barhta hua hissa likh rahe hain,
junior roles patle ho rahe hain, aur freelance boards capable generalists se bhare hain jo ek doosre
se aur khud model se compete kar rahe hain. Asal sawal "mujhe job kaise mile?" nahi hai — asal sawal
hai: **"jab intelligence aur code sasta ho jaye, to main asal mein contribute kya karta hoon?"**

**Companies AI se kaam nahi bana pa rahin.** Woh har jagah AI projects shuru kar rahi hain, aur
taqreeban **95% custom enterprise pilots** koi measurable financial return nahi dikhate (MIT,
2025). Models weak nahi hain. Masla yeh hai ke koi bhi unhein company ke real data, real rules, real
approvals, aur real logon mein fit nahi karta. Yehi wahi masla hai jo pichle part mein "Why This
Book Exists" ne bataya. **Missing cheez excitement nahi, method hai.**

Ab yeh do sentences ek doosre ke bagal mein rakho — yehi is book ka maqsad hai. Ek taraf capable log
hain jinka koi raasta andar nahi. Doosri taraf real paisa hai aur koi raasta through nahi. In donon
ke darmiyan faasla na talent shortage hai na technology shortage — yeh ek **deployment gap** hai, aur
uski width bilkul ek job ke barabar hai.

## Deployment Gap Ka Pul

Ek developer akela ek dark cliff ke kinare khara hai jabke ek lit bridge deployment gap ke paar ek
working office ki taraf jata hai — jahan "Vendor-Neutral Vertical FDE" ka pillar do governed
Systems of Record thaame khada hai, aur teen outcomes wait kar rahe hain: job, freelance, startup.

Book ka technical hissa aapko yeh job karna sikhata hai. Business hissa aapko **iska paisa lena**
sikhata hai, kyunke jo skill koi khareedta nahi woh hobby hai. Woh hissa ek series hai — market se
shuru hoti hai, aapke pehle invoice par khatam hoti hai, aur "iska paisa kaise banega?" ko motivational
nahi, **engineering sawal** ki tarah treat karti hai.

Capstone ek argument par khatam hota hai — jo upar wali chaar survival skills ka **commercial form**
hai. Jab intelligence aur code sasta ho jaaye, market **kam** deta hai us cheez ke liye jo aap kar
sakte ho — kyunke zyada rivals bhi kar sakte hain. Market pay karta hai us cheez ke liye jo aap
**hold** karte ho. Isliye series aapko kuch aisa banane mein madad karti hai jise aap hold kar sakein:
ek profession ka governed knowledge, ek jurisdiction mein, ek real expert ke sath banaya gaya.

## Teen Routes

**Route 01 — The Job 💼:** FDE ke tor par apply karo, aisa portfolio le kar jo kisi aur candidate ke
paas nahi. Wahan se shuru karo jahan tenure gate nahi — independent services firms, aur clients jo
pehle se aapka kaam dekh chuke hon.

**Route 02 — The Open Market 🌐:** Marketplace category pehle se maujood hai, aur zyadatar log
generalists hain. Is route ka advantage: labour market local nahi — wahi contract Karachi, Lagos, ya
Bangalore se jeeta ja sakta hai. Projects monthly retainers ban jaate hain.

**Route 03 — Your Own Startup 🚀:** Vertical ek domain startup ban jata hai jise aap apne expert ke
sath co-own karte ho: expert twin, domain products jo ek baar bante hain aur baar baar bikte hain,
aur engagements jo retainers ban jaati hain.

Sabse honest version sabse aage jaata hai. Zyadatar readers pehle governed knowledge systems aur AI
Workers clients ke liye bana kar kamayenge — is ke liye kisi domain expert ya apne vertical ki
zaroorat nahi, aur yeh sabse jaldi paisa deta hai. Apna vertical business bara prize hai aur mushkil
gate — woh gate ek **insan** hai, koi skill nahi: ek senior professional jo aapke sath yeh banane
ko tayyar ho.

**Technical hissa aapko workforce banana sikhata hai. Business hissa aapko kuch own karna sikhata
hai.** Dono ek hi source se aate hain aur sath parhe jaane chahiye.
