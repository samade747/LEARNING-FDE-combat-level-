## Yeh Chapter Kis Baare Mein Hai

Yeh poori book ka **entry point** hai — yahan se pata chalta hai ke "The AI Agent Factory" asal
mein hai kya. Short jawab: yeh ek **canonical source** (authoritative master version) hai us
tareeqe ke liye jisse **Digital FTEs** — role-based AI employees — banayi jati hain, aur unse
**AI-Native Companies** khari ki jati hain. Yeh sirf ek book nahi, balke ek pura ecosystem hai: book
khud, ek AI tutor (Zia Tutor AI), ek AI building partner (Zia Developer AI), aur derivative books ka
ek growing family.

Chapter yeh bhi samjhata hai ke jab AI intelligence sasti ho rahi hai, to insaan ke paas kaunse 4
skills bachte hain jo hamesha scarce rahenge — aur woh 10-80-10 Rule se kaise judte hain, jo aage
poori book mein baar baar aayega. Phir yeh batata hai ke yeh book kis ke liye hai, kaise parhi jaye,
aur "getting paid" ka business side kya hai — kyunke sirf building sikhna kaafi nahi, uska paisa
kaise banta hai yeh bhi zaroori hai.

> **practice** hai jo aap adopt karte hain, aur us practice ka naam hai: **spec-driven, human-supervised**
> tareeqa jisse AI Workers manufacture hoti hain.
