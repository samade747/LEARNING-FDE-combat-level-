# 00 — Four Skills to Survive the Agent Era

> **Rokti hai galti:** "Maine socha AI se tez rehne ke liye mujhe zyada smart banna hai" — jabke asal
> sawal yeh nahi hai. Sawal hai: **jab intelligence sasti ho jaye, to mere paas kya reh jata hai jo
> koi bhi cheen nahi sakta?**

## Race Jo Jeeti Nahi Ja Sakti

AI ab zyada se zyada desk work mein zyada insano se behtar perform kar raha hai. Yeh koi prediction
nahi — yeh **abhi ka reality** hai, aur trend sirf ek hi taraf jata hai. Isliye honest sawal ab
"main AI se zyada smart kaise banu?" nahi raha. Woh race jeeti nahi ja sakti. Asal sawal hai: **jab
intelligence khud sasti ho jaye, to insan ko kya chahiye?**

Jawab hai — chaar skills. Kyun yeh chaar hi, koi aur nahi? Kyunke har ek ek hi test paas karti hai:
**yeh scarce reh jati hai jab intelligence sasti ho jaye.** AI likhne, analysis, code, aur design —
sab mein execution ki cost zero ki taraf le ja raha hai. Jo woh nahi gira sakta woh hai: **kya
banana hai** iska faisla, AI ko direct karne ka **hunar**, result **sach hai ya nahi** yeh judge
karne ki **samajh**, aur logon ke darmiyan **trust**. Jo bhi skill AI absorb kar sakta hai, woh kar
lega — aur uski qeemat gir jayegi. Yeh chaar skills wahan khare hain jahan yeh collapse pohanch
nahi sakta.

Teeno pehli skills ek loop banati hain: wahi **10-80-10 Rule** jo is book ke har chapter mein
guzarta hai. Chothi skill us loop se bahar hai — koi chapter usay sikha nahi sakta.

## Skill 01 — Pehla 10%: Direction Set Karna 🎯

Yeh faisla karna ke banana kya hai. Goal ko frame karna, aur apni niyat (intent) ko aisi
specification mein badalna jise AI execute kar sake. AI taqreeban kuch bhi execute kar sakta hai.
Insaan yeh decide karte hain ke worth karne wala kya hai.

Is book mein: [the Thesis](../thesis/README.md), spec-driven development, aur har specification
jo aap likhenge.

## Skill 02 — Beech Ka 80%: AI Ko Orchestrate Karna 🤖

Yeh sirf chatbot ko prompt karna nahi hai. Aap poori jobs AI agents ko delegate karte hain aur
unhein ek team ki tarah manage karte hain. General agents drive karna, AI Workers manufacture
karna, execution layer chalana — yeh dahai ki sabse zyada demand wali skill hai.

Is book mein: crash courses, poora factory floor.

## Skill 03 — Aakhri 10%: Sach Judge Karna ⚖️

AI jo produce kare usko validate karna. Har cheez ko sawal karna — is book ko bhi. Jab machines
duniya ka zyada tar content generate kar rahi hon, to jo insan sach ko plausible se alag pehchan
sakta hai, uske paas woh **veto** hai jo matter karta hai.

Is book mein: har workflow ka aakhri 10% — verification, evaluation, aur AI era mein critical
thinking.

## Skill 04 — Loop Se Bahar: Logon Se Connect Hona 🤝

Communication, compassion, doosre insan ko "dekha jaana" mehsoos karana. AI yeh sab simulate kar
sakta hai, lekin simulate ki gayi "dekhe jaane ki feeling" wohi cheez nahi jo asal mein dekha jaana
hai. Connection ki qeemat hi yeh hai ke woh **genuine** hai, aur genuine woh cheez hai jo simulation
kabhi ban nahi sakti — model chahe kitna hi acha ho jaye. Jab execution sasta ho jaye, to logon ke
darmiyan trust hi woh scarce asset ban jata hai jis par har business chalta hai.

**Is book mein nahi**, kyunke koi chapter yeh sikha nahi sakta. Aapka ghar, aapki classroom, aur
aapki team — yeh isay train karte hain.

## Yeh Chaar Skills Ek Team Banati Hain

Teen skills 10-80-10 Rule hain. Chothi, human connection, loop se bahar hai aur yeh batati hai
ke company mein insaan sirey se kyun rehte hain. Milkar, yeh chaar skills ek **human-agent team**
describe karti hain: insaan loop ke edges par direction set karte hain aur results judge karte hain.
Agents beech mein execution carry karte hain. Insaan woh trust rakhte hain jo koi agent carry nahi
kar sakta.

Yeh book loop train karti hai. Aapki zindagi chothi skill train karti hai. Chaaron directions mein
build karo — idealistic hone ki wajah se nahi, balke isliye ke milkar yeh akela durable advantage
bacha hai.
