# 04 — Ship

## Concept 13: Deploy — Folder Se URL Tak

**[Vercel](https://vercel.com/)** free plan seekhne aur client ko dikhane ke liye kaafi hai, AI seedha
control kar sakta hai:

```text
Connect to Vercel via the CLI and deploy this project.
```

**2 zaroori commands:**
- `vercel` — **preview**, temporary address, client ko *"ye dekho"* ke liye
- `vercel --prod` — **real** address, business ki apni

**3 aadatein:** Previews changes ke liye, real address finished cheezon ke liye. **Final checks live
page pe karo**, apni machine pe nahi. Netlify/Cloudflare Pages bhi same kaam karte hain.

**Apna web address:** `.vercel.app` se koi client khush nahi hota. Address kharido, Vercel settings mein
add karo, seller ke DNS mein values copy karo.

**2 professional aadatein:** **Client ke naam pe kharido**, unke apne account mein. **Findability karo:**
real page title, ek sentence description, **sharing card** (`og.png`, 1200×630px — WhatsApp link ka
card).

## Concept 14: Client Workflow

**6 steps** (Ayesha Rehman, Lahore accountant, example ke sath):

1. **Likhit mein agree karo banane se pehle** — kis ke liye, action, story beats, "finished" ka matlab
2. **Brand dhoondo, phir confirm karo** — online jo mila wo draft hai, client jo bheje wo final
3. **Kahani becho, finished picture nahi** — story-first client substance pe feedback deta hai; page-first
   client "blue ka exact shade" pe atak jata hai
4. **Client se apne phone pe review karwao** — screenshots nahi, preview link, *"phone pe kholo"*
5. **4 boxes se price karo** — Model (~kuch nahi), Taste (aapka time, main line), Media (honest
   conversation), Hosting (free abhi, unke account mein handover ke waqt)
6. **Sahi tareeqe se handover karo** — files, published account, address unke naam, `design.md`, stats

> **Compound move:** Pehle client ke baad, poora process **skill file** ki tarah save karo. Doosra
> client aadhi mehnat mein.

## Concept 15: Cost Discipline

**Pictures pehle price karo:**
```text
Before making any picture or video, estimate the cost of every
planned shot and tell me the total.
```
> **Surprise:** Kimi K3 build pe, **poori website ka code** chai ki keemat se kam hai. **Ek custom video
> baaki sab kuch se zyada mehnga ho sakta hai.**

**Doosri company ka AI conversation costs wapas laata hai:** Kimi K3 pehle-parhe text pe ~10x kam charge
karta hai — discount tabhi tak jab tak conversation ka shuru wala hissa unchanged rahe. Restart karo to
poori keemat wapas.

**Ek complete website ki asal keemat likho:** Ayesha ka site kuch dollars AI time + zero/kam picture
budget hota hai. *"Kya main isay practice kar sakta hoon?"* se *"haan, har hafte"* ban jata hai.
