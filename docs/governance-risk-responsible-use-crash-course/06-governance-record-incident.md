# 06 — Governance Record + Jab Kuch Galat Ho

## Governance Record: Ek Page Jo Meeting Survive Kare

**4 questions** zehan mein useful hain. Organization ke liye useful ban jate jab answers likhi
jati hain.

**Governance Record** = ek workflow ke liye ek page. Now-do-it blocks fill kiye to iske 5 parts
mein se 4 already exist karte. 5th part **Evidence line** hai (Concept 5.5 mein dekha): success
measure, failure threshold, kaun monitor karta aur kitni baar, residual risk.

**Blank field guessed field se behtar hai.** Owner ya route pata nahi to `OPEN QUESTION` likho,
sahi insaan ko bhejo.

## Jab Kuch Galat Ho

Governance yeh promise nahi hai ke koi mistake nahi karega — **mistakes ko early enough surface
karne ki ability hai unhe contain karne ke liye.**

**Common incident types:**

- **Data:** info aisi route gayi jo uske liye approved nahi thi
- **Output:** AI-assisted output serious error ya harmful framing ke sath bahar gaya
- **Action:** AI tool ne send/change/delete/approve/share kiya jo nahi karna chahiye tha
- **Security:** untrusted content ne AI system ko unintended tareeqe se steer kiya
- **Fairness:** ranking/screening process ne logon ko disadvantage kiya lagta hai, review catch
  nahi kar saki

## First-Hour Procedure

1. **Spread rokо.** Forward/repost/unnecessary new copies mat banao.
2. **Facts record karo.** Kya hua, kaunsa data/output/action involved tha, kaunsi route, kab, kuch
   onward gaya ya nahi.
3. **Promptly organization ke incident path se report karo.** Sensitive/regulated cases mein
   timing legally matter kar sakti — perfect explanation ka wait mat karo.
4. **Facts plainly state karo.** Guess mat karo, defend mat karo. Incident owner ko reliable
   account chahiye.
5. **Incident owner ki instructions follow karo.** Deletion/notification/disclosure/evidence-
   preservation khud decide mat karo — legal/contractual/security/forensic consequences ho sakte.

> **Instinct (mat karo):** delete kar do aur kisi ko mat batao; wait karo kuch hota hai ya nahi;
> check karo kisi ne notice kiya ya nahi. **Yeh mistake ko concealment banata, aur data hatae
> bina tumhari visibility hatata.**

**Near misses bhi report karo.** Near miss batata process confusing hai ya approved route bohot
hard hai — full incident ki cost ke bina.

> **Managers ke liye:** organization pehli good-faith-mistake-report-karne-wale insaan ke sath
> kaise behave karti, yehi decide karta agle 10 mistakes report hongi ya chhupayi jayengi.
> Accountability matter karti, aur usable reporting path bhi.

**Delete karna copy hatata, data nahi — mistake ko concealment banata.**
