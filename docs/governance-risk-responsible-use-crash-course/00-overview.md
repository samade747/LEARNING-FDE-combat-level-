# 00 — Overview: Governance, Risk & Responsible Use

## Ek Ordinary Tuesday Se Shuru

Logistics company ka AI ke sath achha saal guzra. Operations reporting time kaat dete, marketing
tez campaigns draft karti, finance routine matching automate karta. Tools ordinary ban jate, log
har use ko "AI decision" treat karna band kar dete.

Phir ek project manager customer spreadsheet (names+account numbers ke sath) AI chat mein daal
deti jo wo har din use karti. Rule todne ki niyat nahi — director ke sawal ka lunch se pehle
jawab de rahi hai.

**Company AI use freeze kar deti jab tak investigate kare.** Jin teams ne kuch galat nahi kiya,
unke workflows chhin jate — kyunke ek ordinary decision ne risk banaya jo organization ignore
nahi kar sakti. **Ek ordinary decision jise kisi ne decision samjha hi nahi.**

Policy document khud isay fix nahi kar sakta — screen par kya hai nahi dekh sakta, task ko
customer names chahiye ya nahi decide nahi kar sakta, naya connector ab messages send bhi kar
sakta hai yeh notice nahi kar sakta. **Yeh decisions wahan hoti jahan kaam hota — isliye tumhari
hain.**

Yeh course inke liye ek chhota decision procedure deta — jo itna quick hai tum chalaoge, aur
likha hua hai aise form mein jo manager review kar sake.

## 30-Second Version

Meaningful work se pehle 4 sawal pucho:

1. **The Case: Kya AI yeh kaam kar sakta?** Haan · **Haan, defined human review ke sath** · Nahi
2. **The Data: Kya yeh info jaa sakti hai?** Haan · **Pehle check karo / control add karo** · Is
   route se nahi
3. **The Capability: Kya main yeh tool/Skill/connector/action on kar sakta?** Enable · **Review
   ke liye escalate karo** · Decline
4. **The People: Kya yeh kisi ko unfairly affect karti ya disclosure chahiye?** Decide+document ·
   **Disclose** · Sawal escalate karo

**Middle answer jahan zyadatar professional judgment rehti** — kyunke tumhe naam deni padti:
reviewer, control, approved route, disclosure, ya escalation.

> **Yeh kab mat chalao:** "Meaningful work" kisi aur ka data touch karti, kisi ka paisa, ya ek
> insaan ke baare mein decision. Apna email reword karna, public article summarize karna qualify
> nahi karta. Trivial task par 10-minute habit 1 hafte mein drop ho jati, phir kuch protect nahi
> karti.

> **Practice safely:** Confidential/personal/regulated/privileged material exercise ke liye AI
> tool mein mat daalo. Made-up example, synthetic data, ya approved entry point use karo.
