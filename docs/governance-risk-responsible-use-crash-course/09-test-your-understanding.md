# 09 — Test Your Understanding

Book ka apna `<Quiz>` block (English, verbatim options summarized + answer/explanation) — 21
scenario-based questions, larger pool se draw hote (12/sitting, is doc mein saare 21). Reasoning
se answer do, wording se nahi.

**Source:** Governance, Risk & Responsible Use Assessment. (Zia Tutor AI connector, window 2,
fetched 2026-10-23.)

---

1. **Team chahti Claude benefits-eligibility par final unreviewed determinations de.**
   **Answer: Inappropriate** — consequence applicant ke liye irreversible, professional
   accountability tool mein transfer nahi hoti. _1.1._

2. **Shortlisting workflow "appropriate with review" labeled hui, 6 hafte mein koi review nahi
   hui.** **Answer:** **Gate kabhi define nahi hui** — no named role, no specific check, no
   point. Label record hui, control kabhi nahi bana. _1.3._

3. **Condolence note fully reversible, zero cost, no regulatory weight — par person owns kare.**
   **Answer:** Criteria interact karte — **human-element criterion akela load-bearing** ho sakta
   jab baqi 3 opposite point karein. _1.2._

4. **High-consequence financial summary phir bhi appropriate ban sakta — kya farq karta?**
   **Answer:** **Named reviewer sign-off karta use hone se pehle** — reversibility+accountability
   restore hoti. _1.2, 1.3._

5. **Spending-trends analyst, regulated personal data policy.** **Answer:** Identifiers ko
   "Customer 1, Customer 2" se replace karo, phir anonymised version par analysis chalao —
   discriminator run kiya (pattern chahiye, log nahi). _2.2, 2.3._

6. **Clinician ko actual patient records ki summary chahiye, redaction task useless bana degi.**
   **Answer:** **Pehle establish karo yeh entry point protected health info ke liye approved hai
   ya nahi** — admin se advance mein, keyboard par nahi. _2.2, 2.5._

7. **Confidential deal documents incognito chat (Team plan) mein, "safe hai" bola.** **Answer:**
   **Galat** — incognito deletion nahi hai, Team/Enterprise par chats organizational retention
   follow karte, standard exports mein appear karte. _2.5, 2.6._

8. **Unannounced product ki draft pricing sheet — 2 log tier par disagree.** **Answer:** **Zyada
   sensitive tier use karo** jab tak confirm na ho — asymmetric cost (2-min confirmation vs
   program). _2.1._

9. **Colleague public-forum Skill share karta notes-to-summaries ke liye, meeting-notes tak
   limited nahi.** **Answer: Escalate karo, enable mat karo** — publisher unknown, full session
   access inherit karti. _3.1, 3.2._

10. **Connectors ko read-only se shuru karne wala reasoning Skills par apply karta.** **Answer:**
    **Error** — Connectors ke scopes hote jo narrow kar sakte; Skill ka scope dial nahi hota,
    session ki access inherit karti — sawal "yeh kya touch kar sakti" ban jata, "kya grant kar
    raha" nahi. _3.1._

11. **Doosri team ki internal Skill offer hoti — course isay sabse hard source case kyun kehta?**
    **Answer:** **"Internal" vetted feel karta bina vetted hue** — unki convenience ke liye broad
    reach, ya purani policy ke against bani ho sakti. _3.1._

12. **Hiring coordinator résumés screen karta, shortlist "qualified candidates" forward karta.**
    **Answer: Bias** — automated screen ne exclusions bina human review ke hatayi, systematic
    disadvantage kabhi notice nahi hoga. _4.1._

13. **Manager ambiguous disclosure question face karta (100s employees affect), koi policy
    settle nahi karti.** **Answer:** **Governance function ko escalate karo**, framework applied
    ke sath + specific unresolved point naam kiya hua — verdict leke nahi aana. _4.3._

14. **Quarterly review: team members personal Claude accounts mein draft client deliverables
    paste kar rahe (approved workspace slow hai).** **Answer: Diligence gap** — friction
    approved-path ko unapproved ki taraf push kar rahi, fix friction hatana hai. _8.3._

15. **Usage audit 3 findings dhoondta: unapproved entry point, no-source-check Skill, skipped
    review gate.** **Answer:** **Koi malicious nahi tha** — sab routine-decisions par drift hai,
    teeno ke habit-level fixes hain. _8.1._

16. **Course governance habits fast rakhne ka argument karta, thorough nahi.** **Answer:** 10-min
    habit busy days par skip hoti, aur busy days mein hi routine decisions ban-ke drift
    accumulate karti — isliye checklist 1-minute se kam mein chalna chahiye. _Section 8._

17. **Anthropic-published research Skill web pages fetch+summarize karti, mailbox se connected
    (send kar sakti). Source check clean pass hui.** **Answer:** **Prompt injection risk bachta**
    — source check batata tool kisne likha, content kisne likha nahi — yeh workflow send bhi kar
    sakti. _3.3._

18. **20 minute pehle client file (personal data) unapproved route par upload hui. Kuch send
    nahi hua.** **Answer:** **Rukho, kya gaya+kab likho, manager/admin ko aaj batao, phir pucho
    kya karna** — delete mat karo (concealment), wait mat karo (poora picture ka). _Section 7._

19. **Organization ke pass koi AI policy nahi, "policy check karo" meaningless lagta.**
    **Answer:** **2 cheezein change hoti** — written reasoning ab policy jitna weight carry karti,
    aur tumhare defaults de-facto policy ban jate — 1-page version explicit likho. _8.4._

20. **Retailer ne delivery-updates ke liye phone numbers collect kiye, marketing ab promotional
    messages draft karna chahta same data se, approved workspace mein already hai.** **Answer:**
    **Purpose open hai** — kya numbers jis reason collect hue wo promotional use cover karta,
    yeh data-owner ka sawal hai, route-approved-hone se answer nahi hota. _2.4._

21. **AI notetaker weekly customer call join karta, record+summarize karta, customers ko nahi
    bataya gaya.** **Answer: The People skip hui** — sab call par affected hain, kuch
    jurisdictions consent require karti, customers baad mein jaan ke routine nahi maanenge. _4.2._
