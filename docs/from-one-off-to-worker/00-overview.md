# 00 — Overview

## Yeh Kiske Liye Hai

Har us bande ke liye jo koi task baar baar, same tareeqe se, agent se solve karta rehta hai — aur
mehsoos karne laga hai ke har baar haath se karna ghalat tareeqa hai zindagi jeene ka. Yeh course
batata hai **kab** rukna hai solve karna aur permanent worker banana, aur **kya** asal mein badalta hai
jab aap cross karte hain.

> **Prerequisites:** [Problem Solving with General Agents](../problem-solving-crash-course/README.md)
> pehle khatam karo — yeh course maanta hai aap already ek session mein achhe se problem solve kar
> sakte ho 7 principles se. Yeh bhi maanta hai aap [Is This an Agent Problem?](../is-this-an-agent-problem/README.md)
> kar chuke ho aur Mode 1 (ek dafa solve karo) vs Mode 2 (permanent worker banao) samajhte ho.

## The Short Version (4 Bullets)

1. **Sirf woh cross karo jo already proven hai.** Aap sirf ek aisi solution se worker manufacture kar
   sakte ho jo haath se, kai baar, cleanly solve ki ho. Yeh repetition waste nahi hai — yehi wajah hai
   aapko pata chalta hai worker ko asal mein kya karna chahiye.
2. **Aap zero se shuru nahi kar rahe.** 4 cheezein jo aap ne pehle se Mode 1 mein haath se ki thin —
   brief, check, steps jo aap ne drive kiye, aur session khud — har ek permanent form mein **promote**
   hoti hai. Yehi poora build hai.
3. **Permanent banane ka rasta do taraf jata hai.** Aap ek personal worker **own** kar sakte ho (halka,
   sirf aapke liye) ya ek Digital FTE **manufacture** kar sakte ho (bhari, organization ke liye). Fork
   ek sawal se decide hota hai: worker kiske liye hai?
4. **Payoff hai task vs asset.** Haath se solve karna labour hai jo **task** ki tarah hai — har baar
   ghante lagte hain. Worker labour hai jo **asset** ki tarah hai — ek dafa banao, aur woh kaam karta
   rahega jab aap so rahe hon.

## Task vs Asset — Economics Ka Ulat-Pher

Jab aap haath se solve karte ho, labour ek **task** hai: time do, ek result milta hai, time gaya. Jab
aap us solution ko worker mein promote karte ho, wahi labour ek **asset** ban jati hai: ek dafa time do,
result baar baar milta rehta hai, jab aap kuch aur karte ho.

**Chart imagine karo:** Diego ki line saal bhar seedhi upar chadhti rehti hai (har hafte haath se). Ana
ki line ek dafa upar jumps karti hai (worker banane ka cost), phir almost flat rehti hai. Do lines kuch
hafton baad cross ho jati hain — us waqt ke baad Ana bohat aage hoti hai, aur gap barhta jata hai.

**Yehi poori book ka dil hai:** labour-as-task se labour-as-asset ki taraf shift.
