# 01 — Part 1: The Signal — Kya Yeh Ready Hai Cross Karne Ke Liye?

> **Rokti hai galti:** "Maine ek permanent worker banaya us task ke liye jo maine abhi tak achhi tarah
> figure hi nahi kiya tha, to maine ek hafta ghalat cheez banane mein lagaya — aur phir dobara banana
> para."

[Is This an Agent Problem?](../is-this-an-agent-problem/README.md) mein Gate 2 ne signal ka pehla aadha
diya tha: task **Mode 2** hai jab teenon dials upar hon — aap ise **often** karte ho, har baar **same
shape** hai, aur yeh **worth** hai effort ke. Trigger simple tha: teesri baar jab same tareeqe se same
kaam karo, ruk kar check karo.

Lekin ek doosra aadha hai jo Gate 2 check nahi kar sakta, aur yehi hai jo log skip karte hain: **kya aap
ne isay asal mein achhi tarah solve kiya hai abhi tak?**

Aap sirf ek **proven** solution se manufacture kar sakte ho. Agar aap ek aise method se worker banao jo
aap abhi bhi figure kar rahe ho, aap ghalat worker banate ho — aur yeh tab pata chalta hai jab effort
laga chuke hote ho. To crossing ka **poora signal do hisson mein hai:**

1. Gate 2 kehta hai Mode 2 (often, same, worth it), **aur**
2. Aap ne task ko Mode 1 mein itni baar cleanly solve kiya hai ke **method badalna band ho gaya hai.**

Doosra hissa asli test hai. Khud se poocho: *pichli teen baar jab maine yeh kiya, kya same tareeqe se
kiya?* Agar haan, shape stable hai — aap ne worker dhoond liya. Agar aap abhi bhi har hafte thora alag
tareeqe se solve karte ho — alag steps, alag checks, fresh decisions — to aap ne **abhi tak** stable
shape nahi dhoondi. Mode 1 mein solve karte raho jab tak settle na ho jaye. Yeh repetitions aapki
automate karne mein failure nahi hain — yeh woh research hai jo worker ko batati hai kya karna hai.

> **Repetitions research hain, waste nahi:** Ek dafa pata chal jaye ke yeh Mode 2 job hai, to haath se
> karte rehna inefficient lagta hai. Nahi hai. Har baar solve karte waqt aapko ek edge case milta hai,
> ek behtar step order, ek check jo matter karta hai. Week 1 par bana worker sab kuch miss kar dega.
> Week 5 par bana worker paanch hafton ke hard-won knowledge par khara hai. **Cross karo jab learning
> slow ho jaye, pehle nahi.**
