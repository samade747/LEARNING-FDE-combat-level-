# 00 — What Just Happened

> **Rokti hai galti:** "Maine socha yeh sirf ek aur tech stock crash tha" — jabke asal khabar yeh

## SaaSpocalypse

4 February 2026 ko, global software stocks ne apna **sabse bura stretch** dekha 2022 ke rate-hike
selloff ke baad se. Taqreeban **$1 trillion** market value software aur services sector se chhe
consecutive sessions mein udh gayi. Traders ne isay **"SaaSpocalypse"** naam diya.

**Trigger:** Anthropic ne Claude Cowork (uska agentic productivity platform) ke liye **gyarah
open-source plugins** release kiye — legal, finance, sales, marketing, aur data analysis ko target
karte huay. Ek plugin NDAs triage kar sakta tha, compliance track kar sakta tha, contracts review
kar sakta tha. Market ka jawab foran tha: Thomson Reuters 16% gira. RELX 14% gira. Salesforce aur
ServiceNow har ek ~7% gaye. **Ek legal plugin akela** jo NDA triage aur compliance tracking handle
karta tha, us ne ek trading session mein software, legal tech, aur professional services se **$285
billion** udha diye.

Message saaf tha: **autonomous agents ab woh complex professional work kar sakte hain jo $200/mahina
software subscriptions ko justify karta tha.** "Seat" ke liye pay karne ka daur — sirf isliye
taake ek insan enterprise software mein buttons click kar sake — khatam ho raha hai.

March tak, verdict official tha. Time Magazine ne Anthropic ko "the most disruptive company in the
world" kaha. Claude tools pay karne wali US companies ka share **20%** tak pohanch gaya January
mein, ek saal pehle 4% se.

**Yeh koi product launch nahi tha. Yeh ek market reclassification thi ke kaam kahan hota hai, aur
kaam ki qeemat kahan baithi hai.**
