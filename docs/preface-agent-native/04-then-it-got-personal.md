# 04 — Then It Got Personal

Ab tak sab kuch companies aur capital ke sath ho raha tha. June 2026 ke aakhri hafte mein, repricing
ne aakhir ek **job title** paida kar diya.

30 June ko, AWS ne **$1 billion** commit kiya ek nayi Forward Deployed Engineering unit ke liye:
engineers ke pods jo client companies ke andar embed ho kar unki AI workforce on-site banate hain.
Do din baad, Microsoft ne jawab diya **$2.5 billion aur 6,000 logon** se: **Microsoft Frontier Co.**
Ek hafte ke andar, do sabse bare cloud providers ne combined **$3.5 billion** ek hi job title ke
peeche laga diye: **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** — woh engineer jo ek organization mein jata
hai aur uski AI workforce end-to-end banata hai. Is role ke liye postings ek saal mein **729%**
barhin, jabke candidate pool sirf ~50% barha. Median offer taqreeban **$190,000** par baithta hai.
**Market ne sirf companies ko reprice nahi kiya. Usne us insan ka naam liya jise woh dhoond nahi
paa rahi.**

Ab upar wala chart yaad karo: Anthropic, India ke chhe IT giants se 3.2× worth, aur 1.9 million jobs
line ke ghalat taraf price hui huin. Microsoft ke announcement ke agle din, un logon mein se ek jisne
woh pyramid banayi thi, usne market ko bataya kaise cross karein. Sanjeev Aggarwal, Daksh ke founder
(India ki BPO industry ke pioneers mein se ek), ne CNBC-TV18 par declare kiya ke FDE services
industry ka successor model hai: taqreeban **100 FDEs** wahi $100 million ka business bana sakte
hain jo 2,000 se 2,500 logon ko lagta tha, aur woh bhi margins par jo purana pyramid model kabhi
nahi dekh sakta tha. Uska projection tha, measurement nahi — lekin notice karo yeh kis ka projection
hai. Yeh woh insan hai jisne hour-selling model banaya, aur ab outcome-shipping model ka elaan kar
raha hai.

Yahan tak ke open freelance market bhi move ho chuki hai: **Upwork ab ek dedicated FDE category
chalata hai**, published project bands aur listed talent ki supply ke sath jo abhi bharni seekhi
nahi.

**Yeh line ko career mein badal diya gaya hai.** Poora naqsha [The Roles This Book Trains](../roles-this-book-trains/README.md)
mein hai: FDE asal mein kya karta hai, kaunse paanch markets isay hire kar rahe hain, aur kyun
vendor-neutral version wohi hai jise market dhoond nahi pa rahi.
