# 02 — Then It Got Capable, Then It Got Industrial

## Then It Got Capable — Claude Mythos

Repricing sirf revenue ke baare mein nahi thi. Early April mein, Anthropic ne **Claude Mythos**
release ki — ek initiative jise **Project Glasswing** kaha gaya. Mythos preview ne khud se
**hazaron zero-day vulnerabilities** discover kar liye har major operating system aur web browser
mein — jisme ek **27-saal purana OpenBSD bug** aur ek **17-saal purana FreeBSD remote code execution
flaw** shamil tha. Yeh expert-level capture-the-flag cybersecurity tasks par **73%** score karti thi,
aur pehla model thi jo ek **32-step simulated corporate network attack** end-to-end solve kar sakti
thi.

Anthropic ne Mythos ko broad release ke liye "too dangerous" declare kiya aur sirf select security
partners ko access diya. Treasury Secretary Bessent aur Fed Chair Powell ne reportedly Wall Street
leadership ke sath closed-door meetings kin systemic risks discuss karne ke liye.

Mythos hi baqi poori book ka concrete version hai: **ek single AI Worker** ek elite human security
team ka kaam kar raha hai — tez, zyada coverage ke sath, aur continuously. Mid-April ke bids jo
Anthropic ko $380B se $800B tak le gaye, sirf revenue acceleration price nahi kar rahe the. Woh price
kar rahe the ke **ek AI Worker ab kya kar sakta hai jo ek elite human team nahi kar sakti.**

**Yehi encoded domain expertise hai. Yehi factory output hai.**

## Then It Got Industrial — Compute Ka Buildout

9 April ko, OpenAI ne apne shareholders ko bataya ke Anthropic ek "meaningfully smaller curve" par
operate kar rahi hai: 2027 tak 7-8 GW compute, jabke OpenAI 2030 tak 30 GW plan kar raha hai. Paanch
din baad, $800B bid aayi. Teen hafte baad, valuation phir move hui.

Early May 2026 mein, Financial Times ne report kiya ke Anthropic **$50 billion** raise consider kar
rahi thi, ~$900 billion pre-money par. **28 May ko yeh close ho gaya.** **$65 billion Series H** ne
Anthropic ka post-money valuation **$965 billion** set kiya — February ke valuation se taqreeban
teen guna, aur chaudha mahine pehle se taqreeban pandhra guna. Annualized revenue **$47 billion**
cross kar chuki thi — paanch mahine pehle se paanch guna.

**Zyada batane wali baat cost side thi.** Anthropic ne **$200 billion** commit kiye paanch saalon
mein Google Cloud ko, aur SpaceX ke Colossus 1 ke tamam GPUs le liye — 220,000+ NVIDIA chips se zyada.
Isne Amazon se 5 GW capacity, Google-Broadcom se 5 GW TPU capacity, Azure se $30B capacity, aur
Fluidstack se $50B ka U.S. infrastructure partnership bhi secure kiya. **Yeh ab software launch ki
spending pattern nahi thi. Yeh ek industrial buildout tha.**

24 April ko, Google ne formally **$40 billion tak** equity commit ki Anthropic mein, $10 billion se
shuru karte huay. Hyperscalers sirf Anthropic ko infrastructure bech nahi rahe the — woh us AI
workforce ko finance karne mein bhi madad kar rahe the jo usi infrastructure par chalti.

Company ki shape badal gayi. Anthropic ab ek AI lab ki tarah valued nahi thi — yeh frontier compute,
electricity, aur data-center capacity ka ek bara buyer ban chuki thi. Jo cheez industrial scale par
manufacture ho rahi thi, sirf model nahi thi. **Woh workforce thi jo us par chalti hai.**

**Chatbot nahi. Seat nahi. The Factory.**
