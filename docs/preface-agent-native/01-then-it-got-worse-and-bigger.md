# 01 — Then It Got Worse, Then It Got Bigger

## Then It Got Worse — Citrini Ka Hypothetical

Teen hafte baad, Monday 23 February ko, ek **7,000-word hypothetical** Citrini Research se viral ho
gaya, aur Dow ek session mein **800 points** gira. Yeh piece koi prediction nahi thi — ek thought
experiment, June 2028 ki tareekh se, jo explore karta tha kya hota hai jab AI agents scale par
white-collar knowledge workers ko displace kar dete hain: mass unemployment, software-backed loan
defaults, financial contagion. **Market ne us thought experiment ko ek trading signal ki tarah
treat kiya.**

Datadog, CrowdStrike, aur Zscaler har ek 9% se zyada gire. IBM 13% gira — 2000 ke baad uski sabse
buri single-day performance. American Express, KKR, aur Blackstone, sab report mein naam liye gaye,
lurhak gaye.

Citrini ki woh line jisne is moment ko capture kiya:

> "For the entirety of modern economic history, human intelligence has been the scarce input. We
> are now experiencing the unwind of that premium."

Jo premium unwind hoti hai, usay kahin flow karna hota hai. Agla section batata hai market ke
mutabiq woh kahan flow karti hai.

## Then It Got Bigger — $800 Billion Ki Bid

Saat hafte baad, repricing wapis Anthropic tak aayi — is baar bid ki tarah, sell nahi. Mid-April
2026 mein, Bloomberg ne report kiya ke venture capitalists **$800 billion** ke term sheets offer kar
rahe the — February ke $380B post-money se double se zyada, aur OpenAI ke $852B ke qareeb. Sacra ke
mutabiq, Anthropic ki annualized run-rate March mein **$30 billion** tak pohanch gayi — saal-dar-saal
~1,400% ka izafa.

Comparison jo matter karta hai OpenAI se nahi — un incumbents se hai jo disrupt ho rahe hain. $800B
par, Anthropic **India ki chhe sab se bari IT services companies** (TCS, Infosys, HCLTech, Wipro,
LTIMindtree, Tech Mahindra) ke combined market cap se **3.2 guna zyada** worth hai — jo companies
milkar taqreeban **1.9 million logon** ko employ karti hain aur pichle saal ~$100 billion combined
revenue banayin. Anthropic ke paas taqreeban **1,000 employees** hain.

Market yeh nahi keh raha ke Anthropic India ki IT services industry se zyada valuable **company**
hai. Market keh raha hai ke Anthropic repricing ke **sahi taraf** baitha hai, aur woh 1.9 million
jobs **ghalat taraf** — kyunke woh software categories jo woh workers banate, integrate, aur
maintain karte hain, wahi categories hain jinhein replace kiya ja raha hai. **Yehi woh taraf hai
jise yeh book aapko occupy karna sikhati hai.**
