# 03 — What the Market Is Pricing, and the Jobless Boom

## The Early Tell, In Retrospect

Yeh sab surprise nahi hona chahiye tha. June 2023 mein, Thomson Reuters ne **$650 million** pay
kiye Casetext ke liye — jiska CoCounsel AI assistant complex legal evaluations par **97%** score
karta tha. Yeh acquisition primarily software ke baare mein nahi thi — yeh **encoded legal
expertise** ke baare mein thi: substantive legal work karne ki ability jo pehle expensive human
professionals maangta tha. CoCounsel proof-of-concept tha, teen saal pehle SaaSpocalypse ne is
pattern ko poori market mein visible bana diya.

## Market Kya Price Kar Rahi Hai

Volatility hata do, aur paanch signals nazar aate hain:

- **SaaSpocalypse** ne sabit kiya autonomous agents woh professional work kar sakte hain jiske liye
  seat-based software charge karti thi.
- **Citrini** ne sabit kiya market disruption ko software se aage, poori knowledge work category
  tak expect karti hai.
- **$965B round** ne sabit kiya market abhi, aaj, un companies ko pay karne ke liye tayyar hai jo
  woh agents manufacture karti hain — Anthropic ko ~1,000 employees par taqreeban ek trillion dollar
  price kiya gaya.
- **Mythos** ne sabit kiya AI Workers ab woh kaam kar sakte hain jo koi human team match nahi kar
  sakti — tez nahi, sasta nahi, **behtar.**
- **Compute buildout** ne sabit kiya yeh workforce infrastructure scale par manufacture ho rahi hai,
  ordinary software product ki tarah nahi.

Paanch alag events. Ek coherent thesis: **value un logon se un logon ki taraf shift ho rahi hai jo
software use karte hain, un logon ki taraf jo un agents ko own karte hain jo us par chalte hain.**

## The Jobless Boom

Ek second-order implication hai jise market ne abhi absorb nahi kiya. Modern economic history mein,
recession ka matlab hamesha ek cheez raha hai: economy sikudti hai, aur uske sath jobs bhi gayab ho
jaati hain. Yeh do hamesha sath move karti hain. AI buildout is link ko tor deta hai. Jaise Forbes
writer Brandon Kochkodin ne late May 2026 mein argue kiya, AI infrastructure mein jaane wala paisa
GDP ko upar dhakel sakta hai chahe human workforce jo uski growth mein share karti thi, neeche
sikur jaye. Yeh ek **jobless boom** hai: economy expand hoti hai, lekin paychecks uske sath nahi
chaltin.

Pehle, Citrini scenario ne is displacement ko disaster mein khatam hote imagine kiya tha: mass
layoffs, defaults, market crash. Kochkodin isi cheez ka **quieter version** describe karta hai.
Displacement ab bhi hota hai, lekin usay mark karne ke liye koi crash nahi — kyunke AI buildout
poore waqt GDP aur corporate profits ko upar le jata rehta hai. Economy paper par healthy dikhti
hai jabke human work ki value simply pay hona band ho jaati hai.

Yehi humare usual instruments ko tor deta hai. Hum hamesha jobs aur growth ko healthy economy ka
sign parhte aaye hain. Jab output barh sakta hai employment gire ke sath, yeh signals kuch sach
batana band kar dete hain.

**Agar economy human labor ke bagair barh sakti hai, to sirf ek jagah safe hai: us side jo woh
labor own karti hai jo kaam kar rahi hai.** Yehi woh taraf hai jise yeh book aapko occupy karna
sikhati hai.
