# Project 2 — Connect One App, Read-Only, And Use It

**Time:** ~20-30 min · **Tool:** Claude.ai

## Steps

1. Ek app connect karo (Drive ya Gmail easiest). **Read-only rakho.**
2. Real sawal pucho jo usi app ke andar ki cheez chahta:

   Drive ke liye:
   ```text
   Find my most recent document in my Drive, pull the three facts or
   numbers that matter most, and give me a one-line summary of what it is.
   ```

   Gmail ke liye:
   ```text
   Summarize my most recent email thread, and list anything it is
   waiting on me to do.
   ```

3. AI answer deta bina kuch upload kiye. Phir khud bolo (loud ya likho) ke kitna access diya, aur
   decide karo kya yeh task kabhi write access chahegi.
