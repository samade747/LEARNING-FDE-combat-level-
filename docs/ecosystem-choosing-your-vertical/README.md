## Yeh Chapter Kis Baare Mein Hai

[The FDE AF Model](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md) batata hai vertical kya hoti hai aur woh paisa
kaise kamati hai. Yeh chapter agla sawal poochta hai: **kaunsi vertical aap ko banani chahiye?** Guess
mat karo. Ek method follow karo. Yeh chapter woh method deta hai: **paanch steps, aath tests, aur teen
rules jo kabhi mat todo.**

Plain words mein poora page: sabse bara market mat chuno. Sabse aasan honest sale chuno. Ek achi
vertical mein teen cheezein hoti hain — success start hone se pehle measure ho sakta hai, ek real expert
knowledge ke peeche khada hota hai, aur ek shared builder har customer ko serve kar sakta hai. Yeh page
aapko woh vertical dhundne ka checklist deta hai, do honest exits deta hai jab koi candidate checklist
fail kare, aur kaam ka order batata hai: customer se number poochne se pehle aap profession ki knowledge
ka ek chota piece build karte ho.

## Sabse Zaroori Baat

> **Ek galat beachhead teen mahine leta hai; ek galat vertical ek saal leta hai.** Beachhead badal sakte
> ho, kyunke aap jo bhi banate ho woh usi vertical ke andar rehta hai. Vertical khud badalna aasan nahi —
> corpus, builder rules, aur customer relationships sab usi ke andar rehte hain. To: vertical bohot dhyan
> se chuno, aur beachhead jaldi chuno.
