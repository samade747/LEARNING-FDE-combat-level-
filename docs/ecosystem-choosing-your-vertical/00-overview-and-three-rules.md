# 00 — Teen Rules Aur Ek Bara Warning

Pehle, [FDE AF Model](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md) se teen rules yaad karo.

**Launch rule.** Koi vertical committed domain expert ke bina launch nahi hoti. Expert twin hi vertical
ka product hai. Bina expert ke vertical sirf ek **corpus** hai (documents ka collection).

**First-job test.** Ek Digital FTE ke liye behtareen pehli job mein chaar properties hoti hain: kaam roz
ya har hafte repeat hota hai, output measure ho sakta hai, ek supervisor already yeh kaam review karta
hai, aur galtiyan fix ho sakti hain.

**Order rule.** Pehle banao, phir becho. Vertical business mein kuch bhi shuru nahi hota jab tak
[System of Record ka ek slice](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md#layer-4-customer-instances) exist na
kare: ek professional outcome, poori tarah cover kiya hua. Jis graduate ke paas kuch governed nahi hai
uske paas buyer ko dikhane ko kuch nahi hai, aur jis buyer ko kuch nahi dikhaya gaya woh apna starting
number nahi bataega. To slice woh step nahi jo customer ka intezaar kare. Yeh woh step hai jo ek
customer **produce** karta hai.

Is page ki har cheez inhi teen rules ko protect karne ke liye hai. Abhi check karna baad mein ek saal ke
kaam ke baad problem dhundne se bohot sasta hai.

## Ek Bara Warning: Beachhead vs Vertical

Ek **beachhead** vertical ke andar woh ek segment hai jahan aap sabse pehle banate ho. Beachhead badal
sakte ho, kyunke jo bhi banate ho woh usi vertical ke andar rehta hai. Lekin khud vertical badalna aasan
nahi — corpus, builder rules, aur customer relationships sab usi ke andar jeete hain. To: **vertical
bohot dhyan se chuno, aur beachhead jaldi chuno.**

Yeh saal kyun itna zaroori hai, iski wajah yeh hai. Ek vendor-neutral Forward Deployed Engineer client
mein [do Systems of Record](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md) le kar jata hai. Pehla method hai, aur
ecosystem yeh deta hai: yeh book, gehri aur already governed, har client par same. Doosra profession hai,
aur woh khud banati hai. Sirf doosri uski apni hai. To ek galat vertical sirf ek galat market nahi hai —
yeh ek saal us ek asset par lagaya gaya waqt hai jo woh maalik banne wali thi, aur aakhir mein dikhane
ko kuch nahi.
