# 02 — Step 2: Selling Ease Ke Liye Screen Karo

Sabse bara market goal nahi hai. **Sabse aasan sale** goal hai. Kisi bhi deep kaam se pehle, candidate ko
chhe sawalon par score karo. Har sawal 0 se 10 points, phir average nikalo.

1. **Kya success ek main number se define ho sakti hai?** Ek [contract of success](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md#layer-4-customer-instances)
   ko ek starting number aur ek target number chahiye. Dono taraf se measure ho sakein. Jaise: hours per
   file, tickets per day, days per filing. Phir do teen guardrail numbers add karo — jo behtar hote hue
   bhi kharab nahi hone chahiye. Ek audit file ke liye main number hours per file hai, guardrail hai
   reviewer correction rate. Speed jo errors create kare, success nahi hai.
2. **Kya buyer is problem par pehle se paisa kharch kar raha hai?** Aap buyer ki already ho rahi spending
   ko redirect karna chahte ho, nayi budget banana nahi. Nayi budget ko kai meetings chahiye. Existing
   spending ko aksar kam.
3. **Pehle deployment ka rasta kitna saaf hai?** Jis domain mein koi regulator na ho, wahan zyada score.
   Har regulatory gate score kam karta hai, kyunke har gate pehli sale mein mahine jorta hai.
4. **Kya knowledge legally mil sakti hai?** Public laws aur government documents aksar sabse aasan case
   hote hain (lekin unki bhi apni open license parhni parti hai). Licensed standards workable hain,
   lekin license likhwana zaroori. Private platform policies jo har mahine badalti hain sabse mushkil
   case hain.
5. **Kya aapke network mein koi expert probably maujood hai?** Abhi confirmed nahi, sirf probably. Aunty,
   purana boss, professor, bees saal ke practitioner colleague. Confirm Step 4 mein hoga.
6. **Kya ek builder har customer ko serve kar sakta hai?** Model ka strict rule: [ek builder per domain,
   har customer ki alag copy kabhi nahi](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md#layer-3-vertical-ecosystems).
   Poocho: kya is domain ki companies itni similar hain ke ek shared builder sab ke liye kaam kare? Agar
   har customer ko apna version chahiye, yeh domain model ki economics ko shuru hone se pehle hi tor
   deta hai.

| Score | Matlab |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Koi evidence nahi, ya sakht barrier |
| 3–4 | Kamzor evidence, unsolved bare problems |
| 5–6 | Mumkin, lekin uncertain |
| 7–8 | Strong evidence, problems solvable lagte hain |
| 9–10 | Confirmed evidence aur unusually saaf rasta |

Score bina evidence ki ek line ke sirf ek **feeling** hai, score nahi.

**6/10 ya zyada: continue karo. 6 se kam: exit lo.**

Do exits hain, dono honest outcomes hain, koi failure nahi:

- Agar candidate sawal 1 aur 2 par high score kare lekin baaqi mein fail ho jaye, yeh ek **service
  domain** hai. Layer 1 System of Record builds aur Layer 4 engagements se serve karo. Isse paisa kamao.
  Iske liye trio mat banao.
- Agar candidate broadly fail ho jaye, **park kar do.** Likh do kya badalna chahiye taake dobara dekha
  jaye.

> **Service domain koi kamtar outcome nahi hai.** Ek aam wajah: knowledge zyada tar har customer ki apni
> documents hoti hai, to koi shared corpus nahi hota. Doosri wajah: kisi expert ne knowledge likhi hi
> nahi hoti. Graduate earning ladder [Layer 1 se shuru](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md) hoti hai.
> Client ke apne manuals se ek governed System of Record banana paid work hai. Yeh reputation banata hai.
> Kai graduates ko pehle service domains mein kamana chahiye aur apna vertical baad mein, real experience
> ke saath, chunna chahiye.

## Written Evidence Kyun Zaroori Hai

Yeh rules strict lag sakte hain: har score ke saath evidence ki ek line, koi invented numbers nahi,
honest exits likhe hue. Yeh style preferences nahi hain — decision-making ki science inke peeche hai.

**Confidence aur accuracy alag skills hain.** Steve Fleming ki lab (UCL) dikhati hai ke kuch log jaante
hain ke woh kab galat honge, kuch nahi jaante — apni raw ability se qata-nazar. Har score ke saath ek
sentence of evidence likhna aapki confidence ko **test karta hai**, trust nahi.

**Overconfidence learning rokti hai.** Ek PNAS study mein sabse dogmatic log sabse kam extra information
maangne wale the — jab unhe sabse zyada zaroorat thi. Screen aapko woh information dhundne majboor karti
hai.

**Humility, forecasting mein intelligence se jeetti hai.** Igor Grossmann ki research suggest karti hai
ke intellectually humble experts long-term trends ko smarter lekin proud experts se behtar predict karte
hain, kyunke woh apne views zyada update karte hain.

Aur ek aakhri technique jaan-boojh kar is page mein hai: apni decisions ko third person mein describe
karne wale log "I" aur "my" likhne walon se measurably behtar judgment dikhate hain. To "meri vertical"
mat likho. Likho "ek graduate is domain ko consider kar raha hai," aur us graduate ke evidence ko reviewer
ki tarah score karo.
