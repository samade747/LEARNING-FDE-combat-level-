# 01 — Step 1: Ek Body of Professional Work Naam Karo, Industry Nahi

"Banking" koi vertical nahi hai. "Healthcare" koi vertical nahi hai. Yeh **industries** hain. Ek industry
mein kaam ki kai qismein hoti hain. Poori industry ke liye ek System of Record ek library hoti, ek corpus
nahi — aur koi bhi akela expert uske peeche khada nahi ho sakta.

Ek vertical professional work ka ek sankra hissa hota hai: shared knowledge, recognizable experts,
repeatable Workers, common buyer needs, aur ek reusable builder wala kaam. Accounting jaisa ek
recognized profession aksar behtareen starting point hota hai. Lekin body of work poori profession se
bhi sankra ho sakta hai: corporate credit, recruitment, tax practice.

Ek simple test: kya aap ek insaan imagine kar sakte ho jiski **poori career** yehi kaam ho? Agar haan,
to yeh kaafi sankra hai. Agar jawab hai "acha, kai alag tarah ke log," to aap ne ek industry naam ki hai.
Isay chota karo aage barhne se pehle.

## Ulta Ghalti: Bohot Sankra Bhi Galat Hai

Ab ulti ghalti, jo 2026 mein zyada aam hai. Ek narrow, single-purpose agent bhi vertical nahi hai.
Market "vertical AI agent" phrase do alag maano mein use karta hai, aur sirf ek hamara hai. Kuch writers
ka matlab ek agent hota hai jiska deliberately narrow kaam ho: sirf prospects dhundna aur kuch nahi, ya
sirf ek tarah ka message draft karna aur rukna. Doosron ka matlab ek system hota hai jo ek industry ke
liye bana ho, us industry ki knowledge aur compliance rules ke saath preloaded. Yeh book hamesha doosra
matlab lete hai.

Pehla matlab galat nahi, bas alag cheez hai, aur is book mein iska pehle se naam hai. Ek agent jiska ek
hi narrow kaam ho woh **Digital FTE** hai, aur kitna narrow banana hai woh **first-job test** ka sawal
hai (vertical decision nahi). Farq commercially matter karta hai. Ek narrow agent ek product hai jo aap
bech sakte ho. Ek vertical governed profession hai jo aapko us product ko agle customer ke liye dobara,
zero se shuru kiye bina, banana deti hai. Pehle vertical chuno, phir uske andar Workers ke naam Step 2
mein tay karo.

**Cut it smaller** — teen examples:
- Healthcare → Revenue-cycle management → Medical coding review
- Customer support → Technical support → Tier 1 incident resolution
- Banking → Trade finance → Document checking
