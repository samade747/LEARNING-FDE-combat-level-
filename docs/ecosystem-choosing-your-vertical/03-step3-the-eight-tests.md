# 03 — Step 3: Aath Tests Chalao

Screen poochta hai: kya yeh domain becha ja sakta hai? Aath tests poochte hain: **kya yeh domain ek trio
carry kar sakta hai?**

Har test likh kar, evidence ke saath jawab do. Score: Pass = 1 point, Partial = 0.5, Fail = 0. Aage barhne
ke liye **6.5/8** chahiye.

Ek rule sab points se upar hai: **agar Test 3 fail ho, vertical evaluation wahin khatam.** Total score
matter nahi karta, kyunke launch rule tor nahi sakti. Failed candidate wahin jata hai jahan Step 2 ke do
exits the — service domain (agar sale hoti hai) ya parked (agar nahi).

**Test 1 — Corpus Shape.** Model domain knowledge ko [teen forms](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md#layer-3-vertical-ecosystems)
mein organize karta hai: ek corpus jise agent cite kare, rules ka ek map jo hamesha loaded ho, aur
reflexes (procedures jo agent step-by-step follow kare, poori tarah). Kya is profession ki knowledge
inhi teen forms mein fit hoti hai? Evidence: das sabse zaroori sources, teen rules jo kabhi na tootein,
aur teen complete procedures naam karo.

**Test 2 — First Workers.** Kya aap teen ya zyada Digital FTEs naam kar sakte ho jo first-job test pass
karte hain? Evidence: teen named Workers, har ek ka starting number, aur woh human role jo aaj yeh kaam
review karta hai.

**Test 3 — Expert Availability.** Kya koi named insaan hai jo apni persona aur apni written material
aapki startup ko license karega? Material rights-cleared honi chahiye. Yeh insaan
[Subject Matter Expert as Skill Author](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/roles-this-book-trains)
hai. Aam failure: domain mein kai practitioners hain lekin koi author nahi. Jo knowledge kabhi likhi
nahi gayi woh capture ho sakti hai, lekin mahinon ka waqt lagta hai. Expert search ko ek deadline do.

**Test 4 — Governing Rules.** Is kaam ko kaunse rules govern karte hain, aur kya aap unhe naam kar sakte
ho? Jurisdictional kaam (tax, accounting, banking, medical coding) ke liye: country/region, regulator,
aur versioned public rules naam karo. Non-jurisdictional kaam (sales, support, recruitment) ke liye:
professional frameworks ya policies jinhe domain ke experts authority maante hain. Har jurisdiction ek
alag build aur alag opportunity hai — ek trio jo ek country ke liye bana, doosre country ke rules ke
liye dobara bana sakte ho.

**Test 5 — Market Aur Economics.** Kaun kharidta hai? Kitne buyers hain? Purchasing process kitna
mushkil hai? Kya revenue recurring hai? **Money numbers invent mat karo.** License cost ek fact hai jo
poocha ja sakta hai. Engagement price ek guess hai jab tak pehli buyer conversation usay test na kare.

**Test 6 — Governance Feasibility.** Kya corpus ka owner, versions, reviews, aur access control mahinon
mein ban sakte hain, saalon mein nahi? [Search akela System of Record nahi hai](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md#layer-1-the-content-system-of-record-component-the-sor-kernel).

**Test 7 — Competitive Position.** Is profession ko kaun already serve kar raha hai? Companies naam
karo. Phir ek sentence likho: jab customer poochega "aap kyun, hamare current vendor ke bajaye?" tab
jawab kya hoga? Jo sentence aksar jeetta hai: *hum aapka current system replace nahi karte; hum usay
extend karte hain, aur profession ka judgment layer uske saath rakhte hain.*

> **2026 mein Test 7:** Big Four verticals bana rahi hain. March 2026 mein PwC ke US CEO Paul Griggs ne
> announce kiya ke firm hourly billing ke alternatives dega, aur apni tax/consulting practice ke hisson
> ko AI-powered tools mein convert karega jo clients directly use kar sakein — PwC One ki shakal mein,
> chhe automated services ke saath shuru hua. Yeh model ko confirm karta hai — sabse bara incumbent bhi
> wahi kar raha hai jo FDE AF Model describe karta hai. Aur yeh bar bhi barhata hai. Lekin domain band
> nahi karta — teen wajah: **jurisdiction** (Big Four apni sabse badi markets pehle banate hain), **buyer
> size** (unke clients sirf bare hain; zyada tar professions mid-size aur chhote firms se bhare hain), aur
> **expert** (unka platform unki methodology carry karta hai; aapka ek named insaan ke bees saal, aapko
> licensed — yehi Test 3 hai, aur scale usay copy nahi kar sakti). Score par honest asar: Test 7 se aadha
> point kam, fail nahi.

**Test 8 — Regulatory Gate.** Agar koi regulator buyer ko govern karta hai, uske kaunse rules aapke kaam
ko chhuen ge? Regulated buyers ke liye safe default boundary: **preparation and recommendation**. Worker
taiyar karta hai aur recommend karta hai. Ek named insaan approve aur act karta hai. Kuch bhi jo undo na
ho sake, automatically nahi hota.
