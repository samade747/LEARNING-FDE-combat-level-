## Ek Starting Longlist, Example Scores Ke Saath

Yeh list apni khud ki screen ke liye starting point hai, final jawab nahi. Chaar warnings pehle: yeh
scores ek waqt aur jagah (2026, Pakistan/Gulf) ka assessment hain — apna khud dobara score karo; high
score vertical nahi banata (Test 3 hamesha decide karta hai); aapka network is table se behtar hai; aur
zyada tar naam starting points hain, finished verticals nahi — Step 1 har ek par lagana hai.

List chhe families mein bati hai, kyunke ek hi family ke domains ek hi tarah fail hote hain. **Har naam
ka matlab hai "agentic AI era version"** — "Payroll" ka matlab payroll software nahi, payroll AI Workers
hai.

## Patterns Jo Seekhne Hain

1. **Har high scorer mein teen cheezein same hain:** ek main number mein success, paisa jo buyer already
   kharch karta hai, likhe hue rules.
2. **Har low scorer mein ek cheez same hai:** ek slow door — regulator, procurement office, ya committee.
3. **Us domain se hoshiyar raho jo model kha jata hai.** Agar general model bina corpus, expert, ya
   governance ke poora kaam kar de, wahan koi vertical nahi hai.
4. **Screen aur tests alag cheezein measure karte hain.** Family 3 high screens se bhari hai jo service
   domains ban jate hain. Family 5 low screens se bhari hai jo waiting strong verticals hain.
5. **Agentic era score karo, SaaS era nahi.** "Yahan achi software hai?" mat poocho. Poocho "kya AI
   Worker, governed corpus ke saath, is domain ka kaam kar sakta hai?"

hai). **Moat** sabse gehra expert advantage hai (copy karna mushkil). **Option** ek candidate hai jo open
expert search aur deadline se zinda rakha jata hai. Committed expert ke bina domains **service domains**
ki tarah neeche chalte hain.

competitor ek fact hai jo likha jata hai, chorne ki wajah nahi. Aur ek waqt mein sirf ek candidate
validation slot leta hai, kyunke [har vertical foundation ke bugs multiply karti hai](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md).

## Aage Kahan Se Jaana Hai

**Path 1 — Idea ko sasta test karo, kuch banane se pehle.** Har cheez ek **probe** hai: sirf evidence
produce karti hai, aur kuch nahi. Ek probe slice nahi hai — koi owner, versions, ya evaluation set nahi,
kabhi System of Record ka hissa nahi banti. Teen states, order mein: probe, phir thin, phir thick.

- Profession ke real kaam ka sample ek general agent ko browser mein do, dekho first-job idea tikta hai.
- Candidate corpus ka chhota hissa pgvector mein load karo, check karo search aur citation kaam karte
  hain.
- Contract of success ek real spec ki tarah likho.
- Acceptance criteria ek checking sawal hai: customer ke apne reviewers Worker ke output ko kaise verify
  karenge?

**Path 2 — Fixed order mein banao.** Pehle slice, validation window ke andar, gates 1 aur 2 ke baad. Phir
chaaron gates pass hone ke baad, baaqi trio: System of Record thicken hoti hai, phir expert twin jo isi
se sikhata hai, phir domain builder jo isi ke against Workers manufacture karta hai. Poora method
[Designing the Vertical System of Record](../ecosystem-designing-the-vertical-sor/README.md) mein hai.

Aur agar aapka honest jawab abhi "mera koi committed expert nahi hai" hai, to aapka rasta band nahi —
order rule sirf vertical ladder ko govern karti hai. Model aapko bata raha hai kahan se shuru karna hai:
[Layers 1 aur 4 par kamao pehle](../ecosystem-fde-af-model/README.md), service ladder par, jo ek client
se shuru hoti hai aur koi slice nahi maangti.
