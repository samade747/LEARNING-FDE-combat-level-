# 04 — Step 4 Aur 5: Beachhead Chuno, Chaar Gates Se Validate Karo

## Step 4 — Beachhead Chuno, Aur Expert Ko Decide Karne Do

Ek profession-sized vertical mein abhi bhi kai bodies of knowledge aur Worker families hoti hain. Sab
kuch ek saath mat banao. Ek beachhead chuno: woh segment jahan knowledge sabse saaf ho, pehla Worker
sabse zyada checkable ho, aur signed contract of success ka rasta sabse chota ho.

Agar do beachheads buyers, numbers, aur licensing par barabar lagein? **Expert decide karta hai.** Launch
rule expert commitment ko decisive factor banata hai comparable segments ke darmiyan. Yeh buyer evidence
ki jagah nahi leta — ek segment mein committed expert lekin koi buyer na ho, woh abhi bhi failed screen
hai. Lekin do segments jo dono bikte hain, unme se woh chuno jahan expert relationship sabse mazboot ho.

Ek aur rule validation se pehle: **kisi book ka malik hona uski license nahi hai kisi system se serve
karne ki.** Ek standards handbook kharidna aapko usay ek System of Record se serve karne ka haq nahi
deta. Validation se pehle, corpus ke har source ko ek documented rights basis do: public domain, ek open
license, ek existing commercial license, direct written permission, ya ek complete replacement plan. Har
source ke liye ek backup plan bhi likho.

## Step 5 — Chaar Gates Se Validate Karo, 60 Se 90 Din Mein

Validation ek fixed 60-90 din ka period hai jiska ek maqsad hai: guesses ko facts se replace karna. Yeh
poori vertical banane ka waqt nahi — yeh sabit karne ka waqt hai ke vertical banane layak hai.

Rule simple hai: **chaaron gates, ya launch nahi.** Aur chaaron kisi bhi order mein nahi hote — **do
gates pehle aate hain. Phir ek slice banta hai. Phir aakhri do gates mumkin hote hain.**

### Do Gates Jo Kisi Bhi Building Se Pehle Aate Hain

1. **Expert ne sign kiya hai.** "Principle mein razi" nahi. Sign kiya hua. Partnership agreement persona
   license, rights-cleared material, revenue share, aur platform terms cover karta hai.
2. **Rights basis likhit mein confirm ho chuka hai.** Har third-party source ka documented rights basis
   hai jo aapko chahiye uses ke liye.

Yeh do pehle hain kyunke slice dono par depend karta hai. Expert reflex ke andar procedures author karta
hai aur apni files kholta hai archaeology ke liye. Rights basis decide karta hai kaunse sources legally
corpus mein baith sakte hain.

### Phir Ek Slice Banao

Ek professional outcome, poori tarah cover kiya hua: uska outcome contract, uske invariants, uska
decision map, uski exception list, ek derived procedure, uska checker, aur uska evaluation set.
Docusaurus website ki tarah publish kiya hua taake insaan aur agents dono parh sakein.

Do words yahan matter karte hain, aur yeh sirf **breadth** ke baare mein hain, care ke baare mein nahi.
Ek System of Record jis mein sirf ek outcome ho **thin** hai. Kai outcomes wali **thick** hai. Dono apne
andar jo bhi outcomes hain unhe poori tarah cover karte hain. Ek slice jo sirf clean cases handle kare
woh thin nahi, **unfinished** hai — aur woh professional ke pehle mushkil sawal ke saamne nahi tikegi.

## Do Gates Jo Slice Mumkin Banata Hai

3. **Ek named sponsor exist karta hai, ek drafted contract of success ke saath.** Ek real company. Ek
   named executive sponsor. Pehle Worker ke liye ek drafted (abhi unsigned) contract of success:
   starting number, target number, acceptance criteria, forbidden actions, aur pass hone se kya milega.
4. **Money numbers real quotes hain, guesses nahi.** Quoted license costs. Sponsor ka real purchasing
   timeline. Aapka staffing plan.

## Launch Ke Baad, System of Record Pehle Aata Rehta Hai

Gates pass ho gaye. Ab aap trio banate ho. Model building ka order fix karta hai: **domain System of
Record pehle aata hai. Hamesha.** Expert twin isi se sikhata hai. Domain builder Workers ko isi mein
point karne wale skills deta hai. Har Worker jo aap kabhi manufacture karoge, isi se rules aur procedures
cite karega. Twin ya builder pehle banao, unke paas kuch trusted parhne ko nahi hoga — agents guess
karenge, aur guess karne wale agents becha nahi jaa sakta.
