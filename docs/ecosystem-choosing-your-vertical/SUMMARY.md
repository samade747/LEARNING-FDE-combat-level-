# Choosing Your Vertical — Summary

The Ecosystem, Chapter 2/9. FDE AF Model batata hai vertical kya hoti hai; yeh chapter poochta hai **kaunsi
vertical banani chahiye** — 5 steps, 8 tests, 3 rules jo kabhi mat todo. **Sabse bara galti:** ek galat
vertical ek saal leta hai (beachhead badal sakta hai, vertical nahi).

## 00 — Teen Rules Aur Ek Bara Warning

- **Launch rule:** koi vertical committed domain expert ke bina launch nahi hoti — bina expert corpus sirf
  documents ka dher hai.
- **First-job test:** behtareen pehli job — roz/har-hafte repeat, measurable output, existing supervisor
  already review karta, galtiyan fix-able.
- **Order rule:** pehle banao, phir becho — System of Record ka ek slice exist kare (professional outcome
  poori tarah covered) tabhi shuru hota hai. Slice woh nahi jo customer ka intezaar kare — **customer
  produce karta hai.**
- **Beachhead vs Vertical:** beachhead (segment) badal sakte ho, **vertical badalna aasan nahi** (corpus,
  builder rules, customer relationships sab usi mein jeete hain). Vertical dhyan se chuno, beachhead jaldi.

## 01 — Step 1: Body of Work Naam Karo, Industry Nahi

- "Banking"/"Healthcare" industries hain, verticals nahi — poori industry ke liye ek SoR ek library hoti,
  corpus nahi. Test: kya ek insaan imagine ho sakta hai jiski **poori career** yehi kaam ho?
- **Ulti ghalti (2026 mein aam):** bohot sankra bhi galat — narrow single-purpose agent = **Digital FTE**
  (first-job-test sawal hai), vertical nahi. Pehle vertical chuno, phir Workers Step 2 mein.
- Examples: Healthcare→Revenue-cycle→Medical coding review; Support→Tech support→Tier 1; Banking→Trade
  finance→Document checking.

## 02 — Step 2: Selling Ease Ke Liye Screen Karo

- 6 sawal, 0-10 score, average nikalo: (1) success ek main number se define? (2) buyer already spend kar
  raha? (3) deployment path saaf (koi regulator nahi)? (4) knowledge legally mil sakti? (5) network mein
  expert probably? (6) ek builder har customer ko serve kar sakta (customer-specific copies model todti)?
- Score bands 0-2 se 9-10 tak table di gayi. **6/10+: continue. 6 se kam: exit.** 2 honest exits: **service
  domain** (sawal 1-2 high, baaki fail — trio mat banao, Layer 1/4 se serve karo) ya **park kar do**.
- Written evidence zaroori — behavioral science backing: confidence≠accuracy (Fleming/UCL), overconfidence
  learning rokti hai (PNAS), humility forecasting mein jeetti hai (Grossmann), third-person framing behtar
  judgment deta hai.

## 03 — Step 3: Aath Tests Chalao

- Screen poochta hai "bikta hai?", tests poochte hain "trio carry kar sakta hai?" Pass=1, Partial=0.5,
  Fail=0. **6.5/8 chahiye. Test 3 fail = evaluation wahin khatam** (total score matter nahi karta).
- **8 Tests:** (1) Corpus Shape — 3 forms (cite-able corpus, rules map, reflexes)? (2) First Workers — 3+
  named Digital FTEs jo first-job-test pass karein? (3) Expert Availability — named insaan jo persona+
  material license karega (rights-cleared)? (4) Governing Rules — jurisdictional (country/regulator/
  versioned) ya professional frameworks? (5) Market/Economics — buyers, recurring revenue, invent mat
  karo numbers. (6) Governance Feasibility — mahino mein ban sakta, saalon mein nahi? (7) Competitive
  Position — kaun serve karta, "aap kyun" ka jawab. 2026 note: Big Four (PwC One) confirm karta hai model
  ko, half-point deduct hai fail nahi (jurisdiction/buyer-size/expert reasons). (8) Regulatory Gate — safe
  default: preparation-and-recommendation, named insaan approve/act.

## 04 — Step 4 Aur 5: Beachhead Chuno, Chaar Gates Se Validate

- **Step 4:** ek beachhead — clearest knowledge, most checkable Worker, shortest contract-of-success path.
  Tie ho to **expert decide karta hai** (buyer evidence ki jagah nahi leta). Rights basis validation se
  pehle: public domain/open license/commercial license/written permission/replacement plan.
- **Step 5:** 60-90 din fixed window. **Chaaron gates, ya launch nahi** — order fixed: 2 gates pehle → slice
  → 2 gates baad. **Pehle 2:** expert signed (partnership agreement), rights basis likhit confirm. **Phir
  slice:** ek professional outcome poori tarah covered (outcome contract, invariants, decision map,
  exception list, derived procedure, checker, eval set) — Docusaurus publish. **Thin vs thick** (breadth
  ka farq, care ka nahi) vs **unfinished** (sirf clean cases). **Aakhri 2 gates:** named sponsor+drafted
  contract of success, real quoted money numbers. Launch ke baad bhi: **SoR pehle aata hai, hamesha.**

## 05 — Launch Ke Baad Aur Ayesha Ki Poori Kahani

- **Ek Source, Do Readers:** Markdown → Docusaurus (humans) + pgvector/MCP (agents), same governed source,
  double-written nahi. Content load hote hi governance set up karo (owner, review, versions, access).
  Country add karna growth nahi — naya jurisdiction = alag build, methodology hi shared.
- **Ayesha's story (full worked example):** Customer support (highest screen, Test 3 fail → service
  domain), E-commerce ops (Test 4 fail → parked), Accounting (7.5/8 tests, Test 3 = aunty ki 20-saal
  practice) → beachhead chosen. Gates walkthrough → slice built → partner reads it → contract of success
  (4hr → 40min) → real quotes → launch → first engagement delivered in 40 min.

## 06 — Longlist, Humare Apne Choices, Aur Aage Kahan Jaana Hai

- **6 Families longlist** (2026, Pakistan/Gulf-scored starting point): Money & Numbers (Sales 9, Accounting
  8, Tax 8...), Documents & Rules (Medical billing 7.5, Trade docs 7...), Operations (Customer support 9.5
  but Test-3-fails, E-commerce 8 Test-4-fails...), People (Recruitment 7.5...), Hard-Mode (bare prizes/slow
  doors — Telecom, Healthcare, Government), Shrinking Domains (translation/transcription/annotation —
  model already core-task karta hai, warning).
- **5 Patterns:** high scorers share 3 traits (main-number success, existing spend, written rules); low
  scorers share slow-door; beware domains model "kha jata hai"; screen≠tests (Family 3 fails tests, Family
  5 passes but slow); score agentic-era not SaaS-era.
- **Team's own choices:** Prover seat (Sales, launched), Moat seat (Accounting, validation — tax practice
  beachhead), Option seat (HR, conditional). Customer support/E-commerce = service domains. Corporate
  banking/Legal/Healthcare/Insurance/Manufacturing/Government = parked.
- **Path forward:** probe → thin → thick (3 states, order). Fixed build order: slice → gates 1&2 → System
  of Record thickens → expert twin → domain builder. Bina committed expert: Layers 1&4 se kamao pehle
  (service ladder), order rule sirf vertical-ladder ko govern karti hai.
