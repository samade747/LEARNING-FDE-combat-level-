## Yeh Course Kis Baare Mein Hai

Pichle course mein aap ne ek company khadi ki aur **fixed** team chalayi. Yeh course woh ek cheez add
karta hai jo woh company nahi kar sakti thi: **khud grow hona.**

**Poori ride, plain lafzon mein:**
- Naya kaam ata hai jo koi current Worker handle nahi kar sakta
- Company usay dekhti hai, naya hire propose karti hai, aapse "haan" maangti hai
- Aap us hire ko chota budget aur chota trial dete ho, trust karne se pehle
- Pass ho jaye to zyada room dete ho — sirf jitna job ko chahiye
- Kaam khamosh ho jaye to hire pause karte ho; wapis aaye to on karte ho
- Har step likha jata hai — mahino baad bhi pata chalta hai kisne kya kiya
- Poora gap-check schedule par chal sakta hai — company khud gaps dhoondti hai

## Recipe Vs Relationship

| Recipe (Aapke Sath Travel Karta Hai) | Relationship (Isi Company Mein Rehta Hai) |
| --- | --- |
| Capabilities description | Permissions aur scopes jo yahan hold karta hai |
| Probation plan jo prove kiya | Budget aur spend history |
| Engine choice | History aur woh gap jiske liye hire hua |
| | Kisay report karta hai |

**Poora point:** Chunki relationship company ke apne ledger mein rehta hai, aapke zehan mein nahi,
**recipe** hi woh cheez hai jo aap carry karte ho — ek workforce jiska reasoning aap handoff kar sako,
ab aap par depend nahi karta.
