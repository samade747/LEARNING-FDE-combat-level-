# 00 — Scenario 1: Gap Spot Karna (~12 min)

## Ek Idea Jispar Poora Course Khara Hai

> Jab workforce aisa kaam hit kare jo handle nahi kar sakti, woh aap par dump nahi karti. Aapka agent
> **gap spot karta hai, hire draft karta hai, aur usay ek aur board approval ki tarah aapke saamne
> lata hai** — wahi APPROVE/REJECT jo aap CEO ke liye use kar chuke ho. **Hiring ek function call ban
> jata hai, aap gate rehte ho.**

## 2 Decisions Jo Asal Lesson Hain

### 1. Kya Yeh Waqai Ek Gap Hai?

Real gap **3 signals** mein se dikhta hai, kam az kam **2** chahiye:
1. **Koi role isay cover nahi karta** — kaam kisi Worker ki job/skills se match nahi karta
2. **Yeh aata rehta hai, aur barhta hai** — one-off nahi
3. **Kisi ne kiya to woh ghalat wapis ata hai**

> **Trap:** Support mail barh rahi hai (signal 2 fire). Lekin agar koi team member chupke se woh
> tickets handle kar raha hai theek se, to signal 1 aur 3 fire nahi karte. **Yeh routing problem hai,
> capability gap nahi.** Yahan hire karna ek missed assignment cover karne ke liye headcount kharidna
> hai — jo aap har mahine pay karte hain.

### 2. Confirmed Gap Bhi Automatically Hire Nahi Hai

Standing Worker standing cost hai, isliye gap **ek decision kamata hai**, salary nahi:

- **HIRE** — durable, on-mission, aur high-volume ya risky ke wajah se permanent owner chahiye
- **ESCALATE** — consequential ya rare, insan hi decide kare (jaise legal threat)
- **QUEUE** — seasonal, bursty — abhi staff karna galti hai
- **DECLINE** — off-mission, ya abhi standing cost worth nahi

## 3 Candidates, 3 Jawab

| Gap | Fork | Wajah |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reader support | **HIRE** | Refunds/accounts chhuti hai, governed owner chahiye |
| Issue shipping karna | **QUEUE** | Real hai, lekin ek role ek waqt mein staff karo |
| Numbers parhna | **DECLINE for now** | 1000 subscribers par analyst apni cost nahi kama sakta |

**Draft karo:**
```text
On my Northwind board, open a few real pieces of new work: three
reader support questions..., one note that nobody tracks conversion,
one note that nobody owns sending the weekly issue. Judge each against
the three signals... tell me the fork for each, with why.
```

**Done jab:** Ek "Capability gap: reader support" issue board par ho, agent ne dikhaya ho kaunse 2
signals hold karte hain, aur aap ek sentence mein bata sako support kyun HIRE hai jabke shipping QUEUE
aur analytics DECLINE hai.
