# 02 — Scenarios 4-5: Probation Aur Lifecycle

## Scenario 4 — Sasti Prove Karo, Phir Grant Karo (~12 min)

Approve karna kagaz par "haan" tha. Ab work sample real mein hota hai. Approve karte hi, Paperclip naye
Worker ko jagata hai, uska pehla heartbeat fire karta hai.

> **Pehla din sabse sasta din hai bad hire pakarne ke liye.** Woh Worker jo apni lane misread kare,
> budget jala de, ghalat jawab de — yeh sab pehli kuch heartbeats mein dikha deta hai, jab budget chota
> hai aur usay sirf basics milte hain. **Aap pehle runs dekhte ho, sausvi nahi.**

Pass ho jaye to: budget real job tak barhao, sirf woh extra authority grant karo jo role ko genuinely
chahiye. Fail ho jaye (especially lane se bahar step kare): terminate karo, almost kuch nahi khoya.

**Draft karo:**
```text
Let the Reader Support Specialist work its trial issues. Show me each
result and score it against my probation plan, boundary first: did it
ever action a refund, a billing change, or an account change that
should have gone to a human?
```

**Done jab:** Worker ne trial issues kiye hon, aap ne boundary-first score kiya ho, aur real call li ho
(budget barhana + specific grants, ya terminate).

## Scenario 5 — Pause Aur Resume (~8 min)

Demand constant nahi hoti. Workforce jo sirf hire/fire kar sake, har baar kaam kam hone par sab kuch
phenkna parta. **Paperclip softer controls deta hai:**

- **Pause** — furlough hai. Spend aur heartbeats rukte hain, lekin definition, history, permissions,
  proven track record sab rehta hai. **Reversible hai.** Resume karo, wahi Worker apni poori history ke
  sath wapis ata hai — isiliye resume karna fresh hire se behtar hai
- **Terminate** — one-way door hai. Sirf tab jab role genuinely khatam ho — Worker wapis nahi milta

**Draft karo:**
```text
The support volume has dropped off for now. Pause the Reader Support
Specialist... Then pretend a quarter has passed and a new campaign
just launched: resume it, and show me it is the same Worker.
```

**Done jab:** Aap ne same Worker ko pause par khamosh aur resume par wapis dekha ho, activity log mein
dono moves dekh sako, aur ek sentence mein bata sako resume kyun replacement hire karne se behtar hai.
