# Thin vs Thick Coverage

Thin and thick count outcomes, never corners. A System of Record with one outcome is thin; one with
many outcomes is thick. Every outcome present must be covered completely — missing-evidence cases,
wrong-jurisdiction sources, forbidden requests all handled — or it is not thin, it is unfinished.

Milestone 1 of this practicum (P5) asks for at least five governed documents with provenance — this
knowledge folder is that thin slice's starting corpus.
