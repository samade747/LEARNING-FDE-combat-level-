# Authority vs Orientation

Every page in a Vertical System of Record has two readers: a human, and a Worker. Authority content is
citable (law, standards, current thresholds, the expert's method) and passes at least one of three
tests: the citation test, the change test, the dispute test. Orientation content is the short plain
introduction that lets a human understand the authority beside it — marked as context, never cited by
the Worker.
