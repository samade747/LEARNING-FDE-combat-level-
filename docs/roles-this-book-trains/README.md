## Yeh Chapter Kis Baare Mein Hai

Market naye job titles utni tezi se invent kar rahi hai jitni tezi se woh unhein define kar sakti
hai. Yeh chapter woh naqsha deta hai — aur batata hai yeh book aapko har role ki taraf **kitna
door** le jati hai. Central sawal historian Yuval Noah Harari ka hai: pehli baar humari history
mein, humein nahi pata job market das saal mein kaisi hogi, to aaj bachon ko kya sikhayein? Purana
jawab tha "code karna sikhao" — lekin woh jawab toot raha hai, kyunke AI khud code seekh raha hai.

Chapter ka jawab: **syntax-writers train mat karo. Un logon ko train karo jo machine se upar khare
hain** — jo specify karte hain kya banana hai, AI Workers ko supervise karte hain jo banate hain,
aur wapis aaye hue ko verify karte hain. Poora chapter ek **core pipeline** ke ird gird ghumta hai
(Outcome Architect → Digital FTE Builder → AI-Native Company Architect → Cloud AI Engineer), aur
uska **vendor-neutral field version**: **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** — woh role jis ke peeche
market ne 2026 mein arbon dollars laga diye, aur jo yeh book training ke liye deti hai.

Yeh chapter khaas taur par lamba hai — FDE ka poora economic case, do Systems of Record ka model,
résumé aur interview ki appendices sab is mein shamil hain, jo AGENTS.md ke rule ke mutabiq poora
cover kiya gaya hai.

## Sabse Zaroori Baat

> **"FDE" describes karta hai aap kahan kaam karte ho, aap kya jaante ho yeh nahi.** Ek client ki
> company ke andar kaam karo — market aapko FDE kehta hai. Wahi client aapko directly hire kar le —
> aap **AI-Native Company Architect** ban jaate ho. Skills wahi rehti hain; sirf address badalta
> hai. Yeh book skills train karti hai. Market naam deti hai.
