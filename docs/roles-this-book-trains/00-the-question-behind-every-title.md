# 00 — The Question Behind Every Title

> **Rokti hai galti:** "Maine socha AI era mein bhi mujhe bas achi coding sikhni hai" — jabke AI
> khud coding seekh raha hai, aur syntax likhna wohi skill hai jise machine sabse tezi se absorb
> kar rahi hai. Sawal yeh nahi ke kya likhein — sawal yeh hai ke kya **specify**, kya **supervise**,
> aur kya **verify** karein.

## Harari Ka Sawal

Historian Yuval Noah Harari ne modern education ka sabse mushkil sawal poocha hai: **pehli baar
history mein, humein bilkul nahi pata das saal baad job market kaisi hogi** — jiska matlab hai
humein nahi pata aaj bachon ko kya sikhana chahiye. Purana jawab simple tha: unhein code karna
sikhao. Woh jawab toot raha hai, kyunke AI khud code seekh raha hai, aur ek saal ke andar shayad
zyadatar insano se behtar code likhe. Syntax sikhana ek aisi skill sikhana hai jise machine real-time
mein absorb kar rahi hai.

Yeh page Harari ke sawal ka jawab hai. **Syntax-writers train mat karo. Un logon ko train karo jo
machine se upar khare hain:** jo decide karte hain kya banana hai, jo AI Workers ko supervise karte
hain jo woh banate hain, aur jo wapis aaye hue result ko verify karte hain. Syntax automate ho
sakti hai. **Judgment, specification, aur deployment nahi ho sakte** — yeh machine ke sath sath
upar chadhte jaate hain. Neeche har role isi ladder ki ek seat hai, aur is page ki demand data
dikhati hai ke market pehle se isi ke liye pay kar rahi hai — kyunke market bhi Harari ke sawal ka
jawab nahi de sakti, isliye woh un logon ko hire kar rahi hai jo de sakte hain.

## Teen Levels, Ek Line

Poora page ek sentence mein simat jata hai: yeh book aapko AI Workers (Digital FTEs) **banana**
sikhati hai, aur un Workers ko ek company mein **combine** karna jo unhi par chale (AI-Native
Company). Job market ne is sab kuch kar sakne wale insan ka ek garam naam nikala hai: **Forward
Deployed Engineer (FDE)**. Teen levels hain, har ek agle par khara hai: insan (aap), unit (AI Worker
jo aap banate ho), aur enterprise (company jo un Workers se ban ti hai).

**"FDE" batata hai aap kahan kaam karte ho, kya jaante ho yeh nahi.** Client ki company ke andar
kaam karo — market kehta hai FDE. Wahi company aapko hire kar le — wahi skills aapko unka
**AI-Native Company Architect** banati hain. Book skills train karti hai. Market naam deti hai.

## Baseline Jahan Se Sab Shuru Hota Hai

**Foundations — woh floor jo dono modes se pehle hai.** Har reader ek jagah se shuru hota hai:
browser tab mein, Foundations par — prompt karna, agentic work ki do document languages, code jo
kabhi khud nahi likhna, skills aur connectors, aur AI era mein sochna. Koi mode nahi, koi role
nahi, kuch install nahi. Yeh poore naqshe ka floor hai. Yahan sab shuru karte hain, yeh koi title
nahi.

**Mode 1 Practitioner — title nahi, ek proficiency.** Us floor par khare ho kar, aap ek general
agent use karte ho apna kaam tez karne ke liye: reason karna, likhna, code karna, analyze karna,
plan karna, ek outcome ship kar ke session close karna. Yeh Mode 1 hai, aur book isay har kisi ke
liye train karti hai — engineers Claude Code ya OpenCode se, domain experts Claude Cowork ya
OpenWork se, **Seven Principles of General Agent Problem Solving** ke tehat. Yeh woh pehla mode hai
jo har reader Mode 2 ke roles se pehle chalata hai, aur yeh aapko naya job nahi deta — jo job pehle
se hai, usmein aur sharp bana deta hai.
