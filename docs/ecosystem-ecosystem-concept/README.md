## Yeh Chapter Kis Baare Mein Hai

Yeh chapter poori ecosystem ki **origin story** hai — ek textbook, do tarah ke readers, aur ek pattern
jo har profession ke liye repeat ho sakta hai. *The AI Agent Factory* ab sirf ek book nahi rahi. Iske
gird ab ek tutor khada hai jo isay sikhata hai, ek developer agent jo isi se banata hai, ek publishing
pipeline jo isay naye audiences ke liye rewrite karega, aur ek business model jo isi par kamata hai. Yeh
sab mil kar ek **ecosystem** banate hain — parts ka ek set jo ek shared source par jeete aur badhte
hain. Aur is sab ke peeche ek hi decision hai, kehna aasan hai: humne textbook ko ek document ki tarah
treat karna chhod diya aur usay ek **System of Record** — ek governed source of truth jise insaan aur
AI agents dono parhte hain — bana diya.

Yeh page shuru se batata hai: textbooks ke zamane mein learning kaisi thi, AI chat teacher ke tor par
kyun fail hota hai, aur dono problems fix karne se ek pattern kaise bana jo aap apni profession ke liye
repeat kar sakte ho. Koi technical background nahi chahiye — agar aap ne kabhi kisi textbook se kuch
seekha hai, aap yeh poora page follow kar sakte ho.
