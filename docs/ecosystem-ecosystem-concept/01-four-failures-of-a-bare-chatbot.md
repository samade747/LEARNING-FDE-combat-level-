# 01 — Bare Chatbot Ke Chaar Failures

## Usay Sikhaane Ko Kaho, Aur Ghaur Se Dekho

Ek bare model se kaho *"teach me database management systems"* aur dekho asal mein kya hota hai. Ek ke
baad ek, chaar failures saamne aati hain:

1. **Jawab har baar badalte hain.** Kahan se shuru hoga? Kabhi files vs databases se, kabhi seedha first
   normal form se. Kitna gehra jaata hai? Yeh khud decide karta hai, har baar alag. Yeh koi bug nahi jo
   patch ka intezaar kar rahi ho. Yeh **wahi hai jo machine hai**: har token par weighted dice roll hote
   hain. Do baar poocho, do alag courses milte hain. Tees logon ki class ke saamne rakho, tees courses
   milenge.
2. **Koi shuruwaat aur koi ant nahi.** Textbook mein chapter one hota hai aur final chapter. Generic AI
   chat mein dono nahi hote. Aap mahine bhar baat kar sakte ho aur kabhi pata nahi chalega ke poora
   subject cover hua ya sirf ek chhota hissa baar baar repeat hua.
3. **Yeh koi learner record nahi rakhta.** Yeh track nahi karta aap ne kya complete kiya, kahan struggle
   kiya, kahan chhoda. Koi progress report nahi hai, kyunke aapki learning ke baare mein kahin kuch
   record hi nahi hota.
4. **Iska koi method nahi hai.** Iski **pedagogy** sirf woh hai jo usne training data se absorb kiya,
   kuch bhi usay direct nahi karta: koi method decide nahi karta kab poochna hai, kab correct karna hai,
   kab aage barhna hai. Ek achha teacher sikhane se pehle sawal poochta hai, taake aapka level pata chal
   sake. Model teaching ko usi tarah improvise karta hai jaise woh content ko karta hai.

Labs yeh sab jaante hain. Study Mode, guided learning, aur memory yehi hain — machine ke gird serious
product engineering. Lekin ek field seekhna ek journey hai. Ek course sainkron decisions hai ke aage
kya aana chahiye: kaunsa gap band karna hai, kaunsa concept ready hai, kaunsa rukna chahiye. Memory
aapko yaad rakhti hai — naam, projects, preferences. Yeh **learner record nahi rakhti**: aap ne kya
demonstrate kiya, kahan struggle kiya, aage kya ready hai. To yeh har decision aap par chhod deti hai.

## Ab Fair Raho — Yehi Test Textbook Ke Khilaf Bhi Jata Hai

Purani textbook mein governed sequence thi, ek named author ne chuna aur reviewers ne verify kiya, lekin
book ne kabhi sawal nahi poocha, kabhi struggle notice nahi kiya, kabhi yaad nahi rakha aap ne kahan
chhoda tha. Yeh kaam hamesha insaano ka tha: teacher line pakarta, gradebook record rakhta. Aur sab
jaante hain us arrangement ka sabse acha version: **ek teacher, ek learner, lesson aapke gird shaped.**
Jo afford kar sakta tha usne bilkul yehi hire kiya. Classrooms aur cohorts isliye hain kyunke best se
one-on-one kabhi scale nahi ho saka. Textbook ideal nahi thi. **Yeh us teacher ki woh copy thi jo hum
print kar sakte the.**

Is diagnosis ke dono hisse yaad rakho. Model mein governed source aur record ki kami hai. Textbook mein
teacher ki kami thi. Is chapter ka baaqi hissa dono fix karta hai.
