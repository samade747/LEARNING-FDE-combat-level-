# 00 — Textbook Ka Zamana, Aur Us Ka Sawal

## 2022 Tak, Knowledge Ki Ek Shape Thi

Databases seekhne ke liye aap textbook uthate the. Woh relational model normalization se pehle sikhati,
first normal form second se pehle, theory tuning se pehle. Aap sequence negotiate nahi karte the — **wo
sequence hi teaching thi.**

Aur ek book teen logon ko serve karti thi. **Student** us se seekhta. **Teacher** us se sikhata.
**Developer** usay desk par rakhta aur usi se banata. Jab student developer ban jata, yehi system ke
kaam karne ki nishani thi — ek source, sab log same definitions par aligned, same order mein.

Developer kya banata tha? Real businesses ke liye vertical systems: ek ERP, general ledger, inventory
system. Jaise Odoo, SAP, ya Oracle. In sab ne business ka official data store kiya, aur enterprise
software mein inka ek well-known naam hai — **systems of record**. System of record woh ek jagah hai
jahan kisi cheez ka authoritative version rehta hai. Jab Odoo aur ek spreadsheet disagree karein, Odoo
jeetta hai, kyunke Odoo system of record hai.

To purana pipeline yeh tha:

**Textbook → insaan seekhta hai → insaan developer ban jata hai → developer businesses ke liye systems
of record banata hai.**

Har step insaan ne, insaano ke liye kiya.

## Phir AI Ne Har Book Parh Li

ChatGPT aur Claude aaye — products jinka dimagh, ek **large language model**, duniya ki published
writing ke bohot bare hisse se train hua tha, textbooks samet. Model ne sainkron database books se
seekha hai: har normal form, har index strategy, har worked example. OpenAI ne Study Mode ship kiya,
Google ne apna Guided Learning banaya.

Isne humein apni khud ki book ke baare mein ek honest sawal poochne majboor kiya:

> Agar model ne pehle hi sau database textbooks parh li hain, to kisi ko ek aur ki zaroorat kyun hai?

Humne yeh sawal seriously liya. Agar honest jawab "koi wajah nahi" hota, to hum sabse pehle jaan-na
chahte the.

Is sawal ka jawab dhundne ka tareeqa simple tha: khud bare model se kaho *"teach me database management
systems"* aur dekho asal mein kya hota hai. Yehi agla part karta hai.
