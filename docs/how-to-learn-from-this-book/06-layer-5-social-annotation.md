# 06 — Layer 5: Social Annotation with Hypothesis

Ek technical book akele parhna khamosh hota hai, aur woh khamoshi aapke sawalon ko chhupa deti hai.
Isliye is book ka apna **margin** hai.

[Hypothesis](https://hypothes.is/), higher education mein use hone wala open social annotation
platform, **book ke har page mein built-in hai.** Kuch install nahi karna. Aap ek passage
highlight karte ho aur usay ek comment, sawal, explanation, link, ya example attach karte ho, aur
doosre readers wahan reply karte hain. Discussion **usi sentence ke bagal mein** hoti hai jahan
confusion hua — isi liye yeh kaam karta hai.

Yeh woh layer bhi hai jo book ko behtar banata hai. Margin mein har sawal ek signal hai ke kaunsa
paragraph apna kaam nahi kar raha.

## Kaise Use Karein

**1. Sidebar kholo.** Kisi bhi lesson ke upar toolbar mein, uske right end ke menu mein
**Notes & Highlights** choose karo. Har page ke right edge par ek chhota toolbar bhi pinned hai.

**2. Likhne se pehle parho.** Public annotations parhne ke liye account nahi chahiye. Highlighted
passages dikhate hain kahan notes attached hain.

**3. Likhne ke liye login karo.** Annotations banane ke liye free Hypothesis account chahiye,
sidebar se ek minute mein ban jata hai.

**4. Group check karo, phir passage select karo.** Top ka selector control karta hai kaun aapka
note dekhega: **Public** matlab is book ka har reader, aur ek **private group** matlab sirf woh
group. Phir text highlight karo aur **Annotate** (dusron ko nazar aane wala note) ya **Highlight**
(private mark, sirf aapko nazar aayega) choose karo.

**5. Likho, tag karo, post karo.** Annotations plain text, links, aur images le sakte hain. Kisi
doosre note ke neeche **Reply** click kar ke jawab do.

Ek cheez jo aakhir mein confuse karegi: agar page edit hone ke baad annotation post hui thi, to
annotation apna anchored text kho sakta hai aur **orphan** ban sakta hai. Kuch lost nahi hota — yeh
sirf ab kisi passage ki taraf point nahi karta.

> **Annotations ko tag karo:**
>
> | Tag | Iske Liye Use Karo |
> | --- | --- |
> | `question` | Jo samajh nahi aaya |
> | `correction` | Jo stale, ghalat, ya rename ho chuka |
> | `example` | Apni field se ek worked example |
> | `bridge` | Do courses ke darmiyan link |
> | `accounting`, `legal`, `medicine` | Aapka domain, taake professionals ek doosre ko dhoond sakein |
>
> Baad mein tag search karo aur aapke paas ek study guide hai jo kisi ko likhna nahi para.

## Paanch Annotations Jo Likhne Layak Hain

**1. Clarify.** Passage ko simpler language mein dobara kaho.

**2. Connect.** Isay kisi doosre course, field, ya apne tajurbe se jodo.

**3. Question.** Kuch unclear, incomplete, ya debatable naam lo.

**4. Apply.** Batao yeh idea real kaam mein kaise use hogi.

**5. Correct.** Kuch stale flag karo, evidence ke sath.

Sabse valuable panchon mein se honest sawal hai — aur woh jo log chhodne se sabse zyada dartay
hain. Phir bhi chhodo. Agle hafte us paragraph par pohanchne wale ke paas wahi sawal hoga, aur shayad
poochne ki himmat bhi kam.

**Kya na likhein.** "Great point" margin mein kuch nahi jodta. Half page highlight kar ke yeh na
bataana ke kyun matter karta hai bhi avoid karo.

## Teachers Ke Liye: Private Group Use Karo

Week one se pehle ek **private Hypothesis group** banao, aur students ko wahan post karwao, Public
mein nahi. Do cheezein follow karti hain: students ek aisi room mein zyada honest likhte hain jismein
door ho, aur aapko exactly pata chal jata hai kya sikhana hai — class se pehle group parho aur
wahin se session shuru karo.

**Shuru karne mein taqreeban do minute lagte hain.** Notes & Highlights walkthrough deta hai:
panel kholna, free account banana, aur sabse zaroori setting — Public par accidentally post karne
se pehle apna group choose karna.
