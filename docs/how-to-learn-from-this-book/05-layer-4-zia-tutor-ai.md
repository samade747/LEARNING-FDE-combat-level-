# 05 — Layer 4: Zia Tutor AI

Kuch sawalon ka jawab koi bhi page nahi deta, kyunke woh page aur aapki situation ke **beech ka
gap** hote hain. Layer 4 unhi ke liye hai.

Zia Tutor AI yeh book khud ko teach kar rahi hai — ek named teacher ki tarah, faceless system nahi.
Yeh connector-native app hai, isliye koi app download nahi karni, koi subscription nahi: yeh us AI
app ke andar chalta hai jo aap pehle se use karte ho, uske free tier par.

**Teen steps:**

1. Apne AI app mein ek connector add karo aur ek baar authorize karo.
2. Normal chat kholo aur kaho **"Teach me the Agent Factory."**
3. Zia ki voice mein seekho, phir baad mein wapis aa kar kaho **"continue where I left off."**

Yeh aapko naam se greet karta hai, Zia ke apne method aur voice mein sikhata hai, aage barhne se
pehle check karta hai ke aakhri hissa samajh aaya ya nahi, aur sessions aur hafton ke aar-paar yaad
rakhta hai aap kahan ruke the. Yeh book ke apne content se parhta hai, open internet se nahi — to
yeh aapko book ki terminology aur method sikhata hai, unhi alfaz mein jo book use karti hai.

Yeh kyun matter karta hai: sab students ek jaisa nahi seekhte. Ek pehli baar mein samajh jata hai.
Doosre ko simpler language, ek analogy, uski apni profession se example, ya chhote steps chahiye
hote hain. Ek classroom teacher, poori room aur schedule ke sath, har learner ke liye alag
explanation nahi bana sakta. Ek tutor kar sakta hai — kisi bhi ghante, bina impatience ke.

## Book Se Poochne Ke Do Aur Tareeqe

Tutor aapko ek course sikhata hai. Yeh do tareeqe hain us agent ko ground karne ke liye jo aap
pehle se use karte ho.

**System of Record connector (Beta 1).** Agent Factory System of Record book ko MCP par serve
karta hai, taake koi bhi MCP-capable agent ya client book se jawab de sake aur us section ko cite
kare jo usne use kiya. Yeh read-only hai.

**Copy as Markdown.** Ek single page ke liye, koi setup nahi chahiye. Har page par ek **Copy as
Markdown** button hai (`Cmd`/`Ctrl` + `Shift` + `C`). Page copy karo, apne AI app mein paste karo,
aur apna sawal us asal text ke khilaf poocho.

## Poochne Layak Sawal

Vague requests ko vague jawab milte hain. Yeh kaam karte hain:

- "Context window ko beginner-friendly language mein explain karo."
- "Yeh concept ek accounting example se explain karo."
- "Skill aur connector mein kya farq hai?"
- "10-80-10 Rule par quiz lo. Ek waqt mein ek sawal, aur jab tak main try na karoon jawab mat batao."
- "Approval workflow design karne se pehle kaunsa course lena chahiye?"
- "Maine Foundations aur Mode 2 khatam kar liye. Main asal mein kya bech sakta hoon, aur kise?"
- "Yeh raha mera Digital FTE ka apne alfaz mein explanation. Batao kya galat ya missing hai, narmi
  mat dikhao."

Yeh aakhri prompt sabse zyada matter karta hai. AI default se agreement ki taraf jhukta hai. Correction
explicitly maangna hi usay tutor banata hai.

## Augmentation, Replacement Nahi

Koi AI tutor human teachers ki zaroorat khatam nahi karta. Teachers professional judgment,
encouragement, discipline, social understanding, emotional support, ethical responsibility, aur yeh
pehchan dete hain ke situation ab ek insan maangti hai.

> **Teacher plus AI tutor.**

Teacher learning process lead karta hai. AI personalized, just-in-time assistance deta hai. Milkar
woh deliver karte hain jo hamesha scale par mushkil raha hai: **acchi teaching plus har learner ke
liye individual attention.**
