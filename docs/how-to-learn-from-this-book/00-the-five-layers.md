# 00 — The Five Layers

> **Rokti hai galti:** "Maine poora reference shelf front-to-back parhna shuru kar diya" — jabke
> woh shelf sirf **consult-on-demand** ke liye bana hai, kabhi khatam na hone wala. Ya: "Maine sirf
> courses skim kar liye" — jabke skill closing exercises se banti hai, page palatne se nahi.

## Do Kism Ke Relationships

Learning system ki paanch layers hain, aur har ek se aapka **relationship** alag hona chahiye. Yeh
paanch difficulty levels nahi — yeh paanch alag rishte hain:

**Content layers** — sthir baithti hain, sabke liye same, aapka wait karti hain:

| Layer | Yeh Kya Hai | Aap Kya Karte Ho | Kab "Done" Ho |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Introductions** | Orientation pages — yeh kya hai, kise train karta hai, book kis argument par khara hai | **Parho** ek baar, order mein. 2-3 ghante | Digital FTE ko apne alfaz mein define kar sako |
| **2. Crash Courses** | Chhote, high-leverage courses jo 80% daily agentic work cover karte hain | **Practice** karo sequence mein, ek waqt mein ek, keyboard ke sath | Course jo maange woh ship kar chuke ho |
| **3. References** | Reference shelf — glossary, cheatsheets, catalogs | **Consult** karo jab real kaam gap dikhaye. Kabhi front-to-back nahi | Kabhi nahi — saalon tab mein khula rehta hai |

**Interactive layers** — jawab dete hain, do opposite tareeqon se:

| Layer | Yeh Kya Hai | Aap Kya Karte Ho | Kab "Done" Ho |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **4. Zia Tutor AI** | Book khud ko teach kar rahi hai, ek named teacher ki tarah jo yaad rakhta hai aap kahan ruke the | **Poocho** jab bhi koi page samajh na aaye | Kabhi nahi — yeh teacher hai, page nahi |
| **5. Social Annotation** | Wahi shared margin — Hypothesis se har page par built-in | **Discuss** karo, passage ke bagal mein | Kabhi nahi — margin barhta rehta hai jaise readers aate hain |

Layer 4 turant, privately, one-to-one jawab deta hai aur **aapko** sikhata hai — beginner ka sawal
raat do baje bina kisi ke dekhe poocho. Layer 5 slow, publicly, many-to-many jawab deta hai aur
**sabko** sikhata hai — aapka sawal us page par reh jata hai agle reader ke liye jo wahi sentence
par atak jaye.

**Tutor attention scale karta hai. Margin accumulate hota hai. Dono ko use karo, alag lamho ke
liye.**
