# 04 — Layer 3: The References

Socho: aap ek build ke beech mein ho. Screen par ek term real kaam kar rahi hai us sentence mein,
aur aapko uska matlab nahi pata. Aapko course nahi chahiye. Aapko **ek jawab** chahiye, aur phir
wapis build par jaana hai.

Yehi **references** ke liye hain: [Glossary](../glossary), [Cheatsheets](../cheatsheets), aur
Which AI Employees Should You Use in 2026. Yeh **consult-on-demand** layer hai.

Aap yahan **continuous** waqt sabse kam kharch karoge, kyunke yeh layer front-to-back parhne ke
liye design nahi hui. Aap ek specific problem, sawal, ya gap se yahan aate ho. Aap side se aa kar,
jo chahiye woh le kar, chale jaate ho. Aap yeh tab khologe jab:

- Current page assume kare ke aap koi term jaante ho
- Build ke beech mein koi command, flag, ya pattern dekhna ho
- AI workers compare kar ke decide karna ho kaunsa use karein

Crash Courses aapko kaam par lagati hain. References aapko kaam par rakhti hain jab koi detail
missing ho jaaye.

## Just-In-Time Education

Traditional education ek fixed sequence follow karti hai. Students hafton, mahino, ya saalon mein
ek broad subject padhte hain — aksar bohat pehle isse ke unhein pata ho ke woh knowledge kab use
hogi.

AI ek doosra model mumkin banata hai: **just-in-time education.** Aap ek concept us waqt seekhte ho
jab woh useful ban jaye. Poora subject study karne ke bajaye, seedha wahan jaate ho jo current
problem maangta hai — jo ek short explanation, refresher, worked example, ya sahi course dhoondne
ki madad ho sakti hai.

**Yeh pattern ek layer tak mehdood nahi.** Zia Tutor AI se poochna ek just-in-time move hai. Wahi
margin parhna jis paragraph ne abhi confuse kiya. Asal kaam mein, aap aksar teeno ko ek hi das
minute mein use karoge: reference detail deta hai, tutor woh hissa explain karta hai jo follow
nahi hua, aur margin batata hai wahan aur kaun atka.

## On Demand Matlab Foundation-Free Nahi

Aap agent architecture nahi samajh sakte agar pehle na jaante ho agent, tool, context window,
specification, aur workflow kya hain. Aur bhi mushkil: foundation ke bina aap yeh nahi pehchan
sakte ke kaunsa sawal poochna hai — aur ek galat sawal se poocha gaya AI fluently aur confidently
jawab dega. Isliye book Layer 1 aur Foundations courses se shuru hoti hai.

> **Foundation first, then depth on demand.**

Foundation aapko problem pehchanne deta hai. Just-in-time learning aapko usay solve karne ka
knowledge deta hai.
