# 03 — Personal Agents Aur The Two-Layer Model

AI Workers se kaam hota hai. **Identic AI** woh tareeqa hai jis se insan is workforce ko apni taraf se
increasingly direct, govern, aur interface karenge. Agent Factory role-based AI Workers manufacture
karta hai jo tasks execute karein, workflows coordinate karein, aur scale par verified outcomes deliver
karein — lekin insan hi woh **principal** rehta hai jo purpose, values, constraints, aur accountability
define karta hai.

Identic AI ek naya personal layer add karta hai: ek **self-sovereign agent** — individual ki milkiyat,
platform ki nahi — jo aapke context, judgment, aur preferences ko samajhta hai, aur human intent ko
enterprise ke across delegated action mein translate kar sakta hai. Is model mein AI workforce
**execution fabric** hai, jabke identic AI insan ka **representative aur orchestration layer** hai —
jo logon ko routine execution khud karne ke bajaye direction supervise karne deta hai. Isliye future
firm do connected layers par operate karegi: AI Workers **AI Workforce Layer** ke andar, aur personal
agents **Edge Layer** par — insan dono jagah intent set karte hain aur outcomes verify karte hain.

## The Two-Layer Model

| Layer | What It Is | Who It Serves | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Edge Layer** | Personal identic agents | The individual | Human intent translate karta hai, AI Workers ko delegate karta hai, principal ki taraf se govern karta hai |
| **AI Workforce Layer** | Role-based AI Workers | The enterprise | Tasks execute karta hai, workflows coordinate karta hai, verified outcomes deliver karta hai |

Koi bhi layer akele kaam nahi karti. Industrialized workforce ke bina personal agents sirf digital
assistants hain jinke paas command karne wala koi nahi. Personal agents ke bina AI Workforce Layer
insano ko wapis manual orchestration mein dhakel deti hai. **Two-Layer Model** hi Agent Factory thesis
ko complete banata hai: core mein ek industrialized workforce, edge par human sovereignty, aur dono ke
darmiyan contract-language ki tarah **specs**.

> **Companion argument:** Yeh thesis batati hai *kaun* kaam karta hai aur workforce *kaise* banta hai.
