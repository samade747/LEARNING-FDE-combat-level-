## Yeh Chapter Kis Baare Mein Hai

Yeh poori book ka **sabse zaroori page** hai — book khud kehti hai "agar Layer 1 mein sirf teen
pages parhne hain, to Thesis unmein se ek honi chahiye." Yahan woh core vocabulary bantee hai jis
par baqi har chapter khara hai: **Digital FTE**, **AI-Native Company**, **Agent Factory**,
**Two-Layer Model**, **10-80-10 Rule**, aur **Seven Invariants**.

Central claim yeh hai: AI era mein sabse valuable companies software nahi bechengi — woh **AI
employees manufacture karengi**. SaaS era ne subscriptions bechi. Agent Factory era **results**
bechti hai. Insaan intent define karte hain, agents execute karte hain, insaan outcomes verify
karte hain. Chapter yeh bhi dikhata hai ke agents ab payment rails ke through **economic actors**
ban rahe hain — apne aap compute khareedna, data procure karna, services le lena — aur ek company
kaise **Seven Invariants** ke tehat khadi hoti hai jo kisi bhi specific vendor product se independent
rehti hain.

## Sabse Zaroori Baat

> **"The Factory builds the Company; the Company employs Workers; the Workers run against the
> system of record."** Yeh ek sentence poori thesis ka spine hai. Invariants sthir rehti hain, jo
> unhein realize karte hain woh named products badalte rehte hain — building khadi rehti hai chahe
> furniture badal jaye.
