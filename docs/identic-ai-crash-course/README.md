## Yeh Course Kis Baare Mein Hai

Pichle teenon courses mein aap ne ek AI-native company banayi: Workers jo asal kaam karte hain, ek
management layer jo hire/oversee karti hai, aur ek workforce jo khud apne gaps spot karke naye hires
propose karti hai. Yeh sab acha hai, lekin ek catch hai: **har naya hire, har bara refund, har budget
overrun ab bhi aap se approval maangta hai.** 4 Workers ke sath yeh chand taps hain. 400 Workers ke
sath yeh sainkron approvals per week — aap khud woh bottleneck ban jate ho jo system ne hatana tha.

Yeh course aapko aapka apna delegate deta hai: ek personal AI twin jisne seekh liya hai aap kaise
decide karte ho, aur ab wohi tarike se act karti hai. Uska naam **Claudia** hai. Pehle aap usay
banate ho, phir usay loose chorte ho. Course ka title dono halves bolta hai: aap apni **Identic AI**
(apna twin) banate ho, phir woh aapki **chief of staff** banti hai (uska job).

**Poori ride, plain lafzon mein:** Claudia ek regular heartbeat par khud jagti hai, aapki company ki
approval queue parhti hai, har item ke liye sochti hai "kya main yeh khud approve karti, ya aap dekhna
chahenge?", routine wale khud clear kar deti hai (signed, ledger mein note ke sath), important wale
aapke chat app par bhej deti hai apni opinion ke sath, phir wapis so jati hai. Aap kabhi bhi per-decision
loop mein nahi hote — ek baar rules set karte ho, uska loop on karte ho, aur apna hafta clear hota
dekhte ho.

## Do Cheezein Jo Alag Hain

| Kya Woh Hai (Act 1) | Kya Woh Karti Hai (Act 2) |
| --- | --- |
| Aapka twin — jo aap ki tarah decide karna seekhti hai | Aapki chief of staff — jo apne heartbeat par khud queue clear karti hai |
| Wired to nothing, sirf reasoning | Signed identity + bounded mandate ke sath real company govern karti hai |

**Poora point:** yeh do alag cheezein hain. Pehle twin banao (Act 1), phir uska loop on karo (Act 2).
Jab tak Act 2 shuru nahi hota, koi company tasveer mein nahi aati.
