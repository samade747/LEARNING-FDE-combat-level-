# 02 — Part 3: Ek Roster, Saaf Roles (Concepts 6-7)

## Concept 6 — Team Ka Ek Roster Hota Hai

Human-agent team ek roster, ek artifact set, ek workspace share karti hai. To roster likho: **har
member, human aur agent, aur har ek kya own karta hai.**

Roster ek conversation ka output hai, pehle se guess nahi. Yeh aapka **Roles Taxonomy** aur **Digital
FTE taxonomy** hai, ek team ke liye concrete banaya hua.

## Concept 7 — Role Ek Card Hai, Aur Ek Skill File

Har agent ko ek **role card** milta hai: kya own karta hai, kya **nahi** karta, tools/access, kaam kaise
check hota hai, kab insan tak escalate karta hai. Scope **"nahi karta"** utna hi zaroori hai jitna
**"karta hai"** — fuzzy edges wala agent doosron ke kaam mein drift karta hai.

**Tools naam lo** — bina tools ke role sirf title hai. Har role ko sirf woh access do jo chahiye
(least privilege).

**Role ko skill file ki tarah likho.** Yeh woh move hai jo book ke frameworks ko click karwati hai:
agent ke role ko skill mein define karo, role **portable** ban jata hai — organization mein koi bhi
usi type ka doosra agent khada kar sakta hai. **Roles org chart ke boxes nahi, copy-hone-wali skills ban
jate hain.**

**Human-only roles explicit rakho.** Insan wahi threads mein kaam karta hai jahan agents karte hain,
lekin sirf woh roles hold karta hai jo sirf insan hold kar sakta hai — consequential calls, jinki
judgment ki cost hai.

> **Jab ek agent ko doosra agent chahiye:** Kabhi kabhi kaam ek worker ke liye bara hota hai, aur ek
> **lead** agent teammates spawn karta hai. Yeh instinct sahi hai — agla course
> ([Workforce with Paperclip](../workforce-with-paperclip-crash-course/README.md)) isay automate karta
> hai. Aapka roster/role cards uske inputs hain.

**Draft karo:**
```text
Draft a team roster. List every member, human and agent. For each:
role, who owns it, tools and access it needs, autonomy level. Mark
the roles only a human should hold. Then write a full role card for
[my worker]: owns, does NOT own, tools/access, how its work is
verified, escalation trigger.
```

**Check karo:** Har member ka owner aur "does not own" hai? Har agent ka ek clear escalation trigger
hai? Agar 2 members same task claim kar saken, scopes sharp nahi hain.
