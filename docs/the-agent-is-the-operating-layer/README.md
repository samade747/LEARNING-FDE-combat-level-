## Yeh Chapter Kis Baare Mein Hai

Yeh chapter book ke **teen bare arguments** mein se dusra hai (pehla Thesis hai — company kya "own" karegi; teesra [What You Carry In](../what-you-carry-in/README.md) hai — practitioner individually kya "own" kar sakta hai). Yeh wala argument **interface** ke baare mein hai: jab agent kaam khud karne lag jaye, to woh software jo humne kholna seekha tha, uska kya banta hai?

Shuruaat hoti hai 1 June 2026 ko — NVIDIA ne Taipei mein RTX Spark chip launch kiya, aur uske sath Jensen Huang ne ek line boli: *"For forty years, you launched apps. Click. Type. With RTX Spark and Microsoft Windows, you ask — and the PC does the work."* Yeh sirf ek chip launch nahi tha. Yeh 40 saal purani "human operates the computer" arrangement ke khatam hone ki ghoshna thi.

Chapter ka poora structure do "deaths" par khara hai:

1. **Chhoti maut** — SaaS ka destination hona khatam ho jata hai (SaaSpocalypse). Yeh already ho raha hai aur samajhna aasan hai.
2. **Bari maut** — Personal computer khud, jaisa hum use karte hain (apps + OS jise hath se chalate hain), obsolete hota ja raha hai. Yeh mushkil claim hai, aur poora chapter isay build karta hai.

Dono ka mechanism same hai: kaam ko teen parts mein todo (record, capability, interface), aur jab agent capability ko seedha access kar leta hai, interface layer bypass ho jati hai. SaaS ke case mein interface = app ki UI. PC ke case mein interface = poora desktop/OS shell.

Aakhir mein chapter honest bhi hai — yeh nahi kehta ke kal se sab log apps chhod denge. Yeh cost, trust, reliability, aur "hybrid model" jaisi objections ko seriously leta hai, aur batata hai ke yeh transition **years mein hoga, months mein nahi**, aur pehle un jagah shuru hoga jahan kaam digital, bounded, aur recoverable hai (jaise knowledge work aur software development) — bilkul jaisay is repo ka apna FDE training scope hai.

## Sabse Zaroori Baat

> **"The old era was: humans use apps. The new era is: humans delegate work."**
> Interface ab screen full of icons nahi — **interface khud agent hai.** Yeh shift Windows ya macOS ko khatam nahi karti (woh plumbing ban jate hain), balke us "human operator" role ko khatam karti hai jo hum 40 saal se nibha rahe the. Aur yeh sirf tab hoga jab governance — memory, permissions, audit trail, liability — solid ho, na ke sirf UI ki nostalgia khatam ho.
