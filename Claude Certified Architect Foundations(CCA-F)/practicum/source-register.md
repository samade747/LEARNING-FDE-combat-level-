**Tier legend** (source hierarchy — [[ecosystem-designing-the-vertical-sor]]):
- **Tier 1** — authoritative / binding (statute, regulation, official SRO)
- **Tier 2** — official guidance / interpretation (FBR circulars, FAQs)
- **Tier 3** — secondary (practitioner commentary, news) — orientation only, never the citation

**Rule:** ek claim ki citation hamesha highest available tier se. Tier 3 sirf context ke liye.
